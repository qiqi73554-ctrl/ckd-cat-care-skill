Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) is among the most frequent and clinically relevant metabolic disorders in cats, affecting a substantial proportion of the adult and geriatric feline population. The prevalence of CKD increases with age, being estimated at 20–50% in cats over 10 years of age. The etiology of this disorder involves genetic predisposition, dietary factors, as well as exposure to toxins or concomitant diseases that may accelerate the progression of renal lesions. The present study synthesizes data from the scientific literature together with clinical-epidemiological analyses performed on a cohort of 120 cats over a four-year period. The results highlighted an overall prevalence of CKD in the studied cohort, with a significantly higher frequency in cats over nine years of age, particularly affecting purebred animals. A slightly higher prevalence was observed in males compared to females, while reproductive status indicated an increased risk of CKD in neutered patients.

在兽医实践中遇到的内科疾病中，猫慢性肾病（CKD）是最重要的疾病之一，对伴侣动物的寿命和生活质量都有重大影响。该疾病的特点是功能性肾单位的进行性和不可逆丧失，导致肾小球滤过率（GFR）下降以及体内氮质废物的积累[1]。猫慢性肾病（CKD）的流行率因群体和诊断标准而差异很大。在美国和欧洲进行的流行病学研究显示，普通猫群体的总体患病率为1%–3%，但在老年猫中可能超过30%[2,3]。某些品种，如波斯猫和暹罗猫，也似乎更易患此病，这表明其发病机制可能存在遗传成分[4]。
Among internal disorders encountered in veterinary practice, feline CKD represents one of the most important conditions, exerting a major impact on both longevity and quality of life in companion animals. The disease is characterized by the progressive and irreversible loss of functional nephrons, resulting in a decline in glomerular filtration rate and the accumulation of nitrogenous waste products in the body [1]. The prevalence of feline CKD varies considerably according to population and diagnostic criteria. Epidemiological studies conducted in the United States and Europe have reported an overall prevalence of 1–3% in the general cat population, but this may exceed 30% in geriatric patients [2,3]. Certain breeds, such as Persians and Siamese, also appear to be more susceptible to the disease, suggesting a genetic component in its pathogenesis [4].
与慢性肾脏病（CKD）相关的临床体征通常在肾功能已经显著受损时才会出现。最常见的表现包括多尿、多饮、体重下降、厌食和嗜睡 [5,6]。这种缓慢的发病方式使得早期诊断困难，但对于制定适当的治疗方案至关重要。因此，在早期阶段识别肾功能不全不仅需要仔细的临床评估，还需要整合实验室检查（肾功能谱、全血细胞计数、血清生化）和影像学检查，如腹部超声。近期生物标志物的进展强调了对称二甲基精氨酸（SDMA）的作用，这是一种敏感的血清标志物，可以比肌酐更早反映肾小球滤过率（GFR）的下降 [7,8]。因此，目前将SDMA、肌酐和尿素联合使用，已成为评估猫肾功能的重要工具，补充临床和影像学评估。通过监测SDMA和肌酐进行早期诊断，可以及时进行治疗干预，从而可能改善预后 [9]。
Clinical signs associated with CKD often develop late, when renal function is already significantly compromised. The most common manifestations include polyuria, polydipsia, weight loss, anorexia, and lethargy [5,6]. This insidious onset makes early diagnosis difficult, yet crucial for establishing an appropriate therapeutic plan. Consequently, the identification of renal insufficiency at an early stage requires not only careful clinical evaluation, but also the integration of laboratory tests (renal profile, complete blood count, serum biochemistry) and imaging studies such as abdominal ultrasonography. Recent advances in biomarkers have highlighted the role of symmetric dimethylarginine (SDMA), a sensitive serum marker that reflects a decline in glomerular filtration rate earlier than creatinine [7,8]. Thus, the combined use of SDMA, creatinine, and urea currently represents an essential tool for evaluating renal function in cats, complementing clinical and imaging assessments. Early diagnosis, through the monitoring of SDMA and creatinine, allows timely therapeutic interventions that may improve prognosis [9].
此外，由国际肾脏兴趣协会（IRIS）制定的建议 [10] 为猫和狗的慢性肾病（CKD）分期提供了标准化的框架，使疾病进展的监测和个性化治疗策略的实施成为可能。慢性肾病（CKD）的病因多因素、亚临床阶段长以及进展速度可变，使其成为猫医学领域的一大挑战。迄今为止，一些流行病学和队列研究已经调查了来自不同地理区域（如英国、北美和日本）猫的CKD患病率及风险因素。然而，中欧和东欧的区域数据仍然稀缺。
In addition, the recommendations developed by the International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) [10] provide a standardized framework for staging CKD in cats and dogs, enabling both the monitoring of disease progression and the implementation of personalized therapeutic strategies. The multifactorial etiology of chronic kidney disease, the long subclinical course, and the variable rate of progression, make CKD a major challenge in feline medicine. To date, several epidemiological and cohort studies have investigated CKD prevalence and risk factors in cats from various geographic regions (e.g., the United Kingdom, North America, and Japan). However, regional data from Central and Eastern Europe remain scarce.

All statistical analyses were performed using SPSS v.27.0 (IBM Corp., Armonk, NY, USA). A significance threshold of p < 0.05 was adopted.

In addition to the descriptive distribution of feeding practices, it is important to note that optimal dietary management for CKD involves more than protein restriction. According to current clinical nutrition evidence, renal-supportive diets should include: controlled but highly digestible protein levels; restricted phosphorus content (<0.6–0.8% DM) to reduce renal mineral load; increased omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) to alleviate glomerular hypertension and inflammation; reduced sodium to minimize systemic hypertension; balanced potassium levels to correct CKD-associated hypokalemia; enhanced B-complex vitamin supplementation due to increased urinary losses; and antioxidants such as vitamin E and β-carotene to mitigate oxidative stress. Relationship Between Biochemical Markers (SDMA vs. Creatinine)
SDMA与肌酐之间存在正相关且统计学上显著的相关性（Spearman's ρ = 0.84，p < 0.001）。线性回归得出SDMA = 6.41 6.77 ×肌酐（mg/dL），R2 = 0.88（斜率95% CI 6.31–7.22;截距CI 5.09–7.74），表明88%的SDMA变异由肌酐解释。斜率显著大于零（p < 0.001）。调整年龄后，相关性保持显著（部分ρ = 0.73，p < 0.001），表明SDMA与肌酐关系并非仅由年龄驱动。Bland–Altman分析显示测量值与回归预测SDMA值间偏差最小（−0.00 μg/dL）且一致范围狭窄（−4.48至4.48 μg/dL），支持测量范围内良好一致。
A positive and statistically significant correlation was identified between SDMA and creatinine (Spearman’s ρ = 0.84, p < 0.001). Linear regression yielded SDMA = 6.41 + 6.77 × Creatinine (mg/dL) with R2 = 0.88 (95% CI for slope 6.31–7.22; 95% CI for intercept 5.09–7.74), indicating that 88% of SDMA variability was explained by creatinine. The slope was significantly greater than zero (p < 0.001). After adjustment for age, the association remained significant (partial ρ = 0.73, p < 0.001), suggesting that the SDMA–creatinine relationship is not driven by age alone. Bland–Altman analysis showed minimal bias (−0.00 μg/dL) and narrow limits of agreement (−4.48 to +4.48 μg/dL) between measured and regression-predicted SDMA values, supporting good agreement across the measurement range.
图形分析（图9a,b）显示，在中等肌酐水平（2–4 mg/dL）时，SDMA值的离散程度较大，与个体间肾小球滤过率（GFR）和代谢适应的差异一致。在较高肌酐值（>5 mg/dL）时，这一关系变得更加线性和平行，反映出肾单位的严重丧失和肾脏排泄功能的下降。在临床上，这些发现强调了这两种生物标志物的互补诊断价值：SDMA可作为肾小球滤过率（GFR）下降的早期指标，而肌酐仍然是既定肾功能不全的确认标志。对这两项指标的联合评估可提高诊断的敏感性和猫慢性肾病（CKD）分期的准确性。
Graphical analysis (Figure 9a,b) showed greater dispersion of SDMA values at moderate creatinine levels (2–4 mg/dL), consistent with inter-individual differences in glomerular filtration rate and metabolic adaptation. At higher creatinine values (>5 mg/dL), the relationship became more linear and parallel, reflecting advanced nephron loss and reduced renal clearance. Clinically, these findings emphasize the complementary diagnostic value of the two biomarkers: SDMA serves as an early indicator of reduced glomerular filtration rate, while creatinine remains a confirmatory marker for established renal insufficiency. The combined assessment of both parameters enhances diagnostic sensitivity and staging accuracy in feline CKD.

Discussion Chronic kidney disease (CKD) remains a leading cause of morbidity in geriatric cats and represents a major clinical and welfare concern in companion animal practice. The findings of this study reinforce previous epidemiological data indicating that CKD is strongly age-associated, progressive, and irreversible.
在我们的队列中，超过89%的慢性肾病（CKD）病例发生在七岁以上的猫中，这支持了将CKD视为一种退行性、与衰老相关的疾病，而非急性发作性病理的观点，这与Polzin（2011）[1]和Marino等（2014）[3]的研究一致。临床特征以多尿、多饮和体重下降为主，这与之前的报道[2,5,10,14,15,16]一致，确认临床识别通常与生化功能失代偿同时发生。然而，这些症状非特异性，并且与其他内分泌或代谢性疾病（如糖尿病、甲状腺功能亢进）重叠，强调了在老年猫中进行综合生化筛查的诊断价值。Elliott和Barber（1998）[5]同样报告，非特异性临床迹象可能引导临床医生对疑似肾病病例进行生化检查。
In our cohort, over 89% of CKD cases occurred in cats older than seven years, supporting the concept of CKD as a degenerative, senescence-associated disorder rather than an acute-onset pathology, in agreement with Polzin (2011) [1] and Marino et al. (2014) [3]. The clinical profile—dominated by polyuria, polydipsia, and weight loss—is consistent with previous reports [2,5,10,14,15,16], confirming that clinical recognition typically coincides with biochemical decompensation. However, these signs are nonspecific and overlap with other endocrine or metabolic disorders (e.g., diabetes mellitus, hyperthyroidism), emphasizing the diagnostic value of integrated biochemical screening in older cats. Elliott and Barber (1998) [5] similarly reported that nonspecific clinical signs may guide clinicians toward biochemical investigation in suspected renal cases.
年龄被确认是慢性肾病（CKD）风险的主要决定因素，这与Pérez-López等人（2019）[13]及其他流行病学研究[17,18]的结果一致。超过十岁后患病率的上升凸显了对老年猫进行年度或半年一次的生化及尿液监测的重要性，这有助于早期发现并改善临床结果。我们研究人群的年龄分布与Brown等人（2014）[12]描述的患病率范围密切一致，他们发现10岁以上的猫中有30–40%患有CKD。品种倾向性也显而易见，纯种猫（波斯猫、暹罗猫、英国短毛猫）受影响的比例高于杂交猫，这一点与Marino等人的发现一致。
Age was confirmed as the primary determinant of CKD risk, consistent with Pérez-López et al. (2019) [13] and other epidemiological studies [17,18]. The rising prevalence beyond ten years of age highlights the importance of annual or biannual biochemical and urinary monitoring in senior cats, which can facilitate early detection and improve clinical outcomes. The age distribution in our population aligns closely with the prevalence ranges described by Brown et al. (2014) [12], who identified CKD in 30–40% of cats over 10 years. Breed predisposition was evident, with higher proportions of purebred cats (Persian, Siamese, British Shorthair) affected compared with mixed-breed cats, a finding consistent with Marino et al.

生化分析证实了肌酐和SDMA的诊断相关性，其中后者在早期肾功能障碍的敏感性方面更为优越。观察到的SDMA与肌酐之间的高度相关性（ρ = 0.84, p < 0.001; R² = 0.88）支持同时使用这两种生物标志物。即使轻度肾小球滤过率（GFR）下降，SDMA也会升高，因此在明显氮质血症出现之前提供了宝贵的诊断窗口 [7,13,27,28]。Hall 等人（2014）[7] 报告称，在大多数早期慢性肾病（CKD）猫中，SDMA升高早于肌酐增加，而 Pérez-López 等人（2019）[13] 进一步确认了SDMA作为早期生物标志物的作用，与肌肉质量无关。在对七岁以上猫进行健康筛查的常规方案中加入SDMA检测，可显著提高早期CKD的检测率。
Biochemical analysis confirmed the diagnostic relevance of both creatinine and SDMA, the latter providing superior sensitivity for early renal dysfunction. The strong correlation observed between SDMA and creatinine (ρ = 0.84, p < 0.001; R2 = 0.88) supports the concurrent use of both biomarkers. SDMA increases even with mild GFR decline and thus offers a valuable diagnostic window before overt azotemia develops [7,13,27,28]. Hall et al. (2014) [7] reported that SDMA elevation precedes creatinine increases in most cats with early CKD, and Pérez-López et al. (2019) [13] further confirmed SDMA’s role as an early biomarker independent of muscle mass. Routine inclusion of SDMA testing in health screening protocols for cats older than seven years could substantially improve early CKD detection rates.
超声评估虽然只在一部分猫体中进行，但显示结构重塑（皮质变薄、高回声）与生化严重程度之间存在一致关系，在皮质变化显著的猫中观察到更高的肌酐和SDMA值。这些发现与Elliott和Barber（1998）[5]以及Bartlett等（2010）[4]的研究结果一致，他们描述了晚期CKD中类似的超声图像模式。这进一步强化了超声作为诊断和预后评估猫CKD的重要非侵入性辅助手段[29]。总体而言，这些发现强调了猫CKD的多因素性质，受年龄、品种遗传、营养管理和临床监测实践的影响。它们还强调将生化指标、尿液分析和影像学结果整合以实现准确分期和预测的重要性。
Ultrasonographic evaluation, although performed in a subset of cats, revealed a consistent relationship between structural remodeling (cortical thinning, hyperechogenicity) and biochemical severity, with higher creatinine and SDMA values observed in cats with marked cortical changes. These findings are in accordance with Elliott and Barber (1998) [5] and Bartlett et al. (2010) [4], who described similar ultrasonographic patterns in advanced CKD. This reinforces ultrasonography as a valuable non-invasive adjunct in the diagnostic and prognostic assessment of feline CKD [29]. Collectively, these findings underscore the multifactorial nature of feline CKD, influenced by aging, breed genetics, nutritional management, and clinical monitoring practices. They also emphasize the importance of integrating biochemical markers, urinalysis, and imaging findings to achieve accurate staging and prognosis.
4.1. 研究局限性 本研究为回顾性、单中心设计，且样本量有限，可能限制外部效度。由于队列来源于转诊医院，无法排除对更晚期病例的选择偏倚。需要开展纵向、多中心研究以验证这些观察结果，并探索其他风险调节因素，如遗传多态性、合并疾病和早期饮食干预。 4.2. 临床意义 从实际角度来看，结果强调了对七岁以上猫咪进行常规筛查的重要性，应同时使用肌酐和SDMA，并辅以尿液分析和血压测量。早期发现和营养调控仍然是慢性肾病（CKD）管理的基石，能够延缓病情进展并改善生活质量。
4.1. Study Limitations The study’s retrospective, single-center design and limited sample size may constrain external validity. As the cohort originated from a referral hospital, selection bias toward more advanced cases cannot be excluded. Longitudinal, multicenter studies are warranted to validate these observations and to explore additional risk modifiers such as genetic polymorphisms, comorbid conditions, and early dietary interventions. 4.2. Clinical Implications From a practical standpoint, the results highlight the need for routine screening of cats older than seven years using both creatinine and SDMA, coupled with urinalysis and BP measurement. Early detection and nutritional modulation remain the cornerstone of CKD management, capable of delaying progression and improving quality of life.
5. 结论 猫的慢性肾病（CKD）是一种常见的疾病，具有显著的流行率，受年龄和品种的影响很大。其流行率随年龄显著增加，在10–12岁之后达到高峰。使用现代生物标志物，特别是SDMA，为早期诊断和有效监测疾病进展提供了新的机会。本研究的结果与国际文献一致，强调了制定标准化筛查和治疗方案的必要性。清楚了解疾病的流行情况和病因因素，并结合精准的早期诊断，对于有效管理猫的慢性肾病（CKD）至关重要，最终可改善受影响动物的生活质量和寿命。
5. Conclusions Chronic kidney disease in cats is a common condition with significant prevalence, strongly influenced by age and breed. Its prevalence rises markedly with age, reaching peak levels after 10– 12 years. The use of modern biomarkers, particularly SDMA, provides new opportunities for early diagnosis and effective monitoring of disease progression. The results of this study are consistent with the international literature and highlight the need for standardized screening and treatment protocols. A clear understanding of disease prevalence and etiological factors, combined with precise early diagnosis, is essential for the efficient management of feline CKD, ultimately improving both quality and duration of life in affected animals.
未来的研究应集中在遗传和营养风险因素上，以及验证个性化治疗策略。
Future research should focus on genetic and nutritional risk factors as well as on validating personalized therapeutic strategies.
