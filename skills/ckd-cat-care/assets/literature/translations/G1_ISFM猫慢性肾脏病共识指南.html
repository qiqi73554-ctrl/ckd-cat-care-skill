Practical relevance:
慢性肾病（CKD）是老年猫最常被诊断的疾病之一。在大多数猫中，CKD也是一种进行性疾病，可能伴随多种临床和临床病理变化。这些ISFM共识指南由独立的临床医生和学术专家小组开发，旨在为该复杂疾病的诊断和管理提供实用建议。
Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is one of the most commonly diagnosed diseases in older cats. In most cats, CKD is also a progressive disease and can be accompanied by a wide range of clinical and clinicopathological changes. These ISFM Consensus Guidelines have been developed by an independent panel of clinicians and academics to provide practical advice on the diagnosis and management of this complex disease.
临床挑战：
Clinical challenges:
虽然慢性肾病（CKD）是猫常见的临床问题，但疾病的表现因个体而异。因此，需要对患有CKD的猫进行仔细和反复评估，并根据个体需求调整治疗方案。除了处理CKD引起的问题并提高患者的生活质量（QoL）之外，治疗还可能针对减缓疾病的潜在进展，从而延长寿命。在维持患者生活质量至关重要的同时，当需要多种治疗时，这可能会带来挑战。在某些情况下，有必要根据对最有可能惠及个体患者的理解来优先安排治疗。
Although CKD is a common clinical problem in cats, the manifestations of disease vary between individuals. Thus there is a need for careful and repeat evaluation of cats with CKD and adjustment of therapy according to individual needs. In addition to addressing problems arising from CKD and improving quality of life (QoL) for the patient, therapy may also target slowing the underlying progression of disease and hence prolonging life. While maintaining QoL is of paramount importance in our patients, this can be challenging when multiple therapies are indicated. In some cases it is necessary to prioritise therapy, given an understanding of what is likely to most benefit the individual patient.

Diagnosis and assessment of CKD in cats Routine diagnosis of CKD in cats
人类的慢性肾病（CKD）定义为持续（≥3个月）的肾小球滤过率（GFR）（GFR，<60 ml/min/1.73 m²）下降或持续（≥3个月）肾脏损伤的证据（例如结构损伤、蛋白尿）。虽然猫的CKD尚无明确定义，但应遵循类似原则；尤其应有持续的功能性或结构性肾损伤的证据（例如≥3个月的持续时间）。由于CKD在老年猫中更为常见，这些患者应成为更详细且频繁健康评估的重点对象。
CKD in humans is defined as a sustained (⩾3 months) reduction in glomerular filtration rate (GFR, <60 ml/min/1.73 m2) or evidence of sustained (⩾3 months) kidney damage (eg, structural damage, proteinuria). 18 Although CKD has not been clearly defined in cats, similar principles should apply; notably there should be evidence of sustained functional or structural kidney damage (eg, ⩾3 months’ duration). As CKD is more common in older cats, these patients should be targeted for more detailed and frequent health assessments.
国际猫护理/国际猫医学学会、美国猫科兽医协会和美国动物医院协会的建议是，对于7岁以上的猫，每6个月进行一次健康检查（包括体重、身体状况评分和血压评估），并每年至少进行一次选择性的诊断测试（包括血液学、血清生化筛查和常规尿液分析）。历史和临床发现提示慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）的症状，如体重下降、肾脏大小变化、不明原因的脱水、多饮多尿、系统性高血压或不明原因的低尿比重（USG）（<1.035–1.040），也需要进一步检查。
Recommendations from International Cat Care/International Society of Feline Medicine, the American Association of Feline Practitioners and the American Animal Hospital Association suggest health checks every 6 months for cats >7 years of age (including evaluation of body weight, body condition score and blood pressure), together with selected diagnostic testing (including haematology, serum biochemistry screening and routine urinalysis) at least annually.19,20 Historical and clinical findings suggestive of CKD, such as weight loss, altered kidney size, unexplained dehydration, PU/PD, systemic hypertension or an unexplained low USG (<1.035– 1.040), also justify further investigation.

管理方法

Approach to management
慢性肾病（CKD）的管理主要集中在支持性和症状性治疗，目的是改善受影响猫的生活质量（QoL）（尤其是CKD第3期和第4期的猫），并在可能的情况下减缓疾病进展（尤其是在CKD第2期和第3期）。尽管超出了本指南的范围，但对猫进行仔细评估（如前文所述）也应有助于识别某些潜在病因，从而允许进行特定干预，例如肾淋巴瘤、尿路感染、肾结石和输尿管结石。由于该病的慢性特性、定期监测的必要性以及各种干预的可能性，在诊所与猫主人之间建立良好的关系和沟通非常重要。这将有助于制定个性化管理计划，同时考虑主人的意愿和能力，以及猫的需求。需要考虑的问题在下方框中列出。
Management of CKD is largely focused on supportive and symptomatic therapy with the aim of improving the quality of life (QoL) of affected cats (especially those in CKD stages 3 and 4) and, where possible, slowing the progression of disease (especially in CKD stages 2 and 3). Although beyond the scope of these Guidelines, careful evaluation of cats (as outlined earlier) should also allow identification of certain underlying aetiologies that permit specific intervention such as renal lymphoma, UTIs, nephroliths and ureteroliths. Because of the chronic nature of the disease, the need for regular monitoring and the potential for various interventions, establishing a good relationship and good communication between the clinic and the cat’s owner is vital. Management of CKD patients Managing hydration in CKD46,47 CKD is associated with variable obligatory diuresis, and affected cats may be predisposed to dehydration, especially in CKD stages 3 and 4.

纠正脱水46,47

Correcting dehydration46,47
患有不稳定或失代偿期慢性肾病（CKD）的猫可能需要住院和静脉输液治疗，通常使用乳酸林格氏液或哈特曼液。还应考虑可能需要处理的伴随电解质和酸碱失衡。纠正脱水所需的液体量（ml）可通过以下公式计算：体重（kg）× 估计脱水百分比（%）× 1000，并且该液体（连同维持液，例如 50 ml/kg/24 小时）通常在 24–48 小时内给予，尽管某些猫可能耐受更快的复水速度。复水后，可以继续给予维持液，但应仔细监测猫咪以避免液体过量。当氮质血症稳定时，应在出院前逐步减少液体，通常为 2–3 天。
Cats with unstable or decompensated CKD may require hospitalisation and intravenous fluid therapy, typically with lactated Ringer’s solution or Hartmann’s. Consideration should also be given to concomitant electrolyte and acid–base disturbances that may need addressing. The fluid required to correct dehydration (in ml) is calculated from: body weight (kg) × estimated dehydration (%) × 1000, and this (along with maintenance fluids, eg, 50 ml/kg/24 h) is typically provided over 24–48 h, although some cats may tolerate more rapid rehydration. After rehydration, maintenance fluids can be administered but cats should be monitored carefully to avoid fluid overload. When azotaemia is stable, fluids should be tapered over 2–3 days before the patient is discharged.

长期维持水分

Long-term maintenance of hydration
自愿饮水 应始终提供高质量的水源（图8），并建议主人提供多种水源（包括调味水和流动水——例如“宠物饮水机”）以鼓励饮水。在可能的情况下，喂湿食而不是干食也很重要，因为这会增加水的摄入量。48 当脱水成为问题时，可以在食物中额外添加水，但必须确保其他营养摄入不受影响。49 使用喂食管 水也可以通过喂食管给予，在许多情况下，这可能比皮下补液更可取（见下文）。喂食管适用于长期维持水分，是一种更符合生理的方法。当需要时，它也可以提供营养支持。
Voluntary water intake Free access to good quality water should be provided at all times (Figure 8), and owners should be advised to offer a variety of water sources (including flavoured waters and running water – eg, a ‘pet fountain’) to encourage drinking. Feeding a wet diet rather than dry diet where possible is important, as it will also increase water intake. 48 Additional water can be added to the food where dehydration is a concern, but it is important to ensure intake of other nutrients is maintained. 49 Use of feeding tubes Water can also be administered via a feeding tube, and this may be preferable to subcutaneous fluids (see below) in many cases. A feeding tube is suitable for long-term maintenance of hydration and is a more physiological approach. It also allows for nutritional support when needed.

慢性肾脏病中的饮食和矿物质/骨病管理

Managing diet and mineral/bone disease in CKD
饮食干预是人类50岁及兽医患者慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）治疗的支柱。肾脏配方饮食在蛋白质和磷上受到限制，但其他特点包括增加热量密度、限制钠、补充钾、碱化，以及补充B族维生素、抗氧化剂和欧米伽-3脂肪酸。
Dietary manipulation is a mainstay of CKD therapy in human 50 and veterinary patients. Renal formulated diets are restricted in both protein and phosphorus, but other features include an increased calorie density, sodium restriction, potassium supplementation, alkalinisation, and supplementation with B vitamins, antioxidants and omega-3 fatty acids.

蛋白质和磷限制

Protein and phosphate restriction
蛋白质限制和磷限制通常被一起考虑，因为它们是商业肾脏饮食的主要特点，并且被认为带来了主要的益处。猫肾脏饮食通常每100千卡含6–7克蛋白质（高于成年猫推荐允许量每100千卡5克，但低于常见维持饮食每100千卡9–10克的含量）。老年猫（>13岁）的能量需求可能增加，而严重的蛋白质限制可能导致瘦体组织的丧失；因此，在慢性肾病（CKD）中建议进行适度蛋白质限制，同时监测瘦体重、体重和热量摄入（图10）。除了蛋白质限制之外，肾脏饮食与典型维持饮食相比含磷量也少得多。53,54 图10。
Protein restriction and phosphate restriction are considered together as they are the main features of commercial renal diets, and are thought to confer the major benefits seen. Feline renal diets typically contain 6–7 g of protein per 100 kcal (above the 5 g/100 kcal recommended allowance for adult cats, 51 but below the 9–10 g/100 kcal commonly seen in maintenance diets). Energy requirements of older (>13 years) cats may increase and severe protein restriction may lead to loss of lean tissue; 52 thus moderate protein restriction is recommended in CKD, together with monitoring of lean body mass, weight and caloric intake (Figure 10). 管理血清钙

Managing serum calcium
高钙血症是公认的肾损伤原因，但慢性肾病（CKD）也可以引起血清钙的变化，尽管这些通常较轻。离子化低钙血症似乎最为常见，并且往往出现在晚期慢性肾病（CKD）中。72 钙-磷乘积的增加已被与猫的疾病严重程度相关联。73
Hypercalcaemia is a recognised cause of renal injury, but CKD can also cause changes in serum calcium, although these are generally mild. Ionised hypocalcaemia appears to be most common, and tends to be seen in advanced CKD. 72 An increased calcium–phosphorus product has been linked with disease severity in cats. 73

钙三醇治疗

Calcitriol therapy
由于多种机制，包括高磷血症介导的羟化抑制和肾组织丧失，慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）可能导致骨化三醇（活性维生素D）缺乏。补充骨化三醇有可能帮助抑制肾性继发性甲状旁腺功能亢进（SHPT），并已被证明对犬和人有益；但尽管有改善生活质量的轶事报告，低剂量骨化三醇在猫的CKD中尚未显示出相同的益处。此外，骨化三醇的制剂会使猫的精确剂量控制变得困难。在使用此疗法时，还应仔细控制高磷血症，以避免增加血清钙磷乘积。
Calcitriol (active vitamin D) deficiency may occur with CKD due to various mechanisms including hyperphosphataemia-mediated inhibition of hydroxylation and loss of renal tissue. Calcitriol supplementation can potentially help suppress renal secondary hyperparathyroidism and has been shown to be beneficial in dogs and humans; 74 but despite anecdotal reports of improved QoL, low dose calcitriol has not been shown to have the same benefits in feline CKD. 75 Additionally, formulations of calcitriol can make accurate dosing difficult in cats. Hyperphosphataemia should also be carefully controlled when using this therapy to avoid increasing the serum calcium– phosphate product.

管理钾

Managing potassium
猫慢性肾病（CKD）可能导致过度排钾，这可能由于钾摄入减少、呕吐和细胞内转运改变而加重。低钾血症可能引起或加重临床症状，如嗜睡、食欲减退、便秘和肌肉无力，并可能促成酸中毒的发展，但尚未被确定为疾病进展或预后的风险因素。尽管肾脏专用饮食通常会补充钾，但在一些猫中仍可能出现低钾血症。相反，在晚期CKD中偶尔也可能出现高钾血症。
Feline CKD can lead to excessive kaliuresis, which may be compounded by reduced potassium intake, vomiting and transcellular shifts.55,76 Hypokalaemia may cause or contribute to clinical signs such as lethargy, inappetence, constipation and muscle weakness, and may contribute to development of acidosis, but has not been identified as a risk factor for disease progression or outcome.42,44,45 Although renal diets are typically supplemented with potassium, hypokalaemia may still be seen in some cats. Conversely, hyperkalaemia may occasionally be seen in advanced CKD.

管理酸碱平衡

Managing acid–base balance
慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）中的代谢性酸中毒是多因素的，已显示碳酸氢盐治疗可以改善营养（卡路里和蛋白质摄入量、瘦体重）并减缓CKD患者的病情进展。已有报道表明，超过一半晚期（第4期）CKD的猫会发生代谢性酸中毒。然而，食用商业肾脏配方的猫可能具有较高的血清碳酸氢盐浓度。
Metabolic acidosis is multifactorial in CKD 77 and bicarbonate therapy has been shown to improve nutrition (calorie and protein intake, lean body mass) and slow progression in humans with CKD. 78 Metabolic acidosis has been reported to occur in over half of cats with advanced (stage 4) CKD.79,80 However, cats fed commercial renal diets may have higher serum bicarbonate concentrations. 56

Other nutrients

慢性肾病（CKD）中的蛋白尿管理

Managing proteinuria in CKD
在人类医学中，无论慢性肾病（CKD）的原因是什么，诊断时蛋白尿的严重程度都是一个重要的预后指标，而控制蛋白尿可减缓CKD的进展，这在很大程度上与CKD的潜在原因无关。138 CKD通常与肾小球内毛细血管压升高及其他损害肾小球通透选择性的变化有关，导致白蛋白（及其他蛋白质）进入小管液的损失增加；这似乎通过促进小管炎症和纤维化直接促进疾病进展。138 尽管在病理生理学上可能存在物种差异，在患有CKD的猫中蛋白尿增加（通过UPCR评估，而不是常规试纸条，因为后者不适用于评估猫的蛋白尿139,140）也被认为预后较差。37,42,44,120,123 在一项研究中，37只UPCR <0.2的猫据报道中位生存时间约为1000天，而UPCR为0.2–0.4的猫约为500天，UPCR >0.4的猫约为400天。
In human medicine, regardless of the cause of CKD, the severity of proteinuria at the time of diagnosis is an important prognostic indicator, and controlling proteinuria results in slower progression of CKD, largely irrespective of the underlying cause of the CKD. 138 CKD is generally associated with increased intraglomerular capillary pressure and other changes that impair glomerular permselectivity, leading to increased loss of albumin (and other proteins) into tubular fluid; this appears to directly contribute to disease progression by promoting tubular inflammation and fibrosis. 138 Although there may be species differences in pathophysiology, increased proteinuria in cats with CKD (assessed with UPCR and not routine dipsticks, which are inappropriate for assessment of feline proteinuria139,140) is also known to carry a poorer prognosis.37,42,44,120,123 In one study, 37 cats with a UPCR <0.2 were reported to have a median survival time of ~1000 days compared with ~500 days for those with a UPCR of 0.2–0.4, and ~400 days for those with a UPCR >0.4.
在其他地方也报告了非常相似的发现。120 目前，没有证据表明测量尿白蛋白肌酐比率（UACR）而不是尿蛋白肌酐比（UPC）率（UPCR）在猫身上有任何优势，37,141 但健康猫和患有慢性肾病（CKD）猫的尿液蛋白谱非常复杂，需要更多研究。142–146 在人类中，使用血管紧张素受体拮抗剂（ARBs）或ACE抑制剂（ACEIs）治疗在阻断RAAS活化、降低肾小球毛细血管压力、恢复肾小球通透选择性、减少蛋白尿和延缓CKD进展方面是有效的。138,147–149 这些作用部分是由于血流动力学变化，部分是通过改变肾脏非血流动力学重塑实现的。
Very similar findings have also been reported elsewhere. 120 Currently, there is no evidence that measurement of UACR rather than UPCR offers any benefits in cats,37,141 but the urinary proteome in healthy cats and cats with CKD is complex and more studies are needed.142 –146 In humans, treatment with angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs) or ACEIs is effective in blocking RAAS activation, decreasing glomerular capillary pressure, restoring glomerular permselectivity, reducing proteinuria and slowing the progression of CKD.138,147 –149 These effects are partly as a result of haemodynamic changes and partly through modifying non-haemodynamic remodelling in the kidney.

患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫可能会因尿毒素影响中枢化学感受器触发区而出现恶心、呕吐和食欲不振。食欲不振对主人来说是一个重要的生活质量问题，而在CKD患者中可能导致蛋白质和热量营养不良及其许多不良后果。因此，应积极管理食欲下降，同时处理可能导致食欲不振的CKD并发症，如脱水、低血钾、酸中毒和贫血。应考虑使用中枢作用的止吐药，如马洛匹坦、米氮平、昂丹司琼和多拉司琼进行管理（表9）。在对2期或3期CKD猫进行的安慰剂对照试验中，口服服用马洛匹坦2周显示可减少呕吐，而口服米氮平3周不仅减少呕吐，还能增加食欲和体重。因此，米氮平可能是CKD猫营养管理的有益辅助。
Cats with CKD can suffer from nausea, vomiting and inappetence as a result of uraemic toxins affecting the central chemoreceptor trigger zone. Inappetence is a significant QoL concern for owners, 156 and in the CKD patient could result in protein and calorie malnutrition with its many adverse consequences. 157 A reduced appetite should therefore be actively managed, along with complications of CKD that can contribute to inappetence, such as dehydration, hypokalaemia, acidosis and anaemia. Centrally acting antiemetics such as maropitant, mirtazapine, ondansetron and dolasetron158 –160 should be considered for management (Table 9). In placebo-controlled trials of cats with stage 2 or 3 CKD, maropitant (given orally for 2 weeks) was shown to reduce vomiting, 160 and mirtazapine (given orally for 3 weeks) reduced vomiting and also increased appetite and weight. 158 Mirtazapine may therefore be a useful adjunct to the nutritional management of cats with CKD.

There are anecdotal reports of H2 blockers or proton pump inhibitors alleviating inappetence in some feline CKD patients, but the presence and degree of gastric hyperacidity and efficacy of these medications remain unproven. Additionally, although hypergastrinaemia has been reported in feline CKD, 161 gastric ulceration has generally not been observed or reported.73,162 If therapy for hyperacidity in cats is considered, omeprazole appears to be superior to famotidine. 163

慢性肾病（CKD）中的尿路感染管理

Managing UTIs in CKD
患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫的细菌性尿路感染（UTIs）报告的发生频率约为15–30%，老年雌性猫的风险增加。大多数（>70%）这些尿路感染似乎是亚临床的（即没有下尿路症状 [LUTS]），尽管>85%的病例在尿沉渣分析中显示变化（>5个白细胞 [WBCs]/高倍视野 [hpf]，和/或>5个红细胞 [RBCs]/hpf，和/或显微镜下细菌尿）。大肠杆菌占分离菌株的60–75%，而其他微生物包括肠球菌、链球菌、葡萄球菌、肠杆菌、铜绿假单胞菌和克雷伯氏菌属。在CKD患者的常规尿液分析中出现LUTS或检测到脓尿（⩾5 WBCs/hpf）是进行膀胱穿刺尿液培养的指征，但是否应推荐对所有尿液样本进行常规培养存在争议，因为亚临床菌尿的意义尚不确定。
Bacterial UTIs in cats with CKD occur at a reported frequency of around 15–30%,39,164 –166 with older female cats having an increased risk. 39 Most (>70%) of these UTIs appear to be subclinical (ie, without lower urinary tract signs [LUTS]), although >85% show changes on urine sediment analysis (>5 white blood cells [WBCs]/hpf, and/or >5 RBCs/hpf, and/or microscopic bacteriuria). 39 Escherichia coli represents 60–75% of isolates, while other organisms include Enterococcus, Streptococcus, Staphylococcus, Enterobacter, Pseudomonas and Klebsiella species. The presence of LUTS or detection of pyuria (⩾5 WBCs/hpf) in routine urinalysis of CKD patients are indications for bacterial culture of a cystocentesis sample, but whether routine culture of all urine samples should be recommended is controversial, as the significance of subclinical bacteriuria is uncertain.
虽然一些临床医生提倡对所有与CKD相关的尿路感染进行常规治疗（因为猫可能有肾盂肾炎和CKD恶化的风险），但治疗后复发性或再发性尿路感染很常见，亚临床尿路感染的存在与疾病严重程度或明显生存期无关，而不必要的治疗可能增加细菌耐药性的风险。治疗时，应按照国际指南管理尿路感染，根据敏感性测试选择抗菌药物（注意尿液培养应避免使用硼酸管），选择经尿液原形排泄且治疗指数较宽的药物（见表10）。如果需要初始经验性治疗，阿莫西林（11–15 mg/kg 口服，每8小时一次）或增强型阿莫西林是合适的选择；推荐疗程为2–4周，尽管CKD相关尿路感染的最佳治疗持续时间尚不确定。治疗反应应通过在治疗结束后7天重复培养进行监测。
While some clinicians advocate routine treatment of all CKD-associated UTIs (as cats may be at risk for pyelonephritis and deterioration of CKD), recurrent or recrudescent UTIs are common after treatment, 39 the presence of subclinical UTIs has not been associated with disease severity or apparent survival, 39 and unnecessary treatment may risk development of bacterial resistance. When treated, UTIs should be managed according to international guidelines, 167 selecting antibacterials based on sensitivity testing (note that boric acid tubes should be avoided for urine cultures 168 ) that are excreted unchanged in urine and have a wide therapeutic index (Table 10). If initial empirical therapy is needed, amoxicillin (11–15 mg/kg PO q8h) 167 or potentiated amoxicillin 169 are appropriate choices; 2–4 weeks’ therapy has been recommended, 167 although optimum duration of therapy for CKD-associated UTIs is uncertain. 170 Response to treatment should be monitored with repeat culture 7 days after cessation of treatment.

肾移植

Renal transplantation
在某些地区的专科中心，可能可以通过活体供体进行肾脏移植来治疗患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫。这一手术具有诸多影响，包括伦理、经济、福利和监测方面的考虑。尽管在某些患者中可能可行，但肾脏移植超出了本指南的范围。
Kidney transplants from living donors may be available to treat cats with CKD at specialist centres in some regions. This procedure has numerous implications including ethical, financial, welfare and monitoring considerations.173 –176 While it may be viable in some patients, kidney transplantation is beyond the scope of these Guidelines.
