结论与相关性

Conclusions and Relevance
在这项临床队列研究中，较高的慢性肾病（CKD）分期与较高的血清fPL水平相关。由于单独fPL不足以诊断胰腺炎，且其水平可能因肾功能下降而升高，因此在患有CKD的猫中，轻度至中度fPL升高应结合临床症状和胰腺影像学检查结果进行解读。关键词：猫科医学，肾功能不全，氮质血症，胰腺炎，脂肪酶 慢性肾病（CKD）是影响老年猫最常见的疾病之一。在10岁以上的猫中，其报告的患病率超过30%–40% [1,2,3,4,5]。CKD的常见病史和临床症状包括体重下降、多尿、多饮以及持续低尿比重（USG）（USG；< 1.035）[6,7,8,9]。根据国际猫科医学协会的共识指南，猫慢性肾病（CKD）的临床诊断是基于氮质血症伴随尿液浓缩不足来确定的 [10]。
In this clinical cohort, higher CKD stage was associated with higher serum fPL. Because fPL alone is insufficient to diagnose pancreatitis and may increase with reduced renal function, mild-tomoderate fPL elevations in cats with CKD should be interpreted in the context of clinical signs and pancreatic imaging findings. Keywords: Feline medicine, renal insufficiency, azotemia, pancreatitis, lipase Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is one of the most prevalent conditions affecting geriatric cats. Its reported prevalence rates in cats older than 10 years exceed 30%–40% [1,2,3,4,5]. Common historical and clinical symptoms of CKD include weight loss, polyuria, polydipsia, and persistently low urine specific gravity (USG; < 1.035) [6,7,8,9]. According to the consensus guidelines of the International Society of Feline Medicine, a clinical diagnosis of feline CKD is established based on the presence of azotemia in conjunction with inadequate concentrated urine [10].
猫胰腺炎，曾被认为是一种罕见的临床实体，如今已被认为是一种常见疾病，其发生率与人类和犬类相当。2007年一项涉及115例猫尸检的组织病理学研究显示，67%的参与者有胰腺炎的证据；其中慢性胰腺炎、急性胰腺炎（AP）和急性加慢性胰腺炎分别占50.4%、6.1%和9.6%。此外，45%的临床健康猫显示组织病理学上的胰腺炎迹象[11]。血清猫胰腺脂肪酶（fPL）浓度已成为猫胰腺炎非创伤性诊断的敏感且特异的生物标志物[12,13,14,15]。
Feline pancreatitis, once considered an uncommon clinical entity, is now recognized as a frequently occurring condition with a prevalence rate comparable to that observed in humans and dogs. A 2007 histopathological study involving 115 feline necropsy cases demonstrated evidence of pancreatic inflammation in 67% of the participants; chronic pancreatitis, acute pancreatitis (AP), and acute-on-chronic pancreatitis accounted for 50.4%, 6.1%, and 9.6%, respectively. Furthermore, 45% of clinically healthy cats had histopathological signs of pancreatitis [11]. Serum feline pancreatic lipase (fPL) concentration has since emerged as a sensitive and specific biomarker for the non-invasive diagnosis of feline pancreatitis [12,13,14,15].
在人类医学中，大量证据表明尿毒症会对胰腺产生不利影响 [16,17,18]。已有研究记录了终末期肾病（ESRD）与急性胰腺炎（AP）之间的密切关联 [19,20]。一项在德国进行的全国性队列研究，涉及超过70,000名接受血液透析的ESRD患者，报告的急性胰腺炎年发病率为每10万人中67例。这明显高于普通人群中每10万人中19.7例的发病率 [21]。类似地，一项在台湾进行、包括2,603名ESRD患者的前瞻性队列研究发现，ESRD患者中急性胰腺炎的发病率比普通人群高出三倍以上。已经提出了几种病理生理机制来解释这种关系。一种假设认为，腹膜透析液中的钙可能通过腹膜扩散，导致胰腺局部高钙血症，并进一步加重胰腺功能障碍 [22]。
In human medicine, a substantial body of evidence indicates that the pancreas is adversely affected by uremia [16,17,18]. A strong association has been documented between end-stage renal disease (ESRD) and AP [19,20]. A nationwide cohort study conducted in Germany that involved more than 70,000 patients with ESRD undergoing hemodialysis reported an annual incidence of AP at 67 cases per 100,000 individuals. This was markedly higher than the 19.7 cases per 100,000 observed in the general population [21]. Similarly, a prospective cohort study involving 2,603 ESRD patients in Taiwan found that the incidence of AP was more than threefold more prevalent among patients with ESRD than in the general population. Several pathophysiological mechanisms have been proposed to explain this relationship. One hypothesis suggests that calcium in the peritoneal dialysate may diffuse through the peritoneum, leading to localized hypercalcemia within the pancreas and further exacerbation of pancreatic dysfunction [22].
此外，在肾功能不全中发生的胃肠激素浓度增加，如胆囊收缩素、胃抑制多肽和胰高血糖素，可能刺激胰腺酶的过度分泌并导致胰腺损伤 [23]。尽管在人类中已公认肾功能障碍与胰腺炎之间存在关联，但慢性肾病（CKD）与猫胰腺炎之间的关系仍难以明确。重要的是，猫胰腺炎的诊断具有挑战性，通常依赖于临床症状、实验室异常和影像学发现的综合判断，而不是单一生物标志物。此外，胰腺脂肪酶部分通过肾脏清除；因此，即使在没有原发性胰腺炎症的情况下，肾小球滤过率（GFR）下降也可能导致测得的脂肪酶浓度升高。
Additionally, increased concentrations of gastrointestinal hormones such as cholecystokinin, gastric inhibitory polypeptide, and glucagon that occur in renal insufficiency may stimulate pancreatic enzyme hypersecretion and contribute to pancreatic injury [23]. Despite the recognized association between renal dysfunction and pancreatitis in humans, the relationship between CKD and pancreatitis in cats remains difficult to define. Importantly, diagnosis of feline pancreatitis is challenging and typically relies on integration of clinical signs, laboratory abnormalities, and imaging findings rather than a single biomarker. In addition, pancreatic lipase is cleared in part by the kidneys; therefore, reduced glomerular filtration rate may contribute to higher measured lipase concentrations even in the absence of primary pancreatic inflammation.
因此，本研究旨在评估血清 fPL 浓度在不同慢性肾病（CKD）阶段的变化，以及 fPL 与猫常规测量的肾脏生物标志物的关系。我们假设 fPL 浓度会与肾功能下降的标志物相关，从而强调在有 CKD 的猫中解读 fPL 需要谨慎。
Therefore, this study aimed to evaluate how serum fPL concentrations vary across CKD stages and how fPL relates to commonly measured renal biomarkers in cats. We hypothesized that fPL concentrations would show an association with markers of reduced renal function, emphasizing the need for cautious interpretation of fPL in cats with CKD.

临床评估

Clinical assessment
根据血清肌酐浓度、尿比重（USG）和肾脏超声检查结果，将这112只猫分为五组。正常组的血清肌酐浓度 < 1.6 mg/dL，尿比重（USG） ≥ 1.035，且肾脏超声未发现任何异常。慢性肾病（CKD）分期依据2019年国际肾脏兴趣学会（IRIS）指南进行：第1期的特点是血清肌酐浓度 < 1.6 mg/dL且尿比重（USG） < 1.035；第2期为血清肌酐浓度在1.6至2.8 mg/dL之间；第3期为血清肌酐浓度在2.9至5.0 mg/dL之间；第4期为血清肌酐浓度 > 5.0 mg/dL。这个操作性定义用于识别早期CKD（第1期），其特征是尿液浓缩能力不足但没有氮质血症，并代表了IRIS分期系统的研究特定修改，其中使用尿比重（USG）进行CKD诊断而非正式分期 [10]。
The 112 cats were categorized into five groups based on serum creatinine concentration, USG, and renal ultrasonographic findings. The normal group had serum creatinine concentrations of < 1.6 mg/dL, USG of ≥ 1.035, and no detectable abnormalities on renal ultrasonography. CKD staging was performed according to the 2019 International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) guidelines: stage 1 was characterized by serum creatinine concentrations of < 1.6 mg/dL and USG of < 1.035; stage 2 by serum creatinine concentrations between 1.6 and 2.8 mg/dL; stage 3 by serum creatinine concentrations between 2.9 and 5.0 mg/dL; and stage 4 by serum creatinine concentrations of > 5.0 mg/dL. This operational definition was used to identify early CKD (Stage 1) characterized by inadequate urine concentrating ability without azotemia and represents a study-specific modification of the IRIS staging system, in which USG is used for CKD diagnosis rather than formal staging [10].
被认为与慢性肾病（CKD）一致的肾脏超声异常包括皮质回声增强、皮质髓质分界消失、肾轮廓不规则以及肾脏缩小[24]。血清fPL浓度使用Vcheck fPL（Bionote）定量免疫测定法测量，这是一种用于fPL检测的荧光免疫层析测定法。该测定法可在15分钟内提供定量结果，动态测量范围为1–30 ng/mL。根据制造商的建议，数值≤ 3.5、3.6–5.3和≥ 5.4 ng/mL分别被分类为正常、可疑以及超过测定法临界值。
Renal ultrasonographic abnormalities considered consistent with CKD included increased cortical echogenicity, loss of corticomedullary distinction, renal contour irregularities, and reduced renal size [24]. Serum fPL concentrations were measured using the Vcheck fPL (Bionote) quantitative immunoassay, which is a fluorescence immunochromatographic assay designed for fPL detection. The assay provides quantitative results within 15 min and has a dynamic measurement range of 1– 30 ng/mL. According to the manufacturer’s recommendations, values of ≤ 3.5, 3.6–5.3, and ≥ 5.4 ng/mL were categorized as normal, equivocal, and above the assay cutoff, respectively.

Statistical analysis
由于数据呈非参数分布，使用Kruskal–Wallis检验对不同CKD阶段的血清fPL浓度和尿比重（USG）进行了比较。fPL浓度与肾功能生物标志物（包括血尿素氮（BUN）、肌酐、无机磷（IP）、离子化钙（iCa）和USG）之间的关联使用Spearman等级相关系数进行评估。所有p值均为双侧检验，p值<0.05被认为具有统计学意义。统计分析使用IBM SPSS Statistics 22版本（IBM，美国）进行，应用了非参数检验和相关模块。
Comparisons of serum fPL concentrations and USG across CKD stages were performed using the Kruskal–Wallis test due to the non-parametric distribution of the data. Associations between fPL concentrations and renal biomarkers, including blood urea nitrogen (BUN), creatinine, inorganic phosphorus (IP), ionized calcium (iCa), and USG, were assessed using Spearman’s rank correlation coefficients. All p values were two-tailed, and p values of < 0.05 were considered statistically significant. Statistical analyses were conducted using IBM SPSS Statistics, version 22 (IBM, USA), with the nonparametric tests and correlation modules applied.

本研究评估了猫血清fPL浓度与CKD严重程度之间的关联，以及它们与常用肾脏生物标志物的关系。血清fPL随着CKD阶段的加重而升高，并且与氮质血症及尿液浓缩能力下降相关。随着CKD阶段的推进，观察到尿比重（USG）逐渐下降，这与以往研究结果一致，即CKD进展与因肾单位丢失导致的肾脏浓缩能力受损相关[25,26]。然而，在CKD第3期和第4期之间，USG未检出显著差异（p = 0.944）。这一观察结果表明，到第3期时肾单位丢失可能已达到关键阈值，导致浓缩能力下降，而随着CKD进展到第4期，进一步下降幅度较小。
This study assessed the association between serum fPL concentrations and CKD severity in cats, as well as their relationship with commonly used renal biomarkers. Serum fPL increased with worsening CKD stage and was associated with azotemia and reduced urine concentrating ability. A progressive decline in USG was observed with advancing CKD stages, which were consistent with previous findings that associate CKD progression with impaired renal concentrating ability due to nephron loss [25,26]. However, no significant difference in USG was detected between CKD stages 3 and 4 (p = 0.944). This observation suggests that a critical threshold of nephron loss may have already been reached by stage 3, resulting in diminished concentrating capacity with minimal further decline as CKD advances to stage 4.
血清fPL浓度显示与传统的肾脏生物标志物呈正相关，包括血尿素氮（BUN）、肌酐和无机磷（IP），这些指标都反映了肾小球滤过功能受损和全身性氮质血症。这些关联表明肾功能下降可能会影响fPL浓度的测量。猫胰腺炎的诊断本质上仍具有挑战性，仅凭fPL浓度升高通常不足以确立临床显著的胰腺炎。因此，适当的解读需要结合临床症状和胰腺超声检查结果。此外，已知当肾小球滤过率（GFR）下降时，胰腺酶会升高，这可能是由于肾清除减少，这进一步增加了在患有慢性肾病（CKD）猫中解读fPL结果的复杂性。
Serum fPL concentrations showed positive correlations with traditional renal biomarkers, including BUN, creatinine, and IP, all of which reflect impaired glomerular filtration and systemic azotemia. These associations suggest that declining renal function may influence measured fPL concentrations. The diagnosis of pancreatitis in cats remains inherently challenging, and an elevated fPL concentration alone is generally insufficient to establish clinically significant pancreatitis. Appropriate interpretation therefore requires integration of clinical signs and pancreatic ultrasonographic findings. Furthermore, pancreatic enzymes are known to increase when glomerular filtration is reduced, likely due to decreased renal clearance, which further complicates the interpretation of fPL results in cats with CKD.
在一项对诱导慢性肾病（CKD）猫进行的相关实验研究中，报告显示血清猫胰蛋白酶样免疫反应性随CKD显著升高，而血清fPL与健康对照组相比没有显著差异，并保持在参考范围内。这表明某些胰腺生物标志物可能会受到肾功能障碍的不同程度影响，并强调在氮质血症情况下需谨慎解读fPL结果[27]。在人体医学中，终末期肾病（ESRD）与急性胰腺炎（AP）之间的关联已有充分文献记录，并归因于多种机制。首先，高钙血症被认为是晚期CKD中AP的主要原因，无论是否接受透析。流行病学数据显示，未进行透析的CKD 5期患者和正在接受透析的患者分别发生高钙血症引发AP的可能性增加了4.59倍和1.65倍（p < 0.01）[19,20]。
A relevant experimental study in cats with induced CKD reported that serum feline trypsin-like immunoreactivity increased significantly with CKD, whereas serum fPL did not differ significantly from that in healthy controls and remained within reference intervals. This suggests that some pancreatic biomarkers may be variably influenced by renal dysfunction and highlights the need to interpret fPL results cautiously in the setting of azotemia [27]. In human medicine, the association between ESRD and AP is well-documented and attributed to multiple mechanisms. First, hypercalcemia is recognized as a leading cause of AP in advanced CKD, irrespective of dialysis status. Epidemiological data indicate that patients with CKD stage 5 who are not on dialysis and those undergoing dialysis are 4.59- and 1.65-fold, respectively, more likely to develop hypercalcemia-induced AP (p < 0.01) [19,20].
其次，肾脏在胃肠激素如胆囊收缩素的降解中起关键作用；清除功能受损可能导致持续的胰腺刺激并增加急性胰腺炎的风险。第三，血液透析期间低血压与肠系膜缺血有关，而肠系膜缺血是已知的急性胰腺炎诱因，研究将低血压、高超滤率和升高的胰腺酶浓度联系起来[21]。最后，腹膜透析（PD）可通过高甘油三酯血症、局部高钙血症、腹内压升高以及PD相关腹膜炎等机制增加胰腺炎的风险[22,23,28,29,30,31,32,33,34]。
Second, the kidneys play a critical role in the degradation of gastrointestinal hormones such as cholecystokinin; impaired clearance can lead to persistent pancreatic stimulation and increased risk of AP. Third, intradialytic hypotension has been implicated in mesenteric ischemia, a known trigger for AP, with studies linking hypotension, high ultrafiltration rates, and elevated pancreatic enzyme concentrations [21]. Finally, peritoneal dialysis (PD) can increase the risk of pancreatitis through mechanisms including hypertriglyceridemia, local hypercalcemia, elevated intra-abdominal pressure, and PD-related peritonitis [22,23,28,29,30,31,32,33,34].
综合来看，我们的研究结果最符合血清fPL浓度因肾功能下降而被动（非炎症性）升高的情况。在缺乏标准化临床体征评估和胰腺影像学检查结果的情况下，仅凭fPL升高在诊断慢性肾病（CKD）猫的胰腺炎方面存在固有的局限性。在这种情况下，我们的研究为临床医生提供了实际的解读信息。具体来说，当在慢性肾病（CKD）猫中观察到轻度至中度的fPL升高时，这些发现不应单独解读，而应结合相应的临床体征进行评估，并在可行的情况下参考以胰腺为重点的检查。本研究存在一些局限性。首先，缺乏胰腺或肾脏病变的组织病理学确认，这使得无法对潜在疾病做出明确诊断。其次，胰腺超声学检查结果和符合胰腺炎的标准化临床体征未被一致记录，因此无法使用统一的诊断标准来界定胰腺炎。
Taken together, our findings are most consistent with a passive (non-inflammatory) elevation of serum fPL concentrations associated with declining renal function. In the absence of standardized assessment of clinical signs and pancreatic imaging findings, elevations in fPL alone have inherent limitations for diagnosing pancreatitis in cats with CKD. In this context, our study provides a practical interpretive message for clinicians. Specifically, when mild-to-moderate increases in fPL are observed in cats with CKD, these findings should not be interpreted in isolation but rather evaluated in conjunction with compatible clinical signs and, when feasible, pancreatic-focused This study has some limitations. First, histopathological confirmation of pancreatic or renal lesions was not available, which precluded definitive diagnosis of the underlying disease. Second, pancreatic ultrasonographic findings and standardized clinical signs compatible with pancreatitis were not consistently documented, and therefore pancreatitis could not be defined using uniform diagnostic criteria.
第三，呈现时的水合状态没有得到一致或客观的量化，尿比重（USG）和一些实验室指标可能受此因素影响。最后，回顾性横断面设计以及纳入同日检测的猫可能引入选择偏倚，并限制了对时间关系的评估。总之，血清fPL浓度与猫的CKD严重程度及肾脏生物标志物相关。这些结果表明，肾功能下降可能导致更高的测量fPL浓度，并强调在CKD猫中不应单独使用fPL来诊断临床显著的胰腺炎。
Third, hydration status at the time of presentation was not consistently or objectively quantified, and USG and some laboratory parameters may have been influenced by this factor. Finally, the retrospective cross-sectional design and inclusion of cats undergoing same-day testing may have introduced selection bias and limited assessment of temporal relationships. In summary, serum fPL concentrations were associated with CKD severity and renal biomarkers in cats. These results indicate that reduced renal function may contribute to higher measured fPL concentrations and emphasize that fPL should not be used in isolation to diagnose clinically significant pancreatitis in cats with CKD.
