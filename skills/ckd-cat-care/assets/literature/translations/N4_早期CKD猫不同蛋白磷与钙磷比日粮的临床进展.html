结论与临床意义

Conclusions and Clinical Importance
早期慢性肾脏病的猫在长期食用高磷限制饮食后发生高钙血症。增加膳食磷并降低钙磷比可以维持肾功能指标，同时改善钙磷平衡。早期慢性肾脏病的猫可能受益于适度蛋白质和磷限制的饮食。
Cats with early CKD developed hypercalcemia after long‐term feeding of a highly P‐restricted diet. Increasing dietary P and reducing Ca : P ratio maintained renal markers, while improving Ca‐P balance. Cats with early CKD could benefit from moderately protein‐ and P‐restricted diets.

MATERIALS AND METHODS
这项工作由沃尔瑟姆动物福利与伦理审查委员会（项目组合管理号：55429）监督和批准，并在《1986年动物（科学程序）法》的授权下进行。共有19只家短毛绝育猫（9只公猫；10只母猫），中位年龄为4.9岁（范围2.4-9.8岁）被纳入研究。在参与另一项针对健康猫研究膳食磷的研究后，观察到肾功能受损，表现为肾脏超声成像异常（所有猫）及肾小球滤过率（GFR）下降>25%（19只猫中10只）。喂食含有4.8 gP/Mcal（其中3.6 g/Mcal为来自磷酸二氢钠的无机磷[iP]）且钙磷比为0.6的实验性饮食4周，导致的变化与慢性肾病（CKD）一致，通过血清肌酐>1.6 mg/dL确认，并伴随：(a) 尿比重（USG）<1.035（19只猫中10只），(b) 尿白蛋白与肌酐比（UAC）>17 mg/g，相当于尿蛋白与肌酐比（UPC）为0.2-0.4（19只猫中6只），或(c) GFR下降>40%（19只猫中2只）。
This work was overseen and approved by the WALTHAM Animal Welfare and Ethical Review Body (Project Portfolio Management No. 55429) and conducted under the authority of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986. Nineteen domestic short‐haired neutered cats (9 males; 10 females) with a median age of 4.9 years (2.4‐9.8) were enrolled. Impaired renal function, characterized by altered renal ultrasonographic imaging (all cats) and >25% decrease in GFR (10/19) was observed after participation in another study investigating dietary P in healthy cats. Feeding an experimental diet containing 4.8 gP/Mcal (3.6 g/Mcal as inorganic P [iP] from sodium dihydrogen phosphate) and Ca : P = 0.6 for 4 weeks caused changes consistent with CKD as confirmed by serum creatinine >1.6 mg/dL alongside (a) urine specific gravity (USG) <1.035 (10/19), (b) urine albumin to creatinine ratio (UAC) >17 mg/g, corresponding to urine protein to creatinine ratio (UPC) of 0.2‐0.4 (6/19), or (c) >40% decline in GFR (2/19).

研究设计

Study design
在预喂养期之后，对猫进行了为期43个月的机会性纵向病例研究的临床监测。根据临床变量的变化进行了两次饮食干预，以维持其健康。在每次饮食干预期间，定期评估了常规血液学、血液生化、尿液分析和序列（n = 5）动脉血压测量，以及肾小球滤过率（GFR）和钙-磷调节激素（PTH、FGF23和维生素D）（图1）。肾脏超声影像和泌尿结石/肾结石的发生情况每年评估一次。在研究开始和结束时测定了草酸钙（CaOx）和磷酸镁铵（MAP）尿液相对过饱和度（RSS），并在两只有肾结石的猫中在整个研究期间进行监测。在每次饮食干预结束时重新评估国际肾脏学会慢性肾病（CKD）分期。自第6个月起，每周测量体重（BW），每月使用1到9分评分法评估体况评分（BCS）。
After the prefeed period, cats were clinically monitored over a 43‐month opportunistic longitudinal case study. Two dietary interventions were made in response to changes in clinical variables with a view to maintain their health. Routine hematology, biochemistry, urinalysis, and serial (n = 5) arterial blood pressure measurements along with GFR and Ca‐P regulatory hormones (PTH, FGF23, and vitamin D) were assessed regularly within each dietary intervention (Figure 1). Kidney ultrasonographic imaging and occurrence of urolithiasis/renoliths was assessed once a year. Urine relative super saturation (RSS) for calcium oxalate (CaOx) and magnesium ammonium phosphate (MAP) was determined at study start and end and monitored throughout the study in the 2 cats with renoliths. International Renal Interest Society stage of CKD was reassessed at the end of each dietary intervention. Body weight (BW) was measured weekly and body condition score (BCS) monthly from month 6 onwards by a 1 to 9 point algorithm.

饮食干预

Dietary interventions
膳食营养成分如表1所示。各膳食的蛋白质和磷（P）含量均高于国家研究委员会为成年猫制定的推荐摄入量（50 g蛋白质/Mcal 和 0.64 gP/Mcal）。在第一次干预期间（第1至21个月），所有猫均食用低蛋白、低磷的商业临床肾脏膳食（Royal Canin [RC]，法国Aimargues）（LP-LP），符合肾脏膳食PARNUTS（用于特定营养用途的饲料 [2020年3月4日欧盟委员会条例(EU) 2020/354建立了用于特定营养用途的饲料使用清单, http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2020/354/oj]），其无机磷（iP）含量高于预先饲喂的膳食，该膳食专门配制以含有可忽略的iP。前18个月喂食干式配方（LP-LP-干：RC 兽医专用猫肾脏干粮），在第19至21个月期间引入湿式配方（LP-LP-湿：RC 兽医专用猫肾脏湿粮），每种配方提供约50%的维持能量需求（MER）。
Dietary nutrient compositions are shown in Table 1. The protein and P contents of the diets were above the recommended allowance established by the National Research Council for adult cats (50 gprotein/Mcal and 0.64 gP/Mcal). During the first intervention (months 1‐21), all cats received a commercial clinical renal diet (Royal Canin [RC], Aimargues, France) low in protein and P (LP‐LP), within renal diet PARNUTS (feed intended for PARticular NUTritional purposes [Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/354 of March 4, 2020 establishing a list of intended uses of feed intended for particular nutritional purposes, http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2020/354/oj]), containing higher levels of iP than the prefeed diet, which was specifically formulated to contain negligible amounts of iP. The dry format (LP‐LP‐dry: RC Veterinary Diet Feline Renal Dry) was fed during the first 18 months, while from months 19 to 21 the wet format was introduced (LP‐LP‐wet: RC Veterinary Feline Renal), each format providing approximately 50% of the maintenance energy requirement (MER).
由于猫在经过17个月LP‐LP干粮饮食后的临床状况，决定引入(a) 干湿结合的LP‐LP方案，随后(b) 第二次饮食干预（第22-43个月），期间所有猫都喂食一种商业临床老年猫粮，含有较高但适中的蛋白质和磷（MP‐MP饮食：RC Senior Consult Stage 2猫粮［Royal Canin Senior Consult Stage 2于2020年9月重新推出为Royal Canin Early Renal］），两种营养素均高于AAFCO最低标准。该饮食在整个喂养期间均以干湿结合方式提供。在整个试验过程中，饮食每日喂食两次，按能量均等分配。自来水可自由饮用。每日食物量根据前两个月的摄入记录估算。
Because of the clinical status of cats following 17 months on the LP‐LP‐dry diet, decisions were made to introduce (a) the dry‐wet LP‐LP regimen, and subsequently (b) the second dietary intervention (months 22‐43), during which all cats were fed a commercial clinical senior diet containing a higher but moderate amount of protein and P (MP‐MP diet: RC Senior Consult Stage 2 diet [Royal Canin Senior Consult Stage 2 was relaunched in September 2020 as Royal Canin Early Renal]), both nutrients above AAFCO minima. This diet was provided as a dry‐wet regimen throughout the feeding period. Throughout the trial, diets were fed twice daily, split equally on an energy basis. Tap water was provided ad libitum. Daily food amounts were estimated by intake records from the 2 previous months.

统计分析

Statistical analysis
来自猫的数据在LP‐LP‐dry（第1至17个月）下的第1、3、6、11和17个月，以及在MP‐MP‐dry‐wet（第22至43个月）下的第1、3、9、16和22个月被进行log10转换以估算倍数变化，然后拟合线性混合模型，将饮食、时间点及其交互作用作为固定效应，个体猫作为随机效应。在LP‐LP‐dry和MP‐MP‐dry‐wet饮食内，随时间的临床和研究参数差异相对于每种饮食第1个月后的水平（适应期）进行了评估。所有比较获得了估计均值及95%置信区间（CI）和P值。这些计算采用家族错误率调整后的5%水平。统计学显著差异定义为P ≤ .05。同时分析了从预喂食到LP‐LP‐dry、从LP‐LP‐dry到LP‐LP‐dry‐wet以及从LP‐LP‐dry‐wet到MP‐MP‐dry‐wet的饮食转变效应。所有分析均使用R版本4.0.0进行。对于血浆PTH和维生素D浓度低于参考下限的猫，提供了描述性分析。
Data from cats after +1, +3, +6, +11, and + 17 months on LP‐LP‐dry (months 1‐17) and after +1, +3, +9, +16, and + 22 months on MP‐MP‐dry‐wet (months 22‐43) were log10 transformed for fold‐change estimation and a linear mixed model was then fitted with diet, timepoint, and their interaction as the fixed effects and individual cat as the random effect. Within LP‐LP‐dry and MP‐MP‐dry‐wet diets, differences in clinical and research parameters over time were established relative to levels after 1 month on each diet (acclimation period). Estimated means with 95% confidence intervals (CIs) and P‐values were obtained for all comparisons made. These were calculated with a family‐wise adjusted 5% level. A statistically significant difference was defined as P ≤ .05. The effect of dietary transition from prefeed to LP‐LP‐dry, from LP‐LP‐dry to LP‐LP‐dry‐wet, and from LP‐LP‐dry‐wet to MP‐MP‐dry‐wet was also analyzed. All analyses were performed by R version 4.0.0. A descriptive analysis is provided for cats presenting with plasma PTH and vitamin D concentration below the lower reference range.

19只猫中有15只完成了研究。研究期间有4只猫被安乐死，其中3只在前21个月内，第四只在第40个月。第12个月时，1只猫（IRIS 2级；3岁）发生急性血栓栓塞并伴有后肢瘫痪。尸检确认其患有肥厚型心肌病（HCM）和腹主动脉血栓。肾脏的组织病理学检查显示存在慢性炎症，伴有间质性纤维化和髓质小管的营养不良性钙化。第15个月时，第二只猫（IRIS 2级，4岁）发生急性且无法响应的尿毒症危象（肌酐 = 7.3 mg/dL；尿素氮（BUN） = 84.9 mg/dL）；尸检组织病理学检查发现小管间质性肾炎和HCM。第20个月时，第三只猫（IRIS 1级；7岁）由于反复尿路阻塞被安乐死，经分析与膀胱结石相关，该结石为100%硅。最后一只猫（IRIS 1级，13岁）在第40个月因侵袭性肾上腺肿瘤被安乐死。
Fifteen of the 19 cats completed the study. Four cats were euthanized during the study, 3 during the first 21 months, and a fourth cat during month 40. At month 12, 1 cat (IRIS 2; 3 years) developed an acute thromboembolism accompanied by hindlimb paralysis. Postmortem examination confirmed HCM and thrombosis of the abdominal aorta. Histopathologic investigation of the kidneys revealed chronic inflammation with interstitial fibrosis and dystrophic calcification in the medullary tubules. At month 15, a second cat (IRIS 2, 4 years) developed acute and nonrespondent uremic crisis (creatinine = 7.3 mg/dL; BUN = 84.9 mg/dL); postmortem histopathologic examination identified tubulointerstitial nephritis and HCM. At month 20, a third cat (IRIS 1; 7 years) was euthanized because of recurrent urinary tract obstruction linked to a bladder stone analyzed as 100% silica. The last cat (IRIS 1, 13 years) was euthanized at month 40 because of an invasive adrenal tumor.

Ca, P, FGF23, PTH, and vitamin D

Some particularities of this study concern the nature of CKD and the dietary management of these cats before study entry. As the etiology of CKD was because of P overload, dietary P was quickly restricted before study entry by feeding the experimental diet specifically formulated to contain low protein, low P, and negligible amounts of iP. Both the etiology and the early dietary management of these cats with such a P‐restricted diet might differ from the general cat population, possibly making the outcomes of the current study somewhat unique. In fact, after the 5‐month stabilization period with the experimental diet, plasma PTH and FGF23 had reverted to normal values. Most of the cats that completed the 43‐month study (15/19; 79%), displayed improvement in clinical parameters, with the majority of cases (11/15; 73%) finishing at IRIS 1 CKD, which apparently differs from the outcome of older cats with idiopathic CKD (IRIS 2‐4) maintained on a renal diet.

尽管在使用 LP-LP 干配方后从第 1 个月起平均 GFR 并未显著变化，但在 6 个月后观察到 SDMA 和 FGF23 的升高（这两者与 GFR 呈负相关）可能是肾功能下降的早期指标，这是由于高钙血症和泌尿结石的发生所致。值得注意的是，高钙血症已被证明会在大鼠中降低 GFR 和肾血流，而在患有肾结石的猫中也报道了 SDMA 的早期升高。虽然泌尿结石的形成涉及众多因素，但考虑到总高钙血症猫血清 Ca×P > 55 mg²/dL²（这是用于 CKD 人类预防软组织钙化的参考水平）以及持续性肾结石猫 CaOx RSS 的增加，高钙血症可能是其中的一个促成因素。虽然与泌尿结石没有直接关联，但所有患有总高钙血症的猫以及大多数（4/6 猫）患有泌尿结石的猫在第 17 个月都出现了频繁的 CaOx 晶体。
Although mean GFR did not significantly change from month 1 onwards while on LP‐LP dry, the increase in SDMA and FGF23, which are negatively correlated with GFR, observed after 6 months could be an early indicator of declining kidney function as a result of the development of hypercalcemia and urolithiasis. On this note, hypercalcemia has been shown to decrease GFR and renal blood flow in rats and an early increase in SDMA has been reported in cats with renoliths. Although there are numerous factors involved in the formation of uroliths, hypercalcemia was likely a contributing factor, considering the increase of serum Ca × P > 55 mg2/dL2 in cats with total hypercalcemia, which is the advisory level used for humans with CKD to prevent soft tissue calcification, well as the increased CaOx RSS in cats with persistent renoliths. Although not directly linked with urolithiasis, all cats with total hypercalcemia and most (4/6 cats) with uroliths had frequent CaOx crystals at month 17.
只有在安乐死的猫中确认了一只猫的泌尿结石性质，结果显示为100%硅石结石。这是一个意外发现，并且无法直接找到因果因素。在以MP‐MP‐dry‐wet喂养结束时，总高钙血症得到缓解，离子化高钙血症的发生频率减少至15只猫中的3只，并伴随泌尿结石发生频率降低（15只猫中1只），该猫常有草酸钙晶体。因此可以推测，受影响的猫体内可能存在草酸钙泌尿结石，并且这些结石因体积较小在研究期间被排出。除了明显控制高钙血症，喂养MP‐MP‐dry‐wet还与血清肌酐<1.6 mg/dL（IRIS 2期截断值）和血磷<4.5 mg/dL（IRIS目标）相关，这与该饮食观察到的肾小球滤过率（GFR）增加一致。一个月后BUN升高，并可能伴随肾小球滤过率（GFR）的增加，部分原因可能是该饮食蛋白质含量较高。在人体研究中，已有文献报道，在维持水平内，膳食蛋白可以上调肾小球滤过率（GFR），而超过相当于每日蛋白推荐摄入量的阈值后，肾小球滤过率（GFR）会进一步增加。
The nature of the uroliths was only confirmed in 1 of the cats euthanized, this revealing a 100% silica stone. This was an unexpected finding and a causative factor could not be directly linked. At the end of the feeding period with MP‐MP‐dry‐wet, resolution of total hypercalcemia and the reduced frequency of ionized hypercalcemia to 3/15 cats was accompanied by a reduced frequency of urolithiasis (1/15), which had frequent CaOx crystals. It could therefore be speculated that affected cats had CaOx uroliths and that these were voided during the study because of their small size. In addition to the apparent control of hypercalcemia, feeding MP‐MP‐dry‐wet was associated with serum creatinine <1.6 mg/dL (IRIS stage 2 cut off value) and P < 4.5 mg/dL (IRIS target), which is in keeping with the increased GFR observed with this diet. The increased BUN, and potentially GFR, after 1 month could be partly explained by the higher protein content of this diet. In human studies, GFR upregulation by dietary protein has been reported within maintenance levels, increasing above a threshold equivalent to the recommended daily allowance for protein intake.
在研究结束时，这些猫的慢性肾病（CKD）预后被认为是积极的，这是基于血浆FGF23的下降和进一步稳定，以及在使用MP‑MP‑dry‑wet期间观察到高钙血症和肾小球滤过率（GFR）的明显改善。然而，也有一些参数出现恶化，可能提示CKD的轻微进展。这些包括47%的猫的活性维生素D（Calcitriol）下降到<12 pmol/L，以及47%、33%和27%的猫分别出现肾脏回声增强、对比剂CM定义降低以及肾脏大小/形状改变。上述变化与CKD的IRIS分期没有直接关联，可能归因于CKD的自然进展，同时也与猫的年龄增长及原发损伤有关。
The prognosis of CKD in these cats at the end of the study was considered positive based on the decline of and further stabilization of plasma FGF23 and the apparent improvement in both hypercalcemia and GFR observed while on MP‐ MP‐dry‐wet. Nevertheless, there was also a deterioration in some parameters that could be indicative of subtle CKD progression. These included a drop in calcitriol <12 pmol/L in 47% of cats, as well as increased renal echogenicity, reduced CM definition and altered kidney size/shape in 47%, 33% and 27% of cats, respectively, by the end of the study. The above changes were not directly linked to IRIS staging of CKD and could have been ascribed to a natural progression of CKD, associated with cats becoming older in addition to the primary insult.
本研究的主要局限之一是缺乏对照组，这使得无法将观察到的效果单独归因于饮食。饮食干预取决于猫的临床状况，这也解释了最初过渡到低蛋白-低磷（LP-LP）饮食的原因，以控制氮质血症。由于随后采用了不同的饮食，因此不能完全排除可能的延续效应。这一风险部分通过评估长期喂养效果以及使用每种饮食喂养1个月后的值作为第一个时间点来降低。设备故障导致在整个低蛋白-低磷干食（LP-LP-dry）喂养期间无法测量离子钙（iCa），限制了我们确定离子化高钙血症发生时间的能力。总体而言，这里报告的临床数据支持在早期慢性肾病（CKD）且磷水平正常的猫中使用中等蛋白和磷饮食进行长期管理。
One of the main limitations of this study is the lack of a control group, which prevents attribution of the effects to diet alone. Diet intervention was dictated by the clinical status of cats, justifying the initial transition to the LP‐LP diet with a view to control azotemia. As different diets were fed subsequently, a carry‐over effect cannot be fully ruled out. This risk was partly mitigated by evaluating the long‐term feeding effects and by using values after 1 month on each diet as the first timepoint. Equipment failure prevented measuring iCa throughout the LP‐LP‐dry feeding period, limiting our ability to determine the onset of ionized hypercalcemia. Overall, the clinical data reported here support the use of a moderate protein and phosphate diet for the long‐term management of normo‐phosphatemic cats with early CKD.
将这些研究结果外推至患有特发性慢性肾病（CKD）的普通猫群还需要进一步研究。给予早期 CKD（IRIS 1 和 2 期）且血氮水平受控的猫高限制蛋白质（59 g/Mcal）、总磷（0.84 g/Mcal）且钙磷比高（1.9 : 1）的干粮饮食，与在 17 个月后大多数猫出现离子化高钙血症相关。同时还观察到血浆 FGF23、SDMA 增加以及泌尿结石发生频率升高。通过增加饮食湿度、蛋白质和磷含量，并通过三个月干湿饮食方案降低钙磷比，改善了这些情况。将猫转为蛋白质和磷限制较少的饮食（76-98 g蛋白质/Mcal；1.4-1.6 gP/Mcal；钙磷比 1.4-1.6），且磷含量高于大多数市售肾脏饮食的范围，可在长期内降低高钙血症和泌尿结石的发生，同时将血清肌酐、磷、PTH 和 FGF23 保持在参考范围内。
Extrapolation of these findings to cats from the general population with idiopathic CKD warrants further Feeding a dry diet with highly restricted protein (59 g/Mcal), total P (0.84 g/Mcal), and high Ca : P (1.9 : 1) to cats with early stage CKD (IRIS 1 and 2) controlled azotemia, but was associated with the development of ionized hypercalcemia in the majority of cats after 17 months. Increased plasma FGF23, SDMA, and frequency of urolithiasis were also observed. Increasing dietary moisture, protein, and P content and reducing the Ca : P ratio by providing the same diet on a dry‐wet regimen for 3 months improved these conditions. Transitioning cats onto a less protein and P‐ restricted diet (76‐98 g protein/Mcal; 1.4‐1.6 gP/Mcal; Ca : P 1.4‐1.6), with P inclusion above the range seen in most commercially‐available renal diets, decreased the occurrence of hypercalcemia and urolithiasis, while maintaining serum creatinine, P, PTH, and FGF23 within reference ranges over the long term.
