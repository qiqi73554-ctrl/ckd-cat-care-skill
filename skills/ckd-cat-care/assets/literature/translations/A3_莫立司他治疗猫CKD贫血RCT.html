MATERIALS AND METHODS

研究设计

Study design
我们的研究是一项多中心、随机、双盲、平行、安慰剂对照的实地研究。研究根据拜耳动物健康动物护理审查委员会的指南（协议号#205375）并在获得知情宠物主人同意的情况下进行。设计目的是研究每日口服悬浮剂形式的莫利地司他对美国和欧洲客户拥有患有CKD相关贫血猫的疗效和安全性，给药期为28天。
Ours was a multicenter, randomized, double‐masked, parallel, placebo‐controlled field study. It was conducted in accordance with guidelines of the Animal Care Review Committee of Bayer Animal Health (Protocol #205375) and with informed owner consent. 纳入和排除标准

Inclusion and exclusion criteria
为了符合纳入条件，猫必须≥1岁，体重≥2.0公斤，被诊断为贫血（筛查时和第0天红细胞比容PCV ≤ 27%），且与慢性肾病（CKD）相关（基于病史数据和基线血清肌酐浓度>1.8 mg/dL，未分期），尿液培养阴性且治疗前系统性血压（SBP）≤165 mm Hg。猫可以接受皮下晶体液体治疗，并且合并疾病必须至少在2周内稳定，或在存在甲状腺功能亢进的情况下至少稳定6个月。如果猫拟用于繁殖、怀孕或哺乳、有未经治疗的泌尿道感染，或难以处理，则被排除。如果猫曾接受重组促红素（EPO）或输血治疗（或两者），出现严重且与CKD无关的炎症临床症状，存在其他未控制的临床相关疾病，被诊断为猫白血病或猫免疫缺陷病毒，或出现胃肠道出血的临床症状，也将被排除。
To be eligible for inclusion, cats had to be ≥1 year of age with body weight ≥2.0 kg, diagnosed with anemia (PCV ≤ 27% at screening and on Day 0) associated with CKD (based on historical patient data and baseline serum creatinine concentration >1.8 mg/dL without staging), negative urine culture and a systemic blood pressure (SBP) ≤165 mm Hg pretreatment. Cats could be receiving crystalloid fluids SC and comorbidities had to be stable for a minimum of 2 weeks or for at least 6 months in the presence of hyperthyroidism. Cats were excluded if they were intended for breeding, pregnant or lactating, had an untreated urinary tract infection, or were difficult to handle. Cats also were excluded if they had received previous treatment with recombinant EPO or a blood transfusion or both, severe clinical signs of inflammation not attributed to CKD, other uncontrolled clinically relevant medical conditions, had been diagnosed with feline leukemia or feline immunodeficiency virus, or had clinical signs of gastrointestinal bleeding.

随机分组和掩蔽

Randomization and masking
所有参与临床评估、观察或样本收集的研究人员对治疗和研究分组均保持盲态。配药人员以及任何目击或执行治疗的研究人员不参与任何其他角色。为确保盲态，两位字母的治疗代码被随机分配给molidustat或对照产品（CP），猫根据进入研究的顺序随机分配到两个治疗组中的一个，比例为2:1（molidustat: CP）。使用SAS统计软件（版本9.3，北卡罗来纳州凯里市）创建了全局随机化表，并导出到MEDRIO系统（MEDRIO，美国加利福尼亚州旧金山）。猫的研究分组分配由研究现场的配药人员在MEDRIO中进行。
All study personnel involved in clinical evaluations, observations, or collection of samples were masked to treatment and study group. Dispensers and any study personnel witnessing or administering treatments were not involved in any other roles. To ensure masking, 2 alphabetical treatment codes were randomly assigned to the molidustat or control product (CP), and cats were randomly assigned to 1 of 2 treatment groups at a ratio of 2:1 (molidustat: CP) based on order of entry into the study. A global randomization form was created using SAS Statistical Software (version 9.3, Cary, North Carolina) and exported to the MEDRIO system (MEDRIO, San Francisco, California). The assignment of cats to study groups was performed in MEDRIO by the dispenser at the study site.

治疗阶段和主要血液学参数

Treatment phase and primary hematological parameters
主要的临床终点是随时间变化的红细胞压积（PCV）（HCT）和血球比容（PCV）反应。每只符合入组条件的猫在第−7天（±2天）进行筛查评估，包括体格检查、收缩压（SBP）和体重测量、尿液分析及细菌培养、常规血清生化和血液学检查，以及PCV测定。从第0天开始，猫由主人在喂食前每日口服（PO）至少连续28天，给予CP悬液（仅载体）或Molidustat悬液（25 mg/mL）。Molidustat的给药剂量为5 mg/kg，相当于0.2 mL/kg。给予CP猫相同体积的载体。在每次就诊（V1-V5）期间收集血液以评估血液学参数，并记录体重，就诊时间为第0天（V1；治疗前）、第7天（V2）、第14天（V3）、第21天（V4）和第28天（V5）±2。血液还在第0天和第28天采集以评估血清生化。在第0天（治疗前）、第14天和第28天，在猫适应安静环境后采血前，记录3至5次SBP测量值（在20%变化范围内），并取平均值。
Primary clinical endpoints were HCT and PCV responses over time. Each cat qualifying for enrollment underwent a screening evaluation on Day −7 (±2 days) which included physical examination, SBP and body weight measurements, urinalysis and bacterial culture, routine serum biochemistry and hematology, and PCVs. Starting on Day 0, cats were treated PO once daily by the owner before feeding for at least 28 consecutive days with a suspension of either CP (vehicle only) or molidustat (25 mg/mL). Molidustat was administered at a dosage of 5 mg/kg, equivalent to 0.2 mL/kg. The same volume of vehicle was administered to CP cats. Blood was collected to assess hematological parameters with body weights recorded during each visit (V1‐V5), which occurred on Days 0 (V1; pretreatment), 7 (V2), 14 (V3), 21 (V4), and 28 (V5) ± 2. Blood also was collected to evaluate serum biochemistry on Days 0 and 28. Three to 5 SBP measurements (within 20% variation) were recorded and then averaged before blood sampling on Days 0 (pretreatment), 14, and 28 after the cat had a chance to acclimate to a quiet room.
每个研究地点使用超声多普勒或振荡式收缩压（SBP）设备。美国（Antech GLP，北卡罗来纳州莫里斯维尔）和欧洲（IDEXX GmbH，德国路德维希堡）使用中央实验室评估临床病理标本。治疗成功率评估持续至第28天。任何临床发现都记录为不良事件（AEs）或严重不良事件（导致死亡或危及生命的情况），并无论是否与治疗相关，立即报告给研究者。
Each study site used either an ultrasonic doppler or oscillometric SBP device. A central laboratory was used for evaluation of clinical pathology specimens in the United States (Antech GLP, Morrisville, North Carolina) and Europe (IDEXX GmbH, Ludwigsburg, Germany). Treatment success was evaluated up to and including Day 28. Any clinical findings were recorded as adverse events (AEs) or serious AEs (causing death or life‐threatening condition) and immediately reported to the Investigator whether associated with treatment or not.

血液学效应评估

Hematological effect evaluation
每只猫的个体治疗成功定义为主要终点达到临床相关的增加。血液学参数的临床相关增加定义为从基线到第28天（包括第28天）PCV或HCT增加超过25%或绝对增加4个百分点，或两者兼有。
Individual treatment success for each cat was defined as the primary endpoints reaching a clinically relevant increase. A clinically relevant increase in hematological parameters was defined as a >25% increase or an absolute 4% point increase or both in PCV or HCT from baseline up to and including Day 28.

Mean (±SD) PCV of 8 anemic cats with CKD treated with molidustat sodium during a 28‐day treatment phase and for an additional 8 weeks during the continuation phase. CKD, chronic kidney disease. We investigated the ability of molidustat, a novel HIF‐PH inhibitor, to stimulate production of erythrocytes in client‐ owned CKD cats with anemia. Results showed the potential of molidustat to improve HCT and PCV in affected cats when compared to baseline values as early as 14‐21 days after the start of daily PO administration of 5 mg/kg molidustat sodium. Although significant differences in reaching clinically relevant increases in HCT or PCV compared to CP cats were not consistently achieved, the number of individual treatment successes on Day 28 was higher among molidustat‐treated cats (7/14) compared to CP cats (1/5). This lack of significant difference may be attributed to the limited sample size of the study. The number of cats achieving treatment success increased during the continuation phase.
虽然缺乏促红细胞生成素（EPO）被认为是慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）贫血的一个主要因素，但在这些由客户拥有的猫中，其他可能的促成因素可能包括引起炎症的合并疾病、未诊断的甲状腺功能亢进、药物的联合使用，或铁储备不足（功能性或绝对性）。这些因素可能减缓或削弱红细胞生成反应。此外，由于在治疗前未进行CKD分期，其对研究结果的影响尚不清楚。在单独使用或与其他缓解CKD临床症状所需药物联合使用的情况下，在这些CKD猫中，Molidustat的使用安全性在最长12周的使用后得到了证明。
Although lack of EPO is considered to be a major factor in the anemia of CKD, other possible contributing factors potentially present in these client‐owned cats may have included comorbid conditions causing inflammation, undiagnosed hyperthyroidism, coadministration of medications, or insufficient iron stores (functional or absolute). These factors may have slowed or blunted the erythropoietic response. In addition, because CKD staging was not performed pretreatment, its impact on study results is unknown. Molidustat's in‐use safety profile was demonstrated in these CKD cats after up to 12 weeks of use when administered alone or in combination with other medications needed to alleviate clinical signs of CKD.
由于功能性的肾间质外周细胞（REP细胞）持续丧失导致纤维化、尿毒症物质引起的氧化应激、慢性炎症细胞因子持续存在以及铁代谢受损，肾功能衰竭时EPO的生成会逐渐减少。HIF的过度表达与肾纤维化之间的关系不一致，这引发了对HIF-PH抑制剂可能促进肾纤维化的担忧。然而，研究显示，在小鼠中，尽管HIF稳定性增加且周皮细胞中EPO生成增加，HIF-PH抑制剂并不会增加肾纤维化。红细胞刺激剂（ESAs），如重组人EPO，被用作贫血猫的EPO替代品。这种肠外治疗一直是猫贫血管理的重要工具。然而，接受治疗的猫仍报告了多种并发症，如铁缺乏、高血压、关节痛、发热、癫痫、红细胞增多症以及危及生命的纯红细胞再生障碍症。

However, studies in mice have shown that HIF‐PH inhibitors do not increase renal fibrosis despite HIF stabilization and increased EPO production in pericytes. Erythrocyte‐stimulating agents (ESAs) such as recombinant human EPO are used as EPO replacements for anemic This parenteral treatment has been an important tool in the management of anemia in cats. However, numerous complications such as iron deficiency, hypertension, arthralgia, fever, seizures, polycythemia, and life‐ threatening pure red cell aplasia have been reported in treated cats.

A larger and longer study would provide more information regarding SBP. Molidustat, a novel HIF‐PH inhibitor, administered once daily for 28 days at 5 mg/kg significantly increased HCT and PCV as early as 14‐21 days after the start of treatment compared to baseline in anemic cats with CKD.
我们研究的局限性包括统计效能低（样本量小）和持续时间短、基线缺乏慢性肾病（CKD）分期且慢性肾病（CKD）分期对研究结果的影响不明、使用不同实验室（欧洲和美国）进行临床病理评估、若干猫在关键时间点缺失数据以及在共病存在的情况下使用伴随用药。尽管存在这些局限性，我们关于研究molidustat安全刺激贫血猫肾功能衰竭情况下红细胞生成能力的目标已实现。未来需要进行研究以评估在更多贫血慢性肾病（CKD）猫中使用molidustat的效果，并采用血细胞压积（HCT）或红细胞压积（PCV）作为替代参数以便方便地监测红细胞生成反应，因为molidustat可能成为一种创新的治疗选择，满足临床未满足需求，同时安全地改善贫血慢性肾病（CKD）猫的生活质量。
Limitations of our study include low power (small sample size) and short duration, lack of CKD staging at baseline and unknown impact of CKD stage on study results, use of different laboratories (Europe and United States) for clinical pathology assessment, data missing for several cats at critical timepoints and use of concomitant medications in the presence of comorbidities. Despite these limitations, our objectives of investigating the ability of molidustat to safely stimulate erythropoiesis in failing kidneys of anemic cats were fulfilled. Future studies evaluating the use of molidustat in a larger number of anemic CKD cats, using HCT (or PCV) as surrogate parameters to conveniently monitor erythropoietic response, are necessary because molidustat potentially could be an innovative therapeutic option to address an unmet clinical need while safely improving the quality of life of anemic cats with CKD.
