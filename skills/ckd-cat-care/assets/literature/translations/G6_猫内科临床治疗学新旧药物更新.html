Molidustat is indicated for use in cats with non-regenerative anemia associated with CKD and acts by increasing the levels of endogenous erythropoietin, an important hematopoietic growth factor. Erythropoietin is primarily produced by renal peritubular interstitial cells, and its production is stimulated by hypoxia-inducible factor (HIF). During normoxia, HIF is degraded by hypoxia- inducible factor propyl hydroxylase (HIF-PH) enzymes; when there is renal hypoxia, however, this inhibits HIF-PH enzymes, stimulating erythropoiesis (Figure 5).
慢性肾病（CKD）会导致产生促红细胞生成素（EPO）的肾小管周围间质细胞数量减少；这会导致猫在患有此病时促红细胞生成素（EPO）水平下降。然而，由于CKD引起的代谢活动减少，肾脏中仍保持相对较高的氧分压。实验证明，抑制HIF-PH酶的缺氧刺激仍然会引起红细胞生成，这一机制因此为使用HIF-PH抑制剂莫利杜斯塔（molidustat）治疗CKD贫血提供了基础。

Experimentally, a hypoxic stimulus that inhibits HIF-PH enzymes still results in erythropoiesis, 122,123 and it is this mechanism that therefore provides the basis for the use of molidustat, an HIF-PH inhibitor, to treat anemia in CKD.
