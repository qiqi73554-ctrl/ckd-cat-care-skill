Serum vitamin D metabolites do not differ between cats with chronic kidney disease and healthy cats Valerie J Parker 1,✉, Lauren J Reynolds 1, Jessica M Quimby 1 The aim of the present study was to characterize comprehensive vitamin D metabolite profiles in cats with chronic kidney disease (CKD) compared with healthy cats. A total of 33 healthy cats and 66 cats with CKD (six International Renal Interest Society [IRIS] stage 1, 36 IRIS stage 2, 18 IRIS stage 3, six IRIS stage 4) were prospectively enrolled. Serum vitamin D metabolites, including 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 (25[OH]D3), 3-epi-25-hydroxyvitamin D3 (3-epi-25[OH]D3), 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3 (1,25[OH]2D3; calcitriol) and 24,25dihydroxyvitamin D3 (24,25[OH]2D3), were analyzed via liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrophotometry. Vitamin D metabolites, clinicopathologic parameters, age, body weight and body condition score were compared between groups using a Mann–Whitney U-test or t-test as appropriate. Correlations between vitamin D metabolites and clinicopathologic parameters, age and weight were assessed using Spearman’s rank test.
各组之间的任何维生素D代谢物均无显著差异。慢性肾病（CKD）猫与健康猫的中位 25(OH)D3 浓度分别为 28.9 ng/ml（范围 12.2–68.8）和 26.3 ng/ml（范围 11.5–55.9）。慢性肾病（CKD）猫与健康猫的中位 3-epi-25(OH)D3 浓度分别为 9.1 ng/ml（范围 2.2–30.4）和 7.2 ng/ml（范围 1.9–22.9）。慢性肾病（CKD）猫与健康猫的中位钙三醇浓度分别为 141.3 pg/ml（范围 38.6–367.8）和 127.9 pg/ml（范围 71.5–235.8）。慢性肾病（CKD）猫与健康猫的中位 24,25(OH)2D3 浓度分别为 9.8 ng/ml（范围 2.5–32.5）和 8.8 ng/ml（范围 1.2–38.4）。任何维生素D代谢物与年龄或血清肌酐浓度均无相关性，25(OH)D 与血细胞比容（HCT）、血压或尿蛋白:肌酐比值之间也无相关性。
There was no significant difference in any vitamin D metabolite between groups. Median 25(OH)D3 concentrations in cats with CKD and healthy cats were 28.9 ng/ml (range 12.2–68.8) and 26.3 ng/ml (range 11.5–55.9), respectively. Median 3-epi-25(OH)D3 concentrations in cats with CKD and healthy cats were 9.1 ng/ml (range 2.2–30.4) and 7.2 ng/ml (range 1.9–22.9), respectively. Median calcitriol concentrations in cats with CKD and healthy cats were 141.3 pg/ml (range 38.6– 367.8) and 127.9 pg/ml (range 71.5–235.8), respectively. Median 24,25(OH)2D3 concentrations in cats with CKD and healthy cats were 9.8 ng/ml (range 2.5–32.5) and 8.8 ng/ml (range 1.2–38.4), respectively. There was no correlation between any vitamin D metabolite and age or serum creatinine concentration, and no correlation between 25(OH)D and hematocrit, blood pressure or urine protein:creatinine ratio.

Conclusions and relevance
猫慢性肾病（CKD）患者的维生素D代谢产物没有显著差异，这可能对关于补充卡尔西三醇的建议产生影响。关键词：卡尔西三醇、胆骨化醇、钙化二醇、慢性肾病（CKD）-矿物质和骨骼疾病。维生素D是一种必需营养素，用于身体的各种功能。维生素D最常被人们知道的作用是通过骨-甲状旁腺-肾轴维持钙磷稳态。然而，由于维生素D受体的多种作用，它在免疫调节、炎症、胰岛素分泌和造血过程中也发挥重要作用。在猫体内，维生素D代谢始于膳食摄入的维生素D3（胆骨化醇）或维生素D2（麦角钙化醇）。猫缺乏通过紫外线合成维生素D3的能力，而这是人体的主要机制。摄入后，胆骨化醇在肝脏中代谢为25-羟基维生素D（25[OH]D3），然后通过1-α（α）-羟化酶代谢为其活性代谢物卡尔西三醇（1,25-二羟基维生素D3；1,25[OH]2D3）。
Vitamin D metabolites were not significantly different in cats with CKD, which could have implications for recommendations regarding calcitriol supplementation. Keywords: Calcitriol, cholecalciferol, calcifediol, chronic kidney disease-mineral and bone disorder Vitamin D is an essential nutrient used for various bodily functions. Vitamin D is most commonly known for its role in calcium-phosphorus homeostasis via the bone-parathyroid-kidney axis. 1 However, it also plays a significant role in immune regulation, inflammation, insulin secretion and hematopoiesis due to the various roles of the vitamin D receptor.2–4 In cats, vitamin D metabolism begins with dietary intake of vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) or vitamin D2 (ergocalciferol). Cats lack the ability to synthesize vitamin D3 via ultraviolet light, which is the primary mechanism in humans. After ingestion, cholecalciferol is metabolized in the liver to 25-hydroxyvitamin D (25[OH]D3), which is then metabolized via 1-alpha (α)-hydroxylase to its active metabolite, calcitriol (1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3; 1,25[OH]2D3).

Inactivation of 25(OH)D3 by 24-hydroxylase results in the production of 24,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3 (24,25[OH]2D3). Excretion of inactive vitamin D metabolites is accomplished by the biliary system.1–3,5 Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is a common disease in cats characterized by progressively declining renal function, which affects upward of 50% of cats of all ages.
6 慢性肾脏病（CKD）的进展与肾小球滤过率（GFR）降低和高磷血症的发展相关，而高磷血症又会升高血液中甲状旁腺激素（PTH）和成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF-23）-23（FGF-23）的浓度，这些因素都会导致维生素D代谢的改变。7,8 慢性肾脏病中维生素D代谢改变的拟议机制包括：胆钙化醇摄入减少、肝脏中胆钙化醇向25(OH)D3的转化减少、通过1α-羟化酶活化25(OH)D3减少、25(OH)D3和1,25(OH)2D3的失活增加，以及蛋白质丢失性肾病中维生素D结合蛋白的丢失。1,3 由于CKD导致的肾功能和矿物质稳态的这些改变通常被称为CKD-矿物质与骨代谢紊乱（CKD-MBD）。
6 CKD progression is associated with reduced glomerular filtration rate and the development of hyperphosphatemia, which in turn increases systemic concentrations of parathyroid hormone (PTH) and fibroblast growth factor-23 (FGF-23), all of which results in alteration in vitamin D metabolism.7,8 Proposed mechanisms of vitamin D metabolism alteration in CKD include decreased intake of cholecalciferol, decreased conversion of cholecalciferol in the liver to 25(OH)D3, decreased 25(OH)D3 activation via 1α-hydroxylase, increased inactivation of 25(OH)D3 and 1,25(OH)2D3 and loss of vitamin D binding protein in protein-losing nephropathy.1,3 These alterations in kidney function and mineral homeostasis as a result of CKD is generally referred to as CKD-mineral and bone disorder (CKD-MBD).
慢性肾脏病相关的矿物质和骨代谢紊乱（CKD-MBD）在人体中已被广泛研究，而在猫中研究相对较少。根据作者所知，目前尚无研究评估患有CKD猫的全面维生素D代谢物谱。现有文献主要关注甲状旁腺激素（PTH）、成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF-23）23（FGF-23）和1,25(OH)2D3的变化。与健康猫和患有CKD的猫相比，患有尿毒症和终末期CKD的猫的1,25(OH)2D3浓度较低。其他研究显示健康猫与CKD猫的1,25(OH)2D3浓度没有显著差异。最近的研究发现了一种新型维生素D代谢物，即25-羟维生素D3的C-3α-表异构体（3-epi-25[OH]D3），在健康猫的血浆和血清中含量丰富。该代谢物的确切作用尚未确定。鉴于其相对较高的浓度，有必要阐明这种代谢物在患有CKD-MBD的猫体内是否发生变化。
CKD-MBD has been extensively studied in humans and, to a lesser degree, in cats. 5 To the authors’ knowledge, there are no studies evaluating comprehensive vitamin D metabolite profiles of cats with CKD. The available literature primarily focuses on alterations in PTH, FGF-23 and 1,25(OH)2D3. Cats with uremic and end-stage CKD have lower concentrations of 1,25(OH)2D3 compared with healthy cats and cats with CKD. 7 Other studies have shown no major difference in 1,25(OH)2D3 concentrations between healthy cats and cats with CKD. 9 Recent studies have identified a novel vitamin D metabolite, a C-3, alpha-epimer of 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 (3-epi- 25[OH]D3), which is abundantly present in the plasma and serum of healthy cats. 10 The exact role of this metabolite has not yet been established. Given its relatively high concentrations, it is important to elucidate whether this metabolite is altered in cats with CKD-MBD.
本研究的主要目标是评估不同阶段慢性肾病（CKD）猫咪的血清维生素D代谢物的综合状态，并与健康对照组进行比较，包括25(OH)D3、3-epi-25(OH)D3、1,25(OH)2D3和24,25(OH)2D3。我们的假设是，与健康猫相比，CKD猫的25(OH)D3、3-epi-25(OH)D3和24,25(OH)2D3浓度会较低，而1,25(OH)2D3浓度在各组之间不会有差异。
The primary goal of this study was to evaluate the comprehensive serum vitamin D metabolite status in cats with various stages of CKD compared with healthy controls, including 25(OH)D3, 3-epi-25(OH)D3, 1,25(OH)2D3 and 24,25(OH)2D3. Our hypothesis was that cats with CKD would have lower 25(OH)D3, 3-epi-25(OH)D3 and 24,25(OH)2D3 concentrations compared with healthy cats and that 1,25(OH)2D3 concentrations would not differ between groups.

Sample collection and vitamin D metabolite analysis
所有猫在采血前都经历了禁食。采血后，将样本在血清分离管中以343× g 离心10分钟。血清样本储存于−80°C，直至批量送至Heartland Assays实验室（一家维生素D外部质量评估计划认证的实验室）进行维生素D代谢物分析，通过液相色谱-串联质谱法进行。测量的分析物包括血清25(OH)D3、3-epi-25(OH)D3、1,25(OH)2D3和24,25(OH)2D3。
All cats were fasted before blood collection. After blood collection, samples were spun in serum separator tubes at 343× g for 10 mins. Measured analytes included serum 25(OH)D3, 3-epi-25(OH)D3, 1,25(OH)2D3 and 24,25(OH)2D3.

Serum concentrations of (a) 25(OH)D3, (b) 3-epi-25(OH)D3, (c) 1,25(OH)2D3 and (d) 24,25(OH)2D3 in cats with chronic kidney disease (CKD) and healthy cats It was not possible to determine absolute dietary vitamin D3 (ie, cholecalciferol) intake in the cats with CKD given their variable appetites and variety of diets fed in rotation. Approximately 50% of cats with CKD were eating some or all of one or more veterinary therapeutic renal diets. The other cats were eating a variety of adult maintenance diets. Among the veterinary therapeutic renal diets, there was a wide range of cholecalciferol, approximately 17–94 IU per 100 kcal. Many cats with CKD were eating a variety of flavors of Purina Fancy Feast; the amount of cholecalciferol in the many flavors of Fancy Feast encompasses a wide range of 17–138 IU/100 kcal. All of the healthy cats were eating various adult maintenance diets that provided a range of approximately 16–25 IU cholecalciferol per 100 kcal.
在这项研究中，患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫与健康对照猫相比，血清维生素D代谢物没有显著差异。这与在人类和患有CKD的狗中观察到的情况不同，在那些病例中，大多数维生素D代谢物相比健康个体较低，并且在CKD狗的第3期时达到统计学显著水平。血清25(OH)D3浓度可能会受到猫的多种疾病状态和炎症的影响；因此，如果考虑到CKD会引发慢性炎症状态，25(OH)D3浓度在患有CKD的猫中较低也不足为奇。此外，研究显示，患有CKD和贫血的人类25(OH)D3浓度也会独立地偏低。尽管本研究中患有CKD的猫血细胞比容（HCT）显著低于健康对照猫，但这似乎并未影响其25(OH)D浓度。
In this study, cats with CKD did not have significantly different serum vitamin D metabolites compared with healthy control cats. This differs from what has been demonstrated in humans and dogs with CKD, where most vitamin D metabolites are lower compared with healthy counterparts, becoming statistically significant by stage 3 in dogs with CKD.11–13 🔍 Serum 25(OH)D3 concentrations can be affected by a number of disease states and inflammation in cats;2,14,15 thus, it would not have been surprising for 25(OH)D3 concentrations to be lower in cats with CKD if we consider CKD to induce a state of chronic inflammation. In addition, 25(OH)D3 concentrations have been shown to be independently lower in humans with CKD and anemia. 16 Despite the cats with CKD in this study having significantly lower HCT levels than healthy control cats, this did not seem to influence their 25(OH)D concentrations.
血清3-epi-25(OH)D3部分约占猫总25(OH)D3浓度的25–30%，这与在患有心肌病的猫中显示的结果一致。17 在一项针对类风湿性关节炎和慢性肾病（CKD）的人类研究中，血清3-epi-25(OH)D3浓度随着CKD严重程度的增加而升高。18 在纳入本研究的猫中，该代谢物与血清肌酐浓度之间没有相关性。可能是本研究中的猫未受到足够严重的影响以表现出差异。本研究中的1,25(OH)2D3（骨化三醇）结果确实反映了先前的发现。Barber和Elliott 7 证明，骨化三醇浓度仅在终末期CKD时下降，而早期CKD的猫相比健康猫具有更高的骨化三醇浓度。本研究中大多数CKD猫属于轻度肾性氮质血症，只有六只猫被归类为IRIS 4期CKD。
The serum 3-epi-25(OH)D3 fraction made up approximately 25–30% of the total 25(OH)D3 concentration in cats, consistent with what has been shown in cats with cardiomyopathy. 17 In a study of humans with rheumatoid arthritis and CKD, serum 3-epi-25(OH)D3 concentrations increased as severity of CKD progressed. 18 In the cats included in our study, there was no correlation between this metabolite and serum creatinine concentrations. It is possible that the cats in this study were not affected severely enough to demonstrate a difference. The 1,25(OH)2D3 (calcitriol) results in this study do mirror what has been previously demonstrated. Barber and Elliott 7 demonstrated that calcitriol concentrations only decrease with end-stage CKD, and cats with earlier CKD had higher calcitriol concentrations compared with healthy cats. The majority of cats with CKD in this study had mild renal azotemia, with only six cats classified as having IRIS stage 4 CKD.
如果有更多晚期3期和4期CKD（慢性肾病（CKD））猫被纳入研究，可能会显示出维生素D代谢物浓度的更大差异。还需要额外的研究来评估晚期CKD猫的维生素D代谢物。历史上，对于CKD猫，常建议补充骨化三醇（calcitriol）以应对假设的肾性继发性甲状旁腺功能亢进（SHPT）。骨化三醇的使用可能会产生潜在的不良后果，包括诱发高钙血症、高磷血症以及钙磷乘积升高，从而增加软组织矿化的风险。由于骨化三醇可刺激FGF-23的释放，增加骨化三醇浓度可能还有其他非特异性影响。在接受骨化二醇（calcifediol）和帕立骨化三醇（paricalcitol）治疗的狗中，已证实FGF-23浓度升高。遗憾的是，我们在本研究中无法分析PTH和FGF23浓度。
It is possible that if more cats with late stage 3 and stage 4 CKD had been enrolled, a greater difference in vitamin D metabolite concentrations might have been demonstrated. Additional studies are necessary to assess vitamin D metabolites in cats with more advanced CKD. Historically, calcitriol supplementation has been recommended for cats with CKD to address presumed renal secondary hyperparathyroidism. Potential adverse consequences can occur with calcitriol administration, including inducing hypercalcemia, hyperphosphatemia and an increased calcium-phosphate product, which increases the risk of soft tissue mineralization. Since calcitriol stimulates FGF-23 release, there may be additional nonspecific consequences of increasing calcitriol concentrations. Increased FGF-23 concentrations have been demonstrated in dogs receiving calcifediol and paricalcitol.19,20 We were unfortunately unable to analyze PTH and FGF23 concentrations in this study.
磷酸盐稳态与维生素D代谢之间存在关系。血清磷酸盐增加应刺激FGF-23并减少1,25(OH)2D3的活化。因此，口服磷结合剂可能间接影响1,25(OH)2D3的浓度。然而，仅有四只患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫接受了口服磷结合剂，并且各组之间血清磷浓度没有显著差异，因此这不太可能对结果产生贡献。我们分析的最后一种代谢物是24,25(OH)2D3。有研究提出，在人类中，血清24,25(OH)2D3浓度可能可作为血清25(OH)D3浓度的可靠指标，并且25(OH)D3与24,25(OH)2D3的比值可能有助于预测相对的24羟化酶（24[OH]酶）活性，比值降低表示24(OH)酶活性增加，并可能对维生素D治疗产生耐受性。21 在纳入的CKD猫和健康猫之间，这一比值没有差异。
There is a relationship between phosphate homeostasis and vitamin D metabolism. Increased serum phosphate should stimulate FGF-23 and decrease 1,25(OH)2D3 activation. Thus, it is possible that oral phosphate binders could have indirectly affected 1,25(OH)2D3 concentrations. However, only four cats with CKD received oral phosphate binders, and there was no significant difference in serum phosphorus concentrations between groups, making this an unlikely contributor to the results. The final metabolite we analyzed was 24,25(OH)2D3. It has been proposed that, in humans, serum 24,25(OH)2D3 concentration might serve as a reliable indicator of serum 25(OH)D3 concentrations, and that the ratio of 25(OH)D3 to 24,25(OH)2D3 might help predict relative 24hydroxylase (24[OH]ase) activity, with decreased ratios indicating increased 24(OH)ase activity and possible resistance to vitamin D treatment. 21 There was no difference in this ratio between the cats with CKD and the healthy cats included.
最后，饮食显著影响我们结果的可能性似乎不大。与兽医治疗性肾脏饮食相比，猫咪成年维持饮食中提供的胆钙化醇含量并没有很大差异。大多数猫咪所吃的饮食每100千卡大约提供20–25 IU，相对于美国饲料控制官员协会(American Feed Control Officials)的最低要求7 IU/100千卡。22 本研究存在一些局限性。一些健康猫来自研究群体，可能无法代表客户拥有的猫群。我们尝试通过也招募客户拥有的健康猫来缓解这一问题。猫的年龄并不完全匹配。在一项评估心肌病猫25(OH)D3状态的研究中，每增加1岁，25(OH)D3浓度会降低0.98 ng/ml。17 在本研究中，我们未发现年龄与25(OH)D3浓度之间的相关性。
Lastly, it seems unlikely that diet significantly influenced our results. There is not a large difference in the amount of cholecalciferol that feline adult maintenance diets provide compared with veterinary therapeutic renal diets. Most of the diets that cats were eating provided approximately 20–25 IU per 100 kcal, relative to the Association of American Feed Control Officials minimum requirement of 7 IU/100 kcal. 22 There were some limitations to this study. Some of the healthy cats included were from a research colony and may not reflect a client-owned cat population. We attempted to mitigate this by also enrolling client-owned healthy cats. Cats were not perfectly age-matched. In a study assessing 25(OH)D3 status in cats with cardiomyopathy, every 1-year increase in age was associated with a 0.98 ng/ml decrease in 25(OH)D3. 17 We found no correlation between age and 25(OH)D3 concentrations in this study.
同样，尚未在健康成年猫23或健康犬以及患粘液样二尖瓣病的犬24–26中证明年龄与25(OH)D3状态之间存在关系。在患慢性肾病（CKD）的人类中，年龄与25(OH)D3浓度之间也没有明显的相关性27。理想情况下应招募年纪较大的健康猫；然而，要找到年龄超过13岁且没有其他可能影响维生素D代谢的疾病（例如慢性肠病）的猫是具有挑战性的1。食物摄入未被控制，也无法确定本研究中所有猫的精确胆钙化醇摄入量。食欲不振可能影响患慢性肾病（CKD）猫的营养摄入。如果这影响了结果，我们本会预期患慢性肾病（CKD）猫的维生素D代谢物会下降，但我们并未发现这一点。此外，在犬中，需要大剂量胆钙化醇补充才能影响血清25(OH)D3浓度28,29，因此犬只摄入的标准剂量胆钙化醇不太可能显著影响这些猫的25(OH)D3浓度。
Similarly, a relationship between age and 25(OH)D3 status has not been demonstrated in healthy adult cats 23 or healthy dogs or dogs with myxomatous mitral valve disease.24–26 In humans with CKD, there is no distinct correlation between age and 25(OH)D3 concentrations. 27 It would have been ideal to enroll older healthy cats; however, it is challenging to identify cats aged over 13 years that do not have other medical conditions that can also influence vitamin D metabolism (eg, chronic enteropathy). 1 Dietary intake was not controlled, and nor could precise cholecalciferol intake be determined for all cats in this study. It is possible that hyporexia could have influenced decreased nutrient intake in cats with CKD. If this were to have affected results, we would have expected decreased vitamin D metabolites in cats with CKD, which we did not identify. In addition, in dogs, large doses of cholecalciferol supplementation have been required to affect serum 25(OH)D3 concentrations,28,29 making it unlikely that standard amounts of ingested cholecalciferol significantly impacted these cats’ 25(OH)D3 concentrations.
患有慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）的猫与健康对照猫相比，其维生素D代谢物浓度没有显著差异。这些发现表明，涉及CKD-MBD的因素在猫与人类及犬类之间是独特的。对于患有CKD的猫，应谨慎考虑补充钙三醇，理想情况下应基于全面的维生素D代谢物和PTH浓度。作者感谢Deborah Linder博士和Kathryn McGonigle博士在研究样本收集方面的协助。作者注：本研究的部分结果已作为研究摘要在2024年美国兽医内科学院论坛上进行展示。
Cats with CKD did not have significantly different vitamin D metabolite concentrations compared with healthy control cats. These findings illustrate that factors involved in CKD-MBD are unique to cats compared with humans and dogs. Calcitriol supplementation should be considered judiciously in cats with CKD, ideally based on comprehensive vitamin D metabolite and PTH concentrations.
