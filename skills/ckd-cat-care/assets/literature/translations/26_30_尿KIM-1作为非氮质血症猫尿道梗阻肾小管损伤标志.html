Renal tubular injury associated with urethral obstruction may occur in cats before alterations in conventional renal biomarkers become apparent. In this study, urinary concentrations of kidney injury molecule-1 (KIM-1), a biomarker of tubular epithelial injury, were evaluated in nonazotemic cats with urethral obstruction and compared with those of healthy cats. Cats with urethral obstruction may develop tubular damage before conventional indicators of renal dysfunction become abnormal, making early recognition challenging when relying solely on conventional renal biomarkers. This study evaluated urinary kidney injury molecule-1 (KIM-1) concentrations in non-azotemic cats with urethral obstruction and compared its expression with conventional renal biomarkers. Twenty-four male cats were prospectively enrolled and allocated into a control group (n = 12) and a urethral obstruction group (n = 12), all presenting serum creatinine concentrations within the reference interval. Urinary KIM-1 concentrations were quantified by enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) and normalized to urinary creatinine concentrations. Serum creatinine and blood urea nitrogen (BUN) did not differ significantly between groups. In contrast, urinary KIM-1 concentrations were significantly higher in cats with urethral obstruction compared with healthy controls (p < 0.001).

Introduction Acute kidney injury (AKI) can be defined as a spectrum of diseases associated with a sudden onset of renal parenchyma injuries, more typically characterized by generalized kidney failure to meet the body’s excretory, metabolic and endocrine demands [1,2]. The main causes of AKI include the prolonged and indiscriminate use of nephrotoxic drugs, such as non-steroidal anti-inflammatory and chemotherapy drugs, infectious diseases, vasculitis, neoplasms, and obstruction of the urinary tract [1,3,4]. In cats, urethral obstruction is the primary cause of AKI [5].
急性肾损伤（AKI）的临床表现可以从急性尿毒症到无尿不等，这可能导致肾实质损伤和慢性肾病（CKD）的发生[1,3]。因此，及早识别猫的这一状况对于防止肾病进展至肾功能衰竭至关重要。然而，由于目前诊断方法在检测亚临床肾损伤方面的局限性，在该物种中早期诊断AKI具有挑战性[6]。尿液生物标志物的出现以及AKI的分类系统是理解人类AKI与CKD双向关系的重要里程碑[4]。流行病学研究表明，即使是短暂的肾功能障碍，如由脱水引起的肾前性氮质血症，如果氮质血症的原因没有得到及时纠正，也是CKD发展的风险因素[7,8]。
The clinical signs of AKI can range from acute uremia to anuria that can lead to renal parenchymal injury and the development of chronic kidney disease (CKD) [1,3]. Therefore, identifying this condition in cats at early stages is crucial to prevent kidney disease progression to kidney failure. However, early diagnosis of AKI in this species is challenging due to the current limitations of diagnostic approaches in detecting subclinical kidney lesions [6]. The advent of urinary biomarkers and the classification system for AKI are important milestones in understanding the bidirectional relationship between AKI and CKD in humans [4]. Epidemiological studies have shown that even transient kidney dysfunction, such as prerenal azotemia caused by dehydration, is a risk factor for the development of CKD if the cause of azotemia is not previously corrected [7,8].
在人类和患有急性肾损伤（AKI）的重症猫的临床研究中，血清肌酐（sCre）浓度仅略有增加（0.1–0.2 mg/dL），而评估早期生物标志物如肾损伤分子-1（KIM-1）和半乳糖凝集素-3的研究显示，其浓度在血清肌酐增加之前就已升高 [9]。然而，尽管兽医肾脏学取得了进展，猫的急性肾损伤早期诊断仍然具有挑战性，尤其是在非氮质血症患者中。传统的肾脏生物标志物，如血清肌酐和血尿素氮（BUN），主要反映肾小球滤过功能，并且通常只有在功能性肾组织大量丧失后才会增加 [4,9]。相比之下，结构性生物标志物，包括肾损伤分子-1（KIM-1），可以直接提供肾小管上皮损伤的信息，并且可能在可测量的肾小球滤过变化之前被检测到 [10,11,12]。因此，结构性生物标志物作为人类和兽医医学中肾脏损伤检测的辅助工具，已引起越来越多的关注 [4,10,11]。
Clinical studies in humans and cats critically ill with AKI report only a slight increase in serum creatinine (sCre) concentration (0.1–0.2 mg/dL), while studies evaluating early biomarkers such as kidney injury molecule-1 (KIM-1) and galectin-3 reveal an increase in concentration before increases in sCre [9]. However, despite advances in veterinary nephrology, the early diagnosis of AKI in cats remains challenging, particularly in non-azotemic patients. Conventional renal biomarkers, such as serum creatinine and blood urea nitrogen (BUN), primarily reflect glomerular filtration function and typically increase only after substantial loss of functional renal mass [4,9]. In contrast, structural biomarkers, including kidney injury molecule-1 (KIM-1), provide direct information regarding tubular epithelial injury and may be detected before measurable changes in glomerular filtration occur [10,11,12]. Consequently, structural biomarkers have attracted increasing interest as adjunctive tools for the detection of renal injury in both human and veterinary medicine [4,10,11].
最近，Bland 等人[10]描述了猫的肾损伤分子 1（KIM-1）基因及其蛋白。KIM-1 是一种管状跨膜糖蛋白，在没有肾损伤的患者尿液中未检测到[13]。急性肾损伤（AKI）的发生会诱导近曲小管未分化上皮细胞表面表达 KIM-1。这使得在缺血性损伤的猫尿液和组织中检测到 KIM-1 成为可能，其有潜力被用作与 AKI、危重疾病及由于相关缺血过程（包括尿道阻塞）导致的疑似 AKI 相关的肾小管损伤的标志[11,12]。
Recently, the feline Kidney Injury Molecule 1 (KIM-1) gene and its protein were described by Bland et al. [10]. KIM-1 is a tubular transmembrane glycoprotein that is not detected in the urine of patients without kidney injury [13]. The occurrence of AKI induces KIM-1 expression on the surface of undifferentiated epithelial cells of the proximal convoluted tubule. This enabled the detection of KIM-1 in the urine and tissue of cats in cases of ischemic injury, which has the potential to be used as a marker of tubular injury associated with AKI, critical disease and suspected AKI due to associated ischemic processes, including urethral obstruction [11,12].
关于猫早期AKI生物标志物表达的研究很少。Woerde等人的研究[14]显示，患有尿道阻塞的氮质血症猫血清半乳糖凝集素-3（sGal-3）浓度升高。同样，对AKI猫尿液中KIM-1进行评估的研究报告在尿路阻塞、脓毒症及其他肾损伤情况下表达增加[11,14]。然而，对于自然发生尿道阻塞且未出现氮质血症的猫尿液中KIM-1浓度的信息仍然有限。以往大多数评估肾脏生物标志物的研究主要集中于已确立的肾损伤、慢性肾病（CKD）或实验模型。因此，本研究提供了关于在明显氮质血症出现之前，尿道阻塞猫尿液中KIM-1表达的额外信息，从而增进我们对与这种常见猫科急症相关的肾小管损伤的理解。
There are few studies on the expression of early biomarkers of AKI in cats. The study by Woerde et al. [14] demonstrated increased serum galectin-3 (sGal-3) concentrations in azotemic cats with ureteral obstruction. Likewise, studies evaluating urinary KIM-1 in cats with AKI reported increased expression in cases of urinary obstruction, sepsis, and other causes of renal injury [11,14]. However, information regarding urinary KIM-1 concentrations in non-azotemic cats with naturally occurring urethral obstruction remains limited. Most previous studies evaluating renal biomarkers have focused on established kidney injury, chronic kidney disease, or experimental models. Therefore, the present study contributes additional information regarding urinary KIM-1 expression in cats with urethral obstruction before the development of overt azotemia, improving our understanding of renal tubular injury associated with this common feline emergency.

Reference intervals adopted by the Clinical Pathology Laboratory of the Veterinary Hospital were 0.8–1.8 mg/dL for serum creatinine and 20–65 mg/dL for blood urea nitrogen (BUN). All blood and urine samples from cats with urethral obstruction were collected immediately upon admission and before any therapeutic intervention, including urethral catheterization, bladder decompression, intravenous fluid therapy, or stabilization procedures. Following initial clinical evaluation and abdominal ultrasonography, urine samples were obtained by ultrasound-guided cystocentesis for laboratory analyses. Urine samples were collected by cystocentesis, quantifying a total volume of 10 mL, and urine specific gravity (USG) was determined by refractometry (analog optical refractometer RSG32, Akso, São Leopoldo, Brazil). A conversion factor for cats was then applied using the equation: Feline USG = (0.846 × USG reading in human refractometry) + 0.154 by Reppas and Foster [15].
此外，尿液样本使用试剂条和半自动尿液分析仪（Combilizer 13，Human Diagnostics Worldwide，德国威斯巴登）进行分析，以获得尿液分析中pH值、蛋白质、葡萄糖、酮体、胆红素、白细胞和红细胞的测量值。因此，取3毫升的等分试样进行离心（3分钟，365×g），并在30分钟后通过光学显微镜分析沉淀物[15]。因此，尿蛋白和尿肌酐的测量使用半自动生化分析仪（A15 BioSystems，西班牙巴塞罗那）进行。尿液中的蛋白质使用商业BioSystems®试剂盒测定，该试剂盒基于紫金红原理，在端点反应中于620 nm处读取。尿肌酐（uCre）使用Jaffé碱性枸橼酸盐原理测定，采用商业试剂盒（BioSystems®试剂盒）进行端点反应，并在505 nm处读取。为此，尿液样本在蒸馏水中按1:50稀释，以达到检测的线性范围，并对最终值进行适当校正。
In addition, urine samples were analyzed using reagent strips and a semi-automatic urine analyzer (Combilizer 13, Human Diagnostics Worldwide, Wiesbaden, Germany) to obtain measurements of pH, protein, glucose, ketone, bilirubin, leukocytes, and erythrocytes in the urinalysis. Thus, an aliquot of 3 mL was centrifuged (3 min at 365× g), and the pellet was analyzed by light microscopy after 30 min [15]. Therefore, the measurement of urinary protein and urinary creatinine was performed with a semiautomatic biochemical analyzer (A15 BioSystems, Barcelona, Spain). The protein present in urine was determined using a commercial BioSystems® kit, which is based on the pyrogallol red principle, in an endpoint reaction and reading at 620 nm. Urinary creatinine (uCre) was measured using the Jaffé alkaline picrate principle, with the commercial kit (BioSystems® kit) in the endpoint reaction and reading at 505 nm. For this purpose, the urine samples were diluted 1:50 in distilled water to reach the linearity range of the test, with the final value being duly corrected.

The urinary protein/creatinine ratio was the relationship between protein and creatinine values expressed in the same unit (mg/dL). Test validation was performed by determining repeatability. All urine samples were analyzed qualitatively and quantitatively for aerobic bacteria culture, and samples with bacterial growth ≥ 105 colony-forming units/mL were considered positive [16]. 2.3. Determination of Urinary Levels of KIM-1 by Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Urinary KIM-1 levels were quantified using the sandwich enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) (Human Kit from BosterBiological Technology, Fremont, CA, USA) for antigen quantification. Samples were measured by spectrophotometry at 450 nm, in triplicate and their values were expressed as means. A standard curve was plotted with values ranging from 12.5 to 500 pg/mL.

The detection range for KIM-1 was 12.5–500 pg/mL, and the intra-assay coefficient of variation was 3.2% expressed as a ratio to the uCre concentration. Urinary KIM-1 levels were normalized by dividing their values by the uCre values of the same sample and expressed as pg/mg of uCre (KIM- 1/uCre). All measurements were performed in triplicate, and the mean value was used for statistical analysis to ensure analytical reliability. 2.4. Statistical Analysis The software R (4.2.1) was used to perform all statistical tests. The means of serum creatinine, BUN, urinary density, urinary protein creatinine ratio, and KIM-1 of the CG and UOGs were compared using Student’s t-test. Generalized linear models (GLM) were used to test the effects of clinical status, age, weight, and castration on biochemical parameters. Firstly, the possibility of multicollinearity was excluded by calculating the Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) with the “car” package (3.1-5). All factors presented VIF < 2, therefore, none were considered problematic in the model.
为了评估固定分类因素（临床状况和阉割）的方差是否相等，使用了Levene检验[17]。当残差诊断显示残差不服从正态分布时，响应变量在模型重新拟合前使用MASS包（7.3-65）中的Boxcox函数进行转换[18]。针对如下变量（响应因素）构建了五个模型：血清肌酐、血尿素氮（BUN）、尿液密度、尿蛋白肌酐比（UPC）率和KIM-1。在所有变量中，临床状态（CG × UOG）、年龄、体重、阉割及其交互作用被纳入为固定因素。根据Burnham和Anderson[19]的方法，这些因素被逐步移除，以选择具有最低Akaike信息准则及小样本量校正（AICc）的模型。所有数值均以平均值±标准差（SD）表示，p值<0.05的差异被认为具有显著性。用于所有统计分析的个体数据提供在补充材料中（表S1）。
To evaluate the equality of variances of fixed categorical factors (clinical condition and castration), Levene’s test was used [17]. When residual diagnostics indicated non-normality of residuals, the response variable was transformed using the Boxcox function from the MASS package (7.3-65) prior to model refitting [18]. Five models were built for the following variables (response factors): serum creatinine, BUN, urinary density, urinary protein creatinine ratio, and KIM-1. In all variables, clinical status (CG × UOG), age, weight, castration, and interactions were included as fixed factors. According to Burnham and Anderson [19], these factors were removed sequentially to select the model with the lowest Akaike information criterion and correction for small sample sizes (AICs). All values were expressed as mean ± standard deviation (SD) and differences with p-value < 0.05 were considered significant. The individual data used for all statistical analyses are provided in the Supplementary Materials (Table S1).

Discussion The present findings indicate that cats with urethral obstruction exhibited markedly increased urinary KIM-1 concentrations despite normal conventional renal biomarkers, suggesting an association between urinary KIM-1 excretion and tubular injury. These findings are consistent 🔍 with previous studies in humans and experimental animal models of ischemia–reperfusion injury, in which KIM-1 expression increases during the early stages of renal injury [20,21].
本研究的一个重要方面是尿样在尿道导管置入、膀胱减压、液体治疗及其他治疗干预之前采集。因此，尿KIM-1浓度反映了猫在就诊时的临床状态，最大程度地减少了治疗相关因素对生物标志物表达的潜在影响，并强化了解释，即KIM-1水平升高主要与阻塞性疾病本身相关。肌酐和血尿素氮（BUN）是广泛用于肾功能评估的指标；然而，它们主要反映肾小球滤过功能，而非结构性肾损伤。尽管BUN是氮质废物积累的敏感标志，但其浓度可能受多种非肾因素影响。同样，在肾小球滤过减少的情况下，肌酐的肾小管分泌可能增加，从而可能通过高估肾功能掩盖早期肾损伤。因此，具有评估肾小管健康和损伤的临床可用生物标志物的需求是合理的[4]。
An important aspect of the present study is that urine samples were collected before urethral catheterization, bladder decompression, fluid therapy, and other therapeutic interventions. Therefore, urinary KIM-1 concentrations reflected the clinical status of the cats at presentation, minimizing the potential influence of treatment-related factors on biomarker expression and strengthening the interpretation that increased KIM-1 levels were primarily associated with the obstructive condition itself. Creatinine and blood urea nitrogen (BUN) are widely used markers for renal function evaluation; however, they primarily reflect glomerular filtration rather than structural renal injury. Although BUN is a sensitive marker of nitrogenous waste accumulation, its concentration may be influenced by several non-renal factors. Likewise, in cases of reduced glomerular filtration, tubular secretion of creatinine may increase, potentially masking early renal injury through an overestimation of renal function. Therefore, the need for clinically available biomarkers capable of assessing tubular health and injury is justified [4].
在本研究中，对于非氮质血症且存在尿路阻塞（UOG）的猫来说，血清肌酐和血尿素氮（BUN）浓度在诊断肾功能障碍方面均不可靠，这两项指标均保持在该物种的参考值范围内（表1）。我们还发现，即使在控制了年龄、体重和绝育情况后，肌酐也不受临床状态的影响。相反，我们发现BUN受到年龄的影响，年轻动物的值较高。这可能与年轻动物饮食中蛋白质含量较高有关[22]。尽管这些生物标志物目前用于评估肾功能，但血清肌酐和BUN被认为是肾损伤的晚期标志物[9]。
In the present study, neither serum creatinine nor BUN concentrations were reliable in diagnosing renal dysfunction in non-azotemic cats with urinary obstruction (UOG), with both values remaining within the reference values for the species (Table 1). We also found that creatinine was not affected by clinical status, even when controlling for age, weight, and castration. In contrast, we found that BUN was affected by age, with higher values in younger animals. This could be related to the higher protein content used in the diets of young animals [22]. Although these biomarkers are currently used to assess kidney function, sCre and BUN are considered late markers of kidney injury [9].
在UOG猫中观察到的较低尿比重（USG）可能反映了与小管功能障碍相关的尿液浓缩能力受损 [15]。此外，持续性低比重尿提示肾单位小管段的尿液浓缩能力丧失 [15,23]。在猫中，尿比重（USG） ≤ 1.025 已被认为是肾功能下降的指标 [12]，我们的结果支持这一观点。本研究使用的纳入标准旨在选择那些尿道阻塞但血清肌酐浓度仍未出现氮质血症，同时表现出尿液浓缩能力下降的猫。重要的是，猫的评估不仅仅基于临床病史和超声检查。所有动物都接受了完整的临床和实验室筛查，包括体格检查、全血计数、肾脏和肝脏相关的血清生化分析、尿液分析、定量尿培养以及腹部超声检查。
The lower USG observed in UOG cats may reflect impaired urinary concentrating ability associated with tubular dysfunction [15]. Furthermore, persistent hyposthenuria is suggestive of a loss of urinary concentrating capacity in the tubular segments of the nephrons [15,23]. In cats, USG ≤ 1.025 has been considered an indicator of decreased kidney function [12], and our results support The inclusion criteria used in the present study were designed to select cats with urethral obstruction that remained non-azotemic according to serum creatinine concentrations, while presenting reduced urine concentrating ability. Importantly, cats were not evaluated solely on the basis of clinical history and ultrasonography. All animals underwent complete clinical and laboratory screening, including physical examination, complete blood count, serum biochemical analysis of renal and hepatic parameters, urinalysis, quantitative urine culture, and abdominal ultrasonography.
除了与梗阻性泌尿疾病相关的发现外，其余的临床、血液学、生化、尿液和影像学参数均未提示全身性疾病或慢性肾疾病。猫尿道梗阻导致尿比重（USG）降低可以通过与膀胱内和肾内压力增加、肾灌注改变、髓质冲刷以及肾小管浓缩机制受损相关的短暂性肾小管功能障碍来进行生理学解释[5,12,15,23,24]。先前关于尿道梗阻猫的临床数据表明，梗阻猫的尿比重（USG）降低，同时也显示并非所有受影响的猫在诊断时都会出现明显的氮质血症[12]。因此，血清肌酐浓度在参考范围内且尿比重（USG）降低的组合，并不一定表明存在既往的慢性肾疾病。
Apart from findings related to the obstructive urinary condition, the remaining clinical, hematological, biochemical, urinary, and imaging parameters did not suggest systemic disease or chronic kidney disease. Reduced urine specific gravity in cats with urethral obstruction may be physiologically explained by transient tubular dysfunction associated with increased intravesical and intrarenal pressure, altered renal perfusion, medullary washout, and impairment of tubular concentrating mechanisms [5,12,15,23,24]. Previous clinical data in cats with urethral obstruction have shown reduced urine specific gravity in obstructed cats and have also indicated that not all affected cats present overt azotemia at the time of diagnosis [12]. Therefore, the combination of serum creatinine concentrations within the reference interval and reduced USG does not necessarily indicate pre- existing chronic kidney disease.
然而，由于无法获得SDMA、胱抑素-C、组织病理学评估和纵向肾脏随访资料，因此不能完全排除早期或亚临床慢性肾病（CKD）的可能性。因此，在解释患有尿道疾病猫咪的尿液KIM-1浓度时，仍可能存在一定程度的分类偏差，应予以考虑。Xavier Júnior等人[12]在一项评估KIM-1剂量和尿液GGT作为猫急性肾损伤生物标志物的临床研究中观察到，尿液中KIM-1的浓度不等同于临床常规使用的传统标志物浓度；可能该生物标志物受肾小管细胞损伤程度的影响和表达。
However, because SDMA, cystatin-C, histopathological evaluation, and longitudinal renal follow-up were not available, early or subclinical chronic kidney disease cannot be completely excluded. Consequently, a degree of misclassification bias remains possible and should be considered when interpreting urinary KIM-1 concentrations in cats with urethral Xavier Júnior et al. [12], in a clinical study evaluating dosages of KIM-1 and urinary GGT as biomarkers of acute kidney injury in cats, observed that the concentration of KIM-1 in urine is not equivalent to the concentration of conventional markers used in clinical routine; probably, this biomarker is influenced and expressed by the degree of tubular cell injury.
以往在人类和实验模型中的研究一致报道，肾小管损伤后KIM-1的表达增加。一些研究还表明，KIM-1可以帮助区分缺血性急性肾损伤（AKI）与肾前氮质血症和慢性肾病（CKD），并且在区分AKI的各种亚型中也具有一定作用[25,26,27]。在本研究中，我们观察到非氮质血症猫在发生肾后性AKI时，KIM-1水平有所升高。这种升高可能是由于尿道梗阻、闭塞以及尿液滞留，导致膀胱、输尿管和肾脏压力增加所致。由肾内压力维持的这种升高会导致肾实质丧失和白细胞涌入肾实质，释放细胞因子。因此，会出现电解质失衡、由于损伤导致的实质纤维化，以及由尿道梗阻引起的脱水继发的肾缺血[5]。
Previous investigations in human and experimental models have consistently reported increased KIM-1 expression following tubular injury. Some studies have also shown that KIM-1 can help differentiate ischemic AKI from prerenal azotemia and CKD and be useful in differentiating the various subtypes of AKI [25,26,27]. In the present study, we observed an increase in KIM-1 values in cats with post-renal AKI in non- azotemic patients. This increase can be explained due to the urethral obstruction, obliteration, and retention of urinary flow, which cause an increase in intravesical, ureteral, and renal pressure. This increase sustained by intrarenal pressure results in the loss of renal parenchyma and the influx of leukocytes into the renal parenchyma, releasing cytokines. As a result, there is a development of electrolyte imbalances, fibrosis in parenchyma due to the lesion, and renal ischemia secondary to dehydration caused by urethral obstruction [5].
这种缺血将因ATP的缺乏导致肾损伤，Na /K ATP酶泵的功能会受到改变，导致钙进入肾小管细胞，诱导细胞凋亡和急性肾小管坏死 [5,9,26]。目前用于诊断和分级急性肾损伤（AKI）的标准主要依赖血肌酐（sCre）变化 [2]。然而，血肌酐水平的变化时间及伴随的肾小球滤过率（GFR）变化无法准确估计损伤发生时间或功能障碍的严重程度 [1,8,24]。因此，由于多种因素，包括水分超载、高代谢状态、败血症和缺血损伤，AKI的早期阶段可能不会伴随血肌酐水平显著升高，导致诊断和干预延迟。因此，近年来人们对AKI的新生物标志物，特别是在其初期阶段的研究不断加深 [28]。
This ischemia will lead to renal injury due to the absence of ATP, and the Na+/K+ ATPase pump will have its functioning altered, causing the entrance of calcium in tubular cells, inducing apoptosis and acute tubular necrosis [5,9,26]. Current criteria for diagnosis and classification of AKI heavily depend on sCre changes [2]. However, the timing of changes in sCre level and concomitant changes in glomerular filtration rate does not allow for an accurate estimate of time to injury or severity of dysfunction [1,8,24]. Thus, the early phase of AKI may not be associated with a significant increase in sCre levels due to several factors, including water overload, high catabolism, sepsis, and ischemic insults, resulting in delayed diagnosis and intervention. Therefore, the search for new biomarkers of AKI, especially in its initial phase, has intensified in recent years [28].
尿道梗阻可以触发一系列病理生理事件，最终可能导致肾小管损伤。尿液潴留会增加膀胱内压力，这种压力可能传递至输尿管和肾盂，导致肾内压力升高和肾灌注减少[4,24]。这一过程可能导致肾缺血、肾小管上皮细胞损伤、氧化应激、炎症反应，以及在更严重或持续时间更长的情况下，引发凋亡和肾小管坏死[4,24]。这些机制为尿道梗阻的猫尿液中肾小管损伤生物标志物（如KIM-1）排泄增加提供了生物学依据[20,21,24]。
Urethral obstruction can trigger a sequence of pathophysiological events that may culminate in renal tubular injury. Urine retention increases intravesical pressure, which may be transmitted to the ureters and renal pelvis, resulting in increased intrarenal pressure and reduced renal perfusion [4,24]. This process can lead to renal ischemia, tubular epithelial cell injury, oxidative stress, inflammatory responses, and, in more severe or prolonged cases, apoptosis and tubular necrosis [4,24]. These mechanisms provide a biological rationale for the increased urinary excretion of tubular injury biomarkers, such as KIM-1, in cats with urethral obstruction [20,21,24].
本研究中观察到的尿液中KIM-1浓度升高可能反映了继发于缺血和与尿道梗阻相关的肾内压升高的肾小管上皮损伤。缺血在梗阻性肾损伤的病理生理中起核心作用，尽管再灌注对于恢复组织供氧和防止不可逆细胞损伤至关重要，但它也可能导致额外的氧化和炎症损伤[4,24]。将氧合血液重新引入先前缺血的组织，可以通过活性氧种和炎症介质的产生加剧细胞损伤[4,24]。
The increased urinary KIM-1 concentrations observed in the present study may reflect tubular epithelial injury secondary to ischemia and increased intrarenal pressure associated with urethral obstruction. Ischemia plays a central role in the pathophysiology of obstructive renal injury, and although reperfusion is essential to restore tissue oxygenation and prevent irreversible cellular damage, it may also contribute to additional oxidative and inflammatory injury [4,24]. The reintroduction of oxygenated blood into previously ischemic tissues can intensify cellular damage through the generation of reactive oxygen species and inflammatory mediators [4,24].
在解读尿液KIM-1浓度时，还应考虑潜在的混杂因素。猫咪在尿道梗阻时常出现的脱水和血流动力学改变可能导致肾灌注减少和肾小管应激。此外，UOG中的所有猫均表现为伴有炎性尿道栓的猫特发性膀胱炎，无法完全排除下尿路炎症对尿液生物标志物排泄的潜在影响。同样，尽管纳入了无临床或超声表现的年轻非氮质血症猫，不能完全排除亚临床肾病的存在。因此，尿液KIM-1浓度升高应结合临床背景进行解读，而不应被视为肾小管损伤的孤立指标。
Potential confounding factors should also be considered when interpreting urinary KIM-1 concentrations. Dehydration and hemodynamic alterations commonly observed in cats with urethral obstruction may contribute to reduced renal perfusion and tubular stress. In addition, all cats in the UOG presented feline idiopathic cystitis associated with inflammatory urethral plugs, and the potential contribution of lower urinary tract inflammation to urinary biomarker excretion cannot be completely excluded. Likewise, the presence of subclinical kidney disease cannot be definitively ruled out, despite the inclusion of young non-azotemic cats with no clinical or ultrasonographic evidence of chronic kidney disease. Therefore, increased urinary KIM-1 concentrations should be interpreted within the clinical context and not as an isolated indicator of renal tubular injury.
另一个需要考虑的因素是，在猫仍然存在尿道阻塞时测量了尿液中的KIM-1浓度。尽管理论上尿潴留可能会影响尿液生物标志物的浓度，但KIM-1主要被认为是近端肾小管上皮损伤的标志物[20,21]。我们团队之前的研究表明，在解除尿道阻塞后，尿液中KIM-1浓度持续升高，这表明KIM-1升高并不仅仅归因于尿液潴留[12]。尽管如此，尿液潴留自身的影响仍不能完全排除，需要未来进行在阻塞解除前后评估尿液KIM-1的纵向研究。
Another consideration is that urinary KIM-1 concentrations were measured while cats were still obstructed. Although urinary retention may theoretically influence urinary biomarker concentrations, KIM-1 is primarily recognized as a marker of proximal tubular epithelial injury [20,21]. Previous studies from our group demonstrated persistently increased urinary KIM-1 concentrations after relief of urethral obstruction, suggesting that elevated KIM-1 concentrations are not solely attributable to urine retention [12]. Nevertheless, the contribution of urinary retention itself cannot be completely excluded, and future longitudinal studies evaluating urinary KIM-1 before and after relief of obstruction are warranted.
本研究存在一些局限性，包括样本量相对较小以及缺乏纵向随访来评估肾损伤随时间的进展。根据IRIS指南，急性肾损伤（AKI）的诊断和分期主要基于血清肌酐浓度的变化。然而，非氮质血症的早期肾小管损伤患者可能在存在肾损伤的情况下仍无法满足经典分期标准。因此，本研究纳入的猫被解释为疑似与尿道阻塞相关的早期AKI，并在明显氮质血症出现前已显示早期肾小管损伤的证据。本研究的另一个局限性是，并非所有猫的尿道阻塞确切时间都能客观确定，主要基于主人报告的临床病史。
This study has some limitations, including the relatively small sample size and the lack of longitudinal follow-up to assess the progression of kidney injury over time. According to IRIS guidelines, AKI diagnosis and staging are largely based on changes in serum creatinine concentration. However, non-azotemic patients with early tubular injury may not fulfill classical staging criteria despite the presence of renal damage. Therefore, cats included in the present study were interpreted as having suspected early AKI associated with urethral obstruction and evidence of early tubular injury before the onset of overt azotemia. Another limitation of the present study is that the exact duration of urethral obstruction could not be objectively determined in all cats and was based primarily on owner-reported clinical history.
因此，梗阻持续时间和严重程度的差异可能导致受影响猫咪的肾小管损伤程度和尿液 KIM-1 表达的差异。其他局限性包括缺乏补充性的肾脏生物标志物，如 SDMA 和胱抑素-C，以及缺乏肾小管损伤的组织病理学确认。因此，尿液 KIM-1 浓度升高应谨慎解读，作为与尿道梗阻相关的早期肾小管损伤的证据，而非急性肾损伤的确凿确认。还需要进一步的前瞻性研究，包括额外的肾脏生物标志物、组织病理学评估和纵向随访，以更好地阐明 KIM-1 在早期猫急性肾损伤中的作用。
Therefore, variation in the duration and severity of obstruction may have contributed to differences in the degree of tubular injury and urinary KIM-1 expression among affected cats. Additional limitations include the absence of complementary renal biomarkers, such as SDMA and cystatin-C, as well as the lack of histopathological confirmation of tubular injury. Therefore, increased urinary KIM-1 concentrations should be interpreted cautiously as evidence of early renal tubular injury associated with urethral obstruction rather than as definitive confirmation of AKI. Further prospective studies including additional renal biomarkers, histopathological evaluation, and longitudinal follow-up are warranted to better characterize the role of KIM-1 in early feline AKI.
5. 结论 本研究所得结果表明，尿液KIM-1可能提供关于非氮质血症尿道梗阻猫的肾小管损伤的临床相关信息。尽管血清肌酐和血尿素氮（BUN）浓度正常，仍观察到尿液KIM-1浓度升高，这支持其在评估与尿道梗阻相关的肾小管损伤中的潜在应用。然而，鉴于本研究属于初步研究且缺乏纵向结局评估，还需要进一步的前瞻性研究，结合额外的肾脏生物标志物和长期随访，以更好地界定KIM-1在猫急性肾损伤中的临床作用。
5. Conclusions The results obtained in this study indicate that urinary KIM-1 may provide clinically relevant information regarding renal tubular injury in non-azotemic cats with urethral obstruction. Increased urinary KIM-1 concentrations were observed despite normal serum creatinine and BUN concentrations, supporting its potential application in the assessment of renal tubular injury associated with urethral obstruction. However, given the pilot nature of this study and the absence of longitudinal outcome assessment, further prospective studies incorporating additional renal biomarkers and long-term follow-up are warranted to better define the clinical role of KIM-1 in feline acute kidney injury.
