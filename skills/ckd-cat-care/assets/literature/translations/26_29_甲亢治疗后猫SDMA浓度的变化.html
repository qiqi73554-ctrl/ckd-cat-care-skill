This study evaluated whether the concentrations of a biomarker of kidney function called symmetric dimethylarginine (SDMA) in the blood change in hyperthyroid cats after treatment. SDMA concentrations did change after treatment but not as quickly as we would expect, which could suggest that SDMA concentrations are affected by factors other than just kidney function. Hyperthyroidism is the most common endocrinopathy in geriatric cats, and although there is significant geographical variance, studies assessing populations of older cats typically indicate a prevalence in the order of 10%.1 –5 Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is also prevalent in older cats and approximately 10% of untreated hyperthyroid cats have azotaemic CKD, while a further 15% of medically treated hyperthyroid cats become azotaemic after treatment of hyperthyroidism (referred to as ‘masked’ CKD).

It is established that the presence of pretreatment azotaemia negatively impacts the survival of hyperthyroid cats; 6 however, the development of azotaemia after treatment in previously non-azotaemic cats is not associated with reduced survival times. 13 Nevertheless, identifying cats at a higher risk of developing azotaemia after treatment could inform management decisions and enable earlier dietary modification, which can improve long-term survival. 14 Symmetric dimethylarginine (SDMA) is a by-product of L-arginine methylation that is released into the circulation by all nucleated cells after proteolysis. Therefore, investigation of the change in serum SDMA concentrations after treatment of hyperthyroidism with antithyroid medications would be of interest to document whether these differ from those of cats treated with radioiodine. The aim of this study was to investigate the change in serum creatinine and SDMA concentrations in a population of client-owned hyperthyroid cats treated with methimazole or carbimazole. We hypothesised that serum SDMA concentrations would increase in parallel with serum creatinine after the restoration of euthyroidism due to the reduction in GFR. We also hypothesised that baseline serum SDMA concentrations would be higher in cats with concurrent or ‘masked’ CKD.

材料与方法

Materials and methods

病例选择

Case selection
伦理审批已由剑桥大学兽医学系的伦理与福利委员会批准。检索了大学教学医院临床病理数据库中血清总甲状腺素（TT4）浓度高于由健康年长猫群体（7–45 nmol/l）得出的实验室参考区间（RI）的猫血清样本，在2014年至2024年的十年期间。根据信号信息、年龄、临床症状、用药情况、实验室检测数据、共病情况及治疗类型，对这些病例进行纳入筛选。实验室检测数据提取自治疗前后的记录，包括血清TT4、肌酐、尿素浓度以及在可用情况下的尿比重（USG）。
Ethical approval was granted by the Ethics and Welfare Committee of the Department of Veterinary Medicine, University of Cambridge. The university teaching hospital clinical pathology database was searched for feline serum samples with a total thyroxine (TT4) concentration above the laboratory reference interval (RI) derived in a population of healthy older cats (7–45 nmol/l) 25 in the 10-year period between 2014 and 2024. These cases were screened for inclusion based on signalment, age, clinical signs, medications, laboratory testing data, comorbidities and type of treatment. Laboratory testing data were extracted from records from before and after treatment, including serum concentrations of TT4, creatinine, urea and, where available, urine specific gravity (USG).
为了纳入本研究，猫必须具有与甲状腺功能亢进一致的临床症状和/或体格检查发现，血清总T4（TT4）高于45 nmol/l（实验室参考范围上限），并随后开始口服卡比马唑或甲硫咪唑的药物治疗。在开始治疗后1个月（T1）和/或2–9个月（T2）时必须显示复甲状腺功能（定义为血清TT4 7–40 nmol/l）。如果猫被认为全身健康状况不佳、在这些时间点随访血液检查显示甲状腺功能亢进控制不足，或残余储存血清不足以进行SDMA分析，则将其排除在外。氮质血症定义为血清肌酐浓度高于实验室参考值56–153 µmol/l。
For inclusion in the study, cats were required to have clinical signs and/or physical examination findings that were consistent with hyperthyroidism, a TT4 above 45 nmol/l (upper limit of laboratory RI) and to have subsequently started medical treatment with oral carbimazole or methimazole. Euthyroidism (defined as serum TT4 7–40 nmol/l) must have been demonstrated at 1 month (T1) and/or at 2–9 months (T2) after initiating treatment. Cats were excluded if they were deemed to be systemically unwell, if follow-up blood work at these time points demonstrated inadequate control of hyperthyroidism or if there was insufficient residual stored serum for SDMA analysis. Azotaemia was defined as a serum creatinine concentration above the laboratory RI of 56–153 µmol/l).

临床病理学检测

Clinicopathological assays
血液样本在采集后2小时内进行离心，剩余血清在初次生化分析后24小时内冷冻于−80°C。在大多数情况下，血清总甲状腺素（TT4）和肌酐的测定是同时进行的，使用先前验证的人TT4酶免疫分析法25和商业化的肌酐分光光度分析法（OSR6178；Beckman Coulter）。血清对称二甲基精氨酸（SDMA）浓度在采集后最多10年的剩余冷冻血清样本上，用竞争性均相免疫分析法（IDEXX实验室）测定。26
Blood samples were centrifuged within 2 h of collection and residual serum frozen at −80°C within 24 h of initial biochemical analysis. In most instances, serum TT4 and creatinine were measured contemporaneously using a previously validated human TT4 enzyme immunoassay 25 and a commercially available spectrophotometric assay for creatinine (OSR6178; Beckman Coulter). Serum SDMA concentrations were measured on residual frozen serum samples up to 10 years after collection using a competitive homogenous immunoassay (IDEXX Laboratories). 26

甲状腺功能亢进猫的分类

Classification of hyperthyroid cats
甲亢猫根据治疗前后的血清肌酐浓度被分为三组。非氮质血症甲亢猫在治疗前和治疗后T2（2–9个月）时间点的血清肌酐均低于153 µmol/l（实验室参考上限）。隐性氮质血症甲亢猫在基线血清肌酐低于153 µmol/l，但在T1和/或T2的血清肌酐高于153 µmol/l。氮质血症甲亢猫在基线及T1和/或T2的血清肌酐均高于153 µmol/l。
Hyperthyroid cats were divided into three groups based on serum creatinine concentration before and after treatment. Non-azotaemic hyperthyroid cats had a serum creatinine below 153 µmol/l (upper limit of laboratory RI) both before treatment and at the T2 (2–9 months) post-treatment time point. Masked azotaemic hyperthyroid cats had a serum creatinine below 153 µmol/l at baseline but had a serum creatinine above 153 µmol/l at T1 and/or T2. Azotaemic hyperthyroid cats had a serum creatinine above 153 µmol/l at baseline and at T1 and/or T2.

Statistical analysis
统计分析使用商业可得软件（SPSS Statistics for Windows v27.0；IBM）进行。结果以中位数（最小值–最大值）表示，除非另有说明。数据通过直方图视觉评估和Shapiro–Wilk检验进行正态性评估；数据不符合正态分布，因此使用非参数检验。基线连续变量（血清肌酐、尿素、TT4 和 SDMA 浓度）在各组之间使用 Mann–Whitney U 检验或 Kruskal–Wallis 检验进行比较。各组在不同时间点的比较采用 Wilcoxon 符号秩检验分析。SDMA、TT4 和肌酐之间的相关性使用 Spearman 秩相关系数评估。
Statistical analysis was performed using commercially available software (SPSS Statistics for Windows v27.0; IBM). Results are reported as median (minimum–maximum) unless otherwise specified. The data were assessed for normality by visual histogram assessment and Shapiro–Wilk testing; the data were not normally distributed and non-parametric tests were used. Baseline continuous variables (serum creatinine, urea, TT4 and SDMA concentrations) were compared between groups using the Mann–Whitney U-test or Kruskal–Wallis test. Comparisons within groups at different time points were analysed using the Wilcoxon signed rank test. Correlations between SDMA, TT4 and creatinine were assessed using Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient.

此外，在伴有慢性肾病（CKD）（显性和/或掩盖型）的甲状腺功能亢进猫中，血清肌酐和SDMA浓度均未明显高于那些在治疗后仍保持非氮质血症的甲状腺功能亢进猫。有人假设，在恢复甲状腺功能正常后，血清肌酐和SDMA浓度会增加，因为两者都与健康猫的肾小球滤过率（GFR）相关，而在甲状腺功能亢进治疗后GFR会下降。然而，出乎意料的是，血清SDMA浓度在约一个月的治疗后并未增加，而血清肌酐浓度在此期间确实有所增加。我们的发现得到了某些放射性碘治疗甲状腺功能亢进猫的研究结果的支持，这些研究报告称，尽管GFR显著下降，血清肌酐而非SDMA浓度在放射性碘治疗后的第一个月增加，但这些结果与其他研究的发现存在冲突。
Furthermore, neither serum creatinine nor SDMA concentrations were significantly higher in hyperthyroid cats that had concurrent CKD (overt and/or masked) compared with hyperthyroid cats that remained non-azotaemic after It was hypothesised that both serum creatinine and SDMA concentrations would increase after restoration of euthyroidism since both correlate with GFR in healthy cats,15,16 and GFR would decrease after hyperthyroidism treatment.8,9,11 Unexpectedly, serum SDMA concentrations did not increase after approximately 1 month of treatment, whereas serum creatinine concentrations did increase over this time period. Our findings are supported by the results from some studies of radioiodine-treated hyperthyroid cats which reported that serum creatinine, but not SDMA concentrations, increased in the first month after radioiodine treatment despite a significant decrease in GFR,21,22 but conflict with the findings of other studies.
24 由于在本研究中，SDMA 浓度在治疗 2–9 个月后确实有所增加，因此可以提出 SDMA 对肾小球滤过率（GFR）变化的反应可能比血清肌酐更为滞后。然而，先前的研究已经记录到，GFR 和 SDMA 在放射性碘治疗 1 个月后会稳定，而与肌肉量增加相关的血清肌酐变化则持续数月。9,12,24 在我们的研究人群中，血清总甲状腺素（TT4）浓度与血清肌酐浓度呈负相关，但与血清 SDMA 浓度无关。在人类中，血清 TT4 浓度与 GFR 及血清肌酐浓度呈负相关，27 并且在猫接受甲状腺功能亢进治疗后也已记录到 GFR 下降。9,11,28 因此，我们预期在甲状腺功能亢进的猫中，血清 TT4 浓度与 SDMA 浓度之间存在负相关关系。
24 As SDMA concentrations did increase after 2–9 months of treatment in the present study, it could be proposed that SDMA may show a more delayed response to changes in GFR than serum creatinine. However, changes in GFR and SDMA have previously been documented to stabilise 1 month after radioiodine treatment, while changes in serum creatinine associated with increased muscle mass continue for several months.9,12,24 Serum TT4 concentrations were inversely correlated with serum creatinine concentrations but not serum SDMA concentrations in our population. Serum TT4 concentrations are inversely correlated with GFR and serum creatinine concentrations in humans, 27 and GFR is also documented to decrease after treatment of hyperthyroidism in cats.9,11,28 Therefore, we would expect an inverse correlation between serum TT4 and SDMA concentrations in hyperthyroid cats.
我们的研究结果结合其他人的研究结果，支持这样的前提：血清SDMA浓度在甲状腺功能亢进中受肾小球滤过率（GFR）以外的因素影响。20–22 在人类中，甲状腺功能亢进患者的循环SDMA浓度升高，并有人提出甲状腺毒症可能上调SDMA的生成。29,30 有报道显示放射性碘治疗后血清SDMA降低，21 且经历双侧甲状腺切除术后仍维持甲状腺功能亢进的猫，其血清SDMA与血清肌酐的关系更为陡峭。23 因此，可以提出在甲状腺功能亢进中SDMA生成增加可能会降低血清SDMA浓度与甲状腺功能亢进猫的GFR之间的相关性。
Our findings, in conjunction with those of others, support the premise that serum SDMA concentrations are influenced by factors other than GFR in hyperthyroidism.20 –22 In humans, circulating SDMA concentrations are increased in hyperthyroid patients, and it has been proposed that thyrotoxicosis may upregulate SDMA production.29,30 Decreases in serum SDMA have been reported after radioiodine treatment, 21 and cats that have remained hyperthyroid after bilateral thyroidectomy have a steeper relationship between serum SDMA and serum creatinine. 23 Therefore, it could be proposed that increased SDMA production in hyperthyroidism may reduce the correlation between serum SDMA concentrations and GFR in hyperthyroid cats.
在本研究中，最初非氮质血症的甲状腺功能亢进猫在治疗后发展为氮质血症（隐性氮质血症）或未经治疗的甲状腺功能亢进猫在基线就存在氮质血症或隐性氮质血症的合并组（甲状腺功能亢进-CKD组）的血清SDMA浓度，并没有显著高于治疗后仍保持非氮质血症的未治疗甲状腺功能亢进猫。这些数据与先前研究的结果形成对比，在那些研究中，隐性氮质血症猫的血清SDMA浓度高于非氮质血症猫，18,19 尽管血清SDMA浓度在预测接受放射性碘治疗猫的治疗后氮质血症方面敏感性较差。我们的结果可能不同，是由于本研究样本量较小，或者可能反映在甲状腺功能亢进中存在影响血清SDMA浓度的其他因素，而不仅仅是肾小球滤过率（GFR）。
In the present study, serum SDMA concentrations in initially non-azotaemic hyperthyroid cats that developed azotaemia after treatment (masked azotaemic) or the combined group of untreated hyperthyroid cats that were azotaemic at baseline or masked azotaemic (hyperthyroid-CKD group) were not significantly greater than those in untreated hyperthyroid cats that remained non- azotaemic after treatment. These data are in contrast to those from previous studies in which serum SDMA concentrations were higher in masked azotaemic cats compared with non-azotaemic cats,18,19 although serum SDMA concentrations were poorly sensitive in predicting post-treatment azotaemia in these radioiodine-treated cats. Our results may differ because of the smaller sample size of the present study or could reflect the presence of factors other than GFR that affect serum SDMA concentrations in hyperthyroidism.
此外，一些被分类为非氮质血症的猫在治疗前后血清SDMA浓度升高，这可能反映了一些肌肉量低（因此血清肌酐浓度较低）的猫存在并发慢性肾病（CKD）。此外，一些在隐性氮质血症和氮质血症组的猫尽管血清肌酐浓度升高，但血清SDMA浓度仍在实验室参考范围内。这些发现与氮质血症急性肾损伤猫的报告相似，并可能反映猫血清SDMA浓度的高离散性（对于给定样本基于生物学和分析变异组合可能出现的结果范围）。然而，需要血清SDMA浓度与直接肾小球滤过率（GFR）测量之间的相关性来证实这一假设。
In addition, some cats that were classified as nonazotaemic had elevated serum SDMA concentrations both before and after treatment, which could reflect the presence of concurrent CKD in some cats with low muscle mass (and hence lower serum creatinine concentrations). Furthermore, some cats in the masked azotaemic and azotaemic groups had serum SDMA concentrations within the laboratory RI despite the presence of elevated serum creatinine concentrations. These findings mirror those reported in cats with azotaemic acute kidney injury, 31 and might reflect high dispersion (the range of possible results that are feasible for a given sample based on the combination of biological and analytical variation) of serum SDMA concentrations in cats. 32 However, correlation between serum SDMA concentrations and direct measurements of GFR would be required to confirm this hypothesis.
在本研究中，相对较高比例（60%）的最初非氮质血症的甲状腺功能亢进猫在治疗后变为氮质血症。大约15–51%的最初非氮质血症猫在接受放射性碘、手术或药物治疗后会变为氮质血症；然而，本研究中治疗后变为氮质血症的猫比例较高，可能反映了在治疗后要求进行全套生化检查（而不仅仅是TT4）的猫群体的偏差，或者可能反映猫被归类为氮质血症是基于血清肌酐浓度升高的记录，而没有进行尿比重（USG）检测以排除脱水等肾前性原因的氮质血症。本研究中只有26%的猫在治疗前测量了USG，复查时则更少。因此，一些被归类为隐匿性氮质血症的猫在复查时可能存在肾前性氮质血症——例如，由于脱水或其他可逆因素，而不是真正的肾性氮质血症。
In the present study, a relatively high proportion (60%) of initially non-azotaemic hyperthyroid cats became azotaemic after treatment. Approximately 15–51% of initially non-azotaemic cats will become azotaemic after radioiodine, surgical or medical treatment;6,9,19,33,34 however, the high proportion of cats becoming azotaemic after treatment in the present study may reflect bias in the population of cats for which a full biochemistry profile was requested, rather than TT4 alone, after treatment, or could reflect the fact that cats were classified as azotaemic based on documentation of elevated serum creatinine concentrations and without documentation of USG to exclude prerenal causes of azotaemia, such as dehydration. Only 26% of cats in this study had USG measured before treatment and very few at follow-up. Hence, it is possible that some cats classified as masked azotaemic may have had prerenal azotaemia at follow-up – for example, secondary to dehydration or other reversible factors rather than a true renal azotaemia.
此外，根据血清肌酐浓度对猫的肾功能状态进行分类；因此，有可能一些非氮质血症的猫可能同时患有慢性肾病（CKD）。要完全根据肾功能状态对猫进行分类，需要直接测定肾小球滤过率（GFR）；然而，由于该研究是回顾性的，这一操作无法实现。除了前述限制外，这项研究还有一些其他限制。将研究群体进行三向细分导致分组样本量较小，因此一些接近显著的结果（如基线的TT4和SDMA）可能反映了真实的生物学差异，但由于研究统计学上缺乏足够的功效而无法显示。例如，根据本研究的初步数据，每组需要五只猫，才能在5%的显著性水平下，以80%的统计功效检测到6 µg/dl的效应量；而每组需要21只猫，才能使用相同标准检测到3 µg/dl的效应量。
In addition, the classification of cats according to renal status was based on serum creatinine concentrations; therefore, it is possible that some non-azotaemic cats might have had concurrent CKD. Direct measurement of GFR would be required to fully classify cats based on renal status; however, this was not possible because of the retrospective nature of the study. This study had a number of limitations in addition to those already discussed. The three-way subdivision of the population resulted in small group sizes and, as a result, some near-significant results (such as TT4 and SDMA at baseline) may represent real biological differences that could not be demonstrated owing to the study being statistically underpowered. For example, based on the preliminary data in this study, five cats would be needed in each group for the study to be 80% powered to detect an effect size of 6 µg/dl at the 5% significance level, and 21 cats would be needed per group to detect an effect size of 3 µg/dl using the same criteria.
SDMA是在血清样本上测量的，这些样本在–80°C下冷冻长达10年，尽管已有研究显示SDMA在最长24个月内的稳定性，但长期储存对血清SDMA浓度的影响尚不清楚；因此，不能排除样本长期储存对测定的SDMA浓度的影响。进一步研究以确定冷冻保存条件下血清SDMA浓度的长期稳定性将有助于阐明样本长期储存对血清SDMA浓度的影响。此外，仅使用血清TT4浓度来定义甲状腺状态，尽管所有猫在所有时间点的血清TT4浓度均在参考范围内，但鉴于缺乏同时测量血清促甲状腺激素值，以及部分因医源性甲状腺功能减退的猫可能出现低至正常范围的血清TT4浓度的事实，36 医源性亚临床甲状腺功能减退可能存在，而这种情况可能降低肾小球滤过率（GFR），因此不能完全排除其存在。
SDMA was measured on serum samples that were frozen at –80°C for up to 10 years, and although the stability of SDMA been demonstrated for up to 24 months, 35 the effect of longer term storage on serum SDMA concentrations is unknown; therefore, an impact of prolonged sample storage on the measured SDMA concentrations cannot be excluded. Further studies to establish the long-term stability of serum SDMA concentrations when stored frozen would be useful to help clarify the effect of prolonged sample storage on serum SDMA concentrations. In addition, serum TT4 concentrations alone were used to define thyroid status, and although all cats had serum TT4 concentrations within the RI at all time points, given the lack of concurrent serum thyroid-stimulating hormone measurements and fact that some cats with iatrogenic hypothyroidism can have low–normal serum TT4 concentrations, 36 the presence of iatrogenic subclinical hypothyroidism, which might have reduced GFR, could not be fully excluded.
相反，在随访中缺乏支持性临床参数的情况下，尽管本研究使用的参考区间（RI）是基于非甲状腺功能亢进且其他方面健康的老年猫群体得出的，但不能排除血清总T4（TT4）接近参考区间上限的猫存在甲状腺功能亢进控制不良的可能性。作为一项回顾性研究，用药决定、起始剂量、给药间隔以及任何剂量调整均由主治兽医自行决定，并未标准化。对药物每日一次和每日两次给药进行额外比较，将产生样本量过小、统计效力不足的小亚组。最后，在没有额外检查（包括腹部超声）的情况下，不能排除其他可能导致血清对称二甲基精氨酸（SDMA）浓度升高的疾病存在，例如淋巴瘤。伴有慢性肾病（CKD）的甲状腺功能亢进猫的血清SDMA浓度并不高于甲状腺功能复常后仍保持非氮质血症的甲状腺功能亢进猫，且对初始非氮质血症的甲状腺功能亢进猫术后氮质血症的预测价值也不大。
Conversely, in the absence of supportive clinical parameters at follow-up, the presence of poorly controlled hyperthyroidism in cats with a TT4 towards the upper end of the RI cannot be excluded, although the RI utilised in this study was derived using a population of non-hyperthyroid and otherwise healthy senior cats. 25 As a retrospective study, the decisions on medication, starting doses, dosing intervals and any dose adjustments were made at the discretion of the attending veterinarian and were not standardised. Additional comparisons between onceand twice-daily administration of medication would have produced small subgroups with inadequate statistical power. Finally, in the absence of additional investigations, including abdominal ultrasonography, the presence of other diseases that could have increased serum SDMA concentrations, such as lymphoma, could not be excluded. 37 Serum SDMA concentrations were not higher in hyperthyroid cats with concurrent CKD than hyperthyroid cats that remained non-azotaemic after restoration of euthyroidism, and were not useful predictors of post-treatment azotaemia in initially non-azotaemic hyperthyroid cats.
此外，血清 SDMA 浓度在使用抗甲状腺药物治疗 1 个月后，并未与血清肌酐浓度同步升高，也未与总甲状腺素（TT4）浓度呈负相关，这可能表明在甲亢中，除肾小球滤过率（GFR）之外的因素也会影响血清 SDMA 浓度。为了进一步描述甲亢猫群体中 GFR 与血清 SDMA 浓度之间的关系，需要开展更大规模的研究，同时测定甲硫咪唑/卡比马唑治疗的甲亢猫的血清肌酐和 SDMA 浓度，并结合 GFR 的直接测定。
Furthermore, serum SDMA concentrations did not increase in parallel with serum creatinine concentrations after 1 month of treatment with antithyroid medications and were not inversely correlated with TT4 concentrations, which could suggest that factors other than GFR influence serum SDMA concentrations in hyperthyroidism. Larger scale studies measuring serum concentrations of creatinine and SDMA in conjunction with direct measurements of GFR in methimazole/carbimazole-treated hyperthyroid cats are required to further characterise the relationship between GFR and serum SDMA concentrations in this population of hyperthyroid cats.
