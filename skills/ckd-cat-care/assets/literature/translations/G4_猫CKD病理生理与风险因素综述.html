Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is one of the most frequently encountered disorders in cats, having increased in prevalence in recent decades. Although the underlying cause is rarely identified, the common final outcome of feline CKD is tubulointerstitial fibrosis. Knowledge of CKD pathophysiology is necessary for optimal individualised patient management, especially with regard to diagnosis and treatment of extrarenal complications.

Evidence base:
我们对慢性肾病（CKD）发病机制的了解大多是通过人类肾脏病学和实验动物研究推测得出的。因此，仍需要在猫身上进行进一步的研究。猫CKD临床症状的流行情况已有充分记录。猫中与CKD相关的多种并发疾病也有报道，尤其是在老龄猫群中，但大多数这些疾病与CKD之间的因果关系尚无或仅有有限的已发表证据。然而，过去15年进行的研究仍然使我们能够确定影响猫CKD进展的主要风险因素（蛋白尿、血浆磷和血浆肌酐）。
Most of our knowledge of the pathogenesis of CKD is extrapolated from human nephrology and experimental animal studies. There is, therefore, a need for further studies in cats. The prevalence of clinical signs in feline CKD is well documented. Several concurrent diseases associated with CKD have also been reported in cats, especially in the geriatric population, but there is no or only limited published evidence demonstrating a cause-and-effect relationship between most of these conditions and CKD. Studies performed over the past 15 years have nevertheless allowed identification of major risk factors (proteinuria, plasma phosphate and plasma creatinine) influencing the progression of feline CKD.

Clinical challenges:
临床症状出现在肾脏疾病的晚期，因此处于慢性肾病（CKD）高风险的人群应定期进行筛查。不能忽视慢性肾病（CKD）相关的并发症（全身性高血压、继发性肾性甲状旁腺功能亢进、低钾血症、贫血、代谢性酸中毒），因为它们可能影响疾病的进展。疾病进展本身是不可预测的，肾功能可能在很长一段时间内保持稳定。大多数早期慢性肾病（CKD）的猫在死前不会发展到终末期慢性肾病（CKD）。
Clinical signs occur in the late stages of renal disease, so populations at higher risk of CKD should be screened routinely. CKD-associated complications (systemic hypertension, secondary renal hyperparathyroidism, hypokalaemia, anaemia, metabolic acidosis) must not be overlooked as they may affect the progression of disease. Disease progression is itself unpredictable and renal function may remain stable for extended periods. Most cats with early CKD do not progress to end-stage CKD before they die.

猫慢性肾病（CKD）的发病机制

Pathogenesis of feline CKD
小管间质纤维化似乎是猫慢性肾病（CKD）最常见的最终结局（图7和图8）。原发性肾小球疾病很少见——在大多数情况下，肾小球疾病是继发于系统性疾病，特别是肿瘤性疾病（如白血病、淋巴瘤）、感染性和非感染性炎症性疾病（如胰腺炎、免疫介导疾病）。间质纤维化的存在是血浆肌酐浓度的最强组织形态计量学预测因素。小管损伤在疾病早期就发生，在血清尿素和肌酐浓度升高之前。虽然猫在部分肾切除后仍能维持尿液浓缩能力，但在患有CKD的猫中，随着血浆肌酐升高，尿比重（USG）下降，这提示在猫CKD进展过程中，肾小球功能与小管功能密切相关。
Tubulointerstitial fibrosis appears to be the most common final outcome of feline CKD (Figures 7 and 8).2,3,10,48 Primary glomerular diseases are rare – glomerular disease develops secondarily to systemic disease in most cases, specifically neoplastic (eg, leukaemia, lymphoma), infectious and non-infectious inflammatory disorders (eg, pancreatitis, immune-mediated diseases). 49 The presence of interstitial fibrosis is the strongest histomorphometric predictor of plasma creatinine concentration. 38 Tubular damage occurs early in the course of disease, before elevations in serum urea and creatinine concentrations. 50 Although cats are able to maintain urine concentrating ability after subtotal nephrectomy, 51 urine specific gravity decreases as plasma creatinine increases in cats with CKD,52,53 suggesting a close relationship between glomerular and tubular function during the progression of feline CKD.

Other sources of myofibroblasts are pericytes, fibrocytes and tubular epithelial cells that undergo epithelial–mesenchymal transformation. Myofibroblasts express α-smooth muscle actin and vimentin. 猫慢性肾病（CKD）的进展

Progression of feline CKD
慢性肾脏病（CKD）的进展被假设是由于自我维持的恶性循环所致。它被归因于肾损伤后存活（或残余）肾单位功能和结构的不适应性改变；在实验性CKD的猫中，这些主要表现为单个肾单位肾小球滤过率（GFR）、肾小球压力和肾小球体积的增加。由于肾脏具有很大的储备能力，至少需要75%的肾功能受损后才会检测到氮质血症。在猫中，GFR在实验性CKD诱导后可保持稳定长达1年。自发性CKD的猫表现出不可预测的疾病进程，在失代偿前可能经历长时间的肾功能稳定期。例如，在一项研究中，CKD第2期和第3期的猫分别有81%和37%在死亡前未进展至第4期。
The progression of CKD is postulated to result from a self-perpetuating vicious cycle. It is attributed to maladaptive functional and structural changes in surviving (or remnant) nephrons after renal injury; in cats with experimental CKD, these are principally increases in single nephron glomerular filtration rate (GFR), glomerular pressure and glomerular volume. 67 Because of the large reserve capacity of the kidneys, at least 75% of renal function needs to be impaired before azotaemia is detected. 68 In cats, GFR remains stable for up to 1 year after induction of experimental CKD.69–71 Cats with spontaneous CKD show an unpredictable time course of the disease, with extended periods of stable renal function before decompensation (Figures 9 and 10).27,40,72,73 In one study, for example, 81% and 37% of cats with stages 2 and 3 CKD, respectively, did not progress to stage 4 before they died.

猫慢性肾病（CKD）继发性变化的病理生理

Pathophysiology of secondary changes occurring in feline CKD
随着肾小球滤过率（GFR）下降，慢性肾脏病（CKD）的临床表现和并发症逐渐加重，这是由于体内物质（所谓的尿毒症毒素）清除减少，从而导致过度暴露。这些并发症本身也被认为是肾脏疾病进展的主要因素，并增加了CKD患者的发病率和死亡风险。
The clinical signs and complications of CKD progress with declining GFR due to decreased elimination of, and hence overexposure to, endogenous substances (so-called uraemic toxins). These complications are also considered in themselves to be major contributors to the progression of renal disease, and increase the risk for morbidity and mortality in patients with CKD.

系统性高血压

Systemic hypertension
系统性高血压通常与慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）相关。患有CKD的猫高血压的发病机制是多因素的，包括钠排泄受损、RAAS激活、交感神经系统刺激、动脉结构改变、内皮功能障碍和氧化应激。系统性高血压可能通过引起肾小球高血压和蛋白尿而对肾功能产生不利影响；它还增加了其他靶器官损伤（如眼睛、神经系统和心脏）的风险（图11）。图11. 一只患有系统性动脉高血压的猫的背侧视网膜脱离

肾继发性甲亢（RHPTH）是一种适应性且最终不适应的过程，源于肾功能下降、磷酸盐潴留及维生素D生物活性化失败（见上文框）。众所周知，磷酸潴留是慢性肾病（CKD）进展的主要原因。在患有CKD的猫中，观察到高血吞血症和血浆副甲状腺激素（PTH）浓度升高分别为13–100%和47–100%。3,53,77 RHPTH在3期和4期CKD中尤为常见，因为随着稀湿性程度的增加，RHPTH的严重程度也随之增加。3,53,77 血浆成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF-23）23（FGF-23）是一种磷酸激素，参与人类RHPTH的发病机制。在患有慢性肾脏病的猫中，血浆FGF-23与血浆肌酐浓度和PTH高度相关，而FGF-23与GFR呈反比关系。78,79 RHPTH引起的临床症状可能由磷酸钙沉淀到组织引起（见图12），但在慢性肾病（CKD）猫中相对罕见。已有转移性爪骨化80和骨质疏松10的报告。
Renal secondary hyperparathyroidism (RHPTH) is an adaptive and ultimately maladaptive process that develops in response to declining kidney function, phosphate retention and failure to bioactivate vitamin D (see box above). 76 It is well recognised that phosphate retention is a major contributor to the progression of CKD. Hyperphosphataemia and increased plasma parathyroid hormone (PTH) concentrations are observed in 13–100% and 47–100%, respectively, of cats with CKD.3,53,77 RHPTH is particularly common in stages 3 and 4 CKD, as the severity of RHPTH increases with the degree of azotaemia.3,53,77 Plasma fibroblast growth factor 23 (FGF-23) is a phosphaturic hormone involved in the pathogenesis of RHPTH in humans. In cats with CKD, plasma FGF-23 is strongly correlated with plasma creatinine concentration and PTH, whereas there is an inverse relationship between FGF-23 and GFR.78,79 Clinical signs induced by RHPTH may result from calcium phosphate precipitation into the tissues (Figure 12) but are relatively uncommon in cats with CKD. 肾病性贫血

Anaemia of renal disease
在患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫中，尤其是在疾病晚期，经常诊断出表现为红细胞正常色素、正常体积、低增殖性的贫血；大约有30–65%的CKD猫在肾病进展过程中会出现贫血。随着CKD严重程度的增加，红细胞压积（PCV）下降。CKD引起的贫血主要是由于近曲小管成纤维细胞型间质细胞（peritubular fibroblast type-1 interstitial cells）产生红细胞生成素不足。导致贫血的其他因素包括营养不良（如铁缺乏）、红细胞寿命缩短、骨髓纤维化、影响红细胞生成的代谢异常、尿毒症毒素、副甲状腺功能亢进和胃肠道出血。贫血会对CKD猫的生活质量产生负面影响。
A normochromic, normocytic, hypoproliferative anaemia is frequently diagnosed in cats with CKD, especially in end stages of the disease;85,86 about 30–65% of cats with CKD develop anaemia during progression of their renal disease. 86 The PCV decreases as the severity of CKD increases.52,53 CKD-induced anaemia results mainly from inadequate production of erythropoietin by the peritubular fibroblast type-1 interstitial cells. Other contributing factors to anaemia include malnutrition (eg, iron deficiency), shortened red blood cell life span, myelofibrosis, metabolic alterations affecting erythropoiesis, uraemic toxins, hyperparathyroidism and gastrointestinal blood loss. 86 Anaemia adversely affects the quality of life of cats with CKD. 86

代谢性酸中毒

Metabolic acidosis
代谢性酸中毒也很常见，其患病率为22%–88%，取决于慢性肾脏病（CKD）的严重程度。代谢性酸中毒通常在CKD的晚期发生。因血浆碳酸氢盐和氯化物浓度低而导致的阴离子间隙增加被观察到，而低氯血症的发展通常先于酸中毒。代谢性酸中毒可能由钾缺乏引起，但低钾血症的猫通常能够维持正常的静脉血pH值和碳酸氢盐浓度。肾小管铵生成（renal ammoniagenesis）是尿液中氢离子排泄的主要肾脏过程，在CKD中也可能受损。低氯血症可能是由于胃酸过多或呕吐导致胃肠道氯的丢失。代谢性酸中毒会在肾病患者中引起许多不良临床反应，包括食欲减退、呕吐、嗜睡、虚弱和营养不良。
Metabolic acidosis is also frequent, with a prevalence of 22–88% dependent on the severity of CKD.3,10,52 Metabolic acidosis generally occurs late in the time course of CKD. 72 An increase in anion gap due to low plasma bicarbonate and chloride concentrations is observed, 52 with development of hypochloraemia preceding acidosis. 72 Metabolic acidosis may result from potassium depletion, 84 but hypokalaemic cats are generally able to maintain normal venous blood pH and bicarbonate concentration. 52 Renal ammoniagenesis, the main renal process for urinary hydrogen ion excretion, could also be impaired in CKD. 52 Hypochloraemia may result from the loss of chloride through the gastrointestinal tract due to gastric hyperacidity or vomiting. 72 Metabolic acidosis promotes many adverse clinical effects in the renal patient, including anorexia, vomiting, lethargy, weakness and malnutrition. 27

蛋白尿在疾病进展中的作用

Role of proteinuria in disease progression
蛋白尿是肾损伤的一个标志，同时也是慢性肾病（CKD）进展的强烈指示因素。小分子蛋白，如白蛋白，可通过肾小球滤过，并通过受体介导的内吞作用被近曲小管细胞重吸收。这两个过程的功能障碍会导致蛋白尿。蛋白尿通过诱导小管趋化因子表达和补体激活，加速CKD的进展；继而引发炎症和纤维形成。蛋白尿与间质纤维化和肾小球肥大相关，因此可能是与小管间质纤维化相关的小管功能障碍的一个标志。
Proteinuria is a sign of kidney damage, but also a strong indicator for progression of CKD.87,88 Small proteins, such as albumin, are filtered by the glomeruli and reabsorbed by the proximal tubule cells by receptor-mediated endocytosis. Dysfunction of these two processes results in proteinuria. Proteinuria accelerates progression of CKD by induction of tubular chemokine expression and complement activation; this, in turn, leads to inflammation and fibrogenesis. Proteinuria is associated with interstitial fibrosis and glomerular hypertrophy, 38 and might, therefore, be a marker of tubular dysfunction associated with tubulointerstitial fibrosis.
与人类相比，自然发生慢性肾病（CKD）猫的蛋白尿通常较轻微，31,34,46 有 90% 和 49% 的患有 CKD 的猫的尿蛋白/肌酐比（UPC）分别低于 1.0 和 0.25。31 然而，蛋白尿的严重程度在生存时间的预后方面具有意义（图 13）。31,46 尽管蛋白尿通常较轻，ACVIM 关于蛋白尿治疗的共识声明建议，当导致氮质血症的 CKD 猫的 UPC ≥0.4 时进行治疗干预。87 血浆肌酐浓度和收缩压（SBP）与 UPC 增高呈正相关。31 图 13. 蛋白尿对 CKD 猫生存的影响。31 与基线组（UPC <0.2）相比，UPC 为 0.2–0.4 和 >0.4 的死亡或安乐死风险比分别为 2.9 和 4.0。蛋白尿究竟是否促进 CKD 进展，或蛋白尿性 CKD 本身进展更快，目前尚不清楚。

临床症状可能具有非特异性，并且在疾病过程后期才显现，一旦发生了严重的肾组织损伤。许多猫在慢性肾病（CKD）晚期阶段之前几乎没有任何症状。IRIS第2期和第3期的临床症状出现频率相似，但第4期则伴随症状频率和严重性的增加。多尿和多饮是CKD最早且最常见的临床表现。患有CKD的猫在被诊断前的一年内更可能出现多饮和多尿。多饮作为对多尿的代偿性反应，更容易被主人察觉。在晚期阶段，由于体液流失超过摄入，导致多尿引起脱水。
Clinical signs may be non-specific and manifest late in the disease process, once major renal tissue damage has occurred. Many cats remain almost symptom-free until they reach advanced stages of CKD. The frequency of clinical signs is similar in IRIS stages 2 and 3, but stage 4 is associated with increased frequency and severity of signs. 46 Polyuria and polydipsia represent the earliest and most common clinical manifestations of CKD. 27 Cats with CKD are more likely to have had polydipsia and polyuria in the year before they are diagnosed. 89 Polydipsia, which is a compensatory response to polyuria, is more easily recognised by owners. In advanced stages, polyuria results in dehydration as fluid loss from the body exceeds fluid intake.
胃肠道症状是尿毒症最突出的指标，可能也是主人带宠物去看兽医的主要原因。据报道，慢性肾病 (CKD) 猫存在胃泌素血症，90 可能引起胃酸过多、尿毒性胃炎、胃肠道出血、厌食和呕吐。虽然腹泻不常见，但便秘在 CKD 猫中比在狗中更为常见。这既与脱水有关，也与使用磷结合剂有关。91 其他报道的尿毒症临床并发症包括嗜睡、虚弱、抑郁、震颤、肌阵挛、癫痫、肌病、尿毒性心包炎和肺炎、体温过低、贫血和肾性骨营养不良。这些疾病都是多因素的，其发病机制通常在猫中尚未得到研究。
Gastrointestinal signs are the most prominent indicators of uraemia and probably represent the main reason for the owner to consult a veterinarian. Hypergastrinaemia, reported in cats with CKD, 90 may induce gastric hyperacidity, uraemic gastritis, gastrointestinal bleeding, anorexia and vomiting. While diarrhoea is uncommon, constipation is more frequent in cats with CKD than in dogs. It is attributable to dehydration but also to the use of phosphate binding agents. 91 Other reported uraemic clinical complications are lethargy, weakness, depression, tremor, myoclonus, seizures, myopathies, uraemic pericarditis and pneumonitis, hypothermia, anaemia and renal osteodystrophy. These disorders are all multifactorial and their pathogenesis has not generally been investigated in cats.

要点

Key Points
每当猫被诊断出慢性肾病（CKD）时，应特别努力去识别可能有特定治疗的潜在疾病。大多数情况下，CKD的原因仍未确定，在诊断时通常只能检测到非特异性肾小管间质纤维化的迹象。年龄、品种、系统性高血压、心脏疾病和泌尿道感染都被认为是CKD的风险因素，尽管缺乏确凿的证据。无论其起因如何，持续的肾损伤会导致自我持续的不适应性变化，从而推动CKD的进展。肾小管间质纤维化是猫CKD中最常见的组织病理学发现。
Whenever CKD is diagnosed in a cat, particular effort should be made to identify underlying conditions that may have a specific treatment. Most often, the cause of CKD remains unidentified and only signs of a non-specific tubulointerstitial fibrosis can be detected at the time of diagnosis. Age, breed, systemic hypertension, cardiac disease and urinary tract infection are all considered to be risk factors for CKD, although definitive evidence is lacking. Whatever its origin, sustained renal injury leads to self-perpetuated maladaptive changes that drive CKD progression. Tubulointerstitial fibrosis is the most common histopathological finding in cats with CKD.
