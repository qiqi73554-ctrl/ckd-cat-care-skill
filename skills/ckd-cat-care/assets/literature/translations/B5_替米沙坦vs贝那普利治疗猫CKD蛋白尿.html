Cats included in the efficacy analysis were required to be followed to Day 90 (per‐protocol‐population dataset [PPS], Fig 1) whereas all treated cats (intention‐to‐treat [ITT] population) were included in the safety analysis. Baseline characteristics of the groups were compared for homogeneity.

The same approach was followed as used in the reference study,28 in which benazepril was shown to be superior to placebo in a randomized controlled trial assessing the effect of treatment on time‐averaged proteinuria (area under the curve [AUC] UP/C). Because internal pilot and published data4 showed a skewed distribution of the calculated AUC UP/C values, sample size determination and statistical analysis of the primary efficacy variable were based on the log transformed AUC UP/C values.
对于主要疗效分析，计算了基于日志转换加权平均的UP/C基线变化百分比（AUC UP/C 0→t/t）随时间变化的每只猫的汇总值（第0天 = 100%）。AUC原理用于考虑随时间逐渐失访的猫，这一方法被参考研究采用。28 利用正态近似的置信区间（CI）方法评估对苯那普利的非劣性。29 当log(AUC UP/C 0→t/t)差值的95%双侧CI完全位于非劣性界限Δ（−0.108至0.116）以上时，拒绝“治疗劣于阳性对照”的原假设。假设在对数尺度上的非劣性界限为Δ = 0.108，对应的AUC(IVP)/AUC(CP)比值约为1.11，该值来源于一项内部先导研究，该研究包括37只动物，持续8–12周，观察到平均AUC 0→t/t值为106.83%，标准差为28.28%。基于这些数据，预先定义了一个绝对界限为119%，对应相对界限约为1.11，并被许可机构认为具有临床意义。
For the primary efficacy analysis, a summary value based on a log transformed weighted average of UP/C change from baseline (AUC UP/C 0→t/t) in percentage of each cat over time was calculated (Day 0 = 100%). The AUC principle was used to account for progressive loss of cats to follow‐up as used in the reference study.28 Noninferiority to benazepril was based on a confidence interval (CI) approach using the normal approximation.29 The null hypothesis “inferiority of treatment to positive control” was rejected if the 95% 2‐ sided CI of the log (AUC UP/C 0→t/t) difference lay entirely above the noninferiority margin Δ(−0.108 to 0.116). A noninferiority margin of Δ = 0.108 on the log scale, corresponding to a ratio AUC(IVP)/AUC(CP) of approximately 1.11, was assumed and determined from an internal pilot study with 37 animals over 8–12 weeks in which an average AUC 0→t/t value of 106.83% with a SD of 28.28% was observed. Based on these data, an absolute margin of 119% corresponding to a relative margin of approximately 1.11 was defined a priori and was accepted as clinically meaningful by the licensing authorities.
此外，每组内对 UP/C 相对于基线的绝对变化使用单样本符号秩检验进行分析。P 值通过 Bonferroni–Holm 程序进行多重性调整。其他次要疗效变量，包括行为变化和治疗失败次数，对 PPS 人群进行了统计汇总。次要疗效变量未在组间进行统计比较。根据 CONSORT 的建议，对该非劣效性研究进行了有效评估。统计分析使用 SAS 系统 9.2 版本进行。
In addition, absolute change in UP/C from baseline was analyzed within each group using a 1‐sample signed‐rank test. P‐values were adjusted for multiplicity by the Bonferroni–Holm procedure.26 Other secondary efficacy variables, including behavioral changes and number of treatment failures, were summarized statistically for the PPS population. Secondary efficacy variables were not compared between groups statistically. In accordance with the recommendations of CONSORT,30 a valid assessment of this noninferiority study is provided. Statistical analyses were performed using the SAS System Version 9.2.

Cats were recruited between March 2008 and August 2010, with case follow‐up completed in January 2011. More than 2000 cats were screened and 224 cats (ITT population) were recruited with 112 cats randomized to each group (Fig 1). Clinical signs suggestive of CKD were present for >3 months in most cats before inclusion. The baseline demographics, and clinical and laboratory characteristics were distributed homogeneously between groups (Table 1). Cats >11 years predominated in both groups, which had similar mean and median ages (Table 1). Most cats (approximately 80%) were classified as IRIS CKD stage 2 (84/112 and 91/112 for telmisartan and benazepril, respectively). Four cats in IRIS CKD stage 4 were included in the study as a protocol deviation (Table 2), the remainder being IRIS CKD stage 3. Most cats enrolled were borderline proteinuric (UP/C 0.2–0.4), with similar numbers in each treatment group; 3 cats with UP/C < 0.2 were included as a protocol deviation because all other criteria for CKD were fulfilled (Table 2).
51%的猫的收缩压（SBP）结果属于轻度至中度高血压等级（IRIS 分类；150–179 mmHg；表2）。少数SBP >170但 <180 mmHg且在第0天之前通过氨氯地平治疗已稳定的猫被纳入两个组（表1）。两组中大约30%的猫在纳入时正在喂食肾脏饮食（表1）。在实际条件下，偏离计划时间表是不可避免的。这些偏离不超过12天，并被认为不会影响整体研究结果。
Fifty‐one percent of the cats had SBP results in the mild to moderate hypertension class (IRIS classification; 150–179 mmHg; Table 2). A small number of cats with SBP >170 but <180 mmHg that were stabilized on amlodipine treatment before Day 0 was included in both groups (Table 1). Approximately, 30% of cats in both groups were being fed a renal diet at inclusion (Table 1). Deviations from planned time schedule were unavoidable under field conditions. They did not exceed 12 days and were considered to be of no influence on the general study outcome.

The percentage of treatment failures in the PPS population was 15.3% (13/85) for telmisartan and 19.8% (18/91) for benazepril‐treated cats. 特尔米沙坦和贝那普利耐受性良好，大多数报告的不良事件（AEs）都是慢性肾病（CKD）典型症状，大多数不太可能与治疗相关。两组中报告的不良事件（贝那普利组：65只猫，特尔米沙坦组：61只猫出现 ≥1 次AE）包括慢性肾病（CKD）恶化的症状、胃肠道疾病，以及很少数猫出现尿路感染、心脏、口腔、皮肤或呼吸系统疾病、神经系统症状或肿瘤。图1提供了在第90天前导致个体退出研究的不良事件的详细信息。研究结束时，两组的红细胞计数（RBC）、血红蛋白（HGB）和红细胞压积（PCV）（HCT）平均值相当。在2只患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫中（每组各1只）报告临床贫血证据；两只猫同时也在接受氨氯地平治疗。两组中血清钙、钾、钠或磷浓度均未记录到临床相关变化。
Telmisartan and benazepril were well tolerated and most of the AEs reported were typical of the signs of CKD, and the majority was unlikely to be related to treatment. Adverse events that were reported in both groups (benazepril: 65 cats and telmisartan: 61 cats with ≥1 AE) included signs of deterioration of CKD, gastrointestinal disorders, and very few cats with urinary tract infections, cardiac, oral cavity, skin, or respiratory disorders, neurological signs, or neoplasia. At the end of the study, the mean values for red blood cell count (RBC), hemoglobin (HGB) and hematocrit (HCT) were comparable between treatment groups. Clinical evidence of anemia was reported in 2 cats (1 in each group) with CKD; both also were receiving amlodipine. No clinically relevant changes in serum calcium, potassium, sodium, or phosphate concentrations were recorded in either treatment group.
我们研究的目的是比较特尔米沙坦与贝那普利在主要终点指标——尿蛋白/肌酐比值（UPC）（UP/C）从基线变化方面的疗效。尽管该研究设计为非劣效性研究，特尔米沙坦在事先约定的优越性标准方面接近达到对贝那普利的优越性。在所有评估时间点，与基线相比，接受特尔米沙坦治疗的猫UP/C均显著降低，而接受贝那普利治疗的猫则未达到统计学显著性。特尔米沙坦是一种选择性AT1受体拮抗剂，在治疗浓度下不会与AT2受体或其他关键生理重要受体或酶结合。选择性阻断AT1受体为调节RAAS提供了另一种方法。特尔米沙坦在人类成人中获得了两个适应症的许可：治疗原发性高血压，以及在患有动脉血栓性心血管疾病或伴有靶器官损伤的2型糖尿病患者中减少心血管发病率（心血管预防）。
The aim of our study was to compare the efficacy of telmisartan against benazepril with respect to the primary endpoint, change in UP/C from baseline. Although the study was designed as a noninferiority study, telmisartan was close to meeting the a priori agreed upon criterion for superiority to benazepril. The UP/C was significantly decreased compared to baseline at all assessment points in telmisartan‐treated cats, whereas statistical significance was not reached for benazepril‐treated cats. , a selective AT1 receptor antagonist, does not bind to AT2 receptors or other key physiologically important receptors or enzymes at therapeutic concentrations.31 Selective blockade of AT1 receptors offers an alternative approach to the modulation of the RAAS. Telmisartan is licensed for 2 indications in human adults: the treatment of essential hypertension, as well as the reduction in cardiovascular morbidity (cardiovascular prevention) in patients with atherothrombotic cardiovascular disease or type 2 diabetes mellitus with target organ damage.
人类中替米沙坦的肾脏保护作用被认为与选择性阻断AT1受体和部分激动PPAR-γ受体有关。23, 32 本研究中猫的蛋白尿明显下降可能可以用替米沙坦的这种双重作用机制或绕过潜在的ACE-逃逸机制来解释。为了筛选出适合纳入本研究的CKD猫，必须筛选大量猫。研究人群（主要为IRIS CKD阶段2）被认为适合预期的目标人群，因为如果治疗原理是为了减缓疾病进展，那么早期治疗是合理的。本研究中的猫年龄较大，体重略高于参考研究，28 这些发现可能表明猫群体的人口学最近发生了变化。
Renoprotective properties of telmisartan in humans are thought to be related to selective blockade of AT1 and partial agonism of PPAR‐γ receptors.23, 32 The apparently more pronounced decrease in proteinuria in cats in this study might be explained by this dual mode of action of telmisartan or circumvention of potential ACE‐escape mechanisms. A large number of cats had to be screened to identify suitable cats with CKD for inclusion in this study. The study population (predominantly IRIS CKD stage 2) was considered to be appropriate for the intended target population because early treatment is logical if the therapeutic rationale is that of slowing progression. Cats in this study were older and had a slightly higher body weight compared to the reference study,28 findings that probably are indicative of recent demographic changes in the cat population.
根据 CONSORT 的要求，参考治疗的有效性已在一项随机安慰剂对照研究中得到确认。为了进行有效的不劣性评估，有必要确保参与者、研究实施和结果测量与参考研究相似。本研究在研究设计、目标人群和结果测量方面与参考研究高度匹配。主要结果是蛋白尿的下降，通过基线的 UP/C 变化计算（通过 AUC UP/C 0→t/t 评估），之所以选择这一指标，是因为参考研究中使用了该指标。此外，蛋白尿已被证明是猫慢性肾病（CKD）进展和生存的独立预测因子。参考研究中报告的局限性已得到解决，以便在本研究中进行无偏倚的结果评估。因此，在纳入前，进行了尿液培养和甲状腺功能亢进筛查。
According to the requirements of CONSORT,30 efficacy of the reference treatment had been established in a randomized placebo‐controlled study.28 For a valid assessment of noninferiority, it is necessary that participants, study conduct, and outcome measures are similar to the reference study.28 This study closely matched the reference study with respect to study design, target population, and outcome measures. The primary outcome, decrease in proteinuria, calculated by UP/C change from baseline (as assessed by the AUC UP/C 0→t/t), was chosen because it was used in the reference study. Furthermore, proteinuria has been previously demonstrated to be an independent predictor of progression and survival of cats with CKD.4, 11, 12, 13 Limitations reported for the reference study28 were addressed to allow an unbiased assessment of outcome in this study. Thus, before inclusion, urine culture and screening for hyperthyroidism were undertaken.
甲状腺功能亢进症是已知的慢性肾病（CKD）猫的共病，可能影响蛋白尿和肝酶活性。为了不影响疗效和安全性结论，将血清 T4 浓度>3.1 μg/dL 的猫排除在外，该浓度位于参考范围的上三分之一，旨在排除伴有 CKD 的甲状腺功能亢进猫，因为其T4浓度降低可能使甲状腺功能亢进的诊断复杂化。本研究中超过 90% 的尿样是通过膀胱穿刺获得的，这比参考研究中 60% 的采集比例更为理想。本研究中尿液沉渣异常会触发尿培养。在 14 只猫中（苯那普利组 5 只，特米沙坦组 9 只），经确诊为细菌性泌尿道感染，因此被排除在疗效分析之外。
Hyperthyroidism is a known comorbidity in cats with CKD and may influence proteinuria and liver enzyme activity.33 In order not to bias efficacy and safety conclusions, cats with a T4 concentration >3.1 μg/dL, which is within the upper third of the reference range, were excluded, with the aim of excluding cats with concomitant hyperthyroidism and CKD in which suppressed T4 concentrations may complicate diagnosis of hyperthyroidism.34 More than 90% of urine samples collected in this study was obtained by cystocentesis, which compares favorably with 60% collected by this method in the reference study.28 Abnormal urine sediment triggered urine culture in this study. In 14 cats (5 in the benazepril and 9 in the telmisartan group), bacterial urinary tract infection was confirmed leading to exclusion from the efficacy analysis.
先前有报道表明，相比安慰剂，贝那普利在评估不同随访期的情况下能够降低慢性肾病（CKD）猫的蛋白尿，但并未显著改善生存时间，也未能减缓CKD进展。参考研究显示，安慰剂组的平均UP/C值较基线升高，而贝那普利似乎可以防止这种升高。与参考研究类似，本研究中贝那普利防止了蛋白尿的增加，而在接受替米沙坦治疗的猫中，只有在180天后蛋白尿相较基线有所下降。本研究持续了180天，因此无法评估替米沙坦对CKD猫的生存影响。仍需进一步研究以评估替米沙坦对CKD猫生存时间的影响。
Benazepril previously has been reported to decrease proteinuria in cats with CKD in comparison to placebo when assessed over a variable follow‐up period, but not to significantly improve survival time,28 or slow CKD progression.35 The reference study showed that time‐averaged UP/C increased above baseline in the placebo group, and benazepril seemed to prevent this increase. Similar to the reference study,28 in this study, benazepril prevented an increase in proteinuria while a decrease in proteinuria relative to baseline after 180 days was present only in telmisartan‐treated cats. This study was conducted for 180 days, thus the effect of telmisartan on survival of cats with CKD could not be evaluated. Additional studies are warranted to assess telmisartan's effect on survival time in cats with CKD.
本研究中观察到的UP/C相对于基线的下降是否具有临床意义可能值得质疑。先前的一项研究表明，在诊断时高UP/C（进行性病例与非进行性病例的中位值分别为0.26与0.15）和高血浆磷浓度是猫慢性肾病（CKD）氮质血症进展的重要独立风险因素，而且诊断时UP/C的微小差异具有临床相关性。在一项尸检研究中，蛋白尿与间质纤维化和肾小球肥大有关。这些蛋白尿与慢性肾病（CKD）不良结局之间的关联不能证明因果关系，但为研究特尔米沙坦对猫慢性肾病（CKD）进展或生存时间的影响提供了依据，因为本研究表明特尔米沙坦在减少蛋白尿方面的有效性。
It might be questioned whether the decrease in UP/C relative to baseline seen in this study is clinically relevant. A previous study demonstrated that high UP/C (median value of 0.26 versus 0.15 in progressive versus nonprogressive cases, respectively) and high plasma phosphate concentration at diagnosis are significant independent risk factors for progression of azotemia in cats with CKD and that small differences in UP/C at diagnosis are clinically relevant.12 In a postmortem study, proteinuria was associated with interstitial fibrosis and glomerular hypertrophy.36 These associations between proteinuria and negative CKD outcomes do not prove causality but provide the rationale for investigating telmisartan's effect on progression of CKD or survival time in cats because this study demonstrated telmisartan's efficacy in decreasing proteinuria.
这些关联也支持这种观点，即如果蛋白尿的减少具有肾脏保护作用并延长患慢性肾病（CKD）猫的生存期（这一点尚待证明），相对较小的UP/C下降（例如，从蛋白尿转为边缘性蛋白尿，或从边缘性蛋白尿转为非蛋白尿），或者随着时间防止UP/C升高，可能具有临床相关性。与参考研究28不同，我们的研究在入组时及研究结束时监测了收缩压（SBP），因为已知高血压与猫的慢性肾病（CKD）和蛋白尿有关。PPS人群在研究结束时的SBP在两组中都与基线相当。我们的研究并非设计用于评估降压疗效，因此需要进一步工作来评估对血压的影响。在ITT人群中，有16只猫（见表1），而在PPS人群中有11只猫曾接受过氨氯地平预处理（分别为苯那普利组和特米沙坦组的3只和8只）。
These associations also support the contention that, if a decrease in proteinuria is renoprotective and prolongs survival in the cats with CKD (which remains to be proven), relatively small decreases in UP/C (eg, from proteinuric to borderline proteinuric or from borderline proteinuric to nonproteinuric) or prevention of an increases in UP/C over time could be clinically relevant. Unlike the reference study,28 in our study SBP was monitored at inclusion and at study conclusion, because hypertension is known to be associated with CKD and proteinuria4 in cats. The SBP of the PPS population at the end of the study was comparable to baseline in both groups. Our study was not designed to assess antihypertensive efficacy, and additional work is necessary to assess effects on blood pressure. In the ITT population, 16 cats (Table 1), and in the PPS population 11 cats were pretreated with amlodipine (3 and 8, in the benazepril and telmisartan group, respectively).
为了排除氨氯地平对两组主要疗效变量的潜在影响，对未接受氨氯地平治疗的猫进行了相同的分析，结果几乎相同：主要变量log AUC→t/t的观察到的治疗差异为0.112，整体平均UP/C值在替米沙坦组显著变化（P = .013），而在贝那普利组未显著变化（P = .16）。此外，在排除接受氨氯地平治疗的猫后，180天时被分类为蛋白尿、边缘蛋白尿或非蛋白尿的猫的百分比与基线相比非常相似。血液学变量被监测以评估红细胞计数的变化，这些变化可能由CKD的进展或药物与RAAS的相互作用引起。在基线时，大约3%的猫红细胞计数低于参考范围（5.0 × 10^12/L）。在参考研究中，约10%的猫在基线时红细胞计数低于参考范围。两组之间的平均红细胞计数在研究结束时相当，并且在参考范围内。
To exclude a potential effect of amlodipine on the primary efficacy variable in the both groups, the same analysis was performed without the amlodipine‐treated cats, with almost the same results: observed treatment difference for the primary variable log AUC→t/t: 0.112, and significant change in overall mean UP/C value (P = .013) for the telmisartan but not (P = .16) for the benazepril group. Furthermore, the percentages of cats classified as proteinuric, borderline proteinuric or nonproteinuric at Day 180 in comparison to baseline were very similar when amlodipine‐treated cats were excluded. Hematology variables were monitored to assess changes in RBC counts, which might be caused either by progression of CKD or by drug interaction with the RAAS. At baseline, approximately 3% of cats had RBC counts below the reference range (5.0 × 1012/L). In the reference study,28 approximately 10% of the cats had RBC counts below the reference range at baseline. The mean RBC counts were comparable between groups and within the reference range at the end of the study.
在研究结束时，两组中红细胞计数低于参考范围的猫的比例略有增加。临床贫血的证据在2只猫中报告（每组各1只）。这两只猫均同时接受了氨氯地平治疗，因此无法评估治疗的作用。抑制RAAS的药物可能通过阻断AT-II对红细胞生成的作用而降低红细胞总量。37, 38 本研究的结果表明，这种潜在的红细胞计数下降在临床上并不重要。慢性肾病（CKD）猫中有20–25%发生低钾血症。39, 40 在本研究中，研究期间没有任何猫出现低钾或高钾血症。由于任何药物使用的首要要求是安全性，因此本研究还收集了不良事件的数据。两组的死亡和退出研究的数量都很少。两组报告的不良事件是慢性肾病（CKD）猫预期会出现的情况，在大多数情况下被认为与治疗无关。
The proportion of cats with RBC counts below the reference range slightly increased in both groups by study end. Clinical evidence of anemia was reported in 2 cats (1 per group). Both cats received concomitant amlodipine treatment, and thus the role of treatment could not be assessed. Medications that inhibit the RAAS may decrease red cell mass by blocking the effects of AT‐II on erythropoiesis.37, 38 The results of this study suggest this potential decrease in RBC count is not clinically relevant. Hypokalemia occurs in 20–25% of cats with CKD.39, 40 In this study, neither hypo‐ nor hyperkalemia was found in any cat over the study period. Because a primary requirement for the administration of any medication is safety, our study also collected data on AEs. The number of deaths and withdrawals from the study was small in both groups. Adverse events reported in both groups were those expected for cats with CKD and in most cases were judged as unlikely to be treatment related.
总之，替米沙坦能够有效降低蛋白尿，并且对患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫咪治疗是安全的。在主要变量方面，替米沙坦的效果至少与贝那普利相当。实际上，替米沙坦的抗蛋白尿作用似乎更为显著，因为与基线相比，使用替米沙坦可显著降低尿蛋白/肌酐比（UP/C），而贝那普利则没有出现显著变化。我们的研究结果表明，替米沙坦是一种安全且有效的治疗方案，可用于减少猫慢性肾病（CKD）相关的蛋白尿。
In conclusion, telmisartan effectively decreased proteinuria and was safe for treatment of cats with CKD. For the primary variable, telmisartan was at least as effective as benazepril. Indeed, telmisartan's antiproteinuric effect appeared to be more pronounced because significant decreases in UP/C compared to baseline were identified for telmisartan but not benazepril. The results of our study indicated that telmisartan was a safe and effective treatment to decrease proteinuria associated with CKD in cats.
