Cats with CKD have decreased HRQoL. Several modifiable clinical factors are associated with this decreased HRQoL. Keywords: Caregiver burden, anaemia, constipation, appetite, polypharmacy, wellbeing Quality of life (QoL) is a broad concept that is impacted by many features of an individual’s life, including physical health, psychological state, social relationships and salient features of their environment. Health-related quality of life (HRQoL) is concerned with the effects of illness and the impact treatment may have on QoL.1,2 In human medicine, assessment of HRQoL in addition to biomedical outcomes has become an integral part of the understanding of disease management. In the veterinary setting, the assessment of HRQoL is determined by caregiver observation and report of specific behaviours that indicate how the animal might ‘feel’ about its circumstances. The importance of HRQoL to the caregiver is likely a significant component in decisions made regarding the sick pet.

For example, in a study of the perception of HRQoL in cats with heart disease, 93% of caregivers were willing to trade survival time for comfortable QoL. 3 In human patients with chronic kidney disease (CKD), HRQoL is decreased in comparison with healthy individuals and also decreases according to severity and progression of disease.4 –8 Children with CKD have lower HRQoL scores in comparison with healthy children, and children who have more severe clinical symptoms have lower HRQoL scores in comparison with those who do not.9,10 In cats with CKD, QoL scores related to eating (eg, liking food and appetite) and management aspects, such as administration of medication and accessing veterinary care, are lower in comparison with healthy cats. 11 The potential impact of disease stage and specific clinical and physiologic abnormalities associated with CKD has not been studied in cats.
高血压、贫血、电解质异常、食欲紊乱、脱水和便秘等情况常作为慢性肾病（CKD）的后遗症出现，应予以识别和治疗。这些病理变化可能对患者产生负面影响，从而改变健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）。在猫咪中使用不适合这些相互作用的药物或治疗可能会对人–动物关系产生不利影响，并降低生活质量。识别可能与健康相关生活质量下降相关的可调因素，可能有助于临床医生为CKD猫咪制定个性化的治疗建议。
Conditions such as hypertension, anaemia, electrolyte abnormalities, dysrexia, dehydration and constipation commonly occur as sequelae of CKD and should be identified and treated. 12 These pathologic changes may have a negative effect on the patient and thus alter HRQoL. 11 Administration of medications or treatments in cats that are not amenable to these interactions could adversely affect the human–animal bond and detract from HRQoL.3,11 Identification of modifiable factors that might be associated with a decreased HRQoL may aid clinicians in tailoring treatment recommendations for cats with CKD.

Materials and methods

The study was approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC#2019A00000128) at The Ohio State University. Cats were screened for prospective enrolment in 1/2 groups: healthy cats or cats with CKD. All cats enrolled in the study were required to have informed consent signed by the animal’s caregiver. Healthy cats Client-owned healthy cats with normal systemic blood pressure, serum biochemistry profile, complete blood count (CBC), urinalysis and serum total thyroxine measurement were enrolled. Healthy control cats were defined as those with no clinical abnormalities, normal physical examination, a serum creatinine concentration less than 1.6 mg/dl and a urine specific gravity greater than 1.035.

Cats with CKD Cats with stable International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) stage 1–4 CKD were enrolled. Diagnostic tests required before enrolment included systemic blood pressure, serum biochemistry profile, CBC, urinalysis and serum total thyroxine measurement. The diagnosis of CKD was confirmed, and the affected cats were staged by a board-certified small animal internal medicine specialist according to IRIS guidelines. In cats with stage 1 CKD and early stage 2 CKD, diagnosis was based on either ultrasound findings consistent with degenerative renal disease and/or persistent inadequate urinary concentrating ability without an identifiable non-renal cause. Based on the clinical experience of the investigators, cats with CKD were considered to have stable CKD if serum creatinine had not changed by more than 20% on at least two measurements taken at least 2 weeks apart.

HRQoL 工具和临床数据收集

HRQoL instrument and clinical data collection
使用了一种先前发布的基于网络的通用健康相关生活质量(HRQoL)工具（VetMetrica）来测量慢性疾病对猫的影响（见补充材料中的附录）。13–15 在兽医就诊时邀请护理者参与研究，并要求其在一周内完成调查问卷。该工具包含20个基于行为的条目，猫的护理者使用7点评分类型量表进行回答（0 = 最低，6 = 最高），从而生成三个HRQoL领域的分数：活力、舒适度和情绪健康（EWB）。通过将活力和舒适度的分数平均，获得身体健康（PWB）的综合分数。
A previously published web-based, generic HRQoL instrument (VetMetrica) was used to measure the impact of chronic disease in cats (see Appendix in the supplementary material ).13 –15 Caregivers were asked to participate in the study at the time of the veterinary visit and to complete the survey within 1 week. This instrument consists of 20 behaviour-based items to which the cat caregiver responds using a 7-point Likert-type scale (0 = could not be less to 6 = could not be more), resulting in the generation of scores in three HRQoL domains: vitality, comfort and emotional wellbeing (EWB). A summary score for physical wellbeing (PWB) was obtained by averaging vitality and comfort scores.
为了帮助工具的可解释性，原始领域分数（0–6）被标准化，使得在0–100的尺度上，50代表平均健康猫的得分，70%的健康猫得分为44.8或更高。13,14 VetMetrica 工具补充了额外的问题（见补充材料附录），以收集患者的临床历史、疾病状况和管理方案，但这些并不影响HRQoL分数的计算方式。
To aid interpretability of the tool, raw domain scores (0–6) are normalised such that 50 on a scale of 0–100 represents the score for the average healthy cat, and 70% of healthy cats will score 44.8 or higher.13,14 The VetMetrica instrument was supplemented with additional questions (see Appendix in the supplementary material ) to gather information about the patient’s clinical history, disease status and management regimen, but these did not impact the manner in which the HRQoL scores were calculated.

在人类中，多个研究发现，CKD显著影响非透析和依赖透析患者的健康相关生活质量（HRQoL），主要影响身体健康领域，同时也影响心理健康领域。虽然许多研究描述了疾病严重程度和进展与HRQoL下降之间的相关性，但这些结果并不一致。这些发现与我们的研究相似，在我们的研究中，血清肌酐浓度与HRQoL各领域之间未见相关性，但晚期疾病患者的情绪健康（EWB）评分比早期疾病患者下降。类似地，在Bijsmans等人的研究中，与IRIS 2期相比，IRIS 3期和4期CKD猫的HRQoL评分在主观上有所下降，但未达到统计学显著性。
In humans, multiple studies have found that CKD significantly affects HRQoL in both non-dialysis and dialysis-dependent patients, impacting primarily physical but also mental HRQoL domains.4 –8 Although many studies describe a correlation between severity and progression of disease and decreasing HRQoL,4,7 these results are inconsistent. 16 These findings are similar to those of our study, where no correlation between serum creatinine concentration and HRQoL domains was seen, but EWB scores were decreased in late-stage disease compared with early-stage disease. Similarly, in the study by Bijsmans et al, 11 HRQoL scores were subjectively decreased in cats with IRIS stage 3 and 4 CKD, in comparison with IRIS stage 2, but failed to reach statistical significance.
或者，近期关于人类慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）的研究假设，与CKD相关的症状负担可能比疾病分期对健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）的影响更大。8,17–19 常见提到的症状包括疲劳、食欲下降、贫血和便秘。6,8 重要的是，许多CKD症状被认为是可调节的因素，如果通过管理进行干预，有可能改善HRQoL。6,19 在患有CKD的猫中，食欲异常很常见，而食欲失调可能起一定作用。20,21 食欲不振以及伴随的低体重与较差的预后相关。22,23 在我们的研究中，食欲下降的猫的情绪健康评分（EWB）低于食欲正常的猫。在Bijsmans等人的研究中，11 该研究使用不同的工具评估CKD猫，发现与饮食相关的生活质量评分，包括“喜欢食物”和“食欲”，也低于年轻和年老的健康猫。在人类医学中，也存在关于食欲与HRQoL关系的非常相似的发现。
Alternatively, recent studies on human CKD hypothesise that symptom burden associated with CKD likely has a more impactful role on HRQoL than stage of disease.8,17 –19 Symptoms such as fatigue, decreased appetite, anaemia and constipation are commonly mentioned specifically.6,8 Importantly, many of these CKD symptoms are considered modifiable factors, which, if targeted with management, have the potential to improve HRQoL.6,19 Abnormalities in appetite are common in cats with CKD, and appetite dysregulation may play a role.20,21 Poor appetite and concomitantly low body weight are associated with a poorer prognosis.22,23 In our study, cats with decreased appetite had lower EWB scores than cats with normal appetite. In the study by Bijsmans et al, 11 which assessed cats with CKD using a different instrument, QoL scores related to eating, including ‘liking food’ and ‘appetite’, were also decreased in comparison with young and old healthy cats. Very similar findings regarding the relationship between appetite and HRQoL are present in human medicine.
对保守治疗的透析前慢性肾病（CKD）儿童而言，食欲评分为“好”或“非常好”的儿童，其PedsQL（自评及父母代理评分）比食欲被描述为“差”或“非常差”的儿童更高。24名接受血液透析、自我报告食欲为“差”或“非常差”的CKD患者，在包括身体和心理/社会等多个领域的肾病特异性健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）评分较低，这表明食欲下降对患者生活的影响不仅仅体现在食物摄入量和营养状况上。25此外，在老年血液透析患者中，经过食欲刺激剂治疗后，食欲管理与肾病特异性HRQoL评分在心理和身体领域均有所改善。26这些变化伴随着食欲增加、热量摄入增加、体重和体质指数增加。26因此，改善食欲似乎是一个在各种物种中对CKD患者至关重要且可改良的HRQoL因素。6贫血是CKD猫常见的临床病理学表现。
Children with conservatively managed predialysis CKD with ‘good’ or ‘very good’ appetite scores have higher PedsQL (both self and parent-proxy scores) in comparison with children who have an appetite described as ‘poor’ or ‘very poor’. 24 CKD patients receiving haemodialysis who self-reported ‘poor’ or ‘very poor’ appetite have lower kidney disease-specific HRQoL scores in a range of domains, including physical and mental/social, implying that a reduction in appetite has an effect on patients’ lives that goes beyond the impact on food intake and nutritional status. 25 In addition, appetite management in elderly haemodialysis patients has been associated with improvements in both mental and physical domain kidney disease-specific HRQoL scores after treatment with an appetite stimulant. 26 These changes were accompanied by increased appetite, caloric intake, weight and body mass index. 26 Addressing appetite therefore appears to be an important, modifiable HRQoL factor in CKD across species. 6 Anaemia is a common clinicopathologic finding in cats with CKD.
其患病率随着疾病严重程度的增加而上升，在晚期疾病中，有多达53–65%的猫受到影响。23,27,28 它与疾病进展相关，并可能潜在地促进疾病进展。28–30 贫血的临床症状，如嗜睡、虚弱和食欲不振，可能表现得不明显，且容易与其他疾病症状混淆，因此具有显著影响健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）的潜在可能性，这在我们研究中EWB评分显著下降中得到了证实。在人类中，多项研究记录了贫血与HRQoL之间的关系。6,31 在一项关于慢性肾病（CKD）患者的横断面研究中，贫血患者的HRQoL评分低于非贫血患者。31 这一影响在非透析的IRIS 3期和4期CKD患者中更为明显。此外，贫血患者的疲劳和活动受限程度更高。无论使用哪种HRQoL评分系统，这些发现都是一致的，并且在心理和身体领域均有显著性。
Its prevalence increases with disease severity, with up to 53–65% of cats affected in late-stage disease.23,27,28 It is associated with disease progression and may potentially contribute to progression.28 –30 Clinical signs of anaemia, such as lethargy, weakness and poor appetite, may be vague and easily confused with other aspects of disease, and as such have a very plausible potential to effect HRQoL as evidenced by the significant decrease in EWB scores in our study. In humans, multiple studies have documented a relationship between anaemia and HRQoL.6,31 In a cross-sectional study of humans with CKD, patients with anaemia had lower HRQoL scores than those without anaemia. 31 This effect was more apparent in non-dialysis patients with IRIS stage 3 and 4 CKD. In addition, tiredness and impaired activity were both greater in patients with anaemia. Findings were true regardless of the HRQoL scoring system used and were significant in both mental and physical domains.
31 在一项评估达普鲁司他（daprodustat，一种缺氧诱导因子脯氨酰羟化酶抑制剂）的研究中，治疗结果显示，与安慰剂相比，可改善贫血、相关疲劳和健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）。32 重要的是，一项评估慢性肾脏病（CKD）患者伴侣的照护者负担的研究发现，患者贫血与较低的HRQoL以及显著更高的照护者负担相关。33 综合来看，这些发现表明，贫血是改善人类CKD患者HRQoL的一个关键可调节因素。6,19 鉴于贫血在猫CKD中的高发性，这可能成为个性化管理的重要目标。
31 In a study evaluating daprodustat, a hypoxia-inducible factor prolyl hydroxylase inhibitor, treatment resulted in improvements in anaemia, associated fatigue and HRQoL compared with placebo. 32 Importantly, a study assessing caregiver burden in partners of patients with CKD found that patient anaemia was associated with lower HRQoL and significantly higher caregiver burden. 33 Taken together, these findings identify anaemia as a key modifiable factor for improving HRQoL in human CKD patients.6,19 Given the prevalence of anaemia in feline CKD, this may represent an important target for tailored management.
患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫比健康猫更容易发生便秘，并且在因便秘就诊的猫科急诊患者中比例过高。34,35 CKD与便秘之间的关联在人类中也已得到认可，其病因在两种物种中可能是多因素的，归因于水合状态、电解质紊乱（特别是低钾血症）、胃肠生理改变以及药物管理，如磷结合剂。35–40 当考虑对生活质量（HRQoL）的影响时，在过去6个月内有便秘症状的猫——定义为排便困难、排便量减少或干燥，或排便频率下降——其情绪健康（EWB）和身体健康（PWB）生活质量评分低于没有便秘的猫。
Cats with CKD are more likely to experience constipation than healthy cats and are overrepresented in the population of feline patients presenting to the emergency room for constipation.34,35 The association between CKD and constipation is also recognised in humans, and is likely multifactorial in aetiology in both species, attributed to hydration status, electrolyte disturbances (specifically hypokalaemia), gastrointestinal physiologic alterations and pharmacologic management such as phosphate binders.35 –40 When the effect on HRQoL was considered, cats with constipation in the past 6 months – defined as difficulty defecating, smaller or drier stools, or decreased frequency of bowel movements – had lower EWB and PWB HRQoL scores than those without constipation.
这些发现与人体数据类似，显示便秘的存在及其严重程度与身体和心理领域中较低的健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）有关。39,40 因此，便秘被认为是改善人类慢性肾病（CKD）患者HRQoL的可调因素，6 我们的结果表明，对猫科患者采取类似方法可能也有益。向患有CKD的猫咪给予五种或更多药物与所有类别的HRQoL评分降低有关。早期文献显示，许多猫咪照护者在给宠物用药时遇到困难，3,41,42 超过一半的照护者认为这影响了他们与猫咪的关系。41 不论是短期还是长期药物使用，照护者均可能遇到困难，他们会担心药物的相互作用，并有部分人发现自己无法完成整个药物疗程。41 对多种药物的长期使用，这些因素逻辑上更容易加重。
These findings are similar to human data demonstrating that the presence and severity of constipation are associated with poorer HRQoL in both physical and mental domains.39,40 Constipation is therefore recognised as a modifiable factor for improving HRQoL in human CKD patients, 6 and our results suggest a similar approach may be beneficial for feline patients. Administering five or more medications to cats with CKD was associated with lowered HRQoL scores in all categories. Earlier literature demonstrates the difficulty many cat caregivers experience in administering medication to their pets,3,41,42 a phenomenon that over half of caregivers felt altered their relationship with their cat. 41 Difficulty can be experienced with both short-term and long-term medication administration, with caregivers experiencing worry about the interaction and a percentage finding themselves unable to complete a course of medication. 41 These factors are logically compounded with chronic administration of multiple medications.
在患有慢性肾病（CKD）的人类患者中，多药并用与健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）的下降相关。43 当将超多药（⩾10类药物）和重大多药（5到9类药物）与轻度多药（4类或更少药物）进行比较时，超多药和重大多药患者在心理和身体领域的HRQoL评分显著较低。43 因此，药物负担可能是为患有CKD的猫制定治疗计划时的重要考虑因素。鉴于老年猫易患疾病，招募一组年龄与CKD猫匹配的健康猫是不可能的，而且所招募的CKD猫组年龄比健康猫组大。在先前评估有无CKD猫的HRQoL的研究中，也存在无法将年龄与CKD患者匹配的实际问题。11 在该研究中，HRQoL在年轻健康猫（n = 105）、年老健康猫（n = 36）和CKD猫（n = 70）中进行了评估；结论是年轻猫的HRQoL明显优于年老猫和患有CKD的猫。
In human patients with CKD, polypharmacy is associated with decreased HRQoL. 43 When hyperpolypharmacy (⩾10 classes of medications) and major polypharmacy (five to nine classes of medications) were compared with minor polypharmacy (four or fewer classes of medications), patients with hyper- and major polypharmacy had significantly lower HRQoL scores in both mental and physical domains. 43 Therefore, pill burden is likely an important part of developing a treatment plan for feline patients with CKD. Given the propensity for disease in elderly cats, enrolling a population of healthy cats that were age-matched with CKD cats was not possible, and the CKD cat group that was enrolled was older in age than the healthy cat group. The impracticality of age-matching to CKD patients was also present in a previous study assessing HRQoL in cats with and without CKD. 11 In that study, HRQoL was assessed in young healthy (n = 105), old healthy (n = 36) and CKD cats (n = 70); it was concluded that young cats had significantly better HRQoL than older cats and those with CKD.
11 这一点在包括行动能力、排便、玩耍和狩猎等领域尤其明显。11 这可能反映了其他与年龄相关的变化，例如骨关节炎和伴随疾病发生率的增加。然而，在那项研究中，年轻猫的健康状况并未通过检查或实验室工作来确认。在本研究中，当仅考虑10岁以下的慢性肾病（CKD）猫并与健康对照猫进行比较时，受CKD影响的猫的生活质量评分仍然有统计学上的显著下降。此外，在CKD猫组内，所有三个生活质量领域的评分均与年龄呈负相关。总体而言，可以得出结论，年龄对生活质量有影响，但根据本研究的数据，CKD猫评分的变化不仅仅归因于衰老。
11 This was particularly true in domains including mobility, stools, playing and hunting. 11 This may reflect other age-related changes, such as osteoarthritis and increased incidence of concurrent disease. However, in that study, the health of the young cats was not confirmed by examination or laboratory work. In the present study, when only cats with CKD under the age of 10 years were considered in comparison with healthy control cats, there was still a statistically significant decrease in HRQoL scores in the cats affected by CKD. In addition, within the CKD cat group, there was a negative correlation with age for all three HRQoL domains. Overall, it can be concluded that age plays a role in HRQoL, but based on the data in the present study, changes in scores in CKD cats are not just attributable to ageing.
本研究收集的数据证明了VetMetrica工具在评估慢性肾病（CKD）猫咪中的有效性。有效性提供了工具测量其预期测量内容的证据——在此案例中，即HRQoL。然而，有效性并非量表的固有属性;必须收集证据以适应不同目的、特定人群和特定情境的应用。此前的研究已证明VetMetrica在健康猫、患有各种慢性病的猫和患有骨关节炎的猫中具有效性;然而，这是首个验证其在猫慢性肾病（CKD）群体中使用的研究。工具的有效性首先通过构建方法确定，随后测试不同类型的构念效度。其中一种方法“已知组”测试，考察评分是否如假设在不同组间存在差异——例如健康动物与生病动物，或疾病严重度递增的动物（轻度<中度<重度）。
The data collected in this study demonstrate the validity of the VetMetrica instrument for evaluating cats with CKD. Validity provides evidence that a tool measures what it is intended to measure – in this case, HRQoL. However, validity is not an intrinsic property of a scale; evidence must be gathered for its use in different purposes, defined populations and specific contexts. Previous studies have established the validity of VetMetrica in healthy cats, cats with various chronic conditions and cats with osteoarthritis;13,15 however, this is the first study to validate its use in a feline CKD population. Instrument validity is established first through construction methods, followed by testing for different types of construct validity. One such method, ‘known- groups’ testing, examines whether scores differ as hypothesised between groups – for example, healthy vs sick animals, or animals with increasing disease severity (mild < moderate < severe).
在本研究中，通过展示健康猫与慢性肾病（CKD）猫之间的HRQoL（健康相关生活质量）差异，以及IRIS 1/2期与3/4期猫之间、贫血猫与非贫血猫之间、食欲异常与正常猫之间、便秘猫与正常排便猫之间以及不同程度肌肉消耗猫之间的HRQoL差异，支持了已知组的有效性。已知组验证中仍可能出现分数重叠，因为不期望完全分离，并且即使在“正常”群体中也会出现生物学变异。值得注意的是，与CKD猫相比，健康猫的变异性明显较小。这是意料之中的，更大的变异性可以归因于病猫群体的异质性。一般而言，分数重叠可能是由于IRIS分期之间界限模糊所致。
In the present study, known-groups validity was supported by demonstrating different HRQoL profiles between healthy cats and those with CKD, between cats with IRIS stage 1/2 vs stage 3/4, anaemic vs non-anaemic cats, cats with abnormal vs normal appetite, cats with constipation vs normal stool and cats with varying degrees of muscle wasting. Overlapping scores are still possible with known-group validation as complete separation is not expected and biological variation will be seen even in a ‘normal’ population. It is perhaps worth noting that there is much less variation in the healthy cats compared with the CKD cats. This is to be expected and that greater variability can be attributed to the heterogeneity of the sick population. In general, overlap could be due to the blurring of the distinction between IRIS stages.
特别是，健康猫与1/2期慢性肾病（CKD）猫之间的重叠，可能与1期猫可能无症状，因此在其身体健康福祉（PWB）和情绪健康福祉（EWB）方面呈现为健康猫有关。本研究的局限性包括依赖看护者报告的数据，这些数据基于观察而非客观测量，可能不准确（例如，便秘的评估）。在猫科患者中，个体猫的真实体验无法真正评估，使得看护者的感知成为健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）评估的核心，尽管这种感知可能受到未被识别因素的影响。然而，VetMetrica 是一种基于观察者的工具，其中看护者报告可被解读为反映动物情绪状态的行为，这种方法为美国食品药品监督管理局所认可，用于非语言个体，包括婴儿、认知受损个体和动物。
In particular, the overlap between healthy cats and those with stage 1/2 CKD is likely to be related to the fact that stage 1 cats may be asymptomatic and therefore present as healthy cats in terms of their PWB and EWB. Limitations of this study include reliance on caretaker-reported data, which were based on observations rather than objective measurements and may have not been accurate (eg, assessment of constipation). In feline patients, a cat’s individual experience cannot be truly assessed, making caregiver perception central to HRQoL evaluation, although such perception may be influenced by unrecognised factors. However, VetMetrica is an observer-based tool in which the caregiver reports behaviours that can then be interpreted to reflect the animal’s emotional state, an approach favoured by the US Food and Drug Administration for non-verbal individuals, including infants, cognitively impaired individuals and animals.
44 在儿童慢性肾病（CKD）患者中，健康相关生活质量（HRQoL）评估发现患者自述与父母评估之间存在中等程度的相关性，身体领域的协议性比心理领域更强。9 另一个限制是健康组中两只血清肌酐处于高正常范围的猫未进行影像学检查，以区分IRIS 1期疾病与健康状态。患有CKD的猫的HRQoL评分低于健康猫。几个可调节的临床症状，如食欲下降、贫血和便秘，与HRQoL下降相关，这表明识别并处理这些异常可能改善受影响猫的福祉。
44 In paediatric CKD patients, HRQoL assessments have been found to correlate moderately between patient self-reports and parental assessments, with stronger agreement for physical domains than mental domains. 9 Another limitation was that two cats in the healthy group with high-normal serum creatinine were not imaged to differentiate IRIS stage 1 disease from a healthy status. Cats with CKD have lower HRQoL scores than healthy cats. Several modifiable clinical signs, such as reduced appetite, anaemia and constipation, are associated with decreased HRQoL, suggesting that identifying and addressing these abnormalities may improve wellbeing in affected cats.
