Introduction Feline chronic kidney disease (fCKD) is among the most common and clinically significant disorders in cats, particularly in older individuals [1,2]. It represents a major cause of morbidity and mortality, with a prevalence that exceeds that reported in dogs [3–5].
根据国际肾脏兴趣协会（IRIS）[6]，普通猫群中慢性肾病（CKD）（fCKD）的患病率在1.0%到3.0%之间[2,5,7,8]，而在老年猫中可能高达80%[1,2,7,9]。慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）的特征是一个或双肾的结构和/或功能损伤持续至少三个月。这种不可逆的肾脏结构和/或功能丧失可能在不同时间内保持稳定，但会随时间进展[9]。肾小管间质性肾炎是fCKD中最常观察到的组织病理学病变；然而，确切的病因仍不清楚[1,2,10]。现有证据表明其病因是多因素的，由遗传易感性、个体易感性和环境影响共同决定[10]。
According to the International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) [6], the prevalence of fCKD in the general feline population ranges between 1.0 and 3.0% [2,5,7,8], while in geriatric cats it may reach up to 80% [1,2,7,9]. Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is characterized by structural and/or functional damage of one or both kidneys that persists for at least three months. This irreversible loss of renal structure and/or function may remain stable for variable periods, but progresses over time [9]. Tubulointerstitial nephritis is the most commonly observed histopathologic lesion in fCKD; however, the precise underlying cause remains unclear [1,2,10]. Current evidence supports a multifactorial nature, shaped by genetic predisposition, individual susceptibility, and environmental influences [10].
多种因素会增加慢性肾病（CKD）的风险或促进其进展。年龄是最稳定的风险因素 [1,2,9–12]，而某些品种 [1,8,13,14] 和营养状态 [1,8,14] 也被认为有关。接触治疗性或诊断性药物 [1] 以及存在合并症——例如甲状腺功能亢进、心血管疾病、尿路感染或像猫免疫缺陷病毒 (FIV) 和猫白血病病毒 (FeLV) 这样的病毒感染——进一步促进疾病进展 [1,8,10,14,15]。诊断基于病史、体格检查、临床病理数据、影像学发现，并在必要时基于组织病理学结果 [16,17]。血清肌酐（sCr）虽然被广泛使用，但在疾病早期对变化不敏感，并且受肌肉质量、体液状况、年龄、饮食以及所使用测量方法差异等非肾因素影响 [16,18,19]。最近，对称性二甲基精氨酸 (SDMA) 和尿蛋白/肌酐比值（UPC）（UP/C）已被采纳为临床实践中的额外标志物 [16,20]。
Several factors increase CKD risk or contribute to its progression. Age is the most con- sistent risk factor [1,2,9–12], while certain breeds [1,8,13,14] and nutritional statuses [1,8,14] have also been implicated. Exposure to therapeutic or diagnostic agents [1] and the pres- ence of comorbidities—such as hyperthyroidism, cardiovascular disease, urinary tract infections, or viral infections like FIV and FeLV—further contribute to disease progres- sion [1,8,10,14,15]. Diagnosis is based on history, physical examination, clinicopathological data, imaging findings, and, when indicated, histopathology results [ 16,17]. Serum creatinine (sCr), though widely used, is insensitive in the early disease course and influenced by non- renal factors such as muscle mass, hydration, age, diet, and variation in measurement methods used [16,18,19]. More recently, symmetric dimethylarginine (SDMA) and the urine protein-to-creatinine (UP/C) ratio have been adopted as additional markers in clinical practice [16,20].
然而，fCKD 的诊断往往延迟，通常只有在临床症状出现后才能确立 [1,16]。常用生物标志物的低敏感性、尿液收集的困难以及主人推迟就医的倾向，都导致早期疾病的漏诊 [19]。这些诊断挑战表明，需要大规模、真实世界的数据来更好地了解 fCKD 在典型临床环境中的表现和进展。一旦确诊，疾病分期对于临床管理就变得至关重要。基于上述生物标志物，IRIS 已建立了 fCKD 的四期分级系统。当猫咪处于稳定和水合状态时，根据血肌酐（sCr）和/或对称二甲基精氨酸（SDMA）值将其分类到 IRIS 各期，并进一步根据蛋白尿和血压测量进行子分期 [20]。
However, the diagnosis of fCKD is often delayed, usually established only once clinical signs emerge [1,16]. Low sensitivity of commonly used biomarkers, challenges in urine collection, and owners’ tendency to postpone veterinary consultation contribute to the underdiagnosis of early disease [ 19]. These diagnostic challenges are suggestive of the need for large-scale, real-world data to better understand how fCKD presents and progresses in a typical clinical setting. Once a diagnosis is achieved, disease staging becomes essential for clinical management. Based on the biomarkers mentioned above, IRIS has established a four-stage grading system for fCKD. Cats are classified into IRIS stages according to sCr and/or SDMA values when they are stable and hydrated, and are further substaged based on proteinuria and blood pressure measurements [20].
猫慢性肾病（CKD）的临床表现通常不具有特异性，并且可能直到发生显著的肾损伤后才会出现[1]。多尿和多饮通常是最早期和最常见的表现[21]。随着疾病进展，其他症状如体重下降、消化道紊乱、嗜睡和脱水会越来越常见[1,22]，反映了肾功能下降和整体状况恶化的系统性影响。CKD会负面影响猫的生活质量（QoL），导致活动减少、食欲不振以及饮食调整或药物管理困难。因此，评估生活质量为评估疾病严重程度和治疗干预效果提供了额外维度[23]。来自人类医学的见解进一步强调了其重要性：研究一致表明，与健康个体相比，CKD会降低生活质量[24,25]，且在晚期阶段会不断恶化[26]。
Clinical signs of CKD in cats are often non-specific and may not appear until significant renal damage has occurred [1]. Polyuria and polydipsia are usually the earliest and most common manifestations [21]. As the disease advances, other signs such as weight loss, gastrointestinal disturbances, lethargy, and dehydration become increasinglycommon [1,22], reflecting the systemic impact of reduced renal function and the decline in overall condition. CKD negatively affects a cat’s quality of life (QoL), leading to reduced activity, poor appetite, and difficulties with dietary modifications or medication administration. Therefore, assessing QoL provides an additional dimension for evaluating disease severity and the effectiveness of therapeutic interventions [23]. Insights from human medicine further Vet. 慢性肾病（CKD）的长期性质也会影响主人们的心理健康。研究报告显示，照顾患有慢性或晚期疾病宠物的主人中，压力、抑郁和焦虑水平较高，同时生活质量（QoL）降低 [27]。这些发现突显了患有CKD的猫咪及其主人的福祉密切相关，强调在临床管理和研究中需要同时关注双方。近年来，真实世界数据（RWD）和真实世界证据（RWE）的出现，为医学研究创造了新的机会 [28]。美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）[29] 将RWD定义为来自医疗记录、保险索赔或疾病登记等来源的健康相关数据，而RWE是通过分析这些数据生成的临床证据。然而，在兽医学中，仅有少数研究将RWE纳入常规实践 [30,31]。将这些方法应用于猫CKD（fCKD）研究，可为猫咪及其主人在临床表现和生活质量方面提供新的视角，改善疾病管理，并支持日常实践中更明智的决策。
The chronic nature of CKD also impacts owners’ mental health. Higher levels of stress, depression, and anxiety, as well as reduced QoL, have been reported among caregivers of pets with chronic or terminal conditions [27]. These findings highlight that the wellbeing of cats with CKD and their owners is closely linked, emphasizing the need to address both in clinical management and research. In recent years, the emergence of real-world data (RWD) and real-world evidence (RWE) has created new opportunities in medical research [28]. The FDA [29] defines RWD as health-related data from sources such as medical records, insurance claims, or disease registries, while RWE is the clinical evidence generated from their analysis. In veterinary medicine, however, only a limited number of studies have incorporated RWE into routine practice [30,31]. Applying these methods to fCKD could provide new perspectives on both the clinical presentation and QoL of cats and their owners, improve disease management, and support more informed decision-making in daily practice.

Participating veterinarians identified eligible cats (a) by searching their clinic’s medical records for already diagnosed fCKD cases and (b) by enrolling new fCKD cases that were admitted to their clinics between October 2023 and July 2024. In both cases, owners who agreed to participate admitted their cats to the veterinary clinics and provided written informed consent during their appointments for clinical examination and blood sampling.
参与研究的大多数猫主人为女性（175/208，84.1%）。男女主人表现出相似的平均年龄（分别为45.7岁和46.4岁）和年龄范围（分别为20–78岁和20–74岁）。主人的人口统计学特征，如性别和年龄，并未显示出显著的模式。详细的人口统计信息见补充材料（表S7，补充材料）。 2.3. fCKD病例的招募 符合条件的猫为客户拥有，年龄一年以上，健康状况稳定，并由负责的兽医确认确诊为任何IRIS阶段的CKD。若猫处于妊娠期、哺乳期、饮食不均衡或在入组前七天内住院，则不予纳入。进一步的排除标准包括可能混淆fCKD评估的共病，如急性肾损伤、内分泌疾病（如无法控制的甲状旁腺功能亢进）、特定的先天性或阻塞性肾疾病，以及已怀疑或确诊的恶性肿瘤。
Most cat owners who participated in the study were female (175/208, 84.1%). Both female and male owners exhibited similar mean age (45.7 and 46.4, respectively) and age range (20–78 and 20–74, respectively). Owner demographics, such as sex and age, showed no notable patterns. Detailed demographics are included in the Supplementary Materials (Table S7, Supplementary Materials). 2.3. Enrolment of fCKD Cases Eligible cats were client-owned, aged one year or older, in a stable health condition, and with a diagnosis of CKD confirmed by the responsible veterinarian at any IRIS stage. Cats were excluded if they were pregnant, lactating, on an unbalanced diet, or had been hospitalized within seven days prior to enrollment. Further criteria for exclusion were comorbidities that could confound the assessment of fCKD, such as acute kidney injury, endocrine disorders (e.g., uncontrolled hyperparathyroidism), specific congenital or obstructive renal conditions, and suspected or confirmed malignancy.
最近接受过已知会引起肾脏损伤的寄生虫或传染性疾病（如钩端螺旋体病）治疗的猫，或同时参与涉及任何试验性产品使用的临床试验的猫，也被排除在外（表 S1，补充材料）。鼓励从业者在两个时间点收集血清样本，并在可行时收集尿样，以协助 cCKD 的诊断和 IRIS 分期；血清和尿液样本分别用于生化和尿液分析。前述数据收集表在两个时间点均需填写。
Cats recently treated for parasitic or infectious diseases known to cause kidney damage (e.g., leptospirosis) or concurrently participating in a clinical trial involving the administration of any investigational product were also excluded (Table S1, Supplementary Materials). Practitioners were encouraged to collect serum and, when feasible, urine samples at two Timepoints [Timepoint 1 (Day 0–7 from first consultation) and Timepoint 2 (Day 30–37 from first consultation)] to assist with cCKD diagnosis and IRIS staging; they were submitted for biochemical and urine analysis, respectively. The previously mentioned data collection forms were completed at both Timepoints.
2.4. 实验室分析 样品被送往位于希腊雅典的一家商业兽医实验室，该实验室获得了 ISO 17025 认证 [39] 并通过了 ISO 9001 认证 [40]（Vet in Progress Plus, Attiki, Greece）。血清生化参数使用 SIEMENS Dimension EXL 配备 LM 临床化学分析仪（Siemens Healthineers, Erlangen, Germany）进行分析。电解质浓度使用 JOKOH EX-DS 电解质分析仪（JOKOH Co., Ltd., Kanagawa, Japan）测定。血清分析包括血尿素氮（BUN）、白蛋白（ALB）、总蛋白（TP）、磷（P）、钙（Ca 和 Ca ）、钾（K）、钠（Na）和氯（Cl），以及 Na/K 比值。血肌酐（CREA）使用经验证的酶法测定 [41,42]，实验室对此获得 ISO 17025 认证。尿液中肌酐、尿蛋白以及尿蛋白与肌酐比值（UPC）（UP/C）在尿样中测定。
2.4. Laboratory Analysis The samples were sent to a commercial veterinary laboratory in Athens, Greece, which is accredited under ISO 17025 [39] and certified with ISO 9001 [40] (Vet in Progress Plus, Attiki, Greece). Serum biochemical parameters were analyzed using a SIEMENS Dimension EXL with an LM clinical chemistry analyzer (Siemens Healthineers, Erlangen, Germany). Electrolyte concentrations were measured using a JOKOH EX-DS electrolyte analyzer (JOKOH Co., Ltd., Kanagawa, Japan). Serum analysis included blood urea nitrogen (BUN), albumin (ALB), total proteins (TP), phosphorus (P), calcium (Ca and Ca++), potassium (K), sodium (Na), and chloride (Cl), along with the Na/K ratio. Blood creatinine (CREA) was measured using a validated enzymatic assay [ 41,42], for which the laboratory is accredited under ISO 17025. Urinary creatinine, urinary protein, and the urinary protein-to-creatinine ratio (UP/C) were measured in urine samples.
2.5. CKD 诊断与 IRIS 分期 终末期猫慢性肾病（CKD）（fCKD）的最终诊断是基于生化分析结果、猫的病史、临床病理发现以及负责兽医酌情进行的任何附加诊断测试（如影像学检查）。IRIS 分期由负责的兽医根据 IRIS 指南确定 [43]。血清肌酐浓度是大多数病例中用于分期的主要生物标志物，反映了常规临床实践。当可获得时，SDMA 值会被纳入 IRIS 分期，但由于在整个研究人群中并不一致可得，因此未纳入分析。同样，分期所需的数据，如通过尿蛋白/肌酐比值（UPC）（UP/C）评估蛋白尿，在总体研究人群中也未统一获得。兽医分配的 IRIS 分期被用作在真实世界环境中评估每只猫疾病进展的实际指标。
2.5. CKD Diagnosis and IRIS Staging The final diagnosis of fCKD was based on the results of the biochemical analysis, the cat’s medical history, clinicopathological findings, and any additional diagnostic tests performed (e.g., imaging) at the discretion of the responsible veterinarian. The IRIS stage Vet. Sci. 2026, 13, 192 5 of 21 was determined by the responsible veterinarians according to IRIS guidelines [43]. Serum creatinine concentration was the primary biomarker used for staging in the majority of cases, reflecting routine clinical practice. SDMA values were incorporated into IRIS staging when available, but since they were not consistently available across the entire study population, they were not included in the analysis. Similarly, data required for substaging, such as proteinuria assessment via UP/C, were not uniformly available in the overall study population. The practitioner-assigned IRIS stage was used as a practical indicator of each cat’s disease progression in a real-world setting.
所有共病，包括贫血和高血压的诊断，均由负责的兽医根据其临床评估以及其所在诊所可用的诊断工具作出。没有向参与的兽医提供标准化的诊断方案或具体的定量阈值（例如用于高血压的收缩压（SBP）具体值），因此反映了真实世界中的临床决策过程。 2.6. 统计分析 统计分析使用 IBM SPSS Statistics（版本 29.0.2.0，美国纽约阿蒙克）和 R（版本 2024.04.1）进行。所有统计检验均为双尾检验，p 值 <0.05 被认为具有统计学意义。所有分析仅使用时间点 1 的数据。
The diagnosis of all comorbidities, including anemia and hypertension, was made by the responsible veterinarian based on their clinical assessment and the diagnostic tools available at their individual practice. No standardized diagnostic protocol or specific quantitative thresholds (e.g., a specific value for systolic pressure for hypertension) were provided to the participating veterinarians, thus reflecting the real-world clinical decisionmaking process. 2.6. Statistical Analysis Statistical analyses were conducted using IBM SPSS Statistics (V ersion 29.0.2.0, Armonk, NY , USA) and R (V ersion 2024.04.1). All statistical tests were two-tailed, and ap-value of <0.05 was considered statistically significant. All analyses utilized only data from Timepoint 1.
描述性统计用于总结研究人群的人口学和临床特征。连续变量的正态性使用Kolmogorov–Smirnov或Shapiro–Wilk检验进行评估。合并症根据主要器官系统分类，如表S8（补充材料）所示。分类变量采用卡方检验。非参数比较使用Kruskal-Wallis检验和Mann–Whitney U检验。单因素方差分析后进行事后配对比较，采用带Bonferroni校正的t检验，对正态分布的连续变量进行参数比较时使用t检验。ANOVA结果应用Benjamini–Hochberg校正以控制多重比较。对于统计学显著的结果，使用Dunn检验结合Bonferroni校正的事后检验进行配对比较。
Descriptive statistics were employed to summarize demographic and clinical characteristics of the study population. Normality of continuous variables was assessed using the Kolmogorov–Smirnov or Shapiro–Wilk tests. Comorbidities were classified into categories based on the primary organ system, as detailed in Table S8 (Supplementary Materials). Chi-square tests were applied for categorical variables. Kruskal-Wallis and Mann– Whitney U tests were used for non-parametric comparisons. One-way analysis of variance was followed by post hoc pairwise comparisons using t-tests with a Bonferroni correction, and t-tests were used for parametric comparisons of normally distributed continuous variables. A Benjamini–Hochberg correction was applied to the ANOVA results to control for multiple comparisons. Dunn’s test with a Bonferroni correction post hoc test was used for pairwise comparison when statistically significant results were recognized.
共病被分析为类别变量（存在/不存在）。使用多步骤方法分析fCKD与共病对HRQL评分的关联。首先，使用置换多变量方差分析（PERMANOVA）评估类别变量（如共病的存在）与多维VetMetrica™评分的整体关联。接下来，计算95%置信区间（CI）的比值比（OR），以评估特定共病与生活质量下降的可能性。在此分析中，“下降”的HRQL被定义为在该特定领域内得分低于对照组（无任何共病的猫）的中位数得分。
Comorbidities were analyzed as categorical (present/absent) variables. A multi-step approach was used to analyze the association of fCKD and comorbidities with HRQL scores. First, Permutational Multivariate Analysis of Variance (PERMANOVA) was used to assess the overall association of categorical variables (like presence of comorbidity) with multidimensional VetMetrica™ scores. Next, odds ratios (OR) with 95% confidence intervals (CI) were calculated to evaluate the likelihood of QoL deterioration associated with specific comorbidities. For this analysis, a ‘deteriorated’ HRQL was defined as a score falling below the median score of the control group (cats without any comorbidities) for that specific domain.
最后，进行了多元线性回归分析，以确定各变量（特定合并症）与每个领域HRQL评分恶化之间的独立关系。数据集中少于十只猫的合并症被合并到一个“其他所有”类别中，以确保每个预测变量的样本量充足和模型稳定性。为了评估临床症状（二元变量）的存在与IRIS分期报告的fCKD严重程度之间的相关性（在此分析中被视为有序变量），计算了点二列相关系数（rpb）。仅对至少有20例的临床症状进行了比例检验，以确保检验的有效性。
Finally, a multiple linear regression analysis was conducted to determine the independent relationship between the variables (specific comorbidities) and the deterioration in HRQL scores for each domain. Comorbidities in fewer than ten cats in the dataset were merged into a single “All Other” category to ensure adequate sample size and model stability for each predictor. To evaluate the correlation between the presence of a clinical sign (binary variable) and the severity of fCKD as depicted by the IRIS Stage reported (considered in this analysis as an ordinal variable), the point-biserial correlation coefficient (rpb) was calculated. The equality of proportions of tests was conducted only for clinical signs with at least 20 cases to ensure the validity of the test.

Discussion This study characterized the clinical presentation, HRQL, and the association of comorbidities in a large, real-world population of cats with fCKD, providing novel real-world evidence from a multicenter cohort that links comorbidities with both clinician-assessed clinical features and owner-reported HRQL. Most of the enrolled fCKD cases were in early IRIS stages, as assigned by the practitioners. The principal finding was the significant and independent negative association of comorbidities with HRQL scores across all domains.
虽然我们的结果证实了随着IRIS分期升高HRQL预期下降，但它们显示伴随疾病与较差的生活质量显著相关，强调了全方位诊断和管理fCKD的必要性。我们的研究表明，身体状况恶化，如体重下降和肌肉流失，与IRIS晚期阶段相关，并发现猫咪最困扰其临床症状与主人认知之间存在差异。我们研究中的一个关键发现是贫血与较差HRQL之间存在强烈关联，舒适度、情绪健康和身体健康的恶化比值比超过12.0。这在临床上具有重要意义，因为贫血会直接导致虚弱、疲劳和运输氧能力下降，这将在活力和身体健康领域中有所体现。
While our results confirmed the expected decline in HRQL with advancing IRIS stage, they demonstrated that concurrent diseases are significantly associated with poor QoL, emphasizing the necessity of a holistic approach to the diagnosis and management of fCKD. Our study showed the association of deteriorating physical condition, such as lower body weight and muscle loss, with advanced IRIS stages and identified a discrepancy between the clinical signs considered most troublesome for the cat versus the owner. A critical finding in our study was the strong association between anemia and poor HRQL, with odds ratios for deterioration exceeding 12.0 for Comfort, Emotional Wellbeing, and Physical Wellbeing. This is clinically relevant, as anemia directly leads to weakness, fatigue, and reduced oxygen-carrying capacity, which would be reflected by the Vitality and Physical Wellbeing domains.
因此，我们的结果建立在之前的观察基础上，即贫血通过其整体影响不利地影响慢性肾病（CKD）患者的生活质量[47]，并直接促成晚期CKD猫的尿毒症综合征的临床表现[48]。鉴于在CKD背景下贫血的复杂性，这些发现尤为重要。CKD相关贫血的病理生理是多因素的，包括红细胞生成减少、红细胞寿命缩短以及红细胞丢失增加[47]。虽然已知随着IRIS阶段的进展，由于肾组织和促红细胞生成素（EPO）的逐渐丧失，贫血会加重[2,49]，但其与生存时间的直接关系尚未确定，并且各研究结果存在冲突[12,50]。通过展示贫血与生活质量的负相关，我们的发现将猫CKD中贫血的管理提升为主要治疗目标。
Our results, therefore, build on previous observations that anemia adversely affects the quality of life of CKD patients through its overall effects [47] and contributes directly to the clinical expression of uremic syndrome in cats with advanced CKD [48]. These findings are particularly impactful given the complex nature of anemia in the context of CKD. The pathophysiology of CKD-associated anemia is multifactorial, involving reduced erythropoiesis, shortened erythrocyte lifespan, and increased erythrocyte loss [47]. While anemia is known to intensify with advancing IRIS stage due to progressive loss of renal tissue and erythropoietin production [2,49], its direct relationship with survival time is not established, with conflicting evidence across studies [ 12,50]. By demonstrating a negative association with QoL, our findings elevate the management of anemia in feline CKD to a primary therapeutic target.

Our study supports these findings as the strong association of oral/dental disease with lower Comfort (OR = 5.16) and Emotional Wellbeing (OR = 7.10) scores indicates that the clinical burden is associated with chronic pain and distress, which directly impairs the cat’s QoL. In our study, the presence of infectious diseases was a significant factor in patient wellbeing, showing a positive correlation with deterioration across all four HRQL domains. Infectious diseases were associated with a 9.34-point decline in Emotional Wellbeing and a 7.77-point decline in Comfort. This positions the negative association of infectious disease as intermediate between the more severe association with anemia (e.g., an 18.79-point drop in Emotional Wellbeing) and the moderate effects of oral comorbidities (e.g., a 7.25-point drop in Emotional Wellbeing).
我们研究人群中记录的特定病毒性疾病包括猫免疫缺陷病毒（FIV）占6.73%，猫传染性腹膜炎（FIP）占2.40%，猫白血病病毒（FeLV）占1.92%。病理学关联在FIP中最为明显，已知其可引起肉芽肿性肾炎 [55]。虽然FeLV主要与肿瘤性疾病相关，但也有人提出其可能与慢性肾病（CKD）有关联 [1,55]。FIV的作用更具争议；然而，它已被发现可能诱发肾脏损伤，从而合理地导致我们观察到的人类生活质量（HRQL）下降 [15,56]。因此，管理这些感染对于生活质量至关重要，这一发现与现有逆转录病毒疾病指南一致，指南建议处理合并症以改善患者预后 [57]。
The specific viral diseases recorded in our study population comprised FIV in 6.73%, FIP in 2.40%, and FeLV in 1.92% of cats. The pathological link is clearest with FIP , which is known to cause granulomatous nephritis [55]. While FeLV is primarily associated with neoplastic processes, a potential link to CKD has also been suggested [ 1,55]. The role of FIV is more controversial; however, it has been linked to the induction of renal lesions that could plausibly contribute to the decline in HRQL we observed [ 15,56]. Therefore, managing these infections is critical for quality of life, and this finding aligns with existing guidelines for retroviral diseases, which recommend addressing comorbidities to improve patient outcomes [57].
系统性负担在患者的身体状况中有明显体现。本研究发现猫体成分与兽医分配的IRIS分期之间存在关系，随着疾病进展，BCS和MCS显示出下降迹象。这一发现验证了这些简单的临床评估可作为疾病严重程度和猫整体健康状况的指标。这一模式反映了fCKD对体成分的多因素影响。受影响的猫体重减轻通常是逐渐的，并可能在临床诊断前数月就开始[1,8,13,22,58]。其原因包括食欲减退、恶心、呕吐和腹泻——晚期常见症状——以及与慢性炎症相关的代谢变化和蛋白质或热量摄入减少[59]。随着时间的推移，这些机制会导致整体性体重减轻和选择性肌肉消耗，表现为肌肉减少症或恶病质，具体取决于潜在原因[9,58,60,61]。
The systemic burden is visibly reflected in the patient’s physical condition. The present study identified a relationship between feline body composition and practitioner-assigned IRIS stage, with BCS and MCS showing signs of decline as the disease progressed. This finding validates these simple, in-clinic assessments as indicators of disease severity and the cat’s overall health status. This pattern reflects the multifactorial impact of fCKD on body composition. Weight loss in affected cats is often gradual and may begin months before clinical diagnosis [1,8,13,22,58]. It is influenced by reduced appetite, nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea—common signs in later stages—as well as by metabolic alterations associated with chronic inflammation and decreased protein or caloric intake [59]. Over time, these mechanisms contribute to both generalized weight loss and selective muscle wasting, manifesting as sarcopenia or cachexia, depending on the underlying cause [9,58,60,61].
鉴于这些相互关联的机制，仅凭体重无法完整反映慢性肾病（CKD）猫的营养和临床状况。结合体况评分（BCS）和肌肉评分（MCS）的评估，可以更准确地评估疾病的影响和进展。这种综合方法越来越受到主要兽医组织的重视[18,44,58,62]，它们建议每次临床就诊不仅要测量体重，还要评估身体和肌肉状况。定期监测这些参数能够更早发现营养状况的恶化[18,58]，并有助于及时干预，以保持瘦体重并改善CKD猫的生活质量。我们的研究提供了定量的兽医科学证据，支持这种综合评估方法。由医生指定的IRIS分期显示的疾病进展显著与身体状况（BCS和MCS）的下降以及身体健康和活力领域的HRQL评分下降相关，表明身体状况恶化对患者日常生活的系统性影响。
Given these interrelated mechanisms, weight alone provides an incomplete picture of nutritional and clinical status in cats with CKD. Incorporating BCS and MCS evaluation allows for a more accurate assessment of disease impact and progression. This combined approach is increasingly emphasized by major veterinary organizations [18,44,58,62], which recommend that each clinical visit include not only weight measurement but also assessment of body and muscle condition. Regular monitoring of these parameters enables earlier detection of nutritional deterioration [18,58] and facilitates timely intervention to preserve lean mass and improve quality of life in cats with CKD. Our study provides quantitative Vet. Sci. 2026, 13, 192 14 of 21 HRQL evidence to support this combined assessment approach. Disease progression as indicated by the practitioner-assigned IRIS stages was significantly associated with a decline in physical condition (BCS and MCS) as well as lower HRQL scores in the Physical Wellbeing and Vitality domains, indicating the systemic impact of the deterioration of physical condition on the patient’s daily life.
本研究进一步记录了几个临床症状与疾病严重程度呈正相关，包括食欲丧失或下降、虚弱或疲劳、情绪低落、毛发状况差以及饮水量增加。排尿增多以及体重或肌肉流失也与慢性肾病（CKD）各阶段的进展显著相关，反映了肾功能障碍的渐进性系统性影响。这些发现与此前报道一致，文献中描述多尿、多饮、厌食和体重下降是猫慢性肾病（CKD）最早且最常见的临床表现之一[3,8,13,22,58]。总体而言，这些症状在CKD各阶段频率和严重程度的逐渐增加，凸显了它们的诊断和预后意义。早期识别食欲、行为或饮水量的微妙变化，可促进及时干预、饮食调整，并改善患病猫的生活质量。
This study further documented that several clinical signs showed a positive correlation with disease severity, including loss or decrease in appetite, weakness or fatigue, depressed mood, poor coat appearance, and increased water consumption. Increased urination and weight or muscle loss were also significantly associated with advancing CKD stages, reflecting the progressive systemic impact of renal dysfunction. These findings are consistent with previous reports describing polyuria, polydipsia, anorexia and weight loss as among the earliest and most frequent clinical manifestations of feline CKD [3,8,13,22,58]. Overall, the gradual increase in both the frequency and severity of these signs across CKD stages highlights their diagnostic and prognostic significance. Early recognition of subtle changes in appetite, behavior, or water intake may facilitate timely intervention, dietary modification, and improved quality-of-life outcomes for affected cats.
除了具体症状的临床流行情况外，本研究还探讨了宠物主人如何看待这些表现对猫咪及其自身的影响。对于猫咪而言，宠物主人的回答反映了先前已确定与疾病严重程度相关的关键临床表现。对于主人自身而言，这些回答反映了日常互动中症状的美学或感官影响。主人们的认知与近期研究结果一致，即患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫咪在生活质量方面显著下降，尤其是在舒适度、活力和情绪健康相关的维度。此外，这些观察结果与近期研究强调主人认知和情绪负担在慢性猫病管理中的重要性一致 [23,35,63]。
In addition to the clinical prevalence of specific signs, the present study explored how these manifestations were perceived by owners in terms of their impact on both the cat and themselves. For the cat, the pet owners’ answers reflect the key clinical manifestations previously identified as correlating with disease severity. For themselves, the answers reflect their aesthetic or sensory implications in daily interaction. The owners’ perceptions are consistent with recent findings that cats with CKD experience a marked reduction in HRQL, particularly in dimensions related to comfort, vitality, and emotional wellbeing. Moreover, these observations align with recent findings emphasizing the relevance of owner perception and emotional burden in the management of chronic feline diseases [23,35,63].
照顾者的压力和挫败感可能会影响遵医行为和护理质量，这突显了支持猫咪福利和主人福祉的沟通策略的重要性 [23,64]。我们的研究结果显示，主人可能无法识别最严重的福利不良指标。这表明兽医在教育主人时的角色十分重要，应将注意力从“美容”迹象转向关键的幸福指标，如食欲、互动和活动水平。我们的研究人群以早期fCKD为主。在时间点1，64.9%的病例被执业医师评定为IRIS 1期和2期。这一分布与基于人群的或筛查研究一致 [7]，并表明日益增长的认知和改进的诊断手段可能导致更早的发现，这对疾病预后和猫咪的生活质量均有益处 [16]。
Caregiver stress and frustration may influence adherence and care quality, highlighting the importance of communication strategies that support both the cat’s welfare and the owner’s wellbeing [ 23,64]. Our findings show that owners may not recognize the most severe indicators of poor welfare. This suggests the veterinarian’s role in educating owners to shift their focus from ‘cosmetic’ signs to the crucial indicators of wellbeing, such as appetite, interaction, and activity levels. Our study population was characterized by a predominance of early stage fCKD. At Timepoint 1, 64.9% of the cases were practitioner-assigned as IRIS Stage 1 and 2. This distribution is consistent with population-based or screening studies [7], and suggests that growing awareness and improved diagnostics may result in earlier detection, which is beneficial for disease prognosis and the cats’ QoL [16].
在对177例在时间点1和时间点2均收集到数据的病例中，观察到临床医生分配的IRIS分期随时间变化的情况占28.8%。总体而言，11.3%的病例升级到更高的分期，而17.51%的病例降至较低的分期。疾病进展中预期会出现分期升高，这可能归因于临床状况的突然恶化。然而，分期降低可能归因于前肾性氮质血症（例如，由脱水或尿路感染引起）得到成功管理、急性慢性肾病（CKD）事件后患者状况稳定[1]，甚至肌酐本身的生物学变异[65]。
Changes in practitioner-assigned IRIS stages classification over time were observed in 28.8% of the 177 cases with data collected at both Timepoints 1 and 2. Overall, 11.3% of the cases moved to a higher stage, while 17.51% shifted to a lower stage. Advancement to a higher stage is expected in a progressive disease, and it can be attributed to a sudden worsening of the clinical condition. A shift to a lower stage, however, may be attributed to the successful management of pre-renal azotemia (e.g., from dehydration or a UTI), the stabilization of a patient after an acute-on-chronic kidney disease event [ 1] or even the inherent biological variability of creatinine [65].
我们研究中fCKD病例的中位年龄为10岁。尽管fCKD传统上被认为是老年猫的疾病[2]，但我们发现相当比例的病例包括中年动物（7–10岁）[66,67]，这也可能反映了早期检测的这一趋势。此外，这可能表明fCKD的多因素性，不仅仅是由于肾单位逐渐丧失和肾小管间质纤维化导致的年龄相关肾功能下降[1]。这与一些报道平均年龄较高的研究形成对比[7,68]，并支持这样的观点，即年龄本身并不是疾病发病或严重程度的唯一决定因素。与文献一致，我们未发现性别或品种的相关性，这表明fCKD在猫群体中是一种普遍存在的疾病[17]。
The median age of fCKD cases in our study was 10 years old. Although fCKD is traditionally considered a disease of geriatric cats [2], our finding that a substantial proportion of cases included middle-aged animals (7–10 years) [66,67] may also reflect this trend of earlier detection. Moreover, it may be suggestive of the multifactorial nature of fCKD beyond age-related renal decline due to progressive nephron loss and tubulointerstitial fibrosis [1]. This contrasts with some studies reporting higher mean ages [7,68] and supports the view Vet. Sci. 2026, 13, 192 15 of 21 that age alone is not the sole determinant of disease onset or severity. Consistent with the literature, we found no association with sex or breed, suggesting that fCKD is a widespread condition in the cat population [17].
关于对患病猫实施的饮食的研究结果强调，其中相当大一部分猫并未严格采用处方肾脏饮食。此外，罐头食品和小包装食品的广泛消费表明，水分摄入问题和食物适口性可能会影响饮食选择，这一因素在慢性肾病（CKD）中尤为关键，因为存在食物厌恶的风险 [18,68]。尽管有充分的证据表明其益处，但长期遵循肾脏饮食仍然是一个重大的临床挑战，常常因食欲下降、食物厌恶以及主人依从性困难而受到限制 [18,68]。本研究存在若干与其真实世界数据设计相关的重要局限性。
The findings regarding the diet administered to the diseased cats emphasize that a substantial portion of them were not managed strictly with a prescription renal diet. Furthermore, the wide consumption of canned food and pouches indicates that hydration concerns and palatability may influence dietary choices, a critical factor given the risk of food aversion in CKD [ 18,68]. Despite strong evidence of benefit, long-term adherence to renal diets remains a major clinical challenge, often limited by reduced appetite, food aversion, and difficulties in owner compliance [18,68]. This study has several important limitations inherent to its real-world data design.
一个主要的限制与临床分期的完整性有关。IRIS 分期和伴随疾病的诊断是由 42 位不同的医生进行的，这可能导致临床评估和 IRIS 分期中存在观察者间的差异。这可能促成了所观察到的分期迁移。分期向较低阶段的转移很可能不仅反映了临床稳定性，还反映了现实环境中基于医生分期的差异。因此，所报告的 IRIS 分期应被理解为“医生指定的临床分期”，而非标准化分类。未来采用集中病例审查的研究可以对这些变量进行标准化。
A primary limitation relates to the integrity of clinical staging. IRIS staging and comorbidity diagnosis were performed by 42 different practitioners, introducing the potential for interobserver variability in clinical assessment and IRIS staging. This may have contributed to the observed stage migration. The stage shift to lower stages likely reflects not only clinical stabilization but also the variability of practitioner-based staging in a real-world setting. Consequently, the IRIS stages reported should be interpreted as “practitionerassigned clinical stages” rather than standardized classifications. Future studies employing a centralized review of cases could standardize these variables.
这一情况因整个研究人群中缺乏SDMA和UP/C数据而进一步加剧。SDMA数据缺乏统一可用，限制了在这项真实世界研究中全面评估其对IRIS分期的贡献。显然，UP/C数据的统一缺失使得无法进行亚分期，并可能导致一些1期猫被错误分类。财务限制导致无法评估完整的肾脏生物标志物面板，以及猫咪尿液采集的挑战，反映了临床环境中的实际困难。然而，未来的研究应包括SDMA等生物标志物以及用于猫咪尿液采集的专业工具。
This is further compounded by the lack of SDMA and UP/C data in the overall study population. The lack of uniformly available SDMA data limited the ability to fully assess its contribution to IRIS staging in this real-world study. Seemingly, the lack of uniformly available UP/C data did not allow sub-staging and potentially led to the misclassification of some Stage 1 cats. Financial restrictions that do not allow for assessing a complete panel of renal biomarkers and the challenge of urine collection in cats reflect practical difficulties in a clinical setting. However, future studies should include biomarkers such as SDMA and specialized tools for urine collection in cats.
另一个限制是记录的共病缺乏标准化的诊断标准。像贫血和高血压这样的情况，都是由临床医生报告为存在或不存在，但没有提供相应的定量数据，例如血细胞比容（HCT）值或具体的血压读数。这导致无法分析这些疾病严重程度与生活质量（HRQL）之间的关联。例如，虽然我们的数据显示贫血的存在与生活质量呈强负相关，但我们无法区分轻度与重度贫血的影响。同样，传染性疾病如FIV和FeLV的诊断也是在未提供具体检测方法或感染临床状态数据的情况下记录的。这限制了对特定病原体或疾病状态如何影响生活质量的更详细解读。虽然受影响的猫数量较少，并且在回归分析中被归入更广泛的‘传染性疾病’类别以减少这种变异性的统计影响，但我们承认缺乏这些细节是一个限制因素。
Another limitation is the lack of standardized diagnostic criteria for the recorded comorbidities. Conditions like anemia and hypertension were reported as present or absent by the practitioners without accompanying quantitative data, such as hematocrit values or specific blood pressure readings. This did not allow the analysis of the association of the severity of these conditions with HRQL. For instance, while our data shows a strong negative association between the presence of anemia and QoL, we cannot differentiate between the impact of mild versus severe anemia. Similarly, diagnoses of infectious diseases such as FIV and FeLV were recorded without accompanying data on the specific testing method or the clinical status of the infection. This limits a more detailed interpretation of how specific pathogens or disease statuses affect HRQL. While the small number of affected cats and their aggregation into a broader ‘Infectious diseases’ category for regression analysis minimizes the statistical impact of this variability, we acknowledge that this lack of detail is a limitation.
未来的研究需要采用标准化的诊断方案，以澄清这些具体关系。本研究的另一个局限性是可能存在与42位不同的执业者和诊所提供数据相关的聚类效应。研究人群包括来自普通和转诊执业者的病例。预计不同的诊所会有不同的诊疗方案或对诊断的重视程度，例如进行牙科检查或验血的频率。这种差异可能影响某些共病的检测率，并引入对健康相关生活质量(HRQL)观察关联的偏差。
Future research employing standardized diagnostic protocols would be necessary to clarify these specific relationships. An additional limitation of this study is the potential for cluster effects related to the 42 different practitioners and clinics contributing data. The study population included cases from both general and referral practitioners. It is expected that different clinics have different protocols or levels of emphasis on diagnostics, such as the frequency of performing dental exams or blood work. This variability could influence the detection rates of certain comorbidities and introduce bias into the observed associations with HRQL.
因此，在解读这些发现时应认识到，观察到的一些差异可能归因于不同诊所之间的差异。虽然我们承认诊所层面的聚类效应可能会影响检测率，但42名执业者之间病例分布不均——其中许多人仅贡献1–2例病例——使得无法使用多层次建模。这种方法可能会导致模型不稳定并损害所报告的比值比的有效性。此外，我们研究的概括性受限于抽样策略。该研究使用了便利样本的执业者，可能无法完全代表希腊所有的兽医诊所。AOH网络内的兽医可能代表了更积极或更主动的群体，这可能导致早期诊断比例较高。因此，我们的研究结果适用于该特定群体的条件和实践，在推广到其他国家或医疗系统时应谨慎。
Consequently, the findings should be interpreted with the acknowledgment that some of the observed variation may be attributable to differences between clinics. While we acknowledge that clinic-level cluster effects could influence detection rates, the uneven Vet. Sci. 2026, 13, 192 16 of 21 distribution of cases across 42 practitioners—many contributing only 1–2 cases—precluded the use of multi-level modeling. Such an approach would have risked model instability and compromised the validity of the reported odds ratios. Additionally, the generalizability of our findings is limited by the sampling strategy. The study utilized a convenience sample of practitioners, which may not be fully representative of all veterinary clinics in Greece. Veterinarians within the AOH network may represent a more engaged or proactive group, which could contribute to the high proportion of early-stage diagnoses. As such, our findings apply to the specific conditions and practices of this cohort, and caution should be taken when generalizing to other countries or healthcare systems.
最后，我们选择分析尽可能大样本量的数据，而没有对177只在两个时间点有数据的猫进行纵向分析。因此，我们研究的横断面性质使我们能够将对共病和生活质量（QoL）的发现解释为关联，而非预测疾病进展的因素。未来对该队列进行纵向分析是必要的，以调查这里识别出的高度相关的共病是否能预测更快速的疾病分期进展或生活质量（HRQL）评分的更快下降。 5. 结论 总之，本研究表明，虽然慢性肾病（CKD）（fCKD）的严重程度是影响生活质量（HRQL）的关键因素，但共病的管理对猫的福祉同样至关重要。我们的真实世界数据表明，患有fCKD的猫不仅仅是“肾病患者”，它们是复杂的个体，其生活质量受共病状况及IRIS分期的影响，我们的数据表明这些关联是独立于疾病分期的。
Finally, we chose to analyze data from the largest possible sample size and not perform a longitudinal analysis on the 177 cats with data from two timepoints. Thus, the cross- sectional nature of our study allows us to interpret our findings on comorbidities and QoL as associations, not as predictors of progression. Future longitudinal analysis of this cohort is warranted to investigate if the highly associated comorbidities identified here are predictive of more rapid stage progression or a faster decline in HRQL scores over time. 5. Conclusions In conclusion, this study demonstrates that while fCKD severity is a key determinant of HRQL, the management of comorbidities is equally critical to a cat’s welfare. Our real-world data demonstrates that a cat with fCKD is not only a ‘kidney patient,’ but a complex case whose QoL is led by their comorbidities and their IRIS stage as well, with our data showing these associations are independent of disease stage.
这些发现对常规临床实践具有直接的临床意义，强调了采用超越单纯肾功能的整体管理方法的重要性。基于这些发现，我们建议临床医生：（1）在常规管理中整合正式的健康相关生活质量（HRQL）评估，以记录患者的生活质量，（2）筛查并治疗高度相关的合并症，特别是口腔/牙科疾病和贫血，以及（3）教育宠物主人监测最具临床相关性的福利下降迹象，例如食欲和活动量的变化。这样的做法可能有助于更有针对性的临床决策，并改善患有慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）（fCKD）猫的福利结果。未来需要进行纵向研究，以确定针对这些因素的针对性干预是否能随着时间推移改善生活质量评分。
These findings have direct clinical implications for routine practice, emphasizing the importance of a holistic management approach that extends beyond renal function alone. Based on these findings, we recommend that clinicians: (1) integrate formal HRQL assessments into routine management to record the patient’s QoL, (2) screen for and treat highly associated comorbidities, particularly oral/dental disease and anemia, and (3) educate owners to monitor for the most clinically relevant signs of welfare decline, such as changes in appetite and activity. Such an approach may support more targeted clinical decision-making and improved welfare outcomes in cats with fCKD. Future longitudinal research is warranted to determine if targeted interventions against these factors can improve HRQL scores over time.
