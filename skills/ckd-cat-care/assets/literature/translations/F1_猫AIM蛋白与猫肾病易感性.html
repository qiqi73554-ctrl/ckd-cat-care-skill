Renal failure is one of the most important social problems for its incurability and high costs for patients’ health care. Through clarification of the underlying mechanism for the high susceptibility of cats to renal disease, we here demonstrates that the effective dissociation of serum AIM protein from IgM is necessary for the recovery from acute kidney injury (AKI). In cats, the AIM-IgM binding affinity is 1000-fold higher than that in mice, which is caused by the unique positively-charged amino-acid cluster present in feline AIM. Hence, feline AIM does not dissociate from IgM during AKI, abolishing its translocation into urine. This results in inefficient clearance of lumen-obstructing necrotic cell debris at proximal tubules, thereby impairing AKI recovery. Accordingly, mice whose AIM is replaced by feline AIM exhibit higher mortality by AKI than in wild-type mice. Recombinant AIM administration into the mice improves their renal function and survival.

在促进急性肾损伤（AKI）修复中，AIM最显著的功能是增强近端小管死细胞碎片的清除。在AKI过程中，肾脏的细胞死亡是由于凋亡和程序性坏死，尤其发生在皮质髓质交界处的近端小管中，这些死细胞会从小管基底膜脱落并物理性阻塞小管腔。这些事件会降低肾小球滤过率（GFR），同时也会促使受损的上皮细胞和浸润的造血细胞产生炎症介质，进一步加剧小管损伤并扰乱小管退行性变化。KIM-1在上皮细胞受损时表达显著增加，因此是一个广为人知的损伤标志物，它是AIM的配体，并诱导小管上皮细胞吞噬AIM沉积的坏死细胞碎片。
The most notable AIM function in facilitating AKI repair is the enhancement of clearance of dead cell debris in the proximal tubules. During AKI, the cell death in the kidney occurs due to apoptosis and necroptosis, particularly in the proximal tubules at the corticomedullary junction, and such dead cells detach from the tubular basement membrane and physically obstruct the tubular lumen. These events reduce glomerular filtration and also induce the production of inflammatory mediators by injured epithelial and infiltrating hematopoietic cells, further exacerbating tubular injury and disturbing the tubular degeneration27–29. KIM-1, the expression of which is highly induced in tubular epithelial cells upon injury and is thus a well-known injury marker 23–25, is a ligand for AIM, and induces the engulfment of AIM-deposited necrotic cell debris by tubular epithelial cells22,30.

We injected mouse rAIM intravenously into AIM felinised mice on days 1, 2 and 3 after IR. The survival of rAIM-injected mice was improved markedly to 80% (Fig. 4a). The serum Cre level in rAIM-injected mice decreased by day 5, whereas in non-treated AIM felinised mice, it continued to increase until the mice died (Fig. 4b). By histology, the ATN score of rAIM-injected AIM felinised mice was improved at day 5 (Fig. 4c). We also confirmed by immunohistochemistry that injected rAIM accumulated at intraluminal debris (Fig. 4d). Thus, rAIM administration ameliorated AKI in AIM felinised mice, implying its therapeutic potential in cats.
在当前研究中获得的各种重要发现中，表明猫AIM的独特特性与猫AKI恢复受损之间可能存在联系的证据尤其值得注意（总结见补充图4）。这一发现强烈支持AKI期间AIM的有效游离对于AKI的恢复是必要的。猫AIM在AKI期间未从IgM五聚体中释放，因此未出现在尿液中。有人可能会认为，负责AIM从IgM游离的机制（目前仍未知）可能在猫身上存在缺陷。然而，这种可能性不大，因为如AIM猫化小鼠所示，猫AIM在AKI的小鼠体内也未从IgM游离，而在这些小鼠中，释放机制必定是活跃的。当然，我们不能排除这样一种可能性：猫AIM不仅可能对IgM表现出更高的结合亲和力，还可能对释放机制不敏感。
Of the various important findings obtained in the current study, the evidence suggesting the linkage between the unique characteristics of feline AIM and the impaired recovery from AKI in cats may be especially noteworthy (Summarized in Supplementary Fig. 4). This finding strongly supports that the effective dissociation of AIM during AKI is necessary for recovery from AKI. Feline AIM was not released from IgM pentamers during AKI, and therefore, did not appear in urine. One might argue that the mechanism responsible for AIM dissociation from IgM, which remains unknown, might be defective in cats. This is not likely, however, because feline AIM did not dissociate from IgM in mice with AKI as shown in AIM felinised mice, where the releasing mechanism must be active. Certainly, however, we cannot exclude a possibility that feline AIM might exhibit not only increased binding affinity to IgM, but also unresponsiveness to the releasing mechanism.
如果是这样，确定猫AIM的这两种特性是否由猫SRCR3结构域引起，特别是由独特的带正电氨基酸簇引起，将会很有趣。未来对释放机制的澄清将回答这个问题。尽管从AKI过渡到CKD仍存在争议34–37，但AKI无疑会使患者容易发展为CKD38–43。因此，由猫AIM导致的AKI恢复不足必然是猫对CKD高度易感的重要风险因素。为了支持这一假设，我们在先前的研究中观察到，在轻度缺血再灌注后，AIM−小鼠（其在AKI期间的肾脏表型本质上与AIM猫化小鼠相同）表现出长期持续的炎症和进行性纤维化，以及肾脏中更高的KIM-1 mRNA水平，相较于野生型小鼠22。
If so, it will be interesting to deter- mine whether the both characteristics of feline AIM are brought about by feline SRCR3 domain, particularly by the unique cluster of positively charged amino acids. Future clarification of the releasing mechanism will answer this question. Although the transition from AKI to CKD is still controversial34–37, AKI certainly predisposes patients to CKD development38–43. Thus, inadequate recovery of AKI due to feline AIM must be an important risk factor for the high susceptibility of cats to CKD. In support of this hypothesis, we observed in a previous study that after mild IR, AIM− mice (whose kidney phenotype during AKI is essentially the same with that in AIM felinised mice) showed chronically persisting inflammation and progressive fibrosis, as well as higher KIM-1 mRNA levels in the kidney compared to wild-type mice 22.
值得注意的是，引起猫CKD的许多原发疾病具有诱发AKI事件的特性，如肾结石、感染性肾炎和腹膜炎，以及暴露于包括某些抗生素和抗炎试剂在内的肾毒素44–46。在这方面，在此类急性事件期间使用AIM治疗可能成为预防猫慢性肾病（CKD）的潜在疗法。当然，猫的慢性肾病（CKD）有一部分可能在没有急性肾损伤既往病史的情况下发生。例如，肥胖及与肥胖相关的疾病包括糖尿病也是慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）的危险因素，实际上，猫的肥胖率正在迅速上升 47–49。AIM 还具有抗肥胖作用：无 IgM 的 AIM 被纳入脂肪细胞，并通过减少细胞内脂肪酸合成酶的酶活性来诱导脂解 14,17。的确，在高脂饮食喂养的 AIM− 小鼠中，脂肪细胞肥大发生得更为显著，内脏脂肪组织的质量更大，导致其肥胖程度超过野生型小鼠14。因此，猫AIM由于其从IgM释放效率较低，可能也会使猫容易肥胖，从而促进慢性肾病（CKD）的发生。
Of note, many primary diseases that cause cat CKD have the properties of AKI-causing incidents such as renal stones, infectious nephritis and peritonitis, and exposure to renal toxins including some antibiotics and anti-inflammatory reagents44–46. In this aspect, AIM administration during such acute events would be a potential therapy to prevent CKD in cats. Certainly, however, a proportion of cat CKD may occur without a preceding history of AKI. For instance, obesity and obesity-associated diseases including diabetes are also risk factors for CKD, and in fact, the prevalence of obesity is increasing rapidly in cats 47–49. AIM also possesses an anti-obesity effect: IgM-free AIM is incorporated into adipocytes and induces lipolysis by reducing the enzymatic activity of fatty acid synthase within the cells 14,17. Indeed, in AIM− mice fed a high-fat diet, hypertrophy of adipocytes occurs more abundantly and the mass of visceral adipose tissues is greater, leading to more advanced obesity than in wild-type mice14. Thus, it is possible that feline AIM, which is characterised by its less efficient release from IgM, may also predispose cats to obesity, thereby contributing to CKD development.
在这方面，AIM 的给药也可能通过抑制肥胖的进展间接对 CKD 的预防有效。当然，评估 AIM 给药是否能直接有效改善 AKI 非依赖性 CKD 也是值得的。总之，除了我们先前在 AKI 小鼠中进行的研究外，我们目前的发现可能进一步证实 AIM 及其从 IgM 中有效释放对肾脏疾病保护的影响，因此支持其未来在人体中的治疗可用性。此外，我们的研究还为开发针对猫肾衰竭的下一代疗法提供了基础，这可能有助于支持它们的健康和延长寿命。
In this regard, AIM administration might also be effective for CKD prevention indirectly by suppressing the progression of obesity. Certainly, it is worthwhile to assess whether AIM administration would be directly effective for the amelioration of AKI-independent CKD. In conclusion, in addition to our previous study in AKI mice, our current findings may further corroborate the impact of AIM and its efficient release from IgM on protection from kidney disease, and thus, support its future therapeutic availability in humans. Moreover, our study provides a basis for the development of a next-generation therapy against renal failure in cats that might support their health and longevity.

Homology modeling. Homology models of AIM SRCR3 domains were generated using the Swiss-Model server (http://swissmodel.expasy.org/SWISS-MODEL)50,51. The SWISS-MODEL template library (SMTL version 2016-04-06, PDB release 2016-04-01) was searched with Blast52 and HHBlits53 for evolutionary related structures matching the target sequences. The template with the highest quality (5a2e.1.A; PDB ID: 5A2E) was selected for model building.
模型是基于目标-模板对齐使用 Promod-II 构建的。目标和模板之间保守的坐标从模板复制到模型中。插入和缺失部分使用片段库重新建模。然后重建侧链。最后，使用力场使生成的模型几何结构规则化。当使用 ProMod-II 进行环建模未获得满意结果时，使用 MODELLER 构建了替代模型。使用 QMEAN 打分函数评估整体和每个残基的模型质量。获得的 QMEAN4 分数分别为：猫模型 −1.95，小鼠模型 −2.36，人类模型 −2.34，猫突变模型 −2.36。分子图像使用 UCSF Chimera 软件包生成，该软件包来自加利福尼亚大学旧金山分校计算机图形实验室（由 NIH P41 RR-01081 资助）。
Models were built based on target-template alignment using Promod-II 54. Coordinates which were conserved between the target and template were copied from the template to the model. Insertions and deletions were remodelled using a fragment library. Side chains were then rebuilt. Finally, the geometry of the resulting model was regularised by using a force field. When loop modelling with ProMod-II did not give a satisfactory result, an alternative model was built with MODELLER55. The global and per-residue model quality was assessed using the QMEAN scoring function56. The obtained QMEAN4 scores were − 1.95, − 2.36, − 2.34, and − 2.36 for feline, mouse, human, and mutant feline models, respectively. Induction of AKI in mice. IR induction was performed as described previously22,58. Briefly, mice (male, 8–12 weeks old) were anaesthetised by intraperitoneal Avertin injection (250 μ g/kg bodyweight), and both kidneys were exposed through a small flank incision. Both renal arteries and veins were occluded with clamps at 37 °C. After the ischaemic period, the clamps were released to induce blood reperfusion. Briefly, the cats were anaesthetised by subcutaneous injection of atropine (0.05 mg/kg body- weight) and intravenous injection of propofol (10 mg/kg bodyweight; Mylan Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., Tokyo) followed by inhalation of isoflurane during the operation.

Ampicillin (20 mg/kg bodyweight) was administered intravenously before and after the operation.
