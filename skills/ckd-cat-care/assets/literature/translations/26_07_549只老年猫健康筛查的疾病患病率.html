Study population and procedures
猫的年龄为≥8岁，并为例行健康检查而就诊，如果有需要长期监测或管理的既往诊断，则被排除。在筛查期间，进行了临床病史采集和体格检查（补充附录A）。牙科评分使用标准化的主观个体评分系统对牙结石和牙龈炎进行评定：0 = 无，1 = 极少，2 = 轻度，3 = 中度，4 = 重度。采集血样进行生化检查（IDEXX 实验室，英国韦瑟比）、血细胞比容（HCT）（PCV）和血浆总蛋白浓度检测。如在过去12个月内未评估过血浆总甲状腺素（TT4）浓度，或临床怀疑甲亢，则测定血浆总甲状腺素（TT4）浓度（西门子 IMMULITE TT4 检测，西门子医疗健康公司，英国北威尔士格温内思兰贝里斯）。
Cats were ≥ 8 years old and presented for routine health screening, and were excluded if they had prior diagnoses requiring long-term monitoring or management. During screening, a clinical history was taken and a physical examination was performed (Supplementary Appendix A). Dental scoring was performed using a standardized subjective individual grading system for tartar and gingivitis: 0 = none, 1 = minimal, 2 = mild, 3 = moderate, and 4 = severe. Blood samples were taken for biochemistry (IDEXX Laboratories, Wetherby, UK), PCV, and plasma total protein concentrations. Plasma total thyroxine (TT4) concentration (Siemens IMMULITE TT4 assay, Siemens Healthineers, Llanberis, Gwynedd, North Wales, UK) was measured if not evaluated in the previous 12 months or if there was clinical suspicion of hyperthyroidism.
如果 TT4 浓度在参考区间 (RI) 3.1-4.3 μg/dL (40-55 nmol/L) 的上部，临床医生建议根据临床怀疑重复测量（通常在 8 周后）或立即测量血浆促甲状腺激素，使用犬类测定法（cTSH；西门子 IMMULITE 犬促甲状腺激素测定法，Siemens Healthineers，Llanberis，Gwynedd，北威尔士，英国）。收缩压 (SBP) 使用多普勒法测量（补充附录 A；多普勒血流探测器型号 811-B，Parks Medical Electronics，Aloha，俄勒冈，美国）。如果 SBP ≥ 160 mmHg，则进行视网膜检查（ClearView 1 或 ClearView 2，Optibrand，科林斯堡，科罗拉多，美国）以评估高血压性脉络膜视网膜病变。如果存在，则开具降压药。如果不存在，建议猫咪返回进行重复 SBP 测量以评估高血压的持续性。收集尿液进行尿液分析，如存在显微镜下菌尿则进行细菌培养。
If the TT4 concentration was in the upper portion of the reference interval (RI) of 3.1-4.3 μg/dL (40-55 nmol/L), clinicians advised repeated measurement (typically after 8 weeks) or immediate measurement of plasma thyroid-stimulating hormone using the canine assay (cTSH; Siemens IMMULITE Canine TSH assay, Siemens Healthineers, Llanberis, Gwynedd, North Wales, UK) based upon clinical suspicion. Systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured using the Doppler method (Supplementary Appendix A; Doppler Flow Detector Model 811-B, Parks Medical Electronics, Aloha, OR, USA). If SBP was ≥ 160 mmHg, retinal examination (ClearView 1 or ClearView 2, Optibrand, Fort Collins, CO, USA) was performed to assess for hypertensive chorioretinopathy.11 If present, antihypertensives were prescribed. If not present, it was recommended that cats be returned for repeated SBP measurement to assess for persistence. Urine was collected for urinalysis with bacterial culture if microscopic bacteriuria was present.

氮质血症性慢性肾病（CKD）

Azotemic CKD
所有猫在健康筛查时都测量了血浆肌酐浓度；227/549（41.3%）的猫同时进行了尿液分析。总体而言，62/542（11.4%）的猫被诊断为含氮性慢性肾病（CKD）（图1B）：其中43/62（69.4%）基于同时血浆肌酐浓度 > 2 mg/dL（>177 μmol/L）且尿比重（USG） < 1.035，19/62（30.6%）基于血浆肌酐浓度 > 2 mg/dL（>177 μmol/L）在至少间隔2周的两次连续就诊中测得。此外，还有16只猫在健康筛查时血氮升高但没有同时取尿样，但未被诊断为含氮性CKD，因为其中9/16（56.3%）在随后的就诊中血氮正常，7/16（43.8%）失访。由于缺少确定疾病状态所需的随访数据，这些后来的猫被排除在整体含氮性CKD估计之外。
All cats had plasma creatinine concentration measured at the time of health screening; 227/549 (41.3%) cats had urinalysis simultaneously performed. Overall, 62/542 (11.4%) cats were diagnosed with azotemic CKD (Figure 1B): 43/62 (69.4%) based on a concurrent plasma creatinine concentration > 2 mg/dL (>177 μmol/L) and USG < 1.035 and 19/62 (30.6%) based on plasma creatinine concentration > 2 mg/dL (>177 μmol/L) on 2 consecutive visits at least 2 weeks apart. An additional 16 cats were azotemic without a concurrent urine sample at the time of health screening, but were not diagnosed with azotemic CKD because 9/16 (56.3%) were non-azotemic at a subsequent visit and 7/16 (43.8%) were lost to follow-up. These latter cats were excluded from the overall azotemic CKD estimate because of missing follow-up data required to ascertain disease status.

Other diagnoses and findings
在549只猫中，有8只（1.5%）被诊断为糖尿病：5只是根据高血糖和葡萄糖尿症单独诊断的，3只是根据高血糖、葡萄糖尿症以及兼容的血清果糖胺浓度（由临床医生自行决定）诊断的。这些猫中没有患有氮质血症慢性肾病（CKD）、高甲状腺功能亢进、 高血压或细菌尿（8只猫中有5只通过膀胱穿刺获取尿样；其中2只送检细菌培养，均为阴性）同时被诊断。通过体格检查，8只猫（1.5%）被怀疑患有肿瘤：5只不明来源的腹部肿块，1只双肾肿大（怀疑为淋巴瘤），1只口腔肿块，1只面部肿块。在5只不明来源的腹部肿块的猫中，一只还同时被诊断为高血压，另一只被诊断为高甲状腺功能亢进。
Diabetes mellitus was diagnosed in 8/549 (1.5%) cats: 5 based on hyperglycemia and glucosuria alone and 3 based on hyperglycemia, glucosuria, and a compatible serum fructosamine concentration (at the clinician’s discretion). None of these cats had azotemic CKD, hyperthyroidism, hypertension, or bacteriuria (urine samples acquired by cystocentesis from 5/8 cats; 2/5 submitted for bacterial culture, both negative) concurrently diagnosed. Neoplasia was suspected in 8/549 (1.5%) cats based on physical examination: 5 abdominal masses of uncertain origin, 1 bilateral renal enlargement (lymphoma suspected), 1 oral mass, and 1 facial mass. Of the 5 cats with abdominal masses of uncertain origin, one was also concurrently diagnosed as hypertensive and another as hyperthyroid.

Findings in previously examined cats
在研究期间，有81只猫（81/549）曾在诊所接受过检查（并被发现健康），并再次接受健康筛查。在这些猫中，有60只（60/81）是在上次检查后≤24个月内再次就诊。关于这些猫，在健康筛查时，有4/59只（6.8%）被诊断为氮质血症慢性肾病（CKD）。在前一次检查时，这4只猫的血浆肌酐浓度均 ≤ 2 mg/dL（≤177 μmol/L），其中2只猫的血浆SDMA浓度 > 14 μg/dL，其中一只的浓度 ≥ 18 μg/dL。58只猫中有2只（3.4%）被诊断为高血压，另外分别有1只猫（1/33；3.0%）被诊断为甲状腺功能亢进症，1只猫（1/60；1.7%）被诊断为糖尿病，这些疾病均发生在不同的猫身上。这60只猫中没有一只是尿培养阳性，9/60只猫的ALT ≥ 2×参考范围，包括甲状腺功能亢进猫、2只高血压猫以及1只氮质血症CKD猫。还有1只猫（1/60；1.7%）有严重的牙结石或牙龈炎或两者兼有（牙结石或牙龈炎评分 = 4）。
Eighty-one cats (81/549) had been examined previously at the clinic (and found to be healthy) and were presented again for health screening during the period of the study. Sixty of these cats (60/81) were presented ≤ 24 months after their previous examination. Regarding these cats, 4/59 (6.8%) were diagnosed with azotemic CKD at the time of health screening. At the prior examination, all 4 cats had plasma creatinine concentration ≤ 2 mg/dL (≤177 μmol/L) and 2 cats had plasma SDMA concentration > 14 μg/dL, one of these had a concentration ≥ 18 μg/dL. Hypertension was diagnosed in 2/58 (3.4%) cats and individual cases of hyperthyroidism (1/33; 3.0%) and diabetes mellitus (1/60; 1.7%) also were diagnosed, all in separate cats. None of these 60 cats had a positive urine culture and 9/60 cats had an ALT ≥ 2× RI, including the hyperthyroid, 2 hypertensive, and 1 of the azotemic CKD cats. One cat (1/60; 1.7%) had severe tartar or gingivitis or both (tartar or gingivitis score = 4).
我们报告了在初级保健环境下对大量健康筛查猫群的疾病流行率和主要临床发现。42%（231/549）的8岁及以上猫只存在≥1种可能受益于兽医干预或进一步检查的疾病，包括甲状腺功能亢进、氮质血症性慢性肾病（CKD）和高血压、严重牙周疾病、糖尿病、疑似肿瘤、疑似原发性胃肠疾病、丙氨酸氨基转移酶（ALT）活性升高（≥2×参考范围）、尿路细菌感染以及原因不明的中到重度贫血。其中一些疾病是进行性发展的，并且已有管理方案显示可以改善预后或缓解疾病后遗症。因此，对年长猫（≥8岁）进行常规健康筛查可发现高频率的先前未识别的异常或疾病，从而可能促进更早的干预，而早期干预与改善预后相关。
We report the prevalence of disease and major clinical findings for a large population of healthscreened cats in a PCP setting. Forty-two percent (231/549) of cats aged 8 years and older had ≥ 1 condition that might benefit from veterinary intervention or further investigation, including hyperthyroidism, azotemic CKD and hypertension, severe dental disease, diabetes mellitus, suspected neoplasia, suspected primary GI disease, increased ALT activity (≥2× RI), bacteriuria, and moderate to severe anemia of unknown cause. Some of these diseases are progressive and have management options shown to improve outcomes or ameliorate disease sequelae.13–17 Hence, routine health screening of older cats (≥8 years old) detects a high frequency of previously unrecognized abnormalities or conditions and, as a consequence may facilitate earlier intervention, which has been associated with improved outcomes.
此外，在一组在当前健康筛查前 ≤ 24 个月接受检查的猫（且在此前评估中未发现明显疾病）的亚群体中，仍然进行了诊断。在这一亚群体中，疾病的流行率和显著发现低于在整个健康筛查猫群中发现的情况。这一结果是预期的，因为这些猫之前已接受过兽医评估，排除了明显疾病，而与首次来诊所、兽医接触程度各异的猫相比。然而，值得注意的是，在这一亚群体中仍发现可能从兽医干预中受益的疾病，这支持了对 ≥ 8 岁猫进行重复健康筛查的价值，这一点也在最近一项比利时对 ≥ 7 岁猫的研究中得到证明。研究中最常见的医疗状况是甲状腺功能亢进症，其患病率为 19.2%。
Moreover, in a subpopulation of cats that were examined ≤ 24 months before their current health screening visit (and found to have an absence of overt disease at this prior assessment), diagnoses were still made. The prevalence of disease and notable findings within this subpopulation is less than that found in the entire population of health-screened cats. This finding was expected because these cats had known veterinary assessments that previously excluded overt disease, compared with those visiting the clinic for the first time with variable degrees of veterinary contact. However, the fact that diseases potentially benefiting from veterinary intervention were still found within this subpopulation supports the value of repeated health screening in cats ≥ 8 years old, as also was demonstrated in a recent study examining cats ≥ 7 years old in Belgium.9 Hyperthyroidism was the most common medical condition identified in our study, with a prevalence of 19.2%.
这一百分比属于之前报道的猫甲状腺功能亢进症的较广流行率范围（0%-21.1%），2,7–9,18–26 但有趣的是，它比在使用英国初级护理实践（PCP）数据的VetCompass动物监测项目进行的大规模研究中报道的年龄≥10岁的猫的8.7%的流行率高出一倍以上。2 这种差异很可能反映了确认偏倚的影响，即我们研究中进行免费前瞻性健康筛查与在没有标准化诊断和纳入标准的多中心PCP环境中评估流行率之间的差异，在后者中许多猫可能未被筛查疾病。前者可能相比于只有在照护者发现临床症状时才带猫去就诊的情况，更有可能增加疾病的检测率。如预期的那样，在无症状猫中测量TT4可能允许在明显临床症状出现之前（例如恶液质、心血管疾病）更早进行疾病诊断和干预。
This percentage falls within the wide prevalence rates for hyperthyroidism in cats reported previously (0%-21.1%),2,7–9,18–26 but interestingly is more than twice the prevalence of 8.7% reported for cats ≥ 10 years in a large-scale study using UK primary care practice (PCP) data from the VetCompass Animal Surveillance Project.2 This disparity likely demonstrates the effect of ascertainment bias, namely, the difference between performing free-of- charge prospective health screening in our study vs evaluating prevalence in a multicenter PCP setting without standardized diagnostic and inclusion criteria where many cats will not have been screened for the disease. The former possibly allows for increased detection of disease compared to situations in which cats are most likely presented only when clinical signs have been detected by a caregiver. As might be expected, measurement of TT4 in asymptomatic cats could allow for earlier disease diagnosis and intervention before overt clinical signs arise (eg, cachexia, cardiovascular disease).
先前的研究报告了在表面健康的猫中甲状腺功能亢进症的发生率，报告的患病率为0%-5.5%，远低于我们的研究。7–9,19,26 这种差异可能与先前研究中使用的年轻猫群体或随时间增加的疾病患病率有关，27 但也可能是由于不同的诊断和纳入标准以及地理位置造成的。在与我们当前研究相同的诊所进行的一项早期研究中，对≥9岁的健康筛查猫报道了甲状腺功能亢进症的基线患病率为5.5%。19 这一发现可能表明，在这项基于英国的初级护理兽医人群中，甲状腺功能亢进症的诊断频率在两次研究期间（2004-2006年与2018-2023年）之间有所上升。
Previous studies that have reported the occurrence of hyperthyroidism in apparently healthy cats, reported prevalences of 0%-5.5%, which is much lower than in our study.7–9,19,26 This difference could be related to the younger cat populations utilized in prior studies or increasing disease prevalence over time,27 but also could be a result of different diagnostic and inclusion criteria as well as geographic location. An earlier study performed at the same clinic as our current study reported a baseline prevalence rate of hyperthyroidism of 5.5% in health-screened cats ≥ 9 years old.19 This finding may suggest that the frequency of hyperthyroidism diagnosis in this UK-based PCP population has increased between the 2 study periods (2004-2006 compared to 2018-2023).
在我们研究中，对549只猫进行筛查后，有11.4%的猫被诊断为氮质血症慢性肾病（CKD）。对于非氮质血症的猫，并未尝试确认CKD的诊断，但我们研究中有11.7%的非氮质血症猫的血浆SDMA浓度高于参考区间（RI），这可能表明早期CKD。这11.4%的氮质血症CKD患病率与其他三项研究（两项来自英国，一项来自比利时）一致，这些研究报告了老年猫的健康筛查，并描述了氮质血症、国际肾脏兴趣协会（IRIS）2期CKD及IRIS ≥ 2期CKD的患病率分别为10.1%、10.6%和7.7%。有趣的是，在我们当前研究中，25.8%被诊断为氮质血症CKD的猫，其血浆SDMA浓度仍在参考区间内。虽然我们所有的氮质血症猫根据血浆肌酐浓度均为IRIS 2期或以上，但其中51.6%的SDMA浓度低于18 μg/dL，这符合IRIS 1期。
Azotemic CKD was diagnosed in 11.4% of the 549 cats screened in our study. No attempt was made to confirm a diagnosis of CKD in non-azotemic cats, but 11.7% of non-azotemic cats in our study had a plasma SDMA concentration above the RI, which may indicate early CKD. This 11.4% prevalence of azotemic CKD is consistent with 3 other studies (2 from the UK and 1 from Belgium) that reported on the health screening of older cats and described prevalences of 10.1%, 10.6%, and 7.7% for azotemia, International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) stage 2 CKD and IRIS stage ≥ 2 CKD, respectively.7,9,19 Interestingly, plasma SDMA concentrations were within the RI for 25.8% of the cats diagnosed with azotemic CKD in our current study. Whereas all of our azotemic cats were IRIS stage 2 or higher based on their plasma creatinine concentrations, 51.6% of them had SDMA concentrations < 18 μg/dL, consistent with IRIS stage 1.
此外，在先前在诊所进行评估≤24个月后发展为氮质血症性CKD的4只猫中，只有2只在较早的检查中血浆SDMA浓度超过参考区间（14 μg/dL）。鉴于据报道在最终诊断为氮质血症性CKD的猫中，SDMA比肌酐更早超过参考区间的比例为81%，这些发现是出乎意料的。然而，最近一项研究在氮质血症性CKD发作前检查猫肠源性尿毒症毒素的变化，发现所有22只猫在发病前6个月内SDMA均保持在IRIS第1期区间内（<18 μg/dL），尽管在氮质血症性CKD诊断时未报告SDMA IRIS分期，中位SDMA浓度为13.4 μg/dL（即仍在参考区间内）。因此，看起来许多被诊断为氮质血症性CKD的猫，其基于血浆肌酐和SDMA浓度所分配的IRIS分期可能存在差异。
Additionally, of the 4 cats that developed azotemic CKD after a prior assessment at the clinic ≤ 24 months earlier, only 2 had plasma SDMA concentrations above the RI (14 μg/dL) at the earlier examination. These findings were unexpected given that SDMA reportedly increases above the RI earlier than creatinine does in 81% of cats ultimately diagnosed with azotemic CKD.28 However, a recent study examining alterations in gutderived uremic toxins before the onset of azotemic CKD in cats found that SDMA remained within the IRIS stage 1 interval (<18 μg/dL) for all 22 cats in the 6 months before developing azotemic CKD, and although SDMA IRIS stages at the point of azotemic CKD diagnosis were not reported, the median SDMA concentration was 13.4 μg/dL (ie, still within the RI).29 It therefore appears that many cats diagnosed with azotemic CKD can have a discrepancy between their assigned IRIS stages based on plasma creatinine and SDMA concentrations.
筛查猫中有7.1%出现高血压，属于其他健康筛查老猫群体报告的患病率范围（2.8%-12.7%）。5,7,9,19 大多数病例未发现高血压的潜在原因（56.8%;21/37猫;所有21猫均进行了血浆生物化学面板，测量血浆肌酐、SDMA和TT4浓度，9/21猫有尿检结果）。其余猫（43.2%;16/37猫）中，潜在继发性高血压最常见的原因是甲状腺功能亢进（9/37猫），其次是非极性慢性肾病（CKD）（6/37猫），1猫有腹部肿块（1/37猫）。
We identified hypertension in 7.1% of the cats screened, which is within the range of prevalences (2.8%-12.7%) reported in other health-screened populations of older cats.5,7,9,19 No underlying cause of hypertension was identified in the majority of our cases (56.8%; 21/37 cats; all 21 cats had a plasma biochemistry panel performed including measurement of plasma creatinine, SDMA, and TT4 concentrations, and 9/21 had urinalysis results). In the remaining cats (43.2%; 16/37 cats), the most common cause of potential secondary hypertension was hyperthyroidism (9/37 cats) followed by azotemic CKD (6/37 cats), and 1 cat had an abdominal mass (1/37 cats).
在进行的健康筛查测试中，未发现明显原因的高血压占主导地位，这是一个意料之外的观察结果，因为先前的研究报告显示，特发性高血压仅占高血压猫的13%-23%（即大多数猫患有继发性高血压）。30–32 然而，这一发现可能归因于各研究在筛查潜在疾病所进行的测试和用于诊断高血压的收缩压（SBP）阈值存在差异，从而导致研究队列的不同。我们研究中使用的平均收缩压（SBP）界值为160 mmHg，这是基于美国兽医内科学会（ACVIM）关于系统性高血压的共识声明33，而早期研究中则为170或175 mmHg。30–32 我们研究中的高血压猫也比先前研究中的更老，这可能导致预期潜在疾病的患病率以及继发性高血压在我们的群体中会更高。
This predominance of hypertension without an overt cause identified on the health screening tests performed is an unanticipated observation given that previous studies report that idiopathic hypertension only accounts for 13%-23% of hypertensive cats (ie, most cats have secondary hypertension).30–32 This finding, however, may be attributable to differences in tests performed to screen for underlying disease and SBP thresholds used to diagnose hypertension among studies, resulting in disparate study cohorts. A mean SBP cut-off of 160 mmHg was used in our study, based on the American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine (ACVIM) consensus statement on systemic hypertension,33 compared with 170 or 175 mmHg in earlier studies.30–32 The hypertensive cats in our study also were older than those in previous studies, possibly leading to the expectation that the prevalence of underlying conditions, and therefore secondary hypertension, would be higher in our population.
然而，在人类中，虽然原发性（特发性）高血压更为常见，但在年轻患者中，继发性高血压的患病率较整体人群更高。缺乏对原发性醛固酮增多症的筛查，以及如前所述，对非氮质血症慢性肾脏病（CKD）作为继发性高血压潜在原因的检测，是解释这些数据的限制因素。以往的研究也存在这些限制，需要纵向随访以评估某些原发性高血压病例是否实际上存在尚未显现的潜在原因，或是否需要持续记录轻度临床病理异常以进行诊断（例如，非氮质血症CKD）。有趣的是，在21例高血压且未发现潜在原因的猫中，4例（19.0%）的血浆SDMA浓度超过参考区间（>14 μg/dL），其中2例≥18 μg/dL。
However, in humans, although essential (idiopathic) hypertension is more common, the prevalence of secondary hypertension is higher in younger patients compared with the entire population.34 A lack of screening for primary hyperaldosteronism and, as previously mentioned, testing for non-azotemic CKD as potential causes of secondary hypertension are limiting factors for interpreting these data. Previous studies share these limitations and longitudinal follow-up would be required to assess if some cases of primary hypertension might in fact have an underlying cause that has not yet emerged or requires persistent documentation of mild clinicopathological abnormalities for diagnosis (eg, nonazotemic CKD). Interestingly, plasma SDMA concentrations were above the RI (>14 μg/dL) in 4/21 (19.0%) cats with hypertension and no underlying cause identified, and ≥ 18 μg/dL in 2 of these.
在接受尿液分析的猫中，有9.7%被发现有菌尿。在我们的方案中，只有在尿沉渣检查中观察到细菌时才会进行尿液培养。最近，有研究报告在犬只中观察到活动性沉渣阴性但尿液培养阳性的低患病率（3.4%）。因此，尽管我们的方法可能导致菌尿患病率略微被低估，但预计影响很小。不幸的是，根据现有的临床记录，大多数猫无法确定是否存在尿路感染（UTI）或亚临床菌尿，猫是否有症状，或治疗是否合适或有效（按最新指南定义）。超过50%的尿液培养阳性猫同时患有甲状腺功能亢进症、氮质血症慢性肾病（CKD）、高血压，或这些情况的某种组合（最常见的是CKD）。甲状腺功能亢进症和CKD是细菌感染的易感因素。
Bacteriuria was identified in 9.7% of cats that had urinalysis. In our protocol, urine culture was only performed if bacteria were observed on urinary sediment examination. Recently, a low prevalence (3.4%) of positive urine cultures with inactive sediment was reported in dogs.35 Therefore, although our methodology may have resulted in a slight underreporting of bacteriuria prevalence, the effect is expected to be minimal. Unfortunately, based on available clinical records, it was not possible for the majority of cats to determine if a urinary tract infection (UTI) or subclinical bacteriuria was present, if the cat was symptomatic or asymptomatic, or if treatment was appropriate or effective as defined by recent guidelines.36 More than 50% of cats with positive urine cultures had concurrent hyperthyroidism, azotemic CKD, hypertension, or some combination of these (most commonly CKD). 除了甲状腺功能亢进症、氮质血症慢性肾病（CKD）、高血压和菌尿症外，我们的研究在常规健康筛查中还发现了其他几种疾病和重要临床发现。这些包括糖尿病、肿瘤以及疑似原发性胃肠疾病。糖尿病在1.5%的猫中被诊断出，这一患病率远高于此前在英国不同猫群体报告的0.4%–0.6%。我们推测，这一较高的患病率可能部分与各自研究人群的年龄有关，因为之前的一项研究报告显示，6岁以上猫的疾病风险增加。然而，对于糖尿病和原发性胃肠疾病而言，考虑到这些疾病通常会伴随临床症状，并且猫是为了常规健康筛查而非因生病而就诊，研究中观察到的患病率令人惊讶。这一发现的可能原因包括照料者可能未能识别猫的疾病症状。
In addition to hyperthyroidism, azotemic CKD, hypertension and bacteriuria, several other diseases, and important clinical findings were detected on routine health screening in our study. These included diabetes mellitus, neoplasia, and suspected primary GI disease. Diabetes mellitus was diagnosed in 1.5% of cats, a much higher prevalence than the 0.4%-0.6% reported previously in diverse populations of cats in the UK.41,42 We hypothesize that this higher prevalence could be in part related to the age of the respective study populations because one of the previous studies reported increased disease risk in cats > 6 years old. However, for diabetes mellitus and primary GI disease, the observed prevalences in our study are surprising considering these conditions are expected to be associated with clinical signs and that cats were presented for routine health screening rather than veterinary attention being sought sooner for an unwell cat. A possible reason for this finding includes a potential lack of recognition of illness by caregivers.
这种情况反映了现实世界中的猫群体，在这些猫中，临床症状可能表现不一致，并且被带到兽医处的情况也各不相同。造成这一现象的因素可能有多种，包括疾病的严重程度和进展速度、猫和照护者的生活方式（例如，室内、室外或半野生猫，以及照护者与猫的互动程度）、以及兽医护理的可获得性和费用。大多数猫都存在口腔疾病，尤其是牙龈炎和牙石积累，6.1%的猫存在严重牙石或牙龈炎或两者兼有，这可能导致建议进一步进行牙科评估，包括检查、在全身麻醉下进行牙齿清洁，有或没有拔牙。
This situation reflects a real-world population where cats may not only display clinical signs inconsistently and be variably presented to a veterinarian. Several factors might account for this finding, including disease severity and rate of progression, cat and caregiver lifestyle (eg, indoor, outdoor, or semi- feral cat and level of caregiver and cat interaction), and availability and cost of veterinary care.43,44 Dental disease, specifically gingivitis and tartar accumulation were present in the majority of cats, and 6.1% had severe tartar or gingivitis or both that would have resulted in a recommendation for further dental assessment, including examination, dental cleaning with or without tooth extractions under general anesthesia.
正如在此处所发现的，牙科疾病在健康筛查的老年猫体检中始终名列前两位最常见的发现之一；在一项研究中，它与毛发打结和睡眠增加相关，可能提示存在慢性口腔疼痛。由于在清醒猫身上无法常规进行完整的口腔检查，且未进行牙科X光检查，因此受牙周疾病影响并可能从牙科手术中受益的猫的数量很可能高于通过我们研究中发现的严重牙龈炎和牙结石积累所估计的数量。猫的ALT活性在14.9%中增加≥RI的2倍，但其中大多数（69.5%）的升高可以通过同时诊断的甲状腺功能亢进或糖尿病来解释。
As found here, dental disease consistently ranks among the 2 most common findings on physical examination of health-screened older cats5,7–9,19,45; in one study, it was associated with a matted coat and increased sleeping, potentially suggesting the presence of chronic oral pain.7 Because a complete oral examination cannot routinely be performed in conscious cats and dental radiographs were not obtained, it is likely that the number of cats affected by periodontal disease and that would benefit from dental procedures is higher than estimated by the detection of severe gingivitis and tartar accumulation in our study.46 The ALT activity was increased ≥ 2× the RI in 14.9% of cats, but the majority (69.5%) of these increases could be explained by the concurrent diagnosis of either hyperthyroidism or diabetes mellitus.
在剩余的30.5%的猫中，ALT活性升高的原因无法确定，但可能表明原发性肝脏疾病（例如胆管炎-胆管肝炎综合征或未被发现的肿瘤）或未被发现的继发性原因（例如亚临床胃肠疾病或胰腺炎）。我们的研究存在一些局限性。类似于照顾者识别和报告疾病的能力会影响研究人群，一部分猫可能是由足够积极寻求健康筛查并将其猫咪报名参加前瞻性研究的照顾者带来。照顾者也可能有意隐瞒猫咪的病史信息，从而使其不被排除在免费评估之外。通过从慈善机构招募猫咪，这一因素可能在一定程度上得到缓解，因为一些照顾者已经有资格享受免费的宠物健康护理。我们的研究使用了为确定前瞻性饮食试验入选资格而进行健康筛查的猫的数据。
In the remaining 30.5% of cats, the cause of the increased ALT activity could not be determined but could have indicated primary hepatic disease (eg, cholangitis-cholangiohepatitis syndrome or undetected neoplasia) or an undiscovered secondary cause (eg, subclinical GI disease or pancreatitis). Our study had some limitations. In a similar way that caregivers’ ability to recognize and report illness will have impacted the study population, a subpopulation of cats will have been examined with caregivers motivated enough to seek health screening and enroll their cats into a prospective research study. Caregivers also may have been motivated to actively withhold information about their cats’ histories that could have excluded them from the free-of-charge assessments. This factor may have been somewhat ameliorated with cats recruited from the charity practice, because some caregivers already would be eligible for free pet health care. Our study used data from cats that were health screened for the purpose of identifying eligibility for a prospective diet trial.
这一特征可能引入偏见，因为研究群体和流程并非明确设计用来回答关于健康筛查实用性的次要问题。然而，尽管有此限制，招募标准（例如，年龄≥8岁，无慢性病诊断）以及实施的标准化临床方案和诊断标准，可能带来了一些益处。排除了需要长期监测和管理的既往慢性病猫，这很可能低估了整体疾病的患病率。然而，似乎年长且有慢性疾病的猫更可能定期就医，因此能发现共病，而我们的研究则提供了对照护者认为良好但如观察到一部分受疾病影响的猫的洞见。
This feature could have introduced bias because the study population and processes were not explicitly designed to answer secondary questions about the utility of health screening. However, despite this limitation, some benefits may have been derived from recruitment criteria that were equally appropriate for both studies (eg, ≥8 years old, absence of chronic disease diagnoses) and the standardized clinical protocol and diagnostic criteria that were implemented. The fact that cats with pre-existing chronic conditions requiring long-term monitoring and management were excluded likely underestimates the prevalence of overall disease. However, it seems probable that older cats with ongoing conditions are more likely to have regular veterinary visits and therefore have comorbidities detected, whereas our study provides insight into a population of cats considered well by caregivers yet, as observed, a proportion are affected by disease.
值得注意的是，我们的研究报告了在招募的特定人群（≥8岁的猫）中健康筛查的价值，且患病率估算不能外推到普通猫群体。对于某些疾病，用于计算患病率的分母群体经过调整（减少），以考虑在健康筛查时或随访所需数据缺失情况下无法确定疾病是否存在的猫。例如，对于尿液分析结果或随访血浆肌酐浓度不可得的氮质血症患者（氮质血症CKD），在健康筛查前12个月而非筛查时测量血浆TT4浓度的猫（甲状腺功能亢进），以及平均收缩压（SBP）≥160 mmHg但没有高血压性脉络膜视网膜病变证据且未进行随访血压测量的猫。根据这些猫是否患有相关疾病，患病率可能被高估或低估。
Of note, our study reports the value of health screening in a recruited and specific population (cats ≥ 8 years old) and prevalence estimates cannot be extrapolated to the general cat population. For some conditions, the denominator population used to calculate prevalence was adjusted (decreased) to account for cats where it could not be determined if a disease was present because of the absence of data at the time of health screening or required follow-up. Examples include azotemic patients for which urinalysis results or follow-up plasma creatinine concentration were not available (azotemic CKD), cats that had their plasma TT4 concentration measured in the 12 months before health screening rather than at the time of screening (hyperthyroidism), and cats with mean SBP ≥ 160 mmHg without evidence for hypertensive chorioretinopathy and not presented for follow-up blood pressure measurement. Depending on whether or not these cats had the disease in question, prevalence could have been overestimated or underestimated.
同样，有2例（1例甲状腺功能亢进和1例高血压猫）虽未符合所述的诊断标准，但基于合理的现实临床推理进行了诊断。总之，在我们研究中，对549只8岁及以上的猫进行常规临床检查和诊断测试，发现有42.1%的猫存在需要兽医干预或治疗的状况。这些数据支持了老龄猫健康筛查的必要性，并强调了兽医检查在发现关注健康问题中的重要性。兽医可以利用这些信息就老龄猫健康筛查的效用和必要性作出循证推荐。
Likewise, 2 cases (1 hyperthyroid and 1 hypertensive cat) were diagnosed outside of the stated diagnostic criteria but based upon reasonable real-world clinical reasoning. In conclusion, routine clinical examination and diagnostic tests identified conditions requiring veterinary intervention or treatment in 42.1% of 549 cats 8 years or older in our study. These data support the need for health screening in aging cats and emphasize the importance of a veterinary examination to detect health conditions of concern. Veterinarians can utilize this information to make evidence-based recommendations on the utility and need for health screening in older cats.
