Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is associated with accumulation of uremic toxins such as indoxyl sulfate (IS) in blood and tubular epithelial cells, which contribute to the progression of CKD. The aim of this prospective, randomised, two-centre clinical pilot study was to evaluate the effect of oral spherical carbonaceous adsorbent Renaltec (Porus One) on IS plasma concentration, progression of CKD, parameters of calcium/phosphate homeostasis (total calcium [tCa], phosphorus, fibroblast growth factor 23 [FGF-23]), urine protein:creatinine ratio (UPC) and faecal dysbiosis index (FDI) in cats with stable CKD International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) stage 2 and 3 over a 6-month period.

A total of 19 cats with CKD were randomly assigned to the Renaltec group (n = 10, eight stage 2, two stage 3) or control group (n = 9, eight stage 2, one stage 3). All cats received standard of care treatment according to IRIS guidelines. Cats in the Renaltec group received an additional 500 mg q24h of Renaltec administered mixed with food or a liquid cat treat. All cats were examined on day 0 (t0), after 3 months (t1) and after 6 months (t2). Statistical analysis was performed using generalised linear mixed models. At t0, the Renaltec group cats had significantly lower tCa (P = 0.026) than the control group cats. At t1, there were no differences between the groups, while at t2, the Renaltec group cats had significantly lower IS (P = 0.045) and UPC (P = 0.001) than cats in the control group. Progression of CKD was noted in two cats from each group. The Renaltec group cats had a significantly lower FDI at t2 compared with t1 (P = 0.023) and t0 (P = 0.009).

Study design

治疗效果评估

Assessment of treatment effect
评估治疗效果的参数包括血浆IS浓度以及通过血清肌酐和SDMA评估的肾功能发展，同时还包括3个月和6个月后的磷、总钙（tCa）和钾浓度。CKD进展被定义为IRIS分期升高和/或肌酐较基线增加超过25％。
Parameters to evaluate the treatment effect were the plasma IS concentration and the development of renal function assessed by serum creatinine and SDMA as well as phosphorus, total calcium (tCa) and potassium concentrations after 3 and 6 months. CKD progression was defined as a change to a higher IRIS stage and/or an increase in creatinine of more than 25% from baseline.

统计分析

Statistical analysis
基于先前发表的IRIS 2期和3期CKD猫的血浆IS浓度平均值（±SD）为15 ± 5 mg/l，以及假设在Renaltec给药6个月内血浆IS可降低50%，如早期对老年猫的研究所示，对样本量进行了效能分析。为了达到80%的检验效能和0.05的P值，每组至少需要八只猫。因此，Renaltec组共纳入10只猫，对照组纳入9只猫。基于这些假设，本研究的最小可检测效应是两组平均血浆IS浓度的绝对差值为7.5 mg/l，对应于较大的效应量（Cohen’s d ≈ 1.5）。因此，该研究的设计能够检测到治疗相关的显著IS浓度下降。
A power analysis was conducted based on the previously published mean (±SD) plasma IS concentration of 15 ± 5 mg/l in cats with IRIS stage 2 and 3 CKD 6 and the assumption of achieving a 50% reduction of plasma IS within 6 months of Renaltec administration, as demonstrated in an earlier study of geriatric cats. 26 For a power of 80% and a P value of 0.05, at least eight cats per group were required. Consequently, 10 cats were included in the Renaltec group and nine cats in the control group. Based on these assumptions, the minimum detectable effect for the study was an absolute difference of 7.5 mg/l in mean plasma IS concentration between groups, corresponding to a large effect size (Cohen’s d ≈ 1.5). The study was therefore powered to detect substantial treatment-related reductions in IS concentration.
收集的数据使用统计软件 R 版本 2023.06.1 进行分析。描述性统计包括将连续变量计算为均值、标准差和中位数及四分位距，分类变量计算为频数和百分比。连续变量的正态分布使用 Shapiro–Wilk 检验进行评估。Renaltec 组和对照组体重的差异使用 Mann–Whitney U 检验评估，年龄差异使用 Student t 检验评估。由于各时间点存在重复测量，选择以个体动物 ID 作为随机效应的广义线性混合效应模型进行分析。始终检查以下模型假设：(1) 使用 Shapiro–Wilk 正态性检验检查残差的正态性；(2) 使用 Bartlett 检验检查组间方差的齐性；(3) 使用 Breusch–Pagan 检验检查异方差性（误差方差的恒定性）。
Collected data were investigated using the statistical software R version 2023.06.1. Descriptive statistics included the calculation of the continuous variables as mean, SD and median with interquartile range and the categorical variables as counts and percentages. The normal distribution of continuous variables was assessed using the Shapiro–Wilk test. The difference between body weight in the Renaltec and control groups was evaluated using the Mann–Whitney U-test, while the difference in age was evaluated using the Student’s t-test. As a result of the presence of repeated measures among the time points, generalised linear mixed-effects models with individual animal ID as a random effect were chosen for analysis. The following model assumptions were always checked: (1) the normality of residuals was checked using the Shapiro– Wilk normality test; (2) the homogeneity of variances between groups was checked using the Bartlett test; and (3) the heteroscedasticity (constancy of error variance) was checked using the Breusch–Pagan test.
如果假设得到满足，则使用广义线性混合效应模型（R软件包 – lmer）。如果假设被违反，则使用稳健线性混合效应模型（R软件包 – robustlmm）。稳健模型用于IS、肌酐、尿素、总蛋白、磷、钙、白细胞、血细胞比容（HCT）、尿比重（USG）、尿蛋白/肌酐比值（UPC）、FDI、FGF-23和体重。广义线性混合模型用于SDMA、白蛋白、钾、氯化钠、钠和血压。P <0.05 被认为有统计学意义。在评估入组的65只具有与慢性肾病（CKD）相关临床症状的猫中，有19只稳定的CKD猫符合纳入标准，并随机分配到Renaltec组或对照组（图1）。Renaltec组包括10只猫（其中八只为IRIS 2期，两只为3期），对照组包括9只猫（其中八只为IRIS 2期，一只为3期）。

The robust model was used for IS, creatinine, urea, total protein, phosphate, calcium, leukocytes, haematocrit, USG, UPC, FDI, FGF-23 and body weight. The generalised linear mixed model was used for SDMA, albumin, potassium, sodium chloride, sodium and blood pressure. P <0.05 was considered significant. Renaltec 的管理

Administration of Renaltec
问卷显示，Renaltec 组的所有猫咪都能可靠地每日一次服用该补充剂，无论是与 Add One 混合，还是与它们的常规食物一起服用。最初拒绝与 Add One 混合的 Renaltec 的猫咪在与常规食物一起提供时很容易接受。主人报告在此调整后没有拒绝情况，并且在整个研究期间没有出现副作用或接受情况的变化。
Questionnaires revealed that all cats in the Renaltec group reliably received the supplement once daily, either mixed with Add One or with their regular food. Cats that initially refused Renaltec mixed with Add One readily accepted it when offered with their regular food. Owners reported no refusals after this adjustment and no side effects or changes in acceptance throughout the study.

治疗结果

Treatment outcome
在 t1 时，对 19 只猫的数据进行了分析，结果显示各组在任何评估参数上均无统计学显著差异。在 t2 时，有来自 Renaltec 组的 8 只猫和对照组的 9 只猫的数据可用。在 t2 之前，Renaltec 组有两只猫被排除：一只在第 174 天因化脓性三联炎被排除，另一只因前肢肿瘤在 t2 前不久被安乐死（具体日期不详）。在 t2 时，Renaltec 组的猫血浆 IS 浓度明显低于对照组的猫（P = 0.045）。在 CBC 参数、肌酐、SDMA、FGF-23、FDI、体重或血压方面，各组之间未观察到显著差异。每组均有 8 只猫的 UPC 值可用于分析。对照组有一只猫因为尿沉渣活跃未进行 UPC 测定。Renaltec 组猫的 UPC 明显低于对照组猫（P = 0.001），尽管两组的数值均保持在非蛋白尿范围内。
At t1, data from 19 cats were analysed, showing no statistically significant differences between the groups for any evaluated parameters. At t2, data from eight cats in the Renaltec group and nine in the control group were available. Two cats in the Renaltec group were excluded before t2: one on day 174 because of suppurative triaditis and another was euthanased shortly before t2 because of a forelimb neoplasm (exact date unknown). At t2, cats in the Renaltec group had significantly lower plasma IS concentrations (P = 0.045) than cats in the control group. No significant differences were observed in CBC parameters, creatinine, SDMA, FGF-23, FDI, body weight or blood pressure between the groups. UPC values of eight cats of each group were available for analysis. UPC was not performed in one cat from the control group because of an active urine sediment. UPC was significantly lower in cats in the Renaltec group than in cats in the control group (P = 0.001) although values in both groups remained within the nonproteinuric range.

在先前的一项短期研究中，老年猫的IS浓度已被报告显著下降。鉴于这些动物没有患慢性肾病（CKD），因此其尿毒素初始水平预计较低，这样的发现与自然发生肾脏疾病的猫不能直接比较。此外，尿毒素浓度的生物学变异性已被证明，这表明即使IS的降低幅度较为适中，也可能具有意义，特别是在自然发生慢性肾病（CKD）的猫中在日常临床条件下实现并持续较长时间时。先前关于AST-120的研究已证明其在各种实验条件下对IS具有降低作用。在大鼠中，在体重每100克0.40–0.45克的剂量下，经1022周和1914周观察到IS水平显著下降，这远高于本研究使用的剂量（每猫每天0.5克，平均体重5.12±1.82千克）。在小鼠中的研究结果则不那么确凿。
Marked reductions in IS concentrations have been reported in geriatric cats in a previous short- term study. 26 Given that these animals did not suffer from CKD and were consequently expected to have a lower initial level of uremic toxins, such findings are not directly comparable with cats with naturally occurring renal disease. Moreover, biological variability in uremic toxin concentrations has been demonstrated, 30 indicating that more moderate reductions in IS may also be meaningful, particularly when they are achieved under routine clinical conditions in cats with naturally occurring CKD and maintained over an extended period. Previous studies on AST-120 have demonstrated its IS-lowering effect across various species under experimental conditions. In rats, significant reductions in IS levels were observed after 1022 and 1914 weeks at dosages of 0.40–0.45 g/100 g body weight, which are substantially higher than the dose used in the present study (0.5 g/cat/day, mean weight 5.12 ± 1.82 kg). In mice, findings have been less conclusive.
一项为期12周的研究在实验性肾功能障碍小鼠中使用含有5% AST-120的标准食物，显示出IS水平下降的非显著趋势，尽管体重转换结果并不明确。31 同样，一项在肾切除小鼠中使用西式饮食（高饱和脂肪酸、碳水化合物且纤维含量低）混入8% AST-120进行三个月的研究，也显示出IS水平下降的非显著趋势。31 在实验性诱导心力衰竭且肾功能正常的狗中，每天按体重1 g/kg剂量使用AST-120四周后，IS水平降低了50%。32 同样，一项针对中重度CKD患者的多中心、随机、双盲、安慰剂对照、剂量递增研究显示，在12周后血清IS水平呈剂量依赖性下降，随着AST-120剂量增加（每位患者每8小时0.9–3 g），浓度逐步降低。
A 12-week study using standard food containing 5% AST-120 in mice with experimentally induced renal dysfunction showed a non-significant trend towards lower IS levels, although body weight conversion was inconclusive. 31 Similarly, a study using a Western diet (diet with high amount of saturated fatty acids, carbohydrates and low fibre content) mixed with 8% AST-120 over 3 months in nephrectomised mice demonstrated a non-significant trend towards decreased IS levels. 31 In dogs with experimentally induced heart failure and healthy kidneys, a 50% reduction in IS levels was observed after 4 weeks of treatment with 1 g/kg body weight AST-120 per day. 32 Similarly, a multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-escalating study in humans with moderate to severe CKD demonstrated a dose-dependent reduction in serum IS levels after 12 weeks, with concentrations progressively decreasing as AST-120 doses increased (0.9–3 g/patient q8h).
24 相比之下，另一项研究报告称，仅在慢性肾病（CKD）患者每天服用 6 g AST-120 至少 6 个月后，IS 水平才显著下降。33 在猫中，一项涉及 13 只实验性诱导 CKD（IRIS 2 和 3 期）并喂食标准化肾脏饮食的研究报告称，经过 28 天的 Renaltec 给药方案（0.5 g 每 24 小时一次和 0.5 mg 每 12 小时一次）后，血清 IS 水平显著下降。然而，到第 56 天时，仅在每日两次给药的情况下，显著下降的效果才得以维持。作者将这一发现归因于尿毒症毒素的显著生物变异性以及样本量较小，可能限制了研究的统计效力。27 总而言之，这些研究强调了不同物种、给药方案和研究持续时间下 IS 降低效果的差异。值得注意的是，无论在人类还是其他动物中，较长的治疗时间和较高剂量都显示出对 IS 水平更一致的降低效果。需要进一步研究以优化 Renaltec 的给药策略，并阐明其对 IS 水平的长期影响，尤其是在基线 IS 水平较高和 CKD 处于晚期的猫中。
24 In contrast, another study reported a significant IS decrease in CKD patients only after a minimum of 6 months of daily therapy with 6 g of AST-120. 33 In cats, a study involving 13 cats with experimentally induced CKD (IRIS stage 2 and 3), fed a standardised renal diet, reported significant serum IS reductions after 28 days of Renaltec dosing regimens (0.5 g q24h and 0.5 mg q12h). However, by day 56, significant reductions were maintained only with twice-daily dosing. The authors attributed this finding to the significant biological variability of uremic toxins and the small sample size, which may have limited the study’s statistical power. 27 In summary, these studies highlight the variability in IS-lowering effects across different species, dosing regimens and study durations. Notably, longer treatment durations and higher doses in both humans and other animals have demonstrated more consistent reductions in IS levels. Further research is required to optimise Renaltec dosing strategies and to clarify its long-term effects on IS levels, particularly in cats with elevated baseline IS levels and advanced stages of CKD.
大量研究表明，蛋白尿是猫慢性肾病（CKD）的重要预后因素。34–37 在本研究中，蛋白尿猫（UPC >0.4）被排除。在t2时，Renaltec组猫的UPC值显著低于对照组。在涉及大鼠和人类的多项研究中也报告了类似的结果。对亚切除肾大鼠进行AST-120治疗的研究显示，其尿蛋白排泄显著低于对照组。14,15,38 这些研究的组织学评估表明，AST-120可能预防肾小球硬化和肾小管间质纤维化，这些都与尿蛋白排泄相关。14,15 此外，一项针对人类的临床研究表明，每日服用AST-120 12个月可显著降低非糖尿病CKD患者的蛋白尿水平，相较于接受对照治疗的患者。研究人员提出，AST-120治疗对减少肾小管损伤具有有益作用。
Numerous studies have demonstrated that proteinuria serves as a critical prognostic factor for CKD in cats.34 –37 In the present study, cats with proteinuria (UPC >0.4) were excluded. At t2, cats in the Renaltec group showed significantly lower UPC values compared with those in the control group. Similar findings have been reported in multiple studies involving rats and humans. Studies in subtotal nephrectomised rats treated with AST-120 showed significantly lower urinary protein excretion compared with the control groups.14,15,38 Histological evaluations from these studies suggest that AST-120 may prevent glomerular sclerosis and tubulointerstitial fibrosis, which are conditions linked to urinary protein excretion.14,15 In addition, a clinical study in humans demonstrated that daily administration of AST-120 over 12 months significantly reduced proteinuria levels in non-diabetic patients with CKD compared with those receiving control treatment. The authors proposed that AST-120 treatment has a beneficial effect on reducing tubular damage.
39 尽管本研究显示UPC有统计学上显著的下降，但这一变化仍处于非蛋白尿范围内，因此在本队列中不太可能具有临床相关性。然而，这一发现仍可能提示Renaltec对尿蛋白排泄具有潜在影响，值得在蛋白尿CKD猫中进一步研究。此外，未来的研究应结合组织病理学分析，以探索对肾小球硬化和肾小管间质纤维化的潜在影响。在本研究的任何时间点，不同组之间的血清肌酐水平或CKD进展均未观察到显著差异。在人类CKD研究中，AST-120治疗超过30周后观察到血清肌酐下降，但在12周后未见此变化。
39 Although the present study demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in UPC, this change occurred within the non-proteinuric range and is therefore unlikely to be clinically relevant in this cohort. However, the finding may still suggest a potential effect of Renaltec on urinary protein excretion that warrants further investigation in proteinuric CKD cats. In addition, future investigations incorporating histopathological analyses are necessary to explore potential effects on glomerular sclerosis and tubulointerstitial fibrosis. No significant differences in serum creatinine levels or CKD progression were observed between the groups at any time point during this study. In human CKD studies, a reduction in serum creatinine was observed after more than 30 weeks of AST-120 treatment 25 but not after 12 weeks.
24 相比之下，接受高剂量 AST-120 处理的大鼠在仅 12 周后肌酐水平即降低。22 由于关于 Renaltec 对患有慢性肾病（CKD）猫的影响的研究数量有限，仍不确定更长时间的治疗是否能在该物种中显著降低肌酐水平。多项研究表明，与健康对照组相比，慢性肾病（CKD）患者的肠道微生物组成发生了改变。40–43 一项研究调查了膳食益生菌在大鼠中的潜在作用，发现血清中胱硫酸盐等尿毒性毒素的水平显著下降，这表明通过调节慢性肾病（CKD）患者的肠道微生物组，在减少某些尿毒性毒素积累方面具有潜在益处。
24 In contrast, rats treated with high doses of AST-120 showed reduced creatinine levels after only 12 weeks. 22 Given the limited number of studies on the effects of Renaltec in cats with CKD, it remains uncertain whether a longer treatment duration could result in significantly reduced creatinine levels in this species. Multiple studies have shown that human patients with CKD exhibit an altered composition of gut microbiota compared with healthy controls.40 –43 A study investigating the potential of dietary probiotics in rats found a significant reduction in serum levels of IS, among other uremic toxins, suggesting a beneficial effect in reducing the accumulation of certain uremic toxins through modulation of the intestinal microbiome in CKD patients.
44 在使用 AST-120 治疗的小鼠和大鼠研究中，显示多种器官中 IS 和 p-甲酚硫酸盐（pCS）的组织积累减少，45 肠道微生物群组成发生变化，这些微生物群参与尿毒症毒素前体的产生，46 同时粪便中吲哚、p-甲酚和酚的排泄增加，血清 IS 和 pCS 水平下降。15 在人类中，经过 3 个月的 AST-120 治疗后也观察到肠道微生物群组成的类似变化；尽管 pCS 水平有下降的趋势，但这些结果未达到统计学显著性。47 一项研究检查了患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫的粪便微生物组和血清 IS 浓度，发现与对照猫相比，其粪便细菌多样性和丰富度降低，同时血清 IS 浓度升高。
44 Studies in mice and rats treated with AST-120 demonstrated decreased tissue accumulation of IS and p-cresyl sulfate (pCS) in multiple organs, 45 a change in the composition of gut microbiota involved in the production of uremic toxin precursors 46 as well as an increase in faecal excretion of indole, p-cresol and phenol and the reduction of serum IS and pCS levels. 15 A similar effect in intestinal microbiota composition was observed in humans after 3 months of AST-120 treatment; although the trend towards reduced pCS levels was noted, these results did not reach statistical significance. 47 A study examining the faecal microbiome and serum IS concentrations in cats with CKD revealed decreased faecal bacterial diversity and richness, alongside higher serum IS concentrations, compared with control cats.
然而，该研究未能建立测量的尿毒症毒素浓度与已知参与其在人体中产生的细菌类型之间的相关性。在本研究中，Renaltec组在治疗后3个月和6个月内的FDI显著下降。这一改善可能归因于使用Renaltec后肠道菌群组成的积极变化。尽管Renaltec是在肠腔中对吲哚的物理吸附剂，但本研究中观察到的FDI显著降低可能表明其对肠道微生物组成的间接影响，这可能是通过底物可用性的改变介导的。这些观察结果与AST-120研究的发现一致，并提出假设，即肠道毒素吸附剂可能同时影响猫慢性肾病（CKD）的系统性毒素负荷和肠道微生物群。为了更好地理解这些影响，需要进一步研究健康猫、患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫及接受Renaltec治疗的猫的FDI。
However, the study was unable to establish a correlation between the concentration of the measured uremic toxins and the types of bacteria known to be involved in their production in humans. 9 In the present study, a significant decrease in the FDI within the Renaltec group was demonstrated at both 3 and 6 months after treatment. This improvement may be attributed to positive changes in gut microbiota composition after administration of Renaltec. Although Renaltec is a physical adsorber of indole in the intestinal lumen, the significant reduction in FDI observed in this study may suggest indirect effects on gut microbial composition, potentially mediated by altered substrate availability. These observations are in line with findings from studies on AST-120 and raise the hypothesis that intestinal toxin adsorbers could influence both systemic toxin burden and the intestinal microbiota in feline CKD. Further studies investigating the FDI in healthy cats, cats with CKD and those treated with Renaltec are warranted to better understand these effects.
在本研究中，尽管没有猫出现贫血，但在对照组中从 t0 到 t2 观察到血细胞比容（HCT）显著下降。虽然下降在统计学上具有显著性，但仍在参考范围内，因此在这小群猫中不被认为具有临床相关性。慢性肾病 (CKD) 的严重程度增加与贫血的发展相关：在 CKD 猫中，5%（IRIS 2 期）、18%（IRIS 3 期）和 53%（IRIS 4 期）会发展为贫血。贫血的主要原因是由于氧气感应机制受损和肾小管周围 EPO 产生细胞 EPO 生成减少而导致的促红素 (EPO) 缺乏。大鼠研究表明，暴露于增加的 IS 水平会增加肾小管细胞的氧消耗，并通过依赖低氧诱导转录因子的机制损害 EPO 产生细胞的氧感应能力。需要纵向研究以确定降低血浆 IS 水平是否能够延缓 CKD 中贫血的进展。本研究存在一些局限性。
In the present study, a significant decrease in haematocrit was observed in the control group between t0 and t2, although none of the cats was anaemic. The decrease, although statistically significant, was within the reference interval and therefore not considered clinically relevant in this small cohort of cats. Increased severity of CKD is associated with the development of anaemia: of cats with CKD, 5% (IRIS stage 2), 18% (IRIS stage 3) and 53% (IRIS stage 4) develop anaemia.48,49 A primary cause of anaemia is erythropoietin (EPO) deficiency due to impaired oxygen-sensing mechanisms and reduced EPO production by peritubular renal EPO-producing cells. 50 Studies in rats showed that exposure to increased IS levels increases oxygen consumption in renal tubular cells 51 and impairs the oxygen-sensing capacity of EPO-producing cells through a hypoxia-inducible transcription factor dependent mechanism. 52 Longitudinal studies are needed to determine whether lowering IS plasma levels could delay the progression of anaemia in CKD The present study has some limitations.
随机化导致了一些基线不平衡（性别、总血钙、尿比重（USG）、伴随用药），这些可能影响观察到的治疗效果。此外，还有少数猫未完成为期6个月的研究，这进一步减少了有效样本量和研究的整体统计效能。还有一些统计学方面的考虑。为了对观察到的22%减幅（平均血尿素素浓度绝对差值3.3 mg/l）进行背景说明，使用与先验样本量分析相同的假设进行了事后敏感性分析。在80%功效下检测到这种较小效应，每组需37只猫（总共74只猫）。以实际样本量（每组平均9.5只）进行计算，观察到的效应所达到的统计功效约为27%，通过R中的pwr包计算得出。53 这表明该研究在检测更微小变化方面统计功效不足，但与其探索性设计一致，该设计基于从先前数据得出的假设大效应来进行检测。
Randomisation resulted in some baseline imbalances (sex, tCa, USG, concomitant medications) that could have influenced the observed treatment effects. In addition, a few cats did not complete the 6-month study period, which further reduced the effective sample size and the overall statistical power of the study. There are also statistical considerations. To contextualise the observed 22% reduction (3.3 mg/l absolute difference in mean IS concentration), a post hoc sensitivity analysis was performed using the same assumptions as the a priori power analysis. Detecting this smaller effect at 80% power would require 37 cats per group (total of 74 cats). With the actual sample size (mean of 9.5 per group), the achieved power for the observed effect was approximately 27%, calculated via the pwr package in R. 53 This indicates that the study was underpowered for detecting subtler changes but aligns with its exploratory design, which was based on detecting a large hypothesised effect derived from prior data.
FDI 尚未在患有慢性肾病 (CKD) 的猫中得到验证，因此在解释时应谨慎。此外，Renaltec 的使用（是否添加 Add One）以及肾脏饮食的组成在个体之间也未标准化。尽管所有猫均只喂食肾脏饮食，但干食和湿食的相对比例在个体之间及随时间变化不同。此外，猫所食用的肾脏饮食来自两个不同品牌或按计算比例配制，其蛋白质和磷含量并不相同（粗蛋白 13.1–18.2 g/MJ 代谢能量 [5.48–7.61 g/100 kcal 代谢能量]；磷 124.6–315.8 mg/MJ 代谢能量 [52.1–132.1 mg/100 kcal 代谢能量]）。此外，只有 Renaltec 组的猫接受 Add One 作为液体零食载体，这可能影响肠道微生物群，应被视为潜在混杂因素。
The FDI has not been validated in cats with CKD, and its interpretation should therefore be approached with caution. Moreover, individual differences in the administration of Renaltec (with or without Add One) and in the composition of the renal diets were not standardised. Although all cats were fed exclusively renal diets, the relative proportions of dry and wet food varied between individuals and over time. In addition, cats were fed renal diets from two different brands or a calculated ration, which did not have identical protein and phosphate contents (crude protein 13.1–18.2 g/MJ ME [5.48–7.61 g/100 kcal ME]; phosphate 124.6–315.8 mg/MJ ME [52.1–132.1 mg/100 kcal ME]). Furthermore, only cats in the Renaltec group received Add One as a liquid treat vehicle, which may have influenced the intestinal microbiota and should be considered a potential confounding factor.
与对照组相比，使用Renaltec治疗的猫在6个月后IS浓度显著降低，UPC也显著降低，尽管UPC值仍处于非蛋白尿范围内。此外，Renaltec组的猫FDI随时间显著改善，而对照组的猫血细胞比容（HCT）和体重随时间显著下降。尽管这些结果令人鼓舞，并提示Renaltec在遵循IRIS指南治疗的基础上可能有潜在益处，但应考虑样本量小和其他研究局限性来解释这些结果。由于这是一次初步研究，需要进一步进行大样本的对照试验以确认这些结果，并探讨Renaltec在猫慢性肾病（CKD）中的长期效果。
Treatment with Renaltec was associated with significantly lower IS concentrations and significantly lower UPC after 6 months compared with a control group, although UPC values remained within the non-proteinuric range. Furthermore, cats in the Renaltec group had a significant improvement of FDI over time, whereas cats in the control group showed a significant decrease of haematocrit and body weight over time. Although these findings are encouraging and suggest a potential benefit of Renaltec in addition to treatment according to IRIS guidelines, they should be interpreted with consideration of the small sample size and other study limitations. As this was a pilot study, further controlled trials with larger populations are needed to confirm these results and to explore the long-term effects of Renaltec in feline CKD.
