Not all forms of dietary phosphorus are equal: an evaluation of postprandial phosphorus concentrations in the plasma of the cat Phosphorus is present in diets as naturally occurring P from raw materials or added as an inorganic salt. However, little is known about postprandial kinetics of P absorption in cats. Here, we describe several studies quantifying postprandial kinetics following the ingestion of diets of varying composition. Briefly, cats were fed a meal consisting of 50 % of their metabolic energy requirement in a randomised crossover design. A pre-meal baseline blood sample was taken via cephalic catheter and repeated measurements taken regularly up to 6 h post-meal to assess the whole blood ionised Ca, plasma P and parathyroid hormone concentrations.

Subsequent data indicate that added inorganic P salts in the diet above 0·5 g P/4184 kJ (1000 kcal) cause an increase in plasma P in cats, while diets below this do not. 磷是一种必需的营养素，参与多种代谢过程，同时也是骨骼健康的关键成分(1)。总体而言，饮食中的磷要么以天然存在的磷形式存在，例如肉类和鱼类等富含蛋白质的成分、骨物质和植物成分，要么来源于添加的无机磷(1)。猫的研究表明，饮食中磷含量越高，尿液和粪便中排泄的磷量也越高(2–4)。然而，这种关系并不简单，因为饮食中磷的消化和吸收可能会受到多种饮食因素的影响(5)。不仅饮食的总磷含量和钙磷比似乎会影响磷的利用率，饮食磷的来源也可能在此过程中发挥作用(6,7)。例如，来自食材的膳食磷，如禽肉粉或肉骨粉，被认为生物利用率低于添加的无机盐，例如含磷的一元和二元钠盐(3,8)。
Phosphorus is an essential nutrient, involved in several metabolic processes as well as a key component in skeletal health(1). Broadly, dietary P is either present as naturally occurring P from protein-rich components such as meat and fish, bony material and plant ingredients or derived from the added inorganic P(1). Studies in cats indicated that the higher the P content of the diet, the higher the amount of P excreted in the urine and faeces(2–4). This relationship is not straightforward, however, as digestion and absorption of dietary P can be altered by a range of dietary factors(5). Not only does the total P content of the diet and the Ca:P ratio appear to influence P availability, but the source of dietary P may also play a role in this process(6,7). Dietary P derived from food ingredients, for example, poultry meal or meat and bone meal, is considered to be less bioavailable than supplemented inorganic salts; for example, P-containing mono and dibasic Na salts(3,8).
以磷为基础的添加剂被广泛用于商业食品制造中，它们具有多种加工功能，包括pH稳定、乳化、膨松、保水和杀菌作用。这种可用性的差异很可能是由于有机材料中所含的磷与体内蛋白质和细胞内信号分子结合，而添加的无机磷通常以可溶性盐的形式包含在饮食中，能够容易解离并被吸收(9)。这种溶解性可能因无机磷的来源而异，例如，易溶于水的单磷酸盐可能会导致尿液中磷的排泄量高于酸可溶单磷酸盐(10)。
P-based additives are widely used in commercial food manufacturing, where they serve a number of processing functions including pH stabilisation, emulsification, leavening, hydration and bactericidal actions. This difference in availability is most likely due to the P contained in the organic materials being bound to in vivo proteins and intra-cellular signalling molecules, whilst added inorganic P is usually included in diets as a soluble salt that is able to readily disassociate and be absorbed(9). This solubility may differ between sources of inorganic P, for example, monophosphates readily soluble in water may lead to higher excretion of P in the urine compared with acid-soluble monophosphates(10).
在健康的人体中，正常的循环磷（P）和钙（Ca）浓度是通过调节钙三醇（1,25-二羟维生素D3）、成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF-23）和甲状旁腺激素（PTH）来维持的(11)。在人类长期摄入高磷饮食的情况下，FGF-23的产生会被刺激；这会下调肾脏钠-磷共转运体的表达，并降低1,25-二羟维生素D3水平，从而导致通过肾脏排泄的磷增加(12)。在猫、狗和大鼠中，观察到餐后血清磷升高会导致游离钙（iCa）减少，从而增加PTH分泌，同时也会降低肾脏对磷的重吸收并增加尿中排泄(13,14)。肾功能的显著下降也会损害磷平衡的调节，导致人体循环磷浓度长期升高(15)。这种反应已与心血管事件、心血管死亡率和全因死亡率相关，并且与慢性肾病（CKD）患者相关(16)。
In healthy humans, normal circulating P and Ca concentrations are maintained via modulation of calcitrol (1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3), fibroblast growth factor (FGF-23) and parathyroid hormone (PTH)(11). During prolonged exposure to a high dietary P intake in humans, FGF-23 production is stimulated; this down-regulates the expression of renal sodium-phosphate co-transporters and decreases 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D3 levels, leading to increased P excretion through the kidneys(12). In cats, dogs and rats, it has been observed that acutely after a meal, an increase in serum P causes a reduction in ionised Ca (iCa) that in turn increases PTH secretion, also leading to decreased resorption of P in the kidney and increased excretion into the urine(13,14). A substantial reduction in kidney function can also impair the regulation of phosphate balance, resulting in chronically elevated circulating phosphate concentrations in humans(15). This response has been associated with cardiovascular events, cardiovascular mortality and all-cause mortality as well as with human patients having chronic kidney disease (CKD)(16).
Dobenecker 等人(6) 最近显示，将高磷饮食、低钙磷比喂给猫 29 天，会导致糖尿和微量白蛋白尿，同时肌酐清除率下降；所有这些都是肾功能障碍的指标(6)。研究表明，仅使用低至占饮食干物质重量 0.5% 的磷补充剂，就可能导致成年大鼠发生肾脏和血管钙化(14,17)。此外，与主要来自天然来源的磷的饮食相比，给予大鼠半纯化饮食会导致肾脏形态学改变，与疾病一致(18)。这些观察结果与文献一致，文献表明天然磷来源的吸收率明显低于无机磷盐补充剂(6,17,19)。
Dobenecker et al.(6) recently showed that feeding a high-P diet, with a low Ca:P, to cats for 29 d caused glucosuria and microalbuminuria with a decreased creatinine clearance; all of which are indicators of kidney dysfunction(6). The use of phosphate supplements as low as 0·5 % by DM weight of diet has been shown to cause renal and vascular calcification in adult rats(14,17). In addition, the feeding of semi-purified diets to rats has been shown to result in morphological changes to the kidneys, consistent with the disease, when compared with diets comprising P from a mostly natural source(18). These observations are consistent with the literature suggesting that the absorption of natural sources of P is considerably lower than inorganic phosphate salt supplements(6,17,19).
我们团队最近的研究发现，猫咪食用含总磷 4.8 g/4184 kJ（1000 kcal）的饮食，其中 3.5 g P/4184 kJ（1000 kcal）以无机磷酸二氢钠（SDHP）形式存在，钙磷比为 0.6，连续喂食 4 周，会出现不可逆的肾脏变化，其特点是肾小球滤过率（GFR）降低、血清肌酐升高、尿白蛋白:肌酐比升高，以及肾脏结构异常的迹象（20）。在随后的研究中，成年猫食用含 SDHP 形式磷 1.5 g/4184 kJ（1000 kcal）、总磷 3.6 g/4184 kJ（1000 kcal）、钙磷比为 0.9 的饮食超过 28 周，血液生化指标平均未显示肾功能异常；然而，部分猫超声检查显示新形成的肾结石和肾结构变化（20）。在测试组中有三例例外，这些猫在喂食期末期出现肾功能异常的迹象，从喂食第 21 周起，血液生化指标显示出临床相关的变化。因此，需要更好地理解膳食磷的吸收情况。
Recent work from our group found that cats fed diets containing total P of 4·8 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal), of which 3·5 g P/4184 kJ (1000 kcal) was in the form of inorganic sodium dihydrogen phosphate (SDHP) and Ca:P = 0·6 for 4 weeks, developed irreversible kidney changes characterised by reduced glomerular filtration rate, elevated serum creatinine and urinary albumin:creatinine ratio and evidence of structural abnormalities within the kidney(20). In a follow-up study, adult cats fed a diet with P from SDHP inclusion of 1·5 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal), 3·6 g total P/4184 kJ (1000 kcal) and Ca:P = 0·9 for over 28 weeks showed no mean changes in blood biochemistry indicative of renal dysfunction; however, there was sonographic evidence indicative of new renoliths and renal structural changes in some cats(20). There were three exceptions in the test group, where cats developed signs of renal dysfunction towards the end of the feeding period, with clinically relevant changes in blood biochemistry apparent from week 21 of feeding. Therefore, there is a need to better understand dietary P absorption.

Although there are data for postprandial serum or plasma P in cats(8) and dogs(21), these measures and the associated regulatory responses have been examined in greater detail in healthy humans as well as in humans with CKD(22). Dietary management of CKD aims to limit P load on the kidneys through a reduction in P intake, but various strategies are also applied to reduce bioavailability, including the use of phosphate binding medications, as well as dietary manipulation by applying different P sources(23,24). The findings of these studies in humans demonstrate that ingestion of added inorganic P increased serum phosphate concentrations in one study(24) and that plasma P decreased following consumption of vegetarian diets in another(23,24), highlighting that postprandial plasma P kinetics are different when different sources of P are ingested. Karp et al.(25) found similar trends in serum phosphate and urinary P excretion, when tripolyphosphate and monophosphate supplements were consumed.
此外，健康的男性志愿者在摄入500毫克磷补充剂后，显示餐后血清磷水平升高，而在服用安慰剂后则出现短暂下降(26)。由于与血浆磷调节相关的生理后果，血浆磷的持续升高被认为是有害的(14)。有研究表明，随着时间的推移，FGF-23的增加可能导致钙依赖性甲状旁腺激素（PTH）抑制的丧失，以及FGF-23本身对甲状旁腺激素（PTH）的抑制，这可能进一步导致肾脏和甲状旁腺等器官的损伤(27,28)。迄今为止，关于影响猫餐后血浆磷浓度的因素的研究相对较少。
In addition, healthy male volunteers fed a 500 mg P supplement showed that serum phosphate increased postprandially, while a transient decrease was apparent after a placebo(26). Sustained increases in plasma P are thought to be harmful due to the physiological consequences associated with the regulation of plasma P(14). There is a suggestion that over time, increased FGF-23 can lead to a loss of Ca-dependent inhibition of PTH, and suppression of PTH by FGF-23 itself, which in turn could lead to damage to organs such as the kidney and parathyroid gland(27,28). To date relatively little work has been undertaken to understand the factors affecting postprandial plasma P concentrations in cats.

This knowledge is vital, particularly as evidence is accumulating to suggest that dietary P may contribute to the aetiology of diseases such as CKD or CVD. Responses to differences in dietary P levels as well as differing bioavailability of various sources of dietary P present in the diets have been determined in cats and dogs(3,6,21,29). With this in mind, we describe herein a series of studies designed to evaluate the postprandial profiles of P in the plasma of cats following a range of dietary interventions.

抽血

Blood sampling
在经过一夜禁食后，给猫咪局部表面麻醉（1毫升 EMLA™ 乳膏 5%；阿斯利康），并置入一根22G头静脉导管，该导管在采样期间保持在位。在采样前进行0.2毫升肝素闪洗并弃液，每次采样后进行2毫升生理盐水冲洗以保持通畅。采集禁食血样以提供基线，然后根据每项具体研究的描述在不同时间间隔采集进一步的样本，直至包括进食后6小时的样本采集。
Following an overnight fast, cats were given topical local anaesthesia (1 ml EMLA™ cream 5 %; AstraZeneca) and a 22G cephalic catheter was positioned, which remained in place for the duration of the sampling period. A 0·2 ml heparin flush and discard was performed before sampling and a 2 ml saline flush followed each sample to maintain patency. A fasted blood sample was collected to provide a baseline, with further samples taken at intervals described in each specific study up to, and including, 6 h post-feeding.

血样处理及分析

Blood sample processing and analysis
血液样本（0.5毫升）采集到锂肝素管中，并保持在冰上，直至在采样后的60分钟内进行分析。使用 Stat Profile Prime 兽医重症监护分析仪（Nova Biomedicals）测定全血中的 iCa。通过在样品测定前后使用三水平质量控制材料和离子选择电极分析来验证仪器性能。其余血样通过在4°C下以2000 g离心10分钟分离血浆。按照厂家说明，使用 AU480 临床化学分析仪（Beckman Coulter）在非灼烧血浆上光度法定量测定磷（以正磷酸盐形式），并以 mmol/l 报告浓度。在测定样品当日的前、中、后，使用双水平质量控制材料以确认仪器性能符合要求。
Blood samples (0·5 ml) were collected into lithium heparin tubes and kept on ice until analysis was performed within 60 min of sampling. iCa was measured in whole blood using a Stat Profile Prime Veterinary Critical Care Analyser (Nova Biomedicals). Performance of the instrument was verified by analysis of tri-level quality control material before and after sample measurement via an ion-selective electrode. Plasma was isolated by centrifugation of the remaining blood samples at 2000 g for 10 min at 4°C. P, in the form of orthophosphate, was photometrically quantified on non-ashed plasma using an AU480 clinical chemistry analyser (Beckman Coulter) according to the manufacturer’s instructions and concentrations reported in mmol/l. Bi-level quality control material before, midway and after sample day measurements was used to confirm acceptable instrument performance.
血浆PTH和FGF-23的分析在伦敦皇家兽医学院进行。血液样本（每项测量1毫升）采集于含EDTA的管中，通过在4°C下以2000 g离心10分钟获得血浆，然后储存于–80°C直至分析。全长血浆FGF-23使用人源双抗体夹心ELISA（Kainos Laboratories）测定，如前所述(31)，PTH浓度则通过先前已验证适用于猫样本的RIA测定(32)。
Analysis of plasma PTH and FGF-23 were performed at the Royal Veterinary College, London. Blood samples (1 ml for each measure) were collected in EDTA containing tubes and plasma obtained by centrifugation at 2000 g for 10 min at 4°C, before being stored at –80°C until analysis. Intact plasma FGF-23 was measured using a human sandwich ELISA (Kainos Laboratories), as previously described(31), and PTH concentrations by a RIA previously validated for use with feline samples(32).

尿液样本分析

Urine sample analysis
为评估肾脏磷排泄，收集了喂食测试饮食后24小时内排出的所有尿液。猫在这24小时采集期间单独饲养，尿液收集在含疏水性沙或玻璃珠的猫砂盆中。尿液样本被用浓盐酸酸化至pH 2（±0.1），并在4°C下以2000 g离心5分钟，然后在AU480分析仪上进行磷分析，如上所述。
All urine passed over 24 h following the feeding of the test diet were collected to assess renal P excretion. Cats were individually housed during this 24 h collection period, and urine was collected in a litter tray containing hydrophobic sand or glass beads. Urine samples were acidified to pH 2 (±0·1) using concentrated hydrochloric acid, and centrifuged at 2000 g for 5 min at 4°C before P analysis was undertaken on the AU480 analyser as described above.

统计功效

Statistical powering
在完成一项试点研究后，进行了样本量分析，以研究在交叉研究设计中使用 2、3 或 4 种饮食时，需要多少只猫才能在测量 6、8 或 10 个时间点时，以至少 80% 的检验力检测血浆磷（P）水平 5–20% 的倍数变化。该试点研究的血浆磷水平用于估计猫之间的变异性（sd=0.0261 log10 mmol/l）、猫内不同饮食之间的变异性（sd=0.0393 log10 mmol/l）以及猫内相同饮食的变异性（sd=0.0395 log10 mmol/l）。基线喂食前水平也估计为 0.0816 log10 mmol/l。然后根据这些变异性估计进行数据模拟（每种情景模拟 1000 次），并在一个时间点人为引入一种饮食的差异。分析按照餐后试点数据分析方法进行（如下所示）。检验力通过模拟中所有相对已改变均值的比较均显著的百分比进行估计，整体家庭误差率为 5%.
Following completion of a pilot study, sample size analyses were performed to investigate the number of cats needed in a crossover study design, with 2, 3 or 4 diets, to detect fold changes with at least 80 % power in plasma P levels of 5–20 % when measuring 6, 8 or 10 time points. Plasma P levels from this pilot study were used to estimate the between-cat variability (sd=0·0261 log10 mmol/l), between-diet within-cat variability (sd=0·0393 log10 mmol/l) and within diet within cat variability (sd=0·0395 log10 mmol/l). The baseline pre-feed levels were also estimated to be 0·0816 log10 mmol/l. Data were then simulated (1000 for each scenario) according to these variability estimates and by inducing a difference for one diet at one time point. The analysis was performed, as per the postprandial pilot data analysis (given below). The power was estimated by the percentage of simulations where all comparisons relative to the mean that was changed were significant, at the overall family-wise error rate of 5 %.

统计分析

Statistical analysis
对于每项研究，使用线性混合效应模型（LMM）分析餐后血浆磷（P）水平（主要测量指标）、iCa、PTH 和 FGF-23，其中时间嵌套在饮食中，饮食又嵌套在动物中作为随机效应，饮食、时间点及其交互作用作为分类固定效应。在分析前，对于血浆 P、PTH 和 FGF-23 数据进行了 log10 转换，因为残差随着数值增大而变异增大的情况被观察到。对于某些测量，由于未采样或实验室设备故障，数据缺失；然而，分析使用了受限最大似然（REML）方法来处理，并假设这些值是随机缺失的。在每个测量指标中，使用族内调整的 5% 水平，在每个时间点比较不同饮食之间的差异，以及在每种饮食中比较禁食状态与随后的每个餐后时间点之间的差异。因此，报告了均值、均值差异或倍数变化，并提供了 95% 的族内置信区间。
For each study, the postprandial plasma P levels (primary measure), iCa, PTH and FGF-23 were analysed using linear mixed effects models (LMM), with time nested in diet nested in animal as the random effects and diet, time point and their interaction as the categorical fixed effects. Plasma P, PTH and FGF-23 data were log10 transformed before analyses where increasing variation in residuals with increasing value was observed. For some measures, data were missing because samples were not taken or of failure of laboratory equipment; however, the restricted maximal likelihood (REML) method of analysis was used to account for this and assumed these values were missing at random. Comparisons were made, using a family-wise adjusted 5 % level for each measure, between diets at each time point and between fasted to each subsequent postprandial time point for each diet. Accordingly, means, differences between means or fold changes were reported with 95 % family-wise CI.
此外，对于每项研究中每种饮食下的每只猫，首先从基线时的血浆磷（P）值中减去所有后续时间点的数值，然后使用梯形法则计算以 mmol/l × h 为单位的曲线下面积（AUC）。为每项试验分别拟合线性混合模型（LMM），以 AUC 作为响应变量。通过对残差的目视检查评估分布假设，所有模型均认为假设成立。每个模型的固定效应为饮食，随机效应为嵌套在动物中的饮食。每个模型中，各种饮食的平均 AUC 及其 95% 置信区间（CI）均有报告。饮食之间的比较以差异、95% CI 及 P 值形式报告。尿磷排泄通过 LMM 分析，猫为随机效应，饮食为固定效应。使用 5% 水平对饮食进行比较。均值及均值之间的差异均报告 95% CI。
In addition, for each cat on each diet in each study, the plasma P at baseline was subtracted from all post-baseline time points, and the trapezium rule was then used to calculate the AUC in mmol/l × h. Separate LMM were fit for each trial with AUC as the response. Distributional assumptions were assessed by visual inspection of the residuals and were deemed to be upheld for all models. The fixed effect for each model was diet, and the random effect was diet nested in animal. From each model, mean AUC with 95 % CI are reported for all diets. Comparisons between diets are reported as differences with 95 % CI and P values. Urinary P excretion was analysed by LMM with cat as a random effect and diet as a fixed effect. A comparison was made between diets using the 5 % level. Means and differences between means are reported with 95 % CI.

Studies 3 and 4 suggest that diets formulated using SDHP v. STPP caused subtle differences in the postprandial plasma P concentrations. This response is broadly in agreement with the findings reported by Karp et al.(25), where ingestion of monophosphate and tripolyphosphate supplements by young healthy women resulted in similar changes in serum phosphate, but urinary Ca excretion reduced following the tripolyphosphate compared with the monophosphate supplement. This was suggested to be due to tripolyphosphate binding more Ca than the monophosphate supplement in the intestine(25). The lack of difference between plasma kinetic profiles of mono- and tripolyphosphates may be due to monophosphates being the only form of P that can be absorbed by the intestine and cross into the bloodstream, according to data in serially killed rats(48). The data shown here demonstrate that SDHP had a larger P AUC compared with that seen in STPP, suggesting that as a source of inorganic P it may have a greater impact on homoeostasis of plasma P than STPP in the cat.
这种差异是否具有生物学意义尚不清楚，但这一发现值得进一步研究。在狗中，已有研究表明，添加含钠和钾的无机磷盐会使餐后2小时血清磷和甲状旁腺激素（PTH）超过正常范围（21）。膳食磷的生物利用度被认为不仅受到磷的来源和膳食含量的影响，还受到钙磷比（Ca:P）的影响，当Ca:P从约1增加到接近2时，会降低磷的消化率和尿磷排泄（2,7,49–51）。为了研究Ca:P对餐后血浆磷动力学的影响，对本研究数据进行了综合分析。结果显示，Ca:P小于1的饮食A和K的血浆磷曲线下面积（AUC）显著高于Ca:P大于1.0的饮食（图10），这种反应主要是由于血浆磷的长期升高所致（图9）。
Whether this difference is biologically meaningful is unclear, but this finding warrants further investigation. In dogs, it has been shown that added inorganic P salts containing Na and K increase 2 h postprandial serum P and PTH above normal ranges(21). The bioavailability of dietary P is considered to be influenced not only by the P source and content of the diet but also by the Ca:P, with an increase in the Ca:P from around 1 to nearly 2, reducing P digestibility and urinary P excretion(2,7,49–51). To investigate the influence of Ca:P on postprandial plasma P kinetics, a composite analysis of the present data was undertaken. This highlighted that diets A and K with a Ca:P <1 had a significantly higher plasma P AUC (Fig. 10) compared with those with a Ca:P >1·0, with this response primarily resulting from a prolonged elevation in plasma P (Fig. 9).
有趣的是，尽管饮食 I 和 K 的总无机磷（P）和添加无机磷水平非常相似，当猫食用钙磷比（Ca:P）为 1.59 的饮食 I 时，血浆磷在 3 小时内回到基线，这进一步支持了这种反应可能是由饮食 Ca:P 的差异引起的。文献中提到的饮食 Ca:P 导致生物利用度降低的机制被认为是由于额外的钙在肠道中与镁形成不溶性、因此不可生物利用的钙—镁—磷酸盐复合物(50)。这种不溶复合物的形成很可能是增加饮食镁含量也能降低磷吸收百分比和尿磷含量的原因(52)。饮食中钙的可用性对于其形成这些不溶复合物的能力也至关重要。对氯化钙和碳酸钙补充差异的研究发现，氯化钙在减少尿磷存在和增加保留率方面比碳酸钙具有更好的效果(49)。
Interestingly, despite the remarkably similar total and added inorganic P levels between diets I and K, plasma P returned to baseline within 3 h when cats were fed diet I with a Ca:P of 1·59, adding further support that this response may have resulted from the difference in dietary Ca:P. The mechanism behind the reduction in bioavailability due to Ca:P in diet stated in the literature is thought to be due to extra Ca being available to form insoluble, and hence non-bioavailable, Ca–Mg–phosphate complexes in the intestinal tract(50). This insoluble complex formation is likely to be the reason that increasing Mg content of the diet has also been shown to decrease the percentage of P absorption and urinary P content(52). The availability of Ca in the diet is also essential for its ability to form these insoluble complexes. Investigation into the differences between calcium chloride and calcium carbonate supplementation has found that calcium chloride had better capacity than calcium carbonate for reducing the presence of P in the urine, increasing retention(49).
需要注意的是，不仅是饮食 I 和 K 在钙磷比（Ca:P）上有所不同；饮食 K 的粗纤维含量也较低，并且食物的碱基超量为负（表 2）。粗纤维会增加粪便干物质和体积，从而可能降低磷和其他矿物质的表观消化率(53)。饮食碱基超量的潜在影响已经在猫体内进行研究，结果显示虽然对血浆磷浓度或磷的表观消化率没有影响，但已知其他矿物质如钙的排泄会受到影响，因此可能对这里显示的一些结果有所贡献(54,55)。另一个可能改变磷消化率和吸收的因素是饮食基质。基于湿块或干颗粒技术的饮食（或表 1 中所述的形式）可能通过改变食物通过时间来影响磷的可利用性，这很可能是由不同基质之间的能量密度差异所驱动(56,57)。
It is important to note that it was not only the Ca:P that differed between diets I and K; diet K also contained less crude fibre and had a negative food base excess (Table 2). Crude fibre increases faecal DM and bulk, which in turn can decrease apparent digestibility of P and other minerals(53). The potential impact of the base excess of the diet has been studied in cats, where although there was no effect on plasma P concentrations or apparent digestibility of P, the excretion of other minerals such as Ca are known to be affected by this and therefore could have contributed to some of the findings shown here(54,55). Another factor that may alter the digestibility and absorption of P is the diet matrix. Diets based on wet chunk or dry kibble technologies (or format as stated in Table 1) may affect the availability of P through changes in transit times, most likely driven by differences in energy density between the matrices(56,57).
对C和L两种湿块状饮食的数据进行可视化评估，并与干颗粒饮食J进行比较，表明干饮食中血浆磷（P）在60分钟后开始升高，而在湿饮食中，血浆P在这一时间点与基线没有显著差异。除此之外，在此处研究的少量饮食中，似乎不同饮食形式之间的差异不大。要得出进一步结论需要在同一研究中比较更多的饮食。需要注意的一点是，这些研究中的饮食配方使用了相似的原材料（在饮食矩阵内），通过不同比例调整总磷或钙磷比。因此，我们无法获知其他原材料（如替代钙或磷来源）是否会对血浆P浓度产生相同的影响。
Visual assessment of data from diets C and L, both wet chunk formats, compared with diet J, a dry kibble diet, suggests that plasma P starts to rise after 60 min in the dry format, while in wet plasma P was not significantly different to baseline at this time point. Aside from this difference, there appears to be little to distinguish between the diet formats in the small set of diets studied here. To draw further conclusions would require a comparison of more diets within the same study. One point to note is that diets for these studies were formulated using similar raw materials (within diet matrix) included at various ratios to alter the total P or Ca:P. As such, we have no information on whether other raw materials (such as alternative Ca or P sources) would have the same effect on plasma P concentrations.
研究表明，当长期喂食猫咪饮食A和K时，会对肾功能产生不良影响(20)。这一观察结果与Dobenecker等人(3,6)的研究总体一致，他们报告在喂食高添加无机磷的饮食并与总磷和补充磷均较低的对照饮食比较时，肾功能指标有所升高。与其他测试的饮食相比，饮食A和K表现出明显不同的动力学特征，在研究的6小时餐后期间，血浆磷水平持续升高。饮食A和K之间观察到的血浆磷动力学特征、离子钙(iCa)和甲状旁腺激素(PTH)浓度的差异，提示磷稳态调控存在差异。两种饮食中SDHP的不同水平显然解释了喂食饮食A的猫血浆磷峰值更高，这种饮食的钙磷比也低于饮食K。这两个因素可能解释了与喂食饮食K相比，喂食饮食A的猫血浆PTH显著升高、血浆iCa下降以及尿磷排泄增加的现象。
Diets A and K have been shown to cause adverse effects on renal function in cats when fed over prolonged periods(20). This observation is broadly in agreement with Dobenecker et al.(3,6) who reported an increase in indicators of kidney dysfunction when a diet high in added inorganic P was fed and compared with a control diet low in both total and supplemented P. Diets A and K displayed a distinct kinetic profile compared with all the other diets tested, showing an extended elevation in plasma P across the 6 h postprandial period investigated. The differences in plasma P kinetic profiles, iCa and PTH concentrations observed between diets A and K suggest differences in the regulation of P homoeostasis. Differing levels of SDHP in the two diets apparently explain the higher peak in plasma P in cats fed diet A, which also had a lower Ca:P than diet K. These two factors may explain the marked increase in plasma PTH, decrease in plasma iCa and increased urinary P excretion observed in cats fed diet A compared with diet K.
饮食 A 的钠含量也较高（每 4184 kJ（1000 kcal）含 3.33 g，相比于每 4184 kJ（1000 kcal）含 1.35 g）且粗纤维含量较低（每 4184 kJ（1000 kcal）含 5.2 g，相比于每 4184 kJ（1000 kcal）含 7.49 g），与饮食 K 相比。已有研究报道，增加钠摄入可通过增加依赖钠的磷酸盐摄取来提高大鼠对矿物质的磷吸收(58)。此外，减少粗纤维的饮食可增加人类对矿物质的吸收(59)。考虑到这些差异，令人惊讶的是，Alexander 等人(20) 在测量 5 天消化率时发现，饮食 A 的磷表观消化率平均为 49.3%，饮食 K 为 51.5%，这可能表明在此情况下，饮食差异对消化率并不重要。在该期间末期，食用饮食 A 的猫血浆磷轻微下降，表明磷调节因素（如 PTH）可能被触发并对大量磷负荷做出反应，试图将血浆磷水平恢复到正常。
Diet A also contained higher Na (3·33 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal) compared with 1·35 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal)) and less crude fibre (5·2 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal) compared with 7·49 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal)) than diet K. Increasing Na has been reported to increase P absorption of minerals through increasing Na+-dependent uptake of phosphate in rats(58). In addition, diets with reduced crude fibre show increased absorption of minerals in humans(59). With these differences in mind, it is surprising to note that when 5 d digestibility was measured by Alexander et al.(20) the mean apparent digestibility of P was 49·3 % for diet A and 51·5 % for diet K and may indicate that the dietary differences are not important for digestibility in this case. The subtle reduction in plasma P towards the end of this period in cats fed diet A indicates that P regulatory factors (e.g. PTH) are likely triggered and responding to the large P load in an attempt to return plasma P to normal.
这在某种程度上模仿了 Hazim 等人(26)报道的反应，当健康男性摄入 500 毫克无机磷补充剂并配以高脂餐时。餐后 6 小时的持续血浆磷水平使得血浆磷在下一餐时仍可能升高，这可能会使肾脏和其他组织在每餐后暴露于逐渐升高的磷浓度，从而解释了当这些饮食长期喂给猫时观察到的不良影响(20)。已显示摄入低钙磷比饮食会导致营养性甲状旁腺功能亢进，导致猫甲状旁腺增大，推测这是为了维持血浆钙浓度(3)。甲状旁腺激素（PTH）的增加促进肠道中钙和磷的吸收，并促进尿磷的排泄(3)。餐后血浆磷的长期升高也可能导致甲状旁腺激素（PTH）的增加，从而降低血浆离子钙水平。已建议增加成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF-23）-23(FGF-23)的产生有助于维持磷的稳态(11,21)。如本数据和 Alexander 等人所示。
This somewhat mimics the response reported by Hazim et al.(26), when healthy men ingested a 500 mg inorganic P supplement with a high fat meal. The sustained plasma P levels 6 h postprandially make it distinctly possible that plasma P may still be elevated at the time of the next feed, potentially exposing the kidneys and other tissues to incrementally higher P concentrations with each meal, which may explain the adverse effects that were observed when these diets were fed to cats over prolonged periods(20). Consumption of a diet with a low Ca:P has been shown to cause nutritional hyperparathyroidism, resulting in enlargement of the parathyroid in cats, presumably as an attempt to maintain plasma Ca concentration(3). The increase in PTH stimulates intestinal absorption of Ca and P and promotes urinary P excretion(3). A prolonged elevation in plasma P postprandially could also lead to increases in PTH production and thereby a reduction in plasma iCa. Increased FGF-23 production has been suggested to aid in maintaining P homoeostasis(11,21). As suggested by data herein and Alexander et al.
(20)，FGF-23 可能在晚期阶段对高升高的血浆磷（P）水平的反应比 PTH 更敏感。研究还表明，如果 FGF-23 水平保持高位，血浆钙（Ca）浓度低时 PTH 的反应会减弱，这可能是长期摄入高可利用性膳食磷导致肾损伤的一种机制(27)。因此，对参与磷稳态调节因素的定量研究对于理解高膳食磷水平的潜在影响至关重要。在本研究中，发现无机磷来源可剂量依赖性地升高血浆磷水平，同时类似地改变血浆 PTH；这种反应显然与总磷和钙磷比（Ca:P）无关。120 分钟时 PTH 达到峰值的例外是饮食 A，如上所述，其在餐后 4 小时升高；以及饮食 K，尽管血浆磷持续升高，但在试验当天未引起 PTH 增加。

Studies have also shown that if FGF-23 levels remain high, there is a reduced PTH response to low plasma Ca concentrations, which could be a mechanism by which kidney damage may occur if highly available dietary P is consumed long-term(27). Quantification of regulatory factors involved in P homoeostasis are therefore essential to understanding the potential impact of high levels of dietary P. In this study, inorganic P sources were found to dose-dependently increase plasma P levels, similarly altering plasma PTH; this response was apparently independent of total P and Ca:P. The exceptions to this peak PTH at 120 min were diet A, which was increased at 4 h post-meal as discussed above, and diet K, which did not cause an increase in PTH during the trial day despite the sustained elevation of plasma P.
此外，与早期的一项大鼠研究一致，这种PTH的增加发生在膳食调整之间iCa没有对应显著下降的情况下，这表明血浆磷可能直接影响PTH的产生（60）。虽然在概念验证研究之后的研究中血浆磷数据仍显示不同膳食之间的差异，但在iCa或尿磷排泄方面未检测到差异。这可能是由于添加的无机磷水平较低（0–1.5 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal)）相比于膳食A（3.5 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal)）。因此，在采样当天，通过膳食添加无机磷可能没有足够刺激，无法引起iCa的下降和/或尿磷排泄的显著增加。24小时尿磷数据在膳食之间没有显著差异可能有两个原因。首先，本研究方案中没有其他膳食像膳食A那样引起血浆磷或PTH的大幅增加（也没有引起iCa的下降）。
In addition, in agreement with an earlier rat study, this increase in PTH occurred without a corresponding significant reduction in iCa between dietary manipulations, suggesting that plasma P may directly affect PTH production(60). Although the plasma P data continued to show differences between diets in the studies following the proof of principle study, differences were not detected in either iCa or urinary excretion of P. This may be due to the lower added inorganic P inclusion levels (0–1·5 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal)) compared with diet A (3·5 g/4184 kJ (1000 kcal)). Consequently there may not have been sufficient stimulus, induced by added inorganic P to the diet, during the sampling day to induce a decrease in iCa and/or a significant increase in urinary P excretion. A lack of significant differences between diets for 24 h urinary P data may be due to two factors. First, no other diets studied in this programme provoked such a large increase in plasma P or PTH (nor a decrease in iCa) as diet A.
这可能表明，在尿中磷排泄发生可测量变化之前，必须达到某个阈值。其次，所使用的方法意味着在开始收集前无法保证膀胱是空的，也无法保证在完成收集前膀胱被排空。因此，有可能在每一个收集点上，并非所有猫在研究日排出的磷都被收集到。有趣的是，尽管24小时磷排泄没有可测量的差异，但在多周喂食下，K 饮食导致的尿磷排泄增加，相比对照组(20)。因此，单次喂食和24小时尿液收集可能不足以识别饮食之间更微妙的差异。总之，这也表明，尽管该模型在显示单次喂食后6小时内饮食差异方面是有用的，但需要长期喂养研究来确定血浆磷动力学波动的后果。
This may indicate a threshold must be reached before any changes in urinary excretion of P are measurable. Second, the methodology used meant that there was no guarantee of an empty bladder before commencing the collection and no guarantee of the bladder being emptied before finishing the collection. As such it is possible that not all the excreted P was collected from all cats on the study day at every collection point. Interestingly, despite no measurable differences in 24 h P excretion, diet K resulted in increased urinary excretion of P when fed over several weeks compared with the control group(20). Thus, a single feed and a 24 h urine collection may not be enough to identify more subtle differences between diets. In summary, this also indicates that although this model is useful in showing differences between diets following the 6 h after a single feed, long-term feeding studies are required to determine the consequences of fluctuations in plasma P kinetics.
根据此处提供的数据，无法推断在第二餐后是否会观察到类似的动力学特征，也无法推测超过6小时时间范围的反应。此外，应当认识到，由于未包含骨吸收标志物以及在24小时尿液收集中使用的方法，关于调节磷（P）稳态的机制得出确定性结论可能存在局限性。总之，目前这一系列研究的结果为日益增多的文献提供了补充，表明在饮食中加入无机磷会导致血浆磷呈剂量依赖性升高，同时伴随甲状旁腺激素（PTH）的升高。然而，这种关系并不简单，当饮食中的钙磷比(Ca:P) <1.0时，与饮食Ca:P >1.5时相比，血浆磷餐后持续升高。此外，我们提供了有限证据表明，添加无机磷的来源可以影响餐后磷的动力学，但仍需进一步研究以表征与食品制造相关的其他形式的反应。
From the data presented here, it is not possible to deduce whether similar kinetic profiles will be observed after a second meal or to extrapolate responses beyond the 6 h time frame. In addition, it should be recognised that firm conclusions around the mechanisms governing P homoeostasis may be limited due to the omission of markers of bone resorption and the methods employed during the 24 h urine collections. In conclusion, the results of the present series of studies add to the growing literature, suggesting that inclusion of added inorganic P in diets results in a dose-dependent increase in plasma P, with a concurrent elevation in PTH(26,29). This relationship is far from straightforward, however, with a sustained postprandial elevation in plasma P apparent when diets with a Ca:P <1·0 were fed compared with that seen when the diet Ca:P was >1·5. In addition, we present limited evidence that the source of added inorganic P can influence postprandial P kinetics, but further work is required to characterise the responses to other forms relevant to food manufacturing.
