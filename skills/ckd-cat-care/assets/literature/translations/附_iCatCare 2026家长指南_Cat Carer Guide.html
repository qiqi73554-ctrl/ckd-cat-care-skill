How CKD is diagnosed A diagnosis of CKD is made by testing blood and urine samples. Substances in the blood (including urea and creatinine) will be increased due to the kidneys’ problems in filtering waste products from the blood.

慢性肾病（CKD）的“分期”：为什么很重要？国际肾脏兴趣学会（IRIS）是由兽医专家组成的小组，已经制定了指导方针，以协助兽医在诊断和治疗狗和猫的肾脏疾病时提供帮助。其核心是基于血液和尿液检测的已建立“分期”方法。通过分配一个“阶段”——从1期（早期/轻度疾病）到4期（严重疾病）——可以为患有CKD的猫提供适当的治疗和监测。随后建议进一步进行“亚分期”，这基于尿液中的蛋白质含量和猫的血压水平。猫的CKD阶段会随着时间的推移逐渐增加，因此定期重新评估非常重要。处于较高疾病阶段的猫可能需要更多的治疗以保持其健康状态。
‘Staging’ of CKD: why is it important? The International Renal Interest Society (IRIS), a panel of expert veterinarians, has produced guidance to assist veterinary practitioners in diagnosing and treating kidney diseases in dogs and cats. Central to this is an established method of ‘staging’ CKD based on blood and urine tests. By assigning a ‘stage’ – from 1 (early/mild disease) to 4 (severe disease) – appropriate treatment and monitoring can be facilitated in cats with CKD. A further process of ‘substaging’ is then recommended, based both on the amount of protein in the urine and the cat’s blood pressure. A cat’s CKD stage will progressively increase over time, and so regular reassessment is important. Cats at higher stages of disease will likely require more treatments to keep them feeling well.

对于患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫咪来说，适当的饮食和药物的提供是管理的基石，但也有各种方法可以优化家庭环境以惠及受影响的猫咪。
An appropriate diet and provision of medications are the cornerstones of management for cats with CKD, but there are also various ways in which the home environment can be optimised to benefit affected cats.
这些修改涉及五个关键领域，被称为健康猫咪环境的‘五大支柱’（图8）。
These modifications cover five key areas, which are referred to as the ‘five pillars’ of a healthy feline environment (Figure 8).

支柱三：提供玩耍和表现捕食行为的机会

Pillar 3: Provide opportunity to play and perform
即使是患有慢性疾病的老猫也能从活动中受益。镇痛（止痛药）可能帮助患有退行性关节病的猫进行轻度活动和低强度玩耍，这也有助于激发其参与的情绪并预防便秘。可以鼓励猫咪在花园里散步，或在短时间内与钓鱼玩具互动。使用带有声音或气味刺激的玩具（例如沙沙材料、猫薄荷、缬草、银藤）、在可预测的时间和地点进行游戏、采用短而一致的游戏时间，并提供侧放的纸箱，有助于安全、舒适的互动。
predatory behaviour Even older cats with chronic illness will benefit from activity. Analgesia (pain medication) may help cats with DJD engage with light activity and low-impact play, which can also help trigger engaging emotions and prevent constipation. Cats could be encouraged to walk around a garden or engage with a fishing toy for short periods. Incorporating toys with auditory or olfactory stimuli (eg, crinkle material, catnip, valerian, silvervine), playing at predictable times and locations, using short, consistent sessions, and offering cardboard boxes placed on their sides supports safe, comfortable engagement.

支柱四：提供积极、一致且可预测的环境

Pillar 4: Provide positive, consistent and predictable
人类与猫的社交互动 允许猫选择互动的类型和时长，绝不强迫抚摸。一些患有慢性疾病的猫可能会减少自我梳理，但喜欢被刷毛；其他猫仍可能对照顾者提供的偶尔零食感兴趣。家庭成员不应直接抱起猫，而应慢慢接近，并使用柔和的声音以防吓到猫。应允许猫根据自身意愿脱离与人的互动。
human–cat social interaction Allow cats to choose the type and duration of interaction and never force handling. Some cats with chronic illness may groom less and enjoy being brushed; others may still have the appetite for occasional treats from their caregivers. Family members should not pick up the cat, but instead approach slowly with a soft voice to prevent startling. The cat should be allowed to disengage from interactions with people at their choosing.

第 2 柱：提供多个分散的资源

Pillar 2: Provide multiple and separated resources
慢性肾病（CKD）猫可能需要更频繁地排尿和饮水，因此应方便它们使用猫砂盆和多处水源。资源应分布在家中各层，并且数量充足（每只猫至少两个此类资源）。食物和水可以放在高置碗中，对患有退行性关节疾病的猫提供水平抓挠机会。猫砂盆应为低边设计并铺有柔软底材，同时需要定期清洁。
Cats with CKD may need to urinate and drink more frequently, so should have easy access to litter trays and multiple water sources. Resources should be distributed across all levels of the home and in sufficient number (at least two of each resource per cat). Food and water can be offered in raised bowls, and horizontal scratching opportunities provided for cats with DJD. Litter trays should be low sided with a soft substrate and regularly cleaned.
