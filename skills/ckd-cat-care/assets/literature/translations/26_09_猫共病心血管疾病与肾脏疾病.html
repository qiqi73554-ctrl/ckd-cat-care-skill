实际意义：
Practical relevance:
心血管疾病和肾脏疾病在猫中非常普遍，尤其是在年长猫中，这两种疾病常常同时存在。心脏与肾脏之间的多种相互作用途径可能导致在其中一种或两种疾病发生时，这些系统功能逐渐下降。在猫中发现心脏或肾脏疾病应促使临床医生考虑该疾病及其治疗可能如何影响另一器官的功能，以及另一器官的共存原发性疾病可能如何影响病例管理和患者预后。
Cardiovascular and kidney diseases are highly prevalent and frequently coexistent in cats, particularly those of advanced age. Several pathways of interaction between the heart and kidneys likely contribute to progressive functional decline of these systems during disease of one or both. Identification of heart or kidney disease in a cat should prompt the clinician to consider how the disease and its treatment might impact the function of the other organ and how coexisting primary disease of the other might impact case management and patient prognosis.
临床挑战：
Clinical challenges:
在充血性心力衰竭、急性肾损伤或慢性肾病（CKD）的管理中，一个常见的目标是维持最佳体液平衡；然而，针对一个系统的许多治疗方法可能与另一个系统的最佳利益相冲突。例如，肾病的治疗通常包括静脉输液治疗，目的是维持平均动脉压和足够的肾血流，而利尿剂几乎总是心衰猫咪降低毛细血管静水压所必需的。在肾脏或心力衰竭的管理过程中，过度积极的容量扩张或减少可能导致另一器官系统受伤或承受过度压力。
A common goal in the management of congestive heart failure, acute kidney injury or chronic kidney disease is to maintain optimal body fluid balance; however, many therapies for disease of one system are at odds with the best interests of the other. For example, treatment of kidney disease often includes parenteral fluid therapy with the goal of maintaining mean arterial pressure and adequate renal blood flow, while diuretics are almost always necessary to reduce capillary hydrostatic pressure in cats with heart failure. Overly aggressive volume expansion or reduction during the management of kidney or heart failure, respectively, can lead to injury to or excessive stress on the other organ system.

心肾轴与心血管–肾脏疾病

The cardiorenal axis and cardiovascular–renal disorders
心脏、血管和肾脏的功能密切相关。考虑一个综合的“心肾轴”是理解这些复杂关系的有用视角。由兽医心血管-肾轴疾病共识小组定义，犬和猫的心血管-肾脏疾病是“由疾病、毒素或药物引起的对肾脏和/或心血管系统的结构和/或功能损伤，导致这些系统之间的正常相互作用受到干扰，从而持续损害一方或双方”。这一方法鼓励临床医生在任一或双方疾病发生时考虑心血管系统与肾脏之间的联系，以及它们与其他疾病状态的相互作用。此外，它强调了疾病在任一或双方进展过程中可能通过的、通常为双向的途径。
The functions of the heart, blood vessels and kidneys are highly interrelated. Consideration of an integrated ‘cardiorenal axis’ is a useful lens through which to view these complex relationships. As defined by the veterinary Cardiovascular–Renal Axis Disorders Consensus Group, cardiovascular– renal disorders of dogs and cats are ‘disease, toxin or drug-induced structural and/or functional damage to the kidney and/or cardiovascular system, leading to disruption of the normal interaction between these systems, to the ongoing detriment of one or both’. 9 This approach encourages clinicians to consider connections between the cardiovascular system and kidneys during disease of either or both, and their interactions with other disease conditions. RAAS 和 SNS 激活

RAAS and SNS activation
虽然肾素-血管紧张素-醛固酮系统（RAAS）和交感神经系统（SNS）的激活最初是为了补偿并支持肾小球滤过率（GFR），但它会导致慢性心肌和肾脏压力。心力衰竭可以被视为一种炎症性疾病，反应性氧种和促炎介质的产生会导致内皮功能障碍和组织纤维化。这些影响可能损害肾血流、氧气输送和GFR；损伤皮质髓质肾小管细胞；并促进肾间质炎症和纤维化，这些都是慢性肾病（CKD）进展的最终共同途径。在患有严重心脏疾病的人类患者中，肾静脉充血和腹内压升高在肾脏损伤中的主要病理生理作用也已得到公认；是否在猫中也是一个因素，目前尚未研究。
While initially compensatory and supportive of glomerular filtration rate (GFR), activation of the renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system (RAAS) and sympathetic nervous system (SNS) leads to chronic myocardial and kidney stress. 10 Heart failure can be viewed as an inflammatory condition,11–13 with the production of reactive oxygen species and pro-inflammatory mediators leading to endothelial dysfunction and tissue fibrosis. These effects can impair renal blood flow, oxygen delivery and GFR; damage corticomedullary renal tubular cells; and promote renal interstitial inflammation and fibrosis, both part of a final common pathway of disease progression in CKD. 14 In human patients with severe heart disease, the major pathophysiologic role of renal venous congestion and increased intra-abdominal pressure in kidney injury is also well recognized;15,16 whether this is a factor in cats is currently unstudied.

低血容量和高血容量

Hypo- and hypervolemia
尿毒症猫如果摄入不足，或因呕吐或腹泻导致液体丢失，可能由于必然性多尿而出现低血容量，从而导致心输出量下降。相反，肾脏疾病可能因积极的肠外液体治疗、异常的肾脏钠和水处理、肾素-血管紧张素-醛固酮系统（RAAS）激活以及慢性贫血而导致高血容量，所有这些因素都可能通过增加心脏工作负荷加剧心脏病和心力衰竭。患有肾脏疾病的猫常需进行肠外液体治疗，以应对过多的肾脏液体损失以及（在尿毒症时）胃肠液体损失。虽然补充液体可以恢复正常血容量和体液平衡，但其使用——尤其是含钠丰富的等渗晶体——如果未根据患者需求谨慎优化，可能导致过量的细胞外液体积。31 当对有潜在心脏疾病的猫长时间或以高速度输注时，这尤其成问题。同样，严重贫血的输血可导致输血相关的循环负荷过重。32

Systemic arterial hypertension is noted in 40–61% of cats with CKD33,34 and 28% of those with acute kidney injury (AKI). 35 It is the most well established pathophysiologic connection between kidney and heart diseases. The relationship between kidney disease and systemic arterial hypertension, 36 and the importance of the heart, vasculature and kidneys as targets of hypertensive end-organ damage, are detailed elsewhere.37,38 In small cross-sectional studies, 48– 85% of systemically hypertensive cats have echocardiographic evidence of hypertensive cardiomyopathy (Figure 3).39–42 However, the clinical relevance of these changes is unclear. Although a cardiovascular cause of death or complication at the time of euthanasia was assigned for 4 (21%) of 19 hypertensive cats who died in one study, 43 the authors of a separate report found no significant difference in mean survival time between hypertensive cats with or without echocardiographic abnormali-ties. 39 It is uncommon for a cat to develop CHF or arterial thromboembolism secondarily to systemic arterial hypertension alone.

电解质紊乱

Electrolyte derangements
肾脏疾病常伴有电解质紊乱。这些包括低钾血症和高钾血症、低钙血症和高钙血症以及高磷血症，这些在急性肾损伤（AKI）或慢性肾病（CKD）猫中常见，并可能影响心血管功能和结构。钾异常可改变心脏的兴奋性，导致心动过缓或心动过速（例如：窦性心动过缓、暂时性心房停顿、窦室节律、室性心动过速、心脏停搏）或心脏传导异常（图4）。在一项回顾性研究中，对96只因急性输尿管或尿道堵塞而出现氮质血症的猫，报道有45只（46%）存在高钾血症，多元分析显示血清钾浓度与心血管事件的发生显著相关——其中34%为心律失常。
Kidney disorders are frequently accompanied by electrolyte derangements. These include hypo- and hyperkalemia, hypo-and hypercalcemia, and hyperphosphatemia, which are common in cats with AKI45–48 or CKD45,49–51 and can affect cardiovascular function and structure. Potassium abnormalities can alter cardiac excitability and lead to brady- or tachyarrhythmias (eg, sinus bradycardia, transient atrial standstill, sinoventricular rhythm, ventricular tachyarrhythmias, asystole)52,53 or abnormalities of cardiac conduction (Figure 4). In one retrospective study of 96 azotemic cats with acute ureteral or urethral obstruction, hyperkalemia was reported in 45 (46%) and serum potassium concentration was significantly associated with the occurrence of cardiovascular events – 34% of which were arrhythmias – based on multivariate analysis.

与慢性肾病（CKD）相关的贫血

CKD-associated anemia
慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）的贫血随疾病阶段的进展而增加其发生率和严重性。50 除了加重肾脏缺氧和氧化应激并加速猫CKD的进展外，72 慢性贫血还会触发有害的适应性反应（例如RAAS和SNS激活），导致细胞外液体量增加、心脏负荷加重，并促进左心室和左心房肥大。73 这些影响可能具有临床相关性（图5）：在一项报告中，对八只严重贫血（即红细胞比容 ⩽18%）且无基础心脏疾病或系统性动脉高血压的猫，发现左心增大和充血性心力衰竭分别发生在100%和71%的猫中。74 尽管这些数据几乎肯定受到选择偏差的影响——所纳入的猫必须接受超声心动图检查——它们仍说明贫血可能带来的严重心血管后果。
The anemia of CKD increases in prevalence and severity with disease stage. 50 In addition to exacerbating renal hypoxia and oxidative stress and accelerating CKD progression in cats, 72 chronic anemia triggers detrimental adaptations (eg, RAAS and SNS activation) that expand extracellular fluid volume, increase cardiac workload, and promote left ventricular and left atrial hypertrophy. 73 These effects can be clinically relevant (Figure 5): in one report of eight severely anemic (ie, hematocrit ⩽18%) cats without underlying heart disease or systemic arterial hypertension, left heart enlargement and CHF were noted in 100% and 71%, respectively. 74 While these data are almost certainly influenced by selection bias – included cats had to have undergone echocardiography – they illustrate the potentially serious cardiovascular consequences of anemia.

RAAS 和 SNS 激活

RAAS and SNS activation
与心脏病类似，肾脏疾病中RAAS和SNS的过度激活也会发生，与贫血无关（图1）。如上所述，慢性血流动力学（例如血容量和血压的升高）和非血流动力学（例如促炎、促纤维化、促氧化应激）效应可能对肾脏和心血管系统造成损害。虽然在患有肾脏疾病的猫中循环RAAS激活的证据不一致，但局部、独立调节的肾内RAAS激活可能是存在的。
As in heart disease, excessive activation of the RAAS and SNS occurs in kidney disease independent of anemia (Figure 1). As mentioned above, the chronic hemodynamic (eg, increases in blood volume and pressure) and non-hemodynamic (eg, pro-inflammatory, pro-fibrotic, pro-oxidative stress) effects can harm the kidneys and cardiovascular system. 80 While evidence for activation of the circulating RAAS in cats with kidney disease is inconsistent, activation of the local, independently regulated, intrarenal RAAS is likely.81–85

心力衰竭方案的次优化

Suboptimization of heart failure protocols
最后，缺乏指导患有晚期肾脏疾病猫咪心力衰竭治疗的普遍证据，而这种疾病可能影响心血管功能和预后。一种容易想象的影响是心力衰竭方案的处方不足和优化不足——这一观点在人体医学的经验中得到支持。例如，在一项回顾性研究中，对20,902名老年心力衰竭患者进行研究发现，无论患者是否患有氮质血症性慢性肾病（CKD）（即血清肌酐 >2.0 mg/dl），接受ACE抑制剂、β-受体阻滞剂或两者联合治疗的患者，其1年死亡风险的降低程度与未使用这些药物的患者相似。然而，在该研究及另一项研究中，这些药物在氮质血症性慢性肾病（CKD）患者中的处方频率低于非氮质血症性患者。
Finally, there is a general lack of evidence to guide heart failure therapies in cats with advanced kidney disease, which might impact cardiovascular function and outcomes. An easily imagined effect is the underprescription and suboptimization of heart failure protocols – an idea that is supported by experience in human medicine. 100 For example, in one retrospective study of 20,902 elderly patients in heart failure, prescription of ACEis, beta-adrenergic blockers or both, was associated with similar reductions in 1-year mortality risk in patients with and without azotemic CKD (ie, serum creatinine >2.0 mg/dl) compared with patients treated with neither drug. 101 However, in that study and anoth-er, 102 these drugs were less often prescribed to patients with azotemic CKD compared with those without.
心血管或肾脏疾病患者的诊断检测建议 对猫的心脏或肾脏疾病的识别应促使临床医生考虑该疾病及其治疗可能如何影响另一器官的功能，以及另一器官的共存原发性疾病可能如何影响病例管理和患者预后。这可能包括对疾病进行完整分期，以及筛查其他可能解释或加重一个或两个系统疾病的疾病和情况（例如甲状腺功能亢进、系统性动脉高血压）。
Recommendations for diagnostic testing in patients with cardiovascular or kidney disease Identification of heart or kidney disease in a cat should prompt the clinician to consider how the disease and its treatment might impact the function of the other organ and how coexisting primary disease of the other might impact case management and patient prognosis. This might include complete disease staging and screening for other diseases and conditions (eg, hyperthyroidism, systemic arterial hypertension) that might explain or exacerbate disease in one or both systems.

In an ideal world, and assuming no practical or financial limitations, all cats with known kidney disease would undergo assessment of cardiac structure and function using complementary information from echocardiography and thoracic radiography. This is because in this species, physical examination findings (eg, presence of a heart murmur or gallop) and thoracic radiography alone have poor sensitivity for the identification of subclinical heart disease and heart enlargement, respectively.1,117,118 Instead, the clinical gold standard for assessment of cardiac structure and function is echocardiography, which allows for the diagnosis, specific phenotyping and staging of heart disease. The findings of thoracic radiography complement echocardiographic data by determining whether signs of CHF are present.
然而，并非在所有情况下或对所有主人来说，心脏超声检查都是可行的。在患有肾病的猫中筛查心脏疾病的最实际原因，是为了识别那些与没有心脏疾病的猫相比，在接受特定干预或压力时并发症（如充血性心力衰竭、动脉血栓栓塞、心律失常）风险显著增加的个体。这在很大程度上相当于患有中度或重度心脏疾病/损伤及心脏扩大猫。广泛可用的循环心脏生物标志物（如N端前脑钠肽原 [NT-proBNP]、心肌肌钙蛋白I）浓度在确定患有肾病的猫是否存在亚临床心脏疾病或损伤方面的准确性，受到多个因素的限制。
Echocardiography is not feasible in all cases or for all owners, however. The most practical reason to screen for heart disease in cats with kidney disease is to identify individuals who, compared with cats without heart disease, are at meaningfully increased risk for complications (eg, CHF, ATE, cardiac arrhythmias) when challenged with certain interventions or stressors. This largely equates to cats with moderate or severe heart disease/injury and enlargement. 119 The accuracy of widely available circulating cardiac biomarker (eg, N-terminal prohormone Btype natriuretic peptide [NT-proBNP], cardiac troponin I) concentrations to establish the presence of subclinical heart disease or injury in cats with kidney disease is limited by several factors.
其中最相关的是，这两种生物标志物都经过肾脏清除，这会降低它们在肾功能异常的猫中对心脏损伤的特异性（表4）。此外，对于它们在急性肾损伤（AKI）或慢性肾病（CKD）猫中与超声心动图相比的表现知之甚少。因此，在这种情况下，仅依靠心脏生物标志物浓度来确诊心脏疾病或损伤并不推荐。然而，如下所述，NT-proBNP浓度在某些情况下可能是筛查方案中的有用组成部分。
Among the most relevant is that both biomarkers undergo renal clearance, which decreases their specificity for cardiac injury in cats with abnormal kidney function (Table 4).120,121 In addition, little is known about their performance compared with echocardiography in cats with AKI or CKD. Therefore, the use of cardiac biomarker concentrations in isolation to rule in heart disease or injury in this situation is not advised. However, NT-proBNP concentration might be a useful component of screening protocols in certain cases, as discussed below.
122 在作者的实践中，强烈建议对患有肾脏疾病并且具备以下一项或多项情况的猫进行超声心动图检查，尤其是在计划进行增加心脏负荷的治疗（如静脉输液、输血）时：（1）有历史或临床迹象提示当前或既往心脏疾病或心力衰竭；（2）胸部X光检查结果提示心脏增大、心源性水肿或积液，或两者兼有；或（3）体格检查结果显示存在增加心肌病风险的迹象（如心脏杂音明显[≥IV/VI级]、奔马律或心律不齐）。25,119 提供的信息可用于制定合理的治疗方案，从而将对病态心脏的血流动力学压力风险降至最低。这种方法得到了研究结果的支持，该研究显示在120只充血性心力衰竭的猫中，有61只（51%）在发作前经历了可能诱发心力衰竭的事件（如静脉输液、麻醉、手术、糖皮质激素使用）。
122 In the authors’ practice, echocardiography is most strongly recommended for cats with kidney disease who have one or more of the following, particularly when treatments (ie, parenteral fluids, blood transfusion) that increase the workload of the heart are planned: (1) historical or clinical findings suggestive of current or previous heart disease or failure; (2) thoracic radiographic findings suggestive of cardiomegaly, cardiogenic edema or effusions, or both; or (3) physical examination findings that, when present, imply increased risk of cardiomyopathy (eg, loud [⩾grade IV/VI] heart murmur, gallop sound or arrhythmia).25,119 The information provided can allow for the formulation of a rational treatment plan that minimizes the risk of excess hemodynamic stressors on a diseased heart. This approach is supported by the findings of one study in which 61 (51%) of 120 cats with CHF experienced antecedent events (eg, intravenous fluid administration, anesthesia, surgery, corticosteroid administration) that might have precipitated the episode.
123 对于无法进行超声心动图检查的猫，通过血清 NT-proBNP 浓度和胸部 X 光片的联合结果，可以帮助识别那些不太可能患有中度或重度心脏疾病或心力衰竭的病例。重要的是，这种方法会将一些患有轻度疾病的猫误判为正常（假阴性），或将正常心脏的猫误判为患病（假阳性），尽管在两项检查结果均正常的老年猫中，‘临床相关’疾病的可能性较低。124 然而，该方法尚未在患有肾病的猫中经过严格测试。 对患有心脏或肾脏疾病的猫进行系统动脉血压评估 鉴于血压异常与心脏和肾脏疾病之间的联系，作者以及兽医心脏病学家和肾脏病专家的共识小组9建议，对患有肾脏疾病的猫进行常规系统动脉血压监测，其目的是识别并纠正病理性系统性动脉高血压或低血压。
123 In cats for whom echocardiography is not feasible, the combined results of serum NT-proBNP concentrations and thoracic radiographs can help to identify cases that are unlikely to have moderate or severe heart disease or failure. Importantly, this approach will misclassify some cats with mild forms of disease (false negative) or normal hearts (false positive), although ‘clinically relevant’ disease is less likely in older cats with normal findings in both tests. 124 However, this approach has not been rigorously tested in cats with kidney disease. Assessment of systemic arterial BP in cats with heart or kidney disease Given the link between BP abnormalities and heart and kidney disease, the authors, as well as a consensus panel of veterinary cardiologists and nephrologists, 9 recommend routine monitoring of systemic arterial BP in cats with diseased kidneys, with the goal of identifying and correcting pathologic systemic arterial hypertension or hypotension.
在作者的实践中，对于具有系统性高血压风险因素、出现充血性心力衰竭临床症状或正在接受已知会影响系统血压药物的中度或重度心脏病猫，也会进行常规血压监测。
In the authors’ practice, routine BP monitoring is also performed in cats with moderate or severe heart disease who have risk factors for systemic hypertension, clinical signs of CHF or are receiving medications known to impact systemic BP.

Considerations for the management of patients with concurrent
心血管和肾脏疾病 在管理充血性心力衰竭（CHF）、急性肾损伤（AKI）或慢性肾病（CKD）时，一个常见的目标是实现并维持最佳的体液平衡。然而，对一个系统的许多疾病治疗在根本上可能与另一个系统的最佳利益相冲突。例如，肾脏疾病的治疗通常包括肠外液体治疗，其目标是维持平均动脉压和足够的肾血流，而在患有CHF的猫中，为了降低毛细血管静水压，几乎总是需要利尿剂。在管理肾功能衰竭或心力衰竭时，过度积极的液体扩张或减少，分别可能导致另一系统的损伤或过度压力。接下来的页面将描述两种常见的临床情景，以说明在管理同时存在两系统基础疾病的猫时所需的平衡。总体而言，方法应是以最小化对另一系统和心血管-肾脏系统风险的方式，治疗导致患者临床症状的失代偿系统。
cardiovascular and renal diseases A common goal in the management of CHF, AKI or CKD is to achieve and maintain optimal body fluid balance. However, many therapies for disease of one system are fundamentally at odds with the best interest of the other. For example, the treatment of kidney disease often includes parenteral fluid therapy with the goal of maintaining mean arterial pressure and adequate renal blood flow, while diuretics are almost always necessary to reduce capillary hydrostatic pressure in cats with CHF. Overly aggressive volume expansion or reduction during the management of kidney or heart failure, respectively, can lead to injury to or excessive stress on the other system. Key Points
✜ 心脏和肾脏疾病在同一只猫中经常共存。在健康和疾病状态下，心脏与肾脏之间存在多种相互作用途径，将这些系统视为一个整体的“心血管–肾轴”是有帮助的。✜ 心脏和肾脏疾病之间最为人所知的联系之一是全身性动脉高血压，这在猫的慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）中很常见，并且可能导致两者的逐步损伤。鉴于血压异常与心脏及肾脏疾病之间的联系，建议对患有肾脏疾病的猫进行系统性动脉血压的常规监测，并使用降压治疗（如氨氯地平、替米沙坦）。
✜ Heart and kidney diseases frequently coexist in the same cat. There are several pathways of interaction between the heart and kidneys during health and disease, and it is helpful to think of these systems as belonging to an integrated ‘cardiovascular–renal axis’. ✜ One of the most well-described links between heart and kidney diseases is systemic arterial hypertension, which is frequently associated with CKD in cats, and can lead to progressive damage to both.
