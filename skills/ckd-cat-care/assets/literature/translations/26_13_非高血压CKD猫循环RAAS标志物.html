The objectives of the present study were (1) to describe equilibrium concentrations of circulating angiotensin peptides and aldosterone in cats with naturally occurring International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) stages 2-4, non-hypertensive chronic kidney disease (NHT-CKD), in cats with naturally occurring SAH, and in apparently healthy cats; and (2) to characterize changes in the equilibrium concentrations of circulating angiotensin peptides and aldosterone in hypertensive cats started on amlodipine, relative to pretreatment values. We hypothesized that (1) compared with age-matched, healthy control cats, cats with NHT-CKD or SAH would have angiotensin peptide and aldosterone concentrations and estimated renin activity levels that indicate activation (NTH-CKD and SAH groups) or a lack of appropriate suppression (SAH group) of the cRAAS; and that (2) antihypertensive treatment with amlodipine besylate would significantly increase angiotensin peptide and aldosterone concentrations.

材料与方法

Materials and methods

研究设计

Study design
这是一项前瞻性、横断面、单中心、观察性研究，于2021年3月至2023年9月期间进行。在所有猫中，cRAAS通过平衡分析在单一时间点进行评估。对于由主治医生开具氨氯地平的高血压猫，在启动治疗后2-4周重新评估cRAAS指标。所有研究活动均获得乔治亚大学兽医学院临床研究委员会批准（批准号CR-594）。所有猫主人在入组前均签署了知情同意书。符合条件的参与者为任何年龄、性别或品种的伴侣猫，具有IRIS 2-4期NHT-CKD或任意病因的SAH，并常规就诊于乔治亚大学兽医教学医院或从教职工和学生拥有的猫中招募。此外，与NHT-CKD猫在年龄和性别上匹配的健康对照猫在同期从教职工和学生拥有的猫中招募。
This was a prospective, cross-sectional, single-center, observational study conducted between March 2021 and September 2023. In all cats, the cRAAS was evaluated by equilibrium analysis at a single time point. For hypertensive cats prescribed amlodipine by the attending clinician, cRAAS markers were also reevaluated 2-4 weeks after initiating therapy. All study activities were approved by the Clinical Research Committee of the University of Georgia College of Veterinary Medicine (approval number CR-594). Informed owner consent was obtained from all cat owners prior to enrollment. Eligible participants were companion cats of any age, sex, or breed with IRIS stages 2-4 NHT-CKD or SAH of any etiology, presented routinely to the University of Georgia Veterinary Teaching Hospital or recruited from staffand student-owned cats. In addition, healthy control cats that were ageand sex-matched to NHT-CKD cats were recruited from staffand student-owned cats during the same period.

筛查访问

Screening visit
对于所有猫咪，筛查访问包括完整的病史；体格检查，包括间接眼底评估；使用多普勒血压计测量收缩压（SBP），并遵循美国兽医内科学会制定的指南18；血常规（CBC）、血清生化分析，包括对称性二甲基精氨酸（SDMA）浓度和总甲状腺素（TT4）浓度；尿液分析及尿蛋白与肌酐比值（UPC）；以及经胸超声心动图评估（除了一个NHT-CKD猫咪）。唯一未进行超声心动图的猫是研究中最早入组的猫，其病史和体格检查未显示心血管疾病的异常。在测定SBP之前，没有给任何猫咪使用抗焦虑药或镇静剂。
For all cats, a screening visit included a complete medical history; a physical examination including indirect fundic evaluation; systolic blood pressure (SBP) measurement using Doppler sphygmomanometry and guidelines set forth by the American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine18; a CBC, a serum biochemistry profile including symmetric dimethyl arginine (SDMA) concentration, and a total thyroxine (TT4) concentration; urinalysis and a urinary protein-tocreatinine ratio (UPC); and transthoracic echocardiographic evaluation (for all but one NHT-CKD cat). The one cat not undergoing echocardiography was the first enrolled in the study and had no historical or physical examination abnormalities suggestive of cardiovascular disease. Anxiolytics or sedatives were not administered to any cat before SBP determination.

入组标准

Enrollment criteria
猫若性情不合宜，则排除;有已知或可能影响RAAS的同时疾病或状况（如心脏病伴任何程度左心房肥大、充血性心力衰竭、容量耗竭、糖尿病及甲状腺功能亢进;或在注册前30天内接受过RAAS抑制剂（如血管紧张素转化酶抑制剂、血管紧张素受体受体阻滞剂和矿物皮质激素受体阻滞剂），在注册前30天或注册前6个月内通过任何给药途径接受皮质类固醇（depot配方）;在注册前7天内接受其他已知直接或间接改变RAAS的药物（如氨氯地平、羟氯嗪、β或肾上腺素受体阻滞剂和利尿剂）;或者如果所有者未同意参与。
Cats were excluded if they had an unsuitable temperament; had concurrent disease(s) or condition(s) with a known or likely effect on the RAAS (eg, heart disease with any degree of left atrial enlargement, congestive heart failure, volume depletion, diabetes mellitus, and uncontrolled hyperthyroidism); or had received a RAAS inhibitor (eg, angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor, angiotensin receptor blocker, and mineralocorticoid receptor blocker) in the 30 days preceding enrollment, had received a corticosteroid by any administration route in the 30 days preceding enrollment or in the 6 months prior to enrollment for depot formulations; had received other medications known to modify the RAAS directly or indirectly (eg, amlodipine, hydralazine, βor αadrenergic blockers, and diuretics) in the 7 days preceding enrollment; or if the owner did not consent to participation.

对照组

Control group
猫咪根据体格检查的正常结果被纳入健康对照组;间接SBP测量（正常，<140 mmHg）;全血细胞计数（CBC）;血清生化特征、单细胞密度（SDMA）和TT4;尿液分析;UPC;以及超声心动图。如果尿比重（USG）≥为1.035，则认为猫的尿液集中能力正常。筛查时局部尿重（USG）为1.030-1034的猫也符合纳入条件，前提是体格检查和反映水分平衡的临床病理变量显示最佳水分状态，且主人报告正常饮水和排尿，且后续肾超声检查（为其他无关研究在某些猫进行）正常，随访时USG为≥1.035，或两者均有。
Cats were included in the healthy control group based on normal findings on physical examination; indirect SBP measurement (normal, < 140 mmHg); CBC; serum biochemistry profile, SDMA, and TT4; urinalysis; UPC; and echocardiography. Cats were deemed to have normal urine concentrating ability if the urine specific gravity (USG) was ≥ 1.035. Cats with a spot USG of 1.030-1034 during screening were also eligible for enrollment provided physical examination and clinicopathologic variables reflective of water balance suggested optimal hydration status, owners reported normal drinking and urination, and if subsequent renal ultrasonographic examination (performed for other unrelated research studies in some cats) was normal, USG at follow-up testing was ≥ 1.035, or both.
为了筛选为健康对照组，猫必须在年龄、性别和绝育状态上与纳入NHT-CKD组的猫匹配，作为配对样本。若年龄差异 ≤ 20%（以NHT-CKD猫为参考），则对照猫被认为是年龄匹配的。
To be screened for inclusion as a healthy control, cats had to be age-, sex-, and neuter-statusmatched as a matched-pair with a cat enrolled in the NHT-CKD group. Control cats were considered age-matched if the age difference was ≤ 20% (using the NHT-CKD cat as a reference).

NHT-CKD组

NHT-CKD group
如果猫被诊断为慢性肾功能变化的慢性肾病（CKD）且被分配为IRIS CKD 2期或更高阶段（即血清肌酐浓度 ≥ 1.6 mg/dL，同时尿比重（USG） < 1.035，且没有明显的肾外原因），则被纳入非高血压慢性肾病（CKD）（NHT-CKD）组，并根据血压分为正常血压（SBP < 140 mmHg）或高血压前期（SBP 140-159 mmHg）亚组。任意蛋白尿亚组的猫都被纳入。系统性无高血压的猫，如伴发甲状腺功能亢进（受控或未受控），则被排除在NHT-CKD组之外。
Cats were included in the NHT-CKD group if diagnosed with CKD based on evidence of chronic changes in renal function and assigned an IRIS CKD stage of 2 or higher (ie, serum creatinine concentration ≥ 1.6 mg/dL with contemporaneous USG < 1.035 and the absence of an apparent extra-renal cause) and substaged as normotensive (ie, SBP < 140 mmHg) or prehypertensive (ie, SBP, 140-159 mmHg). Cats of any proteinuria substage were included. Systemically nonhypertensive cats with concurrent hyperthyroidism (controlled or uncontrolled) were excluded from the NHT-CKD group.

高血压组

Hypertensive group
如果在两次至少间隔1小时的不同测量中记录到平均单次测量收缩压（SBP）≥160 mmHg，或者在有眼部靶器官损伤（如视网膜出血、大疱性视网膜脱离）的猫中记录到单次平均测量SBP≥150 mmHg，则该猫被纳入SAH组。猫可以被诊断为原发性或继发性高血压。历史上接受过I-131明确治疗且在筛查前至少4周内高甲状腺功能得到控制（即血清总T4浓度≤实验室参考范围上限）的高甲状腺功能亢进猫也可以被纳入。所有治疗决定均由主治医生自行决定。被开具任意剂量阿莫洛地平单独治疗的高血压猫，将在开始治疗后2-4周被邀请回诊进行复评。
Cats were included in the SAH group if an average session SBP ≥ 160 mmHg was documented during 2 separate measurement sessions at least 1 h apart, or if a single average session SBP ≥ 150 mmHg was noted in a cat with ocular target organ damage (eg, retinal hemorrhage, bullous retinal detachment). Cats could be diagnosed with idiopathic or secondary hypertension. Historically hyperthyroid cats definitively treated with I-131 and whose hyperthyroidism was controlled (ie, serum TT4 concentration ≤ upper limit of the laboratory reference interval) for at least 4 weeks prior to screening could be included. All treatment decisions were at the discretion of the attending clinician. Hypertensive cats prescribed amlodipine alone at any dosage were invited back 2-4 weeks after initiation of therapy for repeat evaluation.

Sample collection and clinicopathologic analysis
每次就诊时，均通过静脉穿刺和体格检查分别采集血液和尿液样本。样本多在未镇静的猫中采集。8只猫（NHT-CKH组分别为n=5和3只，健康对照组分别为3只），使用布托芬醇（0.2-0.3 mg/kg，静脉注射或静脉注射）以便采集。血液从颈静脉、隐静脉或头静脉采集，立即放入无添加剂的管子中，允许血液凝固（EDTA）。尿液被收集到玻璃管中。血液学、血清生物化学、血清TT4浓度、尿液分析和UPC测定均由佐治亚大学兽医临床病理实验室（雅典，佐治亚州）内部完成。血清被冰封运往商业实验室（缅因州韦斯特布鲁克的IDEXX实验室）进行SDMA浓度测量。采集后30分钟内，血清通过离心分离，并在−80°C下储存，直到批量运输用于测量下文所述的cRAAS分析物。
At all visits, after SBP determination and physical examination, blood and urine samples were collected via venipuncture and cystocentesis, respectively. Samples were most often collected in unsedated cats. For 8 cats (n = 5 and 3 in the NHT-CKH and healthy control groups, respectively), butorphanol (0.2-0.3 mg/kg IM or IV) was administered to facilitate sample collection. Blood was collected from the jugular, saphenous, or cephalic vein and immediately placed into tubes containing no additive, where it was allowed to clot, or EDTA. Urine was collected into glass tubes. Hematology, serum biochemistry, serum TT4 concentration, urinalysis, and UPC determination were performed in-house by the University of Georgia Veterinary Clinical Pathology Laboratory (Athens, Georgia). Serum was shipped on ice to a commercial laboratory (IDEXX Laboratories, Westbrook, Maine) for measurement of SDMA concentration. Within 30 min of collection, serum was separated by centrifugation and stored at −80°C until batch shipment for measurement of cRAAS analytes as described below.
猫在前一晚禁食，并在评估当天早晨禁止进食。虽然样本采集的时间没有标准化，但大多数就诊发生在上午9点至12点之间；在可能的情况下，高血压猫的重新评估会在与相应筛查访问相同的时间进行。
Cats were fasted overnight and food was withheld on the morning of evaluation. Although time of day of sample collection was not standardized, most visits occurred between 9 AM and 12 PM; when possible, reevaluation of hypertensive cats happened at the same time of day as the corresponding screening visit.

cRAAS 组件分析

Analysis of cRAAS components
cRAAS 是通过测量血清中血管紧张素 I、II、III、IV、1-5 和 1-7 及醛固酮的平衡浓度来评估的，使用先前描述的基于 LC–MS/MS 的技术（RASFingerprint；Attoquant Diagnostics GmbH，维也纳，奥地利）。此外，还根据这些浓度计算了酶活性和肾上腺对血管紧张素 II 信号反应的替代性标志物，如先前所述。这些包括 ACE 活性（ACE-S）的替代性测量 = [血管紧张素 II]/[血管紧张素 I]；ACE2 活性（ACE2-S）= [血管紧张素 1-5]/[血管紧张素 II]；PRA（PRA-S）= [血管紧张素 I] / [血管紧张素 II]；以及肾上腺对血管紧张素 II 的反应能力（醛固酮与血管紧张素 II 比值，AA2 比值）= [醛固酮]/[血管紧张素 II]。
The cRAAS was assessed by measurement of serum equilibrium concentrations of angiotensin I, II, III, IV, 1-5, and 1-7 and aldosterone using a previously described LC–MS/MS-based technique (RASFingerprint; Attoquant Diagnostics GmbH, Vienna, Austria).19 In addition, surrogate markers of enzyme activity and adrenal responsiveness to angiotensin II signaling were calculated from these concentrations, as previously described.20-22 These included surrogate measures of ACE activity (ACE-S) = [angiotensin II]/[angiotensin I]; ACE2 activity (ACE2-S) = [angiotensin 15]/[angiotensin II]; PRA (PRA-S) = [angiotensin I] + [angiotensin II]; and adrenal responsiveness to angiotensin II (aldosterone-to-angiotensin II ratio, AA2 ratio) = [aldosterone]/[angiotensin II].
所有超声心动图检查均由董事会认证的兽医心脏病学家或在培训中的心脏病学家进行，使用配备相控阵换能器的超声仪（EPIQ CVx，飞利浦医疗）。在手工约束的动物上，从标准的右侧和左侧胸骨旁视图记录完整的经胸超声心动图研究。^23 结果用于指导上述描述的入组决策。
All echocardiographic examinations were performed by a board-certified veterinary cardiologist or cardiologist-in-training, using an ultrasound unit (EPIQ CVx, Philips Healthcare) outfitted with phased-array transducers. Complete transthoracic echocardiographic studies were recorded from standard right and left parasternal views on manually restrained animals.23 Findings were used to guide enrollment decisions as described above.
心肌病的诊断和分类遵循美国兽医内科学会制定的指南。24 对于出现左心室同心性肥厚（即舒张末期心室壁厚 ≥ 6 mm）且左心室未扩大的猫，被归类为肥厚型心肌病（HCM）表型。24 左心房增大诊断标准为左房与主动脉根部直径比（LA:Ao，见下文） > 1.5 的猫。25,26 为了进行统计分析，每只入组猫记录以下数据：(1) 从二维（2D）短轴测量计算的 LA:Ao 值，26 (2) 任意心室段的最大二维或 M 模式舒张末期左心室壁厚（maxLVWTd，mm；衡量左心室肥厚程度），以及 (3) 从 M 模式测量计算的左心室缩短分数（FS，%；衡量心室收缩功能）。对于每个变量，记录值为 3 到 5 个心动周期测量值的平均值。
Diagnosis and classification of cardiomyopathies conformed to guidelines set forth by the American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine.24 The hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) phenotype was assigned to cats with left ventricular concentric hypertrophy (ie, end-diastolic wall thickness ≥ 6 mm) and a non-dilated left ventricle.24 Left atrial enlargement was diagnosed in cats with a left-atrial-to-aortic root diameter ratio (LA:Ao, see below) > 1.5.25,26 For the purposes of statistical analyses, the following data were recorded for each enrolled cat: (1) LA:Ao calculated from 2-dimensional (2D) short-axis measurements,26 (2) maximum 2Dor Mmode-derived end-diastolic left ventricular wall thickness at any segment (maxLVWTd, mm; a measure of left ventricular hypertrophy severity), and (3) left ventricular fractional shortening (FS, %; a measure of ventricular systolic performance) calculated from M-mode measurements. For each variable, the recorded value reflected the average of values taken from 3 to 5 cardiac cycles.

统计分析

Statistical analyses
使用商业软件（Graphpad Prism 10 for MacOS，版本 10.4.2）和开源软件（R project，版本 4.4.127）进行了统计分析。对于所有分析，P 值 < 0.05 被认为具有统计学意义。通过直方图可视化和 Shapiro–Wilk 检验评估连续数据的分布是否符合正态分布。基线人口学和临床数据以均值 ± 标准差或中位数（第一四分位数；第三四分位数）呈现，分别对应正态分布或非正态分布。对于 RAAS 数据，使用下述方法对线性混合模型进行对数转换比较，同时计算几何均值及其 95% 置信区间。为统计分析的目的，USG 值 > 1.060 的情况赋值为 1.061。低于测定下限的血管紧张素肽浓度报告为该下限除以 2 的值。
Statistical analyses were performed using commercially available (Graphpad Prism 10 for MacOS, version 10.4.2) and open-source (R project, version 4.4.127) software packages. For all analyses, Pvalues < .05 were considered statistically significant. Continuous data were evaluated for normality of distribution by visualization of histograms and the Shapiro–Wilk test. Baseline demographic and clinical data are presented as mean ± SD or median (first quartile; third quartile) if normally or non-normally distributed, respectively. For RAAS data compared with linear mixed models, which were log-transformed as described below, geometric means and their 95% CIs were also calculated. For the purposes of statistical analysis, USG values > 1.060 were assigned a value of 1.061. Angiotensin peptide concentrations less than the assay lower limit of quantification were reported as that limit divided by 2.
对于正态分布和非正态分布的变量，组间比较分别使用单因素方差分析（ANOVA）或Kruskal–Wallis检验进行，配对比较使用Benjamini–Hochberg方法进行调整。分类数据采用Fisher精确检验进行比较。线性混合模型使用R软件包lme428（版本1.1-37）构建，边际均值和组间比较使用软件包emmeans29（版本1.11.0）计算。除非另有说明，否则使用软件包默认设置。为了改善正态分布的近似性，血管紧张素肽和醛固酮浓度以及由这些浓度计算的RAAS替代指标均进行了对数转换。然后使用线性混合模型比较三组之间的RAAS标志物。组别（即健康对照组、NHT-CKD组或SAH组）被设置为固定效应，健康对照组设为参考类别。
For normally and non-normally distributed variables, intergroup comparisons were performed using a one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) test or the Kruskal–Wallis test, respectively, with pairwise comparisons adjusted for using the Benjamini–Hochberg method. Categorical data were compared using Fisher’s exact test. Linear mixed models were built using the R package lme428 (version 1.1-37), and marginal means and group comparisons were calculated with the package emmeans29 (version 1.11.0). Default package settings were used unless otherwise specified. Angiotensin peptide and aldosterone concentrations, and RAAS surrogate indices calculated from these concentrations, were log- transformed to improve the approximation of a normal distribution. Linear mixed models were then used to compare RAAS markers between the 3 groups. The group (ie, healthy control, NHT- CKD, or SAH) was set as the fixed effect with the healthy control group set as the reference category.
健康对照猫与NHT-CKD猫之间的配对被设为随机效应，RAAS标志物被设为因变量。三个组之间的多重比较使用Tukey方法进行调整。额外的模型也考虑了治疗性肾脏饮食的摄入、年龄或两者，并同样设为固定效应，以调整饮食或年龄对组间比较可能产生的影响。在SAH猫中，仅使用Wilcoxon符号秩检验评估阿莫洛地平治疗前后RAAS标志物的差异。由于已知RAAS与电解质平衡以及肾脏和心血管功能之间存在相互作用，进行了探索性Spearman相关分析，分析对象为评估的cRAAS标志物以及选定的临床、临床病理和超声心动图变量。具体而言，探索了RAAS标志物与收缩压（SBP）、血细胞压积、血清钠、钾、肌酐和SDMA浓度、尿蛋白肌酐比（UPC）及三个超声心动图变量（即LA:Ao、最大左心室壁厚舒张期，maxLVWTd，以及射血分数FS）之间的相关性。相关分析使用了所有入组猫的数据，对于SAH猫，仅使用阿莫洛地平治疗前的值。
Pairing between healthy control and NHT-CKD cats was set as a random effect and the RAAS marker was set as the dependent variable. Multiple comparisons between the 3 groups were adjusted for using the Tukey method. Additional models were built with the feeding of a therapeutic renal diet, age, or both, also set as a fixed effect to adjust for possible effects of diet or age on these group comparisons. In SAH cats only, the Wilcoxon signed rank test was used to evaluate differences in RAAS markers before vs after amlodipine treatment. Because of the known interaction between the RAAS and electrolyte balance, and kidney and cardiovascular function,30 exploratory Spearman correlation analysis was conducted between the cRAAS markers evaluated and selected clinical, clinicopathologic, and echocardiographic variables. Specifically, correlations between RAAS markers and SBP; hematocrit; serum sodium, potassium, creatinine, and SDMA concentrations; UPC; and 3 echocardiographic variables (ie, LA:Ao, maxLVWTd, and FS) were explored. Data from all enrolled cats were used in correlation analysis with only pre-amlodipine values used for SAH cats.

在本研究中，将血清中血管紧张素肽和醛固酮的平衡浓度在NHT-CKD猫、未治疗的SAH猫（所有病例均伴有CKD，被认为是原因）以及与NHT-CKD组年龄和性别相似的表面健康猫之间进行了比较。在患有高血压性或非高血压性CKD的猫中，缺乏cRAAS激活的证据。无论是使用未调整模型，还是饮食、年龄或饮食和年龄双重调整模型进行组间比较，未治疗高血压猫的血管紧张素I、II和III的平均浓度均 consistently 低于健康猫，所使用的PRA替代标记（PRA-S）的数值也是如此。尽管总体模式显示SAH猫的cRAAS活动较低，NHT-CKD猫处于中间水平，健康猫较高，但控制饮食、年龄或两者同时因素后，组间各种cRAAS标记的差异是否具有统计学显著性会发生变化。
In the present study, serum equilibrium concentrations of angiotensin peptides and aldosterone were compared between cats with NHT-CKD, untreated SAH (in all cases with concurrent CKD as the presumed cause), and apparently healthy cats of similar age and sex relative to those of the NHT-CKD group. Evidence of cRAAS activation in cats with hypertensive or non-hypertensive CKD was lacking. Regardless of whether unadjusted or diet-, age-, or both diet- and age-adjusted models were used for intergroup comparisons, mean concentrations of angiotensin I, II, and III were consistently lower in untreated hypertensive cats relative to healthy cats, as was the value for the surrogate marker of PRA (PRA-S) used. Although the overall pattern indicated that cRAAS activity was lower in SAH cats, intermediate in NHT-CKD cats, and higher in healthy cats, controlling for diet, age, or both altered whether differences in various cRAAS markers between groups were statistically significant.
例如，在调整了治疗性肾脏饮食的摄入量但未调整年龄的情况下，治疗前高血压慢性肾病 (CKD) 猫、非高血压 CKD 猫和健康猫之间的平均血管紧张素 II 和 III 浓度存在显著差异（按平均浓度从低到高列出）。尽管各组间血清醛固酮浓度无显著差异，但在所有统计模型中，SAH 组的 AA2 比值均高于健康对照组，这提示这些猫可能存在循环系统中血管紧张素 II 非依赖的醛固酮分泌。接受氨氯地平治疗的高血压猫，其经典和替代 cRAAS 被激活，表现为几乎所有测量的血管紧张素肽（即血管紧张素 I、II、III、1-7 和 1-5）及 PRA-S 循环浓度显著增加。
For example, when adjusted for the feeding of a therapeutic renal diet but not age, mean angiotensin II and III concentrations were significantly different between cats with treatment-naı̈ve hypertensive CKD, cats with NHT-CKD, and healthy cats (listed from lowest to highest mean concentrations). Although serum aldosterone concentrations did not differ between groups, the AA2 ratio was higher in the SAH group relative to healthy control groups across all statistical models, suggesting a circulating angiotensin II-independent release of aldosterone in those cats. Hypertensive cats treated with amlodipine experienced activation of the classical and alternative cRAAS characterized by a significant increase in circulating concentrations of almost all measured angiotensin peptides (ie, angiotensin I, II, III, 1-7, and 1-5) and PRA-S.
我们的研究结果与之前一项针对196只年龄在9岁或以上、在英国初诊诊所就诊的猫的大型研究结果相似。在那项研究中，将猫按照是否存在氮质血症（即血浆肌酐 > 2.0 mg/dL）和系统性血压状态进行分组，比对了肾素活性（PRA）和血浆醛固酮浓度。直接测得的PRA在有氮质血症和未经治疗的高血压猫中明显低于非氮质血症、血压正常的猫。此外，未经治疗的高血压氮质血症猫的血浆醛固酮浓度高于氮质血症或非氮质血症、血压正常的猫。尽管我们的检测技术以及定义肾功能损伤和系统性高血压的界限与前述研究不同，但我们同样记录到高血压猫的PRA-S低于血压正常猫。
The findings of our study are similar to those of a previous large study of 196 cats aged 9 years or older that were presented to a first-opinion practice in the United Kingdom. In that study, PRA and plasma aldosterone concentrations were compared between cats grouped according to the presence or absence of azotemia (ie, plasma creatinine > 2.0 mg/dL) and systemic BP status.8 Directly measured PRA was significantly lower in azotemic and non-azotemic cats with untreated systemic hypertension compared with non-azotemic, normotensive cats. In addition, plasma aldosterone concentrations were higher in azotemic cats with untreated systemic hypertension compared to azotemic or non-azotemic normotensive cats. Although our assay techniques and cutoffs for defining renal functional impairment and systemic hypertension differ from those of the previous study, we similarly documented lower PRA-S in hypertensive cats than in normotensive cats.
虽然在本研究中未观察到血清醛固酮平衡浓度的显著差异，但仅评估了 6 只高血压猫，不能排除第二类错误。在之前的一项较大研究中，氮质血症高血压猫的血浆醛固酮-肾素比值高于氮质血症正常血压猫，这表明在高血压猫中观察到的醛固酮升高并非由肾素活性增加及随之循环血管紧张素 II 增加驱动。类似地，本研究发现，与对照组相比，高血压猫的平均 AA2 比值在所有统计模型中均显著升高，并且在未调整年龄的模型中，与 NHT-CKD 比较也显著升高，尽管这似乎主要是由 2 只个体的数据推动的。
While no significant differences in serum equilibrium concentrations of aldosterone were observed in the present study, only 6 hypertensive cats were evaluated, and a type II error cannot be excluded. In the previous, larger study,8 plasma aldosterone-to-renin ratio was higher in azotemic hypertensive cats than in azotemic normotensive cats, suggesting that the higher aldosterone observed in hypertensive cats is not driven by an increase in PRA and downstream increase in circulating angiotensin II. Somewhat similarly, the present study found a significantly higher mean AA2 ratio in hypertensive cats when compared with the control group across all statistical models and to the NHT-CKD in models without age adjustment, though this appeared to be largely driven by data from 2 individuals.
其他评估较小样本猫（n ≤ 16）的研究者也记录到，与健康对照猫相比，患有高血压性慢性肾病（CKD）的猫的肾素活性（PRA）可能相同或更低，而血浆醛固酮浓度则更高6,7，再次表明在这些猫中，醛固酮的释放似乎独立于血管紧张素II的生成，这与低肾素/高醛固酮的原发性高血压患者情况类似32。系统性高血压情况下低于正常的PRA是一种预期的生理反应，并提示RAAS的调节正常；也就是说，系统性血压升高应被肾小球旁器感知，并抑制肾素分泌30。正如正常生理预期的那样，当考虑所有猫时，我们发现几乎所有血管紧张素肽和PRA-S的浓度与收缩压（SBP）呈负相关。这似乎与“cRAAS活化增加部分导致CKD患者系统性高血压”的假说相矛盾。
Others who have evaluated smaller samples of cats (n ≤ 16) have also documented either the same or lower PRA and higher plasma aldosterone concentrations in cats with hypertensive CKD compared to healthy controls,6,7 again suggesting that aldosterone release appears independent of angiotensin II production in these cats, as is true for people with low- renin/high aldosterone essential hypertension.32 Lower-than-normal PRA in the setting of systemic hypertension is an expected physiologic response and suggests normal regulation of the RAAS; that is, elevations in systemic BP should be sensed by the juxtaglomerular apparatus and suppress renin release.30 As expected from normal physiology, we found that the concentrations of almost all angiotensin peptides and PRA-S were negatively correlated with SBP when all cats were considered. This seemingly contradicts the hypothesis that increased activation of the cRAAS is partially responsible for the development of systemic hypertension in patients with CKD.
有趣的是，尽管我们和其他研究者观察到未治疗高血压猫的血浆肾素活性（或其替代指标）以及循环血管紧张素 II 浓度较低，但使用替米沙坦选择性阻断血管紧张素 II 类型 1 受体显著降低了高血压33,34和正常血压35猫的系统性血压。近几十年来，肾内肾素-血管紧张素系统（RAS）在调节肾功能和血压以及在系统性动脉高血压（SAH）发病机制中的作用越来越受到重视。36,37 值得注意的是，肾内RAS的调节似乎与循环系统的调节是独立的，38 并且循环RAS活性标志物在预测猫肾脏血管紧张素肽浓度方面效果不佳。39 在患有高血压慢性肾病（CKD）的猫中，肾内RAS活性仍未被明确界定，因为以往研究缺乏可靠的收缩压（SBP）信息。40-42 因此，有可能（尽管尚未证实）肾内RAS的激活，与循环RAS解耦且无法通过测量循环RAS检测，可能对CKD猫的SAH有所贡献。
Interestingly, despite our and others’ observation of lower PRA (or its surrogate) and circulating angiotensin II concentrations in untreated hypertensive cats, selective blockade of the angiotensin II type 1 receptor using telmisartan significantly reduces systemic BP in both hypertensive33,34 and normotensive35 cats. In recent decades, the role of the intrarenal renin–angiotensin system (RAS) in the regulation of kidney function and BP and in the pathophysiology of SAH has been increasingly highlighted.36,37 Notably, regulation of the intrarenal RAS appears to be independent of that of the circulating system,38 and markers of cRAAS activity are poor predictors of renal angiotensin peptide concentrations in cats.39 Intrarenal RAS activity remains uncharacterized in cats with hypertensive CKD, as reliable information on SBP is lacking in prior studies.40-42 Therefore, it is possible (though unproven) that activation of the intrarenal RAS, uncoupled from and undetectable through measurement of the cRAAS, could contribute to SAH in cats with CKD.
与目前及其他关于自发性慢性肾病（CKD）猫的研究相比，先前在外科诱导CKD的研究中发现，经残肾包裹术后出现系统性高血压的猫，其肾素活性（PRA）和血浆醛固酮浓度高于对照猫，而且接受7/8肾切除术的猫在术后约2个月，其PRA及血浆血管紧张素I、血管紧张素II和醛固酮浓度相对于基线显著增加。可以合理地得出结论，这些发现特定于通过减少功能性肾脏质量而创建的外科模型。
In contrast with the present and other studies of cats with spontaneous CKD,6-8 previous work in surgically induced CKD revealed that cats with systemic hypertension after remnant-wrap have higher PRA and plasma aldosterone concentrations than control cats,9 and that cats undergoing 7/8 nephrectomy have significantly increased PRA and plasma concentrations of angiotensin I, angiotensin II, and aldosterone approximately 2 months after surgery, relative to baseline.4 It would be reasonable to conclude that these findings are specific to surgical models created by reductions in functional renal mass.
然而，一项最近使用残肾慢性肾病（CKD）模型的研究发现，在使用RAS指纹分析年轻成猫（专门培育）的诱导CKD（n = 15，其中6只系统性高血压并接受氨氯地平治疗）时，血清肾素-血管紧张素系统肽和醛固酮的平衡浓度与同龄、同性别的社区健康对照猫（n = 15）相比没有差异。在该研究中，即使在调整收缩压（SBP）和氨氯地平治疗后，诱导CKD猫的血清醛固酮浓度和AA2比值仍然较高。
However, a recent study that used a remnant-kidney CKD model found no difference in serum equilibrium concentrations of angiotensin peptides and aldosterone using RAS-Fingerprint in young adult, purpose-bred cats with induced CKD (n = 15, of which 6 were systemically hypertensive and treated with amlodipine) compared to life-stage- and sex-matched, community-owned healthy control cats (n = 15).43 In that study, serum aldosterone concentrations and AA2 ratio were higher in cats with induced CKD, even after adjustment for SBP and amlodipine treatment.
肾损伤与cRAAS标志物评估之间的时间可能会对这些研究的结论产生重要影响，因为两项显示高血压CKD猫PRA高于正常水平的研究4,9是在损伤后3个月内评估标志物，而最近的一项研究未显示血管紧张素肽的差异，是在损伤后至少27个月才评估标志物。43 此外，在早期研究中，各组的饮食都是标准化的4,9，而最近的一项研究43中，诱导CKD的猫喂食治疗性肾脏饮食，而对照猫喂食维持性饮食。已知钠和钾的饮食摄入会影响RAAS活性。11,12 与维持性饮食相比，猫的治疗性肾脏饮食钠含量较低，钾含量较高44，这预计会刺激RAAS和肾上腺醛固酮的释放。这会放大或掩盖循环中RAAS标志物浓度的组间差异，取决于哪一组摄入了钠和钾含量更高的饮食。
The time between kidney injury and evaluation of cRAAS markers could factor importantly into these studies’ conclusions, as the 2 demonstrating greater-than- normal PRA in cats with hypertensive CKD4,9 evaluated markers within 3 months of injury, whereas the most recent, which did not demonstrate differences in angiotensin peptides, evaluated markers at least 27 months after injury.43 In addition, whereas diet was standardized across groups in the earlier studies,4,9 in the most recent,43 cats with induced CKD were fed a therapeutic renal diet while control cats were fed maintenance diets. Dietary intakes of sodium and potassium have a known influence on RAAS activity.11,12 Compared to maintenance diets, therapeutic renal diets for cats have lower sodium and higher potassium content,44 which would be expected to stimulate the RAAS and adrenal release of aldosterone. This would amplify or mask group differences in circulating concentrations of RAAS markers, depending on the group receiving the diet higher in sodium and potassium.
在本研究中，仅有 8/17（47%）患有 NHT-CKD 的猫和 4/6（67%）患有 SAH 的猫被喂食治疗性肾脏饮食，这表明与早期研究相比，这里的饮食影响可能较小。事实上，对仅有的两种 RAAS 组分——血清血管紧张素 II 和 III——进行组间比较的统计结论，在进行饮食调整后发生了变化。然而，在进行此调整后（而非之前），NHT-CKD 猫的血管紧张素 II 和 III 浓度相较于健康对照显著降低，SAH 猫的血管紧张素 II 浓度相较于 NHT-CKD 猫显著降低（尽管如果同时调整年龄，该效应消失）。
In the present study, only 8/17 (47%) cats with NHT-CKD and 4/6 (67%) cats with SAH were fed a therapeutic renal diet, suggesting that dietary influences of lesser magnitude would be expected here compared to the earlier study. Indeed, statistical conclusions for intergroup comparisons of only 2 RAAS components, serum angiotensin II and III, were altered when these comparisons were adjusted for diet. However, after this adjustment, but not before, angiotensin II and III concentrations were significantly lower in NHT-CKD cats relative to healthy controls, and angiotensin II was significantly lower in SAH cats relative to NHT-CKD cats (though this effect was lost if age was concurrently adjusted for).
由于预期治疗性肾脏饮食会增加RAAS活性，而在两组（NHT-CKD和SAH）中，这些饮食的使用频率最高，但其RAAS活性却低于未使用这些饮食的对照组（健康对照组）（在饮食调整后），这表明饮食可能在一定程度上通过抵消或掩盖其他因素抑制RAAS活性的趋势发挥了作用。在本研究和上述引用的研究中8（评估的是客户拥有的猫），接受氨氯地平的动物中，PRA或其替代指标相比治疗前显著增加。自本研究设计以来，还有3项已发表的研究报道了高血压猫接受氨氯地平治疗后的血清血管紧张素肽平衡浓度，显示与非高血压猫相比，经典及替代RAAS途径的组分浓度均增加。16,17,43 对RAAS标志物与各种生理相关的临床和临床病理变量进行了相关性分析。
Because a therapeutic renal diet would be expected to increase RAAS activity, the fact that 2 groups (NHT-CKD and SAH) in which these diets were fed with the highest frequency had lower RAAS activity than the group (healthy controls) for which they were not used (after adjustment for diet) suggests that diet might have contributed somewhat by counteracting or masking the tendency of other factors to suppress RAAS activity. In the present study and the above-cited study,8 which evaluated client-owned cats, PRA or its surrogate significantly increased in animals receiving amlodipine, relative to pretreatment values. Since the design of our study, 3 other published studies reported serum equilibrium concentrations of angiotensin peptides in hypertensive cats treated with amlodipine, showing increased concentrations of components of both the classical and alternative RAAS pathways relative to non- hypertensive cats.16,17,43 Correlation analyses were performed between RAAS markers and various physiologically relevant clinical and clinicopathologic variables.
由于本分析具有探索性质，所报告的P值未进行多重比较校正，结果需要进一步验证。在我们的研究中，有几种变量与cRAAS标志物显著相关，但相关性较弱（ρ ≤ 0.4）。ρ > 0.4的显著相关性仅在各种cRAAS标志物与SBP及LA:Ao之间发现。在之前的一项研究中，患有心肌病的猫的血清平衡浓度的血管紧张素I、血管紧张素1-7和醛固酮，以及PRA-S值均显著高于健康猫。在我们的研究中，排除了患有严重心脏病的猫，血清醛固酮浓度与LA:Ao显著呈负相关，这可能与血容量状态对RAAS的影响有关。例如，血容量收缩会伴随左心房体积减小（因此LA:Ao较低），预计会与RAAS激活相关，尽管血管紧张素II与该变量未呈显著相关性。
Due to the exploratory nature of this analysis, P-values reported were not adjusted for multiple comparisons, and findings require further validation. Several variables were significantly, though weakly (ρ ≤ 0.4), correlated with cRAAS markers in our study. Significant correlations with ρ > 0.4 were only found for various cRAAS markers and each of SBP and LA:Ao. In a previous study,17 cats with cardiomyopathy had significantly higher serum equilibrium concentrations of angiotensin I, angiotensin 1-7, and aldosterone, and PRA-S values than healthy cats. In our study, which excluded cats with substantial heart disease, serum aldosterone concentrations were significantly negatively correlated with LA:Ao, which could be linked to the effects of volume status on the RAAS. For example, blood volume contracture, which would be accompanied by a smaller left atrial size (and therefore, lower LA:Ao), would be expected to be associated with RAAS activation, although angiotensin II was not significantly correlated with this variable.
本研究的优点包括对猫进行的全面筛查，其中包括超声心动图以描绘共存的心脏病变；排除了同时患有其他疾病的猫以及正在接受已知会影响RAAS药物的猫；评估了经典和替代RAAS途径；并招募了与NHT-CKD组在年龄和性别上匹配的对照猫。本研究也存在重要局限性。首先，样本量较小，尤其是在SAH组，这限制了统计能力去检测组间轻度或中度差异，因此非显著性结果应谨慎解读。此外，由于猫是从定期就诊于兽医教学医院的猫中招募的，样本采集的时间未标准化。由于RAAS活性受昼夜节律影响，这一遗漏可能会混淆我们的结果。
Strengths of the present study include the comprehensive screening of cats, which included echocardiography to characterize co-existing cardiac pathologies; the exclusion of cats with concurrent conditions and cats receiving medications known to affect the RAAS; the evaluation of both classical and alternative RAAS pathways; and the recruitment of control cats that were age- and sex-matched to the NHT-CKD group. This study also has important limitations. First, the sample size was small, particularly in the SAH group, which limits statistical power to detect mild or moderate differences between groups, and nonsignificant findings should be interpreted cautiously. Also, as cats were recruited from those regularly presented to a veterinary teaching hospital, time of day of sample collection was not standardized. Because RAAS activity is subject to circadian periodicity,45 this omission could have confounded our results.
此外，饮食没有标准化，尽管所有猫在取样前都禁食，这应该可以减少进食对cRAAS的影响。此外，在筛查期间既没有进行泌尿道成像，也没有测量血液或尿液中的急性肾损伤标志物，这限制了我们对猫肾脏疾病的描述；然而，所有猫在入组时均临床稳定。最后，我们对RAAS的评估仅限于循环系统的标志物，未探讨肾内RAS。总之，与年龄和性别匹配的对照组相比，我们未在患有NHT-CKD或SAH的猫中检测到cRAAS的激活。应该在患有这些疾病的猫中探讨肾内RAS，以更全面地理解该系统在其病理生理中的作用。
In addition, diet was not standardized, although all cats were fasted prior to sampling, which should decrease the influence of feeding on the cRAAS.46 In addition, neither imaging of the urinary tract nor measurement of blood or urinary markers of active kidney injury was performed during screening, which limited our characterization of the cats’ kidney diseases; however, all cats were clinically stable at the time of enrollment. Finally, our assessment of the RAAS was limited to circulating markers of this system, and the intrarenal RAS was not explored. In conclusion, we did not detect activation of the cRAAS in cats with NHT-CKD or SAH, relative to ageand sex-matched controls. The intrarenal RAS should be explored in cats with these conditions for a more complete understanding of the role of this system in their pathophysiology.
