Materials and Methods
使用前瞻性入组猫咪样本和储存材料的诊断准确性研究。根据诊断将猫咪分类为肾盂肾炎、输尿管梗阻、慢性肾病（CKD）、亚临床菌尿、膀胱炎或健康。比较血清淀粉样蛋白A、中性粒细胞:淋巴细胞比值、单核细胞:淋巴细胞比值和尿液肾损伤标志物-1在各组之间的差异。受试者工作特征曲线分析用于评估炎症生物标志物的表现。在一部分肾盂肾炎猫中连续测定血清淀粉样蛋白A和尿液肾损伤标志物-1。纳入了71只猫的样本（肾盂肾炎 n=22，其他泌尿系统疾病 n=35，健康 n=14）。肾盂肾炎猫的血清淀粉样蛋白A中位数显著高于其他组，重叠极少。在49.1 mg/L的阈值下，血清淀粉样蛋白A的阳性似然比为32，敏感性为0.95（95% CI：0.74至0.99），特异性为0.97（95% CI：0.85至0.99），优于中性粒细胞:淋巴细胞比值。
Diagnostic accuracy study using samples from prospectively enrolled cats and stored material. Cats were classified according to diagnosis as pyelonephritis, ureteral obstruction, chronic kidney disease, subclinical bacteriuria, cystitis, or healthy. Serum amyloid A, neutrophil:lymphocyte ratio, monocyte:lymphocyte ratio and urinary kidney injury marker-1 were compared across groups. Receiver operating characteristic curve analysis assessed the performance of inflammatory biomarkers. Serum amyloid A and urinary kidney injury marker-1 were measured consecutively in a subset of cats with pyelonephritis. Samples from 71 cats (pyelonephritis n = 22, other urological conditions n = 35, healthy n = 14) were included. Median serum amyloid A concentration was significantly higher in cats with pyelonephritis, overlapping minimally with other groups. At a threshold of 49.1 mg/L, serum amyloid A showed a positive likelihood ratio of 32, sensitivity of 0.95 (95% CI: 0.74 to 0.99) and specificity of 0.97 (95% CI: 0.85 to 0.99), outperforming neutrophil:lymphocyte ratio.
血清淀粉样蛋白A在12/14只猫治疗后恢复正常。超过50%的肾盂肾炎和输尿管梗阻猫的尿液肾损伤标志物-1升高，显著高于健康猫及患有亚临床菌尿或膀胱炎的猫。随访期间观察到尿液肾损伤标志物-1的变化。在49 mg/L的阈值下，血清淀粉样蛋白A是区分肾盂肾炎与其他泌尿系统疾病的高度准确的生物标志物。尿液肾损伤标志物-1升高提示近端小管损伤频繁发生。
Serum amyloid A normalised in 12/14 cats post-treatment. Urinary kidney injury marker-1 was elevated in more than 50% of cats with pyelonephritis and ureteral obstruction, significantly higher than in healthy cats and those with subclinical bacteriuria or cystitis. Variation in urinary kidney injury marker-1 was observed during follow-up. At a threshold of 49 mg/L, serum amyloid A is a highly accurate biomarker for differentiating pyelonephritis from other urological conditions. Elevated urinary kidney injury marker-1 suggests frequent proximal tubular injury.

临床意义

Clinical Significance
肾盂肾炎的诊断具有挑战性。血清淀粉样蛋白A可以提高诊断精确性，并支持及时的治疗决策。尿液肾损伤标志物-1有助于识别肾小管损伤；然而，其临床意义仍有待研究。肾盂肾炎（PN）是肾盂和肾实质的感染，最常由起源于下尿路的细菌上行感染引起。与猫的其他尿路感染类似，PN主要见于老年雌性猫，并且潜在的肾脏疾病似乎很常见（Whitehouse 等, 2024）。尽管其可能具有严重性，PN的临床表现可能非特异性，且诊断很少直观明了（Weese 等, 2019）。为了获得确切诊断，需要通过肾盂穿刺获得的尿液阳性培养，这是一项技术上具有挑战性的操作，超出常规可及范围。因此，在大多数临床环境中，诊断依赖于膀胱尿液的阳性培养，并结合提示上尿路感染的临床、实验室和/或超声表现。
Pyelonephritis is challenging to diagnose. Serum amyloid A can improve diagnostic precision and support timely treatment decisions. Urinary kidney injury marker-1 helps identify tubular injury; however, the clinical implications remain to be investigated. Pyelonephritis (PN) is an infection of the renal pelvis and parenchyma, most commonly resulting from ascending bacteria originating in the lower urinary tract. Like other urinary tract infections in cats, PN is mostly seen in elderly female cats, and underlying kidney disease appears to be common (Whitehouse et al., 2024). Despite its potential severity, the clinical presentation of PN can be non-specific, and the diagnosis is rarely straightforward (Weese et al., 2019). To obtain a diagnosis with certainty, a positive culture on urine obtained by pyelocentesis is required, a technically challenging procedure beyond routine reach. Thus, in most clinical settings, the diagnosis relies on a positive bladder urine culture coupled with clinical, laboratory and/or ultrasonographic signs suggestive of upper urinary tract infection.
不幸的是，PN 与其他泌尿系统疾病，尤其是慢性肾病（CKD）和输尿管梗阻（UO）有许多相似特征，导致诊断上存在 considerable 的重叠。此外，涉及共存泌尿系统疾病的病例，如患有 CKD 的猫的亚临床菌尿（SBU）或膀胱炎并不少见，并且与 PN 的鉴别特别复杂。血清淀粉样蛋白 A（SAA）是一种主要的急性期蛋白，在炎症刺激下会迅速升高。血清淀粉样蛋白 A 由肝脏产生，血液中在 8 小时内即可检测到升高（Rossi, 2023）。研究显示，患有炎症性疾病的猫，如上呼吸道疾病、肺炎、子宫积脓、猫传染性腹膜炎和败血症，其 SAA 显著升高（Troia 等, 2017；Yuki 等, 2020）。最近，与患有其他泌尿系统疾病的猫相比，17 只患有肾盂肾炎的猫其 SAA 浓度也显著升高（Kurtz 等, 2024），表明 SAA 可能是这一群体猫的有前途的区分性生物标志物。
Unfortunately, PN shares numerous characteristics with other urological disorders, especially chronic kidney disease (CKD) and ureteral obstruction (UO), resulting in considerable diagnostic overlap. Additionally, cases involving coexisting urological conditions, such as subclinical bacteriuria (SBU) or cystitis in cats with CKD, are not uncommon and are particularly complex to differentiate from PN. Serum amyloid A (SAA) is a major acute-phase protein which increases rapidly in response to an inflammatory stimulus. Serum amyloid A is produced in the liver, and increases are detectable in blood within 8 hours (Rossi, 2023). High magnitude increases of SAA have been demonstrated in cats with inflammatory conditions such as upper respiratory disease, pneumonia, pyometra, feline infectious peritonitis and sepsis (Troia et al., 2017; Yuki et al., 2020). Recently, significant increases in SAA concentrations were also demonstrated in 17 cats with pyelonephritis when compared to cats with other urological conditions (Kurtz et al., 2024), suggesting that SAA might be a promising discriminatory biomarker in this population of cats.
中性粒细胞增多、单核细胞增多和淋巴细胞减少是全身性炎症病例中常见的血液学表现。中性粒细胞:淋巴细胞比值 (NLR) 和单核细胞:淋巴细胞比值 (MLR) 可被视为免疫-炎症反应的指标。它们是易于计算的比值，结合了先天和适应性免疫系统的细胞部分，其组成成分可通过大多数自动化实验室血液分析仪进行的常规全血细胞计数 (CBC) 获得。在患有多种炎症和感染性疾病的猫中，已证明 NLR 和 MLR 会发生改变 (Donato 等，2023; Gori 等，2021)，白细胞比率可能有潜力作为患有 PN 的猫的鉴别标志。
Neutrophilia, monocytosis and lymphopaenia are common haematological findings in cases of systemic inflammation. Neutrophil:lymphocyte ratio (NLR) and monocyte:lymphocyte ratio (MLR) can be considered as indicators of the immune-inflammatory response. They are easily calculated ratios, combining cellular parts of the innate and adaptive immune system, with the components available from a routine complete blood count (CBC) as performed by most automated laboratory haematological analysers. Alterations of NLR and MLR have been demonstrated in cats with various inflammatory and infectious conditions (Donato et al., 2023; Gori et al., 2021), and leukocyte ratios could potentially serve as discriminatory markers in cats with PN.
肾损伤标志物-1（KIM-1）是近端肾小管上皮细胞的清道夫受体，是人类、猫和啮齿类动物缺血性或毒性急性肾损伤的高度特异性标志物。KIM-1在各物种中高度保守，且在确诊和疑似急性肾损伤的猫的尿液和肾组织中均已检测到KIM-1分子（Bland等，2014；Bland等，2017）。PN猫的肾小管损伤程度尚未被研究。本研究的主要目的是研究和评估所选非侵入性炎症生物标志物SAA、NLR和MLR的区分潜力。我们假设PN猫会有全身性炎症，因此其SAA浓度以及NLR和MLR将高于患有其他泌尿系统疾病（如UO、CKD、膀胱炎或SBU或这些疾病的组合）的猫。我们还假设PN猫在治疗后SAA会下降。
Kidney injury marker-1 (KIM-1) is a scavenger receptor of the proximal tubular epithelial cells and a highly specific marker of ischaemic or toxic acute kidney injury in people, cats and rodents. KIM-1 is well conserved across species, and the KIM-1 molecule has been identified in urine and renal tissue of cats with confirmed and suspected acute kidney injury (Bland et al., 2014; Bland et al., 2017). The extent of tubular damage in cats with PN has not been investigated. The primary aim of this study was to investigate and assess the discriminatory potential of selected non-invasive inflammatory biomarkers, SAA, NLR and MLR. We hypothesised that cats with PN would have systemic inflammation and thus increased concentration of SAA as well as higher NLR and MLR than cats with other urological conditions, such as UO, CKD, cystitis or SBU or a combination of these. We also hypothesised that SAA would decrease in response to therapy in cats with PN.
次要目的是研究患有PN的猫的肾小管损伤，并评估尿液KIM-1（uKIM-1）是否可以作为生物标志物，用以识别患有PN的猫，并将其与患有其他泌尿系统疾病的猫区分开来。我们假设患有PN的猫和患有UO的猫存在近端小管损伤的风险，因此其uKIM-1浓度会高于健康猫、患有细菌感染（膀胱炎）或下尿路定植（SBU）的猫，以及患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫。
The secondary aim was to investigate renal tubular injury in cats with PN and to assess whether urinary KIM-1 (uKIM-1) can serve as a biomarker to identify cats with PN, discriminating these from cats with other urological conditions. We hypothesised that cats with PN and cats with UO are at risk for proximal tubular injury and thus would have higher uKIM-1 concentrations compared to healthy cats, cats with bacterial infection (cystitis) or colonisation (SBU) of the lower urinary tract, and cats with CKD.

猫的研究设计和入组

Study design and enrolment of cats
本研究设计为一项诊断准确性研究，报告遵循STARD标准（表S1）。猫咪要么在2020年至2024年期间前瞻性入组本研究，要么从2015年至2019年在丹麦哥本哈根大学的伴侣动物大学医院（UHCA）的既往研究中入组（Godia等，2022；Jessen等，2020；Langhorn等，2019），并将存储的尿液和血液样本以及病历数据纳入本研究（图1）。泌尿系统疾病的猫咪为便利样本，包括在UHCA或瑞典哥德堡的蓝星动物医院（BSAH）就诊并符合以下标准的猫咪，而健康对照猫则为研究目的招募。招募在UHCA进行，除了三只猫来自BSAH。已获得UHCA伦理委员会的伦理许可。所有参与研究的猫的主人均签署了知情同意书。

Recruitment took place at the UHCA, except for three cats that were recruited from the BSAH. Ethical permission was obtained from the Ethical Committee at the UHCA. Informed consent was obtained from owners of all cats participating in this study.

猫的分类

Classification of cats
根据经肾盂穿刺获得的尿液培养阳性结果，猫被归类为PN（确诊PN）。根据临床和/或临床病理学发现提示PN，并结合通过膀胱穿刺获得的尿液培养阳性结果，猫被归类为pPN（推测性PN）。提示PN的临床和临床病理学表现定义为 (i) 系统性炎症的症状，如发热和/或系统性疾病的非特异性症状，如嗜睡/食欲不振/呕吐和/或系统性炎症的血液学标志，如中性粒细胞增多、中性粒细胞减少、杆状核粒细胞存在；以及 (ii) 肾脏受累迹象（肾区疼痛和/或氮质血症）。在以往旨在评估患肾盂肾炎猫咪的微小RNA组的研究中（Godia 等, 2022; Jessen 等, 2020），pPN猫的纳入标准之一是急性期蛋白升高。因此，在2020年前纳入的pPN猫在本研究中被排除在评估SAA作为预测性标志物的分析之外。
Cats were classified as PN (confirmed PN) based on a positive urine culture obtained by pyelocentesis. Cats were classified as pPN (presumptive PN) based on clinical AND/OR clinicopathological findings suggestive of PN in combination with a positive urine culture obtained by cystocentesis. Clinical and clinicopathological findings suggestive of PN were defined as (i) signs of systemic inflammation such as pyrexia AND/OR unspecific signs of systemic illness such as lethargy/inappetence/vomiting AND/OR haematological signs of systemic inflammation such as neutrophilia, neutropenia, presence of band neutrophils AND (ii) signs of renal involvement (renal pain AND/OR azotaemia). In previous studies aimed at evaluating the microRNAome in cats with pyelonephritis (Godia et al., 2022; Jessen et al., 2020), increased acute phase proteins were part of the inclusion criteria for cats with pPN. Cats with pPN included before 2020 were, therefore, excluded from the evaluation of SAA as a predictive marker in this study.
如果猫被诊断患有慢性肾病（CKD）、尿路梗阻（UO）、膀胱炎、无症状性菌尿（SBU）或这些疾病的组合，则被归类为患有其他泌尿系统疾病。CKD 的诊断要求血氮升高的肾脏疾病稳定（定义为血清肌酐高于 174 μmol/L），同时尿比重（USG）低于 1.035，或在超声检查中出现变性改变，并且膀胱穿刺尿液细菌培养为阴性。对于从以前的前瞻性泌尿研究项目中回溯性纳入的猫，如果医疗记录无法评估，研究者之前所作的 CKD 诊断也被认为是可接受的。若经超声评估发现猫存在单侧或双侧输尿管梗阻性疾病，并且同期肾盂穿刺尿液细菌培养为阴性，则可诊断为 UO（完全或部分）。
Cats were classified as having other urological conditions if diagnosed with either CKD, UO, cystitis, SBU, or a combination of these. A diagnosis of CKD required stable azotaemic renal disease (defined as serum creatinine above 174 μmol/L) with a urine specific gravity (USG) below 1.035, or degenerative changes on ultrasonography, and a negative cystocentesis urine bacterial culture. For cats that were retrospectively included from previous prospective urological research projects, a diagnosis of CKD made by the researchers of the previous study was considered acceptable if the medical record was not available for evaluation. UO (complete or partial) was diagnosed if cats had uni- or bilateral ureteral obstructive disease on ultrasonographic evaluation with a concurrently negative pyelocentesis urine bacterial culture.
当猫咪出现下尿路感染的临床症状（尿频、排尿困难、排尿痛、尿外漏和/或血尿）且通过膀胱穿刺获得的尿液培养呈阳性时，诊断为膀胱炎；而如果在缺乏这些临床症状的情况下获得了膀胱穿刺尿液培养阳性，则记为无症状菌尿（SBU）。最后，年龄≥1岁的临床健康猫根据体检和临床病理检查结果（全血计数、生化和尿液分析）无异常，被归类为健康。对于所有猫咪，如果在评估前≤72小时内使用过抗生素，则作为入组研究的排除标准。
Cats were diagnosed with cystitis when presented with clinical signs of lower urinary tract infection (pollakiuria, dysuria, stranguria, periuria and/or haematuria) in the presence of a positive urine culture obtained by cystocentesis, whereas SBU was recorded if a positive cystocentesis urine culture was obtained in the absence of these clinical signs. Finally, clinically healthy cats ≥ 1 year of age were classified as healthy based on an unremarkable clinical examination and clinicopathological findings (complete blood count, biochemistry and urinalysis). For all cats, it was an exclusion criterion if the cats had been receiving antibiotics ≤72 hours before evaluation for study inclusion.

诊断工作

Diagnostic work ‐ up
对患有PN、pPN的动物以及患有其他泌尿系统疾病的猫进行的诊断检查由临床主治医生根据自身判断进行，除治疗后SAA和uKIM-1分析外，这两项分析是为了研究目的而进行的。对每只猫，都在哥本哈根大学兽医诊断实验室进行全血计数（CBC）、血清生化、尿液分析和尿液培养。在瑞典BSAH诊断实验室和丹麦哥本哈根大学SUND Vet Diagnostik进行定量好氧尿培养。细菌种类鉴定采用基质辅助激光解吸/电离飞行时间质谱法（MALDI-TOF）（Vitek MS RUO；BioMérieux）。对于患有PN和UO的猫，还进行了腹部超声检查，包括在肾盂扩张（>2 毫米扩张）情况下通过肾盂穿刺收集尿液以进行好氧培养。
Diagnostic work-up of animals with PN, pPN and cats with other urological conditions was performed according to the discretion of the attending clinician, except for post-treatment SAA and uKIM-1 analysis, which was performed for the purpose of the study. For each cat, a CBC, serum biochemistry, urinalysis and urine culture were performed at the Veterinary Diagnostic laboratory, University of Copenhagen, Denmark. Quantitative aerobic urine culture was performed at SUND Vet Diagnostik, University of Copenhagen, Denmark and at the BSAH diagnostic laboratory, Sweden. Bacterial species identification was performed by matrixassisted laser desorption/ionisation-time of flight (MALDI-TOF) mass spectrometry (Vitek MS RUO; BioMérieux). For cats with PN and UO, abdominal ultrasonography was also performed, including the collection of urine by pyelocentesis in case of pyelectasia (>2 mm dilation) for aerobic culture.

NLR 和 MLR

NLR and MLR
在入组时对全血细胞计数（CBC）进行了分析。根据地点和入院时间，分析使用的仪器为Advia 2120i（西门子医疗, 埃尔朗根, 德国）或Procyte R（IDEXX 实验室, 美国缅因州韦斯特布鲁克）。中性粒细胞增多症定义为使用任一仪器测得>12.6 × 10^9/L，左移定义为带状中性粒细胞计数增加>0.3 × 10^9/L（显微镜确认）。每只猫都计算了中性粒细胞与淋巴细胞比值以及单核细胞与淋巴细胞比值。
A CBC was analysed at the time of inclusion. Depending on location and time of admission, analysis was performed with either the Advia 2120i (Siemens Healthineers, Erlangen, Germany) or the Procyte R (IDEXX Laboratories, Inc., Westbrook, Maine). Neutrophilia was defined as >12.6 × 109/L using either instrument, and left shift was defined as an increased band neutrophil count >0.3 × 109/L (confirmed microscopically). Neutrophil:lymphocyte and monocyte:lymphocyte ratios were calculated for each cat.

血清淀粉样蛋白A

Serum amyloid A
血清淀粉样蛋白A（SAA）在入组时作为生化指标的一部分进行测定。分析在样本采集后48小时内完成，但三只来自BSAH的猫例外，它们的SAA是在采集后储存在−80°C下9年的血清中测定的。在患有PN的猫中，SAA在治疗停止后一周内再次测定。在治疗期间，SAA的测定不属于常规方案，但可由主治医生酌情进行。SAA的浓度在UCPH兽医诊断实验室进行分析（LZ-SAA（Eiken chemicals A/S，日本东京）在Advia 1800, SIEMENS上测定，于2022年6月26日之前，随后在Atellica分析仪上使用VET-SAA（Eiken chemicals A/S，日本东京）测定）。使用任一仪器时，SAA浓度>5 mg/L被定义为升高。进行培养的微生物学家不知道SAA分析结果，而主治医生和研究人员未进行盲法处理。
Serum amyloid A was measured at the time of inclusion as part of the biochemical profile. The analysis was run within 48 hours of sample collection, except for the three cats from BSAH, for which SAA was measured in serum stored at −80°C for 9 years after collection. In cats with PN, SAA was measured again within 1 week of treatment cessation. During treatment, SAA measurement was not part of the protocol but could be performed at the discretion of the attending clinician. The concentration of SAA was analysed at the Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory at the UCPH (LZ-SAA (Eiken chemicals A/S, Tokyo, Japan) on Advia 1800, SIEMENS, before June 26, 2022, then VET-SAA (Eiken chemicals A/S, Tokyo, Japan) on Atellica analyser, SIEMENS). Increased SAA was defined as a concentration > 5 mg/L using either instrument. The microbiologists performing cultures were blinded to the result of the SAA analysis, whereas attending clinicians and investigators were not blinded.

尿KIM-1

Urinary KIM ‐ 1
多余的尿液被分装到1 mL塑料小瓶中，并储存在−80°C。样品被分批处理，并用快递在冰上运送到加拿大圭尔夫大学。样品在运输前身份被隐藏。为了分析，尿样被解冻并短暂离心。取20 μL尿液与400 μL缓冲液混合，其中150 μL混合液用于猫专用侧向流动检测。室温下孵育15分钟后，用手持射频识别阅读器测量测试样品和膜中阳性对照的KIM-1（Bland等，2019）。计算测试样品与对照样品的光密度比值，并归一化至尿比重（USG）1.035。
Excess urine was aliquoted in 1 mL plastic vials and stored at −80°C. Samples were batched and shipped on ice with courier to the University of Guelph, Canada. Sample identity was disguised before shipping. For analysis, urine samples were thawed and briefly centrifuged. A 20 μL aliquot of urine was mixed with 400 μL of buffer, and 150 μL of this mixture was applied to a feline-specific lateral flow assay. After 15 minutes of incubation at room temperature, the KIM-1 of the test sample and of the positive control impregnated into the membrane was measured with a handheld radiofrequency identification reader (Bland et al., 2019). The ratio of the test to control sample optical density was calculated and normalised to a USG of 1.035.

统计分析

Statistical analysis
统计分析使用 MedCalc® 统计软件 22.016 版本（MedCalc Software Ltd，比利时奥斯坦德；https://www.medcalc.org；2023）和 GraphPad Prism 10.0.3 进行。统计显著性设定为 P < .05。正态性和对数正态性检验使用 D'Agostino 和 Pearson 检验进行。连续变量（SAA、NLR、MLR）以中位数及第 25 和第 75 百分位数表示。采用 Kruskal-Wallis 检验以检测猫组之间的统计学显著差异，随后进行 Dunn 事后分析。在一只患有 pPN 的猫和三只患有其他泌尿系统疾病的猫中，SAA 浓度低于 VET-SAA 测定法的下限报告值（<5 mg/L），因此 SAA 浓度被赋值为 0。
Statistical analyses were performed using MedCalc® Statistical Software version 22.016 (MedCalc Software Ltd, Ostend, Belgium; https://www.medcalc.org ; 2023) and GraphPad Prism 10.0.3. Statistical significance was set at P < .05. Tests for normality and log normality were performed using D'Agostino and Pearson tests. Continuous variables (SAA, NLR, MLR) are presented as medians with 25th and 75th percentiles. A Kruskal-Wallis test was applied to detect statistically significant differences between groups of cats, followed by Dunn's post hoc analysis. In one cat with pPN and in three cats with other urological disorders, SAA concentrations were below the lower reporting limit (<5 mg/L) of the VET-SAA assay, and the SAA concentrations were assigned a value of 0.
采用受试者工作特征（ROC）分析计算SAA、NLR和MLR的曲线下面积（AUC），以区分患有PNs（确诊和推定）与患有其他泌尿系统疾病的猫。计算了95%的置信区间（95% CI）。为了获得SAA、NLR和MLR的最佳截断值估计，将与尤登指数最大值（敏感性 × 特异性 - 1；c代表所有可能的截断值）相关联的截断值指定为最佳截断值。由于与尤登指数最大值相关的最佳截断值依赖于50%的疾病患病率、对敏感性和特异性赋予相等权重并忽略各种决策成本，因此也使用MedCalc中的成本方法估算了最佳截断值。简而言之，针对疾病患病率分别为10%、33%和50%，并假设假阳性和假阴性的成本为1，而真正阳性和真正阴性的成本为0，计算了敏感性（SE）、特异性（SP）和正似然比（LR+）。
Receiver operating characteristic analysis was employed to calculate the area under the curve (AUC) of SAA, NLR and MLR to differentiate between cats with PNs (confirmed and presumptive) and cats with other urological conditions. The 95% confidence interval (95% CI) was calculated. To obtain estimates of the optimal cut-off value for SAA, LNR and MLR, the cut-off value associated with the maximum value of the Youden index (sensitivityc + specificityc − 1; with c representing all possible cut-off values) was assigned as the optimal cut-off value. Because the optimal cut-off value associated with the maximum Youden index relies on a disease prevalence of 50%, giving equal weight to sensitivity and specificity and ignoring costs of various decisions, the optimal cutoff value was also estimated using the cost approach in MedCalc. Briefly, sensitivity (SE), specificity (SP) and positive likelihood ratio (LR+) were calculated for disease prevalences of 10%, 33% and 50% and assuming costs of false positive and false negative of 1 compared to costs of true positive and true negative of 0.

我们研究的主要目的是评估血清淀粉样蛋白A（SAA）浓度和白细胞比值区分肾盂肾炎（PN）猫与患有其他泌尿系统疾病（构成相关鉴别诊断）的猫的能力。我们发现，所有患有PN的猫以及除一只患有轻度PN（pPN）的猫外，SAA均有显著增加（21至169倍）。此外，PN猫与其他泌尿系统疾病猫的SAA浓度几乎没有重叠。血清淀粉样蛋白A被证明是比临床症状更敏感的炎症标志物，因为只有48%的肾盂肾炎猫（PN及pPN）出现发热（临界值39.2°C）。在我们处理数据期间，法国的一项研究也支持将SAA用作PN猫的生物标志物（Kurtz等，2024）。研究人群总体上与我们的相似，尽管其他泌尿系统疾病猫的群体更广，分类方法也有所不同（由专家组分类）。
The main aim of our study was to assess the ability of SAA concentration and leukocyte ratios to discriminate cats with PN from cats with other urological conditions constituting relevant differential diagnoses. We found a substantial (21to 169-fold) increase in SAA in all cats with PN, and in all but one cat with pPN. Furthermore, we found minimal overlap in SAA concentrations between cats with PN and cats with other urological conditions. Serum amyloid A proved to be a more sensitive marker of inflammation than the clinical signs, as only 48% of the pyelonephritis cats (PN + pPN) were pyretic (cut-off 39.2°C). During our data processing, another study supporting the use of SAA as a biomarker in cats with PN was published from France (Kurtz et al., 2024). The study population was overall fairly similar to ours, though the group of cats with other urological diseases was wider, and the classification methods varied (classified by panel).
在将患有PN/pPN的猫与患有其他泌尿系统疾病的猫进行比较时，Kurtz及其同事也发现PN猫的SAA浓度显著升高，有趣的是，他们发现其最佳临界值为51.3 mg/L。这些结果与我们的研究一致，表明SAA检测在不同实验室条件下具有可重复性和稳健性，从而增强了我们对结论的信心。乍一看，我们的发现与Kurtz等人的研究似乎与以往关于CKD和下尿路疾病猫SAA的研究存在冲突。然而，这种差异主要反映了所采用的临界值不同。以往的研究（Javard等，2017；Sasaki等，2003；Yuki等，2020）报道患有CKD和/或下尿路疾病的猫存在全身性炎症和SAA升高，使用的临界值为≤5 mg/L，即健康猫的上限。
When comparing cats with PN/pPN to cats with other urological diseases, Kurtz and coworkers also found a significantly higher SAA concentration in cats with PN, and interestingly, they found an optimal cut-off value of 51.3 mg/L. These results are consistent with ours, indicating reproducibility and robustness of the SAA assay despite different laboratory set-ups, and thus strengthening our confidence in the conclusions. At first glance, our findings and those of Kurtz et al. appear to conflict with previous studies on SAA in cats with CKD and lower urinary tract disease. However, this discrepancy mainly reflects differences in cut-off values applied. Previous studies (Javard et al., 2017; Sasaki et al., 2003; Yuki et al., 2020) reported systemic inflammation and increased SAA in cats with CKD and/or lower urinary tract disease, using a cut-off of ≤5 mg/L, the upper limit for healthy cats.
当应用较高阈值50 mg/L——这是我们研究和Kurtz及其同事研究中计算的截断值的近似值——大多数慢性肾病（CKD）和下尿路病例低于该截断值。在Sasaki等人的简短通讯中，只有2/27例下尿路疾病猫和12/75例肾功能衰竭猫的SAA浓度>50 mg/L（Sasaki等，2003）。未提供关于疾病持续时间、下尿路疾病猫是否伴有上尿路受累或伴随疾病的详细信息。Yuki等报告称，CKD和下尿路疾病猫的SAA浓度明显高于健康猫，但未显示个体数值。下尿路疾病的SAA中位浓度为0 mg/L（四分位距：0至53.8），CKD的四分位距为0至5.9 mg/L，提示大多数浓度低于50 mg/L（Yuki等，2020）。同样，Jarvard等。
When applying a higher threshold of 50 mg/L – an approximation of the cut-offs calculated in our study and the study by Kurtz and coworkers – most CKD and lower urinary tract cases fall below this cut-off. In Sasaki et al.'s short communication, an SAA concentration > 50 mg/L was found in only 2/27 cats with lower urinary tract disease and in 12/75 with renal failure (Sasaki et al., 2003). No details were provided on disease duration, concurrent upper urinary tract involvement for the cats with lower urinary tract disease, or comorbidities. Yuki et al. reported significantly higher SAA concentrations in cats with CKD and lower urinary tract disease than in healthy cats, but individual values were not shown. Median SAA concentration in lower urinary tract disease was 0 mg/L (IQR: 0 to 53.8), and in CKD, the IQR was 0 to 5.9 mg/L, suggesting most concentrations fell below 50 mg/L (Yuki et al., 2020). Likewise, Jarvard et al.
在38只慢性肾脏疾病（CKD）猫中，报告的平均血清淀粉样蛋白A（SAA）浓度为8.1 mg/L（标准差6.5），支持了慢性肾脏病中的炎症不会引发高幅度急性期反应的观点（Javard 等，2017）。Degenhardt 等研究了肾性氮质血症猫（急性肾损伤（AKI）、CKD 和急性加慢性肾病（CKD）（AOC））的SAA。在44只猫中，15只的SAA浓度超过50 mg/L；其中四只是PN，其余11只是诊断为AOC、AKI或CKD，并伴有各种合并症，其中一些众所周知与系统性炎症相关（子宫积脓、穿孔性肠道异物、糖尿病酮症酸中毒）（Degenhardt 等，2023）。在CKD猫中，SAA升高在所有病例中都与合并症相关，而在AKI或AOC猫中，高SAA浓度的原因则不太明显。
reported a mean SAA concentration of 8.1 mg/L (SD 6.5) in 38 cats with CKD, supporting the notion that inflammation in chronic renal disease does not elicit a high magnitude acute phase response (Javard et al., 2017). Degenhardt et al. studied SAA in cats with renal azotaemia (acute kidney injury (AKI), CKD and acute-on-chronic kidney disease (AOC)). Of 44 cats, 15 had SAA concentrations >50 mg/L; four had PN and the remaining 11 cats were diagnosed with AOC, AKI or CKD plus a variety of comorbid conditions, some of which are well known to be associated with systemic inflammation (pyometra, perforated intestinal foreign body, diabetic ketoacidosis) (Degenhardt et al., 2023). In cats with CKD, an SAA increase was associated with comorbid conditions in all cases, whereas the cause of a high SAA concentration was less obvious in cats with AKI or AOC.
总而言之，导致患有氮质血症的猫咪SAA浓度升高的原因显然不仅限于肾盂肾炎，这种非特异性的急性期蛋白应结合其他临床和副临床检查结果进行解读。然而，对于患有氮质血症和菌尿的猫咪，如果SAA浓度升高超过50 mg/dL，则可以认为其具有肾盂肾炎的指征，除非发现其他明显的炎症原因。同样，如果SAA低于50 mg/L阈值，则患有氮质血症和菌尿的猫咪不太可能患有肾盂肾炎。在临床环境中，这尤其有帮助，因为在患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫咪中，SBU或膀胱炎由于肾盂肾炎的临床表现非特异性而被认为难以与肾盂肾炎区分。
To conclude this argument, causes of increased SAA concentration in cats with azotaemia are obviously not restricted to PN, and this non-specific acute phase protein should be interpreted in the context of other clinical and paraclinical findings. However, in cats with azotaemia and bacteriuria, an increased SAA concentration > 50 mg/dL can be considered indicative of PN, unless another apparent cause of inflammation is identified. Likewise, PN can be considered unlikely in cats with azotaemia and bacteriuria if SAA lies below the 50 mg/L threshold. This is particularly helpful in a clinical setting, as SBU or cystitis in cats with underlying CKD is considered difficult to discriminate from PN due to the unspecific clinical presentation of the latter.
本研究进一步发现，患有PN的猫在抗菌治疗过程中SAA浓度会下降。临床意义包括SAA可能在指导治疗持续时间方面发挥作用，从而将抗菌治疗针对个体猫。然而，我们的研究并未检验基于SAA反应来定制治疗，并且在治疗过程中SAA浓度作为反应生物标志物也未进行系统评估。中性粒细胞:淋巴细胞比值被认为是一个潜在有趣的生物标志物，因为它结合了先天免疫和适应性免疫系统的部分功能。研究发现，与其他猫相比，患有PN的猫的NLR显著较高。然而，各组之间的数值有重叠，且阳性检测结果仅略微增加PN的可能性。与血常规中的其他成分相比，如中性粒细胞增多和杆状核中性粒细胞增多，NLR是否能提供额外的区分价值仍值得怀疑。
This study further identified that the SAA concentration in cats with PN decreases with antimicrobial treatment. The clinical implications include a potential role for SAA to guide the duration of treatment and thus target antimicrobial therapy to the individual cat. However, tailoring therapy based on SAA response was not examined in our study, and the SAA concentration as a response biomarker during treatment was not systematically assessed. Neutrophil:lymphocyte ratio was considered a potentially interesting biomarker as it combined parts of both the innate and adaptive immune system. The NLR was found to be significantly higher in cats with PN compared to other cats. However, values overlapped between groups, and a positive test result only modestly increased the likelihood of PN. It is doubtful that NLR provides any added discriminatory value when compared to other components of the blood count, such as neutrophilia and an increased number of band neutrophils.
中性粒细胞：淋巴细胞比率可能在监测高比率猫的治疗中发挥作用；然而，这在我们的研究中未进行测试。有趣的是，Kurtz及其同事的研究中也评估了NLR，但未发现各组之间有显著差异（Kurtz等，2024）。我们研究的第二个目标是评估PN猫的肾小管损伤，我们在超过50%的PN和尿潴留猫中检测到轻度至明显的尿KIM-1升高，证实近端小管损伤在这些猫群体中很常见。我们还在PN和尿潴留猫中检测到尿KIM-1浓度的较大变异，这可以归因于几个因素。近端肾小管细胞产生KIM-1并将其释放到尿液中，取决于损伤的严重程度、范围和持续时间。严重的急性肾损伤在24小时后会导致近端小管远端段KIM-1显著增加，并发生在尿比重（USG）下降之前（Bland等，2017）。但严重损伤也可能引起细胞肿胀、细胞死亡和尿液流动阻塞。
Neutrophil:lymphocyte ratio may potentially play a part in monitoring of treatment in cats with increased ratios; this, however, was not tested in our study. Of interest, NLR was also assessed in the study by Kurtz and coworkers and was not found to be significantly different between groups (Kurtz et al., 2024). The second aim of our study was to assess tubular damage in cats with PN, and we detected mild to marked uKIM-1 elevations in more than 50% of cats with PN and UO, confirming that proximal tubular injury is common in these populations of cats. We also detected large variations in uKIM-1 concentrations in cats with PN and UO, which can be attributed to several factors. Production of KIM-1 by proximal renal tubular cells and its shedding into urine depend on the severity, extent and duration of injury. Severe AKI results in markedly increased KIM-1 in the distal segment of the proximal tubular cells after 24 hours and occurs before decreases in USG (Bland et al., 2017). But severe injury can also cause cell swelling, cell death, and obstruction of urine flow.
如果这种情况是单侧发生的，来自未受影响肾脏的低尿KIM-1可能会与来自受伤肾脏的少量高尿KIM-1混合，导致整体尿KIM-1值从轻度到显著增加，甚至可能正常，这些值可能会随着尿液流动的恢复和/或上皮再生而变化。在我们的研究中，在有连续样本的九只猫中，有四只猫在初次就诊和第一次随访之间显示出尿KIM-1的显著变化（图6），强调了连续尿KIM-1测量比单次样本更有可能反映损伤和修复的程度。我们研究中另一个重要因素涉及检测到的炎症的解剖定位。上行感染可能局限于肾盂（肾盂炎）或扩展到肾实质（肾盂肾炎），这些情况在临床实践中无法可靠区分。
If this occurs unilaterally, low uKIM-1 from a non-affected kidney may mix with smaller volumes of high uKIM-1 from an injured kidney and result in overall mildly to markedly increased, or even normal, uKIM-1 values, which may change as urine flow is re-instituted and/or the epithelium regenerates. In our study, four out of nine cats, from which consecutive samples were available, displayed marked changes in uKIM-1 between initial presentation and the first follow-up visit (Fig 6), stressing that sequential uKIM-1 measurements are likely more useful than single samples to measure the extent of injury and repair. Another factor of importance in our study concerns the anatomic localisation of the inflammation detected. Ascending infection may be confined to the renal pelvis (pyelitis) or extend into the renal parenchyma (pyelonephritis), conditions that cannot be reliably differentiated in clinical practice.
从解剖学上看，KIM-1 主要由近曲小管上皮细胞表达（Bland 等，2017）。因此，仅限于肾盂的感染可能不会引起显著的 KIM-1 表达。在急性肾损伤（AKI）的前临床啮齿动物模型中，解剖部位的重要性得到了强调，其中患有肾盂肾炎（PN）相关 AKI 的大鼠尿液中 KIM-1（uKIM-1）水平升高，而仅患急性 PN 的大鼠则未见升高（Skowron 等，2018）。基于组织病理学，该研究的作者得出结论，肾脏炎症的定位在有 uKIM-1 升高和无 uKIM-1 升高的大鼠之间存在差异。最后，疾病的慢性化可能伴随肾小管纤维化或萎缩，导致 KIM-1 产生减少或缺失（Zhang 等，2008）。这在我们研究的患有 PN 或尿路阻塞（UO）、伴随多种未知持续时间疾病（包括慢性肾病（CKD） CKD）的猫群体中可能具有相关性。最近一项针对猫的研究也提出，随着 CKD 进展，KIM-1 产生能力下降，其中 CKD 猫的 uKIM-1 浓度与健康猫无显著差异（Kornya 等，2025）。
Anatomically, KIM-1 is expressed predominantly by proximal tubular epithelial cells (Bland et al., 2017). Therefore, infections confined to the renal pelvis may not provoke significant KIM-1 expression. The importance of anatomic localisation was highlighted in a preclinical rodent model of AKI, in which uKIM-1 was increased in rats with PN-associated AKI, but not in rats with acute PN alone (Skowron et al., 2018). Based on histopathology, the authors of that study concluded that localisation of renal inflammation differed between rats with and without uKIM-1 increases. Lastly, disease chronicity may involve fibrotic or atrophic tubules, leading to reduced or absent KIM-1 production (Zhang et al., 2008). This may be of relevance in our population of cats with PN or UO suffering from multiple coexisting conditions of unknown duration, including CKD. Declining capacity of KIM-1 production with advanced CKD was also proposed in a recent study of cats, in which uKIM-1 concentrations of cats with CKD did not differ from those of healthy cats (Kornya et al., 2025).
本研究存在一些局限性。样本量不是通过样本量计算得出的，而是基于可行性（经肾盂穿刺确诊的PN病例较少）以及可用资金来确定的。我们采用病例对照设计来招募猫，而不是连续招募所有出现泌尿系统疾病的猫。尽管在诊断研究中通常使用病例对照招募，但这存在固有的选择偏倚风险。然而，在我们的研究中，泌尿系统疾病对照组的猫在复杂性（血氮增高和伴随疾病的存在）方面与PN或pPN猫相似，从而提高了结果的普遍适用性。本研究中使用了两种不同的SAA检测方法，即LZ-SAA和VET-SAA，后者也用于Kurtz及其同事的研究（Kurtz等，2024）。已发表的LZ-SAA和VET-SAA检测方法比较显示，在将样本分类为正常或升高方面具有高度一致性，尽管绝对值存在一定差异（Waugh等，2022）。
There are some limitations to the present study. The sample size was not derived from a sample size calculation but was based on feasibility (PN cases confirmed by pyelocentesis are rare) and available funding. We used a case–control design for enrolment of cats rather than consecutive enrolment of all cats presenting with urological disease. Though case–control enrolment is commonly used in diagnostic studies, it carries an inherent risk of selection bias. However, in our study, the control group of cats with urological disease resembled cats with PN or pPN in terms of complexity (azotaemia and presence of comorbidities), thus increasing the generalizability of the Two different SAA assays were used in the present study, the LZ-SAA and the VET-SAA, the latter used also in the study by Kurtz and coworkers (Kurtz et al., 2024). Published comparisons of the LZ-SAA and VET-SAA assays report high concordance in classifying samples as normal or increased, despite modest differences in absolute values (Waugh et al., 2022).
2022年6月转用VET-SAA检测方法后，对十只动物进行了SAA定量，其中六只的SAA值非常高（199至848 mg/L），其余四只的SAA值<5 mg/L。因此，虽然检测方法的转换可能引入了轻微的分析差异，但不太可能影响本研究结果的解读。来自三只猫的血清样本在分析SAA之前储存了很长时间。在猫中，研究表明SAA在-20°C下可长期稳定（Ceron等，2005）。在本研究中，样本一直储存在-80°C，并且冻融循环次数很少。在这种条件下，猫SAA显著降解被认为不太可能。最后，三份储存了9年的样本的SAA浓度分别为199、640和848 mg/L。即使假设在储存期间发生了一些SAA蛋白降解，也不会改变本研究的结论。
The transition to the VET-SAA assay in June 2022 resulted in SAA quantification for ten animals, six of which had very high SAA values (199 to 848 mg/L) and the remaining 4 had SAA values <5 mg/L. Therefore, while the assay transition may have introduced minor analytical variation, it is unlikely to have impacted the interpretation of results in this study. Serum samples from three cats were stored for an extensive time period before analysing SAA. In cats, SAA has been shown to remain stable for prolonged periods at −20°C (Ceron et al., 2005). In the present study, samples were stored continuously at −80°C with minimal freeze–thaw cycles. Under these conditions, significant degradation of feline SAA is considered unlikely. Finally, the SAA concentrations in the three samples stored for 9 years were 199, 640 and 848 mg/L, respectively. Even if hypothetically some SAA protein degradation took place during storage it would not have altered the conclusions of this study.
总之，我们发现患有PN的猫SAA水平升高，并且在治疗后下降。当应用50 mg/L的阈值时，SAA大大增加了泌尿系统疾病猫患肾盂肾炎的可能性。此外，我们的结果表明，近端小管损伤在患有PN和UO的猫中很常见。小管损伤的进展最好通过连续检测来评估；然而，uKIM-1变化的临床意义仍有待明确。
In conclusion, we found that SAA increases in cats with PN and decreases in response to therapy. When applying a threshold of 50 mg/L, SAA increases the likelihood of pyelonephritis substantially in cats with urological diseases. In addition, our results indicate that proximal tubular injury is common in cats with PN and cats with UO.
