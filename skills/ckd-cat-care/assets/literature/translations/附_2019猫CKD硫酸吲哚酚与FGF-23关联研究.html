1. 引言

1. INTRODUCTION
靛基质硫酸盐（IS）是一种与蛋白质结合的尿毒症毒素，来源于饮食蛋白质，主要通过肾脏排泄。已有报道表明，其水平会随着人类肾功能受损程度的加重而增加，也见于实验动物，特别是犬和猫。此外，IS水平升高还被发现与人类慢性肾病（CKD）患者的预后相关，并与猫慢性肾病（CKD）的进展相关。一些动物研究表明，IS还在抑制内皮细胞增殖和成骨细胞功能中起作用。这些研究结果提示，IS的积累可能是导致人类慢性肾病（CKD）患者骨异常的因素之一，它可能改变钙和磷的浓度并影响其调节。
Indoxyl sulfate (IS) is a protein-bound uremic toxin derived from dietary protein and mainly eliminated by renal excretion.1 It has been reported to increase with the severity of impaired renal function in humans2 and laboratory animals,3 specifically dogs and cats.4, 5 Additionally, increased IS also has been found to be related to prognosis in humans with chronic kidney disease (CKD)6 and to be associated with progression in cats with CKD.4 Some studies in animals have indicated that IS also plays a role in the inhibition of endothelial proliferation and osteoblast function.7 These findings suggest that accumulation of IS may be factor involved in the bone abnormalities found in human CKD patients in whom it may alter calcium and phosphate concentrations and affect their regulation.
成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF-23）-23（FGF-23）由骨细胞和成骨细胞分泌，以应对血清磷浓度的升高，它与甲状旁腺激素（PTH）共同作用，调节血清磷浓度、活性维生素D（钙三醇）和磷的稳态。已有报告指出，FGF-23的浓度也会随人类和实验动物肾功能受损的严重程度而增加，并导致体内磷酸钙沉积。研究还表明，FGF-23在猫和狗的慢性肾病（CKD）严重程度增加时也会升高。此外，FGF-23浓度升高被发现与人类CKD的进展相关，以及与患CKD的猫的生存时间缩短相关。据报道，人类的吲哚硫酸盐（IS）与FGF-23独立相关，并参与矿物质代谢。慢性肾病（CKD）是一种在猫中患病率较高的常见疾病，已有研究报道IS和FGF-23与疾病的严重程度及预后相关，但尚不清楚在患CKD的猫中IS是否与FGF-23存在关联。
Fibroblast growth factor-23 (FGF-23), which is secreted by osteocytes and osteoblasts in response to increases serum phosphate concentration, acts with parathyroid hormone to regulate serum phosphate concentration, calcitriol, and phosphate homeostasis.8 The concentration of FGF-23 also was reported to increase with the severity of impaired renal function in humans9 and laboratory animals10 and results in the deposition of calcium phosphate within the body.11 Studies also have shown that FGF-23 increases with the severity of CKD in both cats12 and dogs.13 Furthermore, an increased FGF-23 concentration was found to be associated with progression of CKD in people9 and shorter survival time in cats with CKD.14 Indoxyl sulfate in humans has been reported to correlate independently with FGF-23 and to be involved in mineral metabolism.15 Chronic kidney disease is a common disease with a high prevalence in cats, and both IS and FGF-23 have been reported to be related to severity and outcome but it is not yet known whether or not an association of IS with FGF-23 exists in cats with CKD.
因此，我们的目标是：(1) 阐明血清FGF-23浓度是否与猫在不同CKD阶段的IS浓度及其他临床变量相关；(2) 评估使用IS和FGF-23作为猫CKD进展预测标志物的可能性。
Therefore, our aims were to: (1) elucidate whether serum FGF-23 concentrations are correlated with IS concentrations and other clinical variables across the various stages of CKD in cats and (2) evaluate the possibility of using IS and FGF-23 as predictive markers for CKD progression in cats.

2. 材料与方法

2. MATERIALS AND METHODS
2.1. 患者和样本收集 从前往台湾大学兽医医院就诊的猫收集血浆和尿液样本，并储存在−80°C直至进行ELISA和高效液相色谱（HPLC）分析。所有构成对照组的病例均未出现任何慢性肾病（CKD）的临床症状，并且符合我们的纳入标准，即它们接受了全血细胞计数（CBC）、血清生化分析，并且其血尿素氮（BUN）和血清肌酐浓度在正常范围内（即BUN浓度<30 mg/dL，血清肌酐浓度<1.6 mg/dL）。这些猫还进行了尿液分析和肾脏超声检查，结果也均在正常范围内。
2.1. Patient and sample collection Plasma and urine samples were collected from cats that attended National Taiwan University Veterinary Hospital and stored at −80°C until ELISA and high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC). All cases that formed the control group lacked any clinical signs of CKD and also met our inclusion criteria, namely they had CBC, serum biochemistry analysis, and had blood urea nitrogen (BUN) and serum creatinine concentrations within normal limits (ie, BUN concentration <30 mg/dL and serum creatinine concentration <1.6 mg/dL). These cats also had urinalysis performed and ultrasound examination of their kidneys and these findings also were within normal limits.
血清生化分析异常（血清肌酐浓度≥1.6 mg/dL）且具备以下条件之一的猫：有临床症状史（如多尿和多饮）、尿液分析异常（尿比重（USG）<1.030或基于尿蛋白与肌酐比值（UPC）[UPC]的持续性蛋白尿>0.5，且在三次检测中至少间隔两周）、有或没有异常超声表现（小而不规则的肾脏，皮质髓质分界减小），持续时间至少3个月，这些猫被纳入慢性肾病（CKD）组。若存在以下任一情况的患者将被排除在研究之外：急性肾脏疾病或急性氮质血症加重（血清肌酐浓度在7天内增加>0.3 mg/dL），或同时患有甲状腺功能亢进、下泌尿道疾病，或其他非肾脏疾病（肝脏、心脏、感染性或肿瘤性疾病）的任何临床症状。所有CKD猫进一步使用国际肾脏兴趣协会（IRIS）分期系统进行分组。
Cats with abnormal serum biochemistry analysis (serum creatinine concentration ≥1.6 mg/dL) and showing 1 of the following conditions: a history of clinical signs (eg, polyuria and polydispsia), abnormal urinalysis (urine specific gravity <1.030 or persistent proteinuria based on urine protein-to-creatinine ratio [UPC] >0.5 on 3 occasions and >2 weeks apart), with or without abnormal ultrasonographic findings (small irregular kidneys, decreased corticomedullary distinction) for at least 3 months were enrolled in the CKD group. Patients were excluded from the study if they had 1 of the following problems: acute kidney disease or acute worsening azotemia (>0.3 mg/dL increase in serum creatinine concentration within 7 days) or when they had concurrent hyperthyroidism, lower urinary tract disease, or any clinical signs of other nonrenal diseases (hepatic, cardiac, infectious, or neoplastic disease). All cats with CKD were further grouped using the International Renal Interest Society (IRIS) staging system.
根据IRIS慢性肾病（CKD）分期，这些患者被分为1、2、3或4期。符合条件的慢性肾病（CKD）患者被分为进展组和非进展组。进展定义为血清肌酐浓度增加0.5 mg/dL或死亡，包括在3个月期间因疾病进展而安乐死的患者。 2.2. 通过HPLC测量尿毒素总浓度 使用配备泵（Hitachi L7100，日立，日本东京）、荧光检测器（Hitachi F-1050，日立，日本东京）和反相HPLC柱（Mightysil RP-18 GP, 4.6 × 250 mm, 5 μm）的HPLC系统测定总尿毒素浓度。吲哚硫酸钾盐溶解于甲醇中作为标准溶液，并储存在-20°C，避光保存。
In accordance with IRIS CKD staging, these patients were classified into stages 1, 2, 3, or 4. Eligible CKD patients were classified into a progression group and a non-progression group. Progression was defined as a 0.5 mg/dL increment in serum creatinine concentration or death, including those that were euthanized for progression within the 3-month period. 2.2. Measurement of IS by HPLC Total IS concentration was determined using an HPLC system equipped with a pump (Hitachi L7100, Hitachi, Tokyo, Japan), fluorescence detector (Hitachi F-1050, Hitachi, Tokyo, Japan) and reversed phase HPLC column (Mightysil RP-18 GP, 4.6 × 250 mm, 5 μm). Indoxyl sulfate potassium salt was dissolved in methanol as a standard solution and stored at −20°C and protected from light.

One-hundred microliters of plasma were mixed with 360 μL of acetonitrile and vortexed for 30 seconds before the mixture was centrifuged at 8160g for 5 minutes at 4°C. The supernatant then was filtered through a 0.22 μm polyvinylidene difluoride syringe filter before HPLC was performed. The analytical conditions were similar to those previously described.5 Briefly, the mobile phase consisted of sodium acetate buffer (pH 4.5) and acetonitrile (10:90, V/V). The flow rate was 1 mL/min and the injection volume was 10 μL. The excitation and emission wavelengths were set at 280 and 375 nm, respectively. Under these conditions, the retention time of IS was 2.34 ± 0.01 minutes. The limit of detection and limit of quantification were 0.05 and 0.2 mg/L, respectively.

Measurement of FGF ‐ 23 by commercial feline FGF ‐ 23 ELISA kit Feline FGF-23 concentrations were measured in plasma samples using a commercial quantitative sandwich ELISA Kit (MyBioSource, San Diego, California) for the detection of intact FGF-23 by following the manufacturer's instructions. Each sample was performed in duplicate and the final concentration is presented as the mean of the 2 concentrations. All chemicals and reagents were supplied by the kit (MyBioSource), including 6 vials of standard solutions: 1000, 500, 250, 125, 62.5, and 31.2 pg/mL, sample diluent, horseradish peroxidase-conjugate reagent, washing solution, chromogen solution A, chromogen solution B, and stop solution.

Statistical analysis Statistical analysis was performed using SPSS software (v.20). The normality of each data set was tested using the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test. Differences in categorical variables were analyzed by either the Chi-square test or Fisher's exact test. Spearman correlation coefficients, Pearson correlation coefficients, and linear regression were calculated to determine the associations between different variables. Logistic regression was applied to evaluate the correlation between variables and CKD progression and to calculate odds ratios. The probability of the F value to be entered and probability of the F value to be removed during multiple regression analysis were set at F ≤0.05 and F ≥0.1, respectively.

4. 讨论

4. DISCUSSION
虽然有报道称IS和FGF-23都是CKD严重程度的生物标志物，并且可以预测病情进展，但据我们所知，本研究是首个在CKD猫中研究IS与FGF-23之间关系并将其与其他因素同时评估的研究。与其他研究类似，IS和FGF-23随着CKD严重程度的增加而升高，并能够作为猫CKD进展的预测指标。有趣的是，多元逻辑回归显示，在调整了其他已报道的与病情进展相关的变量（如HCT、Hgb和磷）后，IS和FGF-23仍与CKD进展独立相关。此结果确认了IS和FGF-23与CKD进展相关的重要性，这些发现与人类医学和兽医医学的先前研究相似。然而，我们还利用这两个因素构建了一个在预测病情进展时具有更高AUC的方程，因此同时使用这两个因素总体上是一个更精确的测量方法。
Although both IS and FGF-23 have been reported to be biomarkers for the severity of CKD and to be predictors of progression, to the best of our knowledge, ours is the first study to investigate the relationship in cats with CKD between IS and FGF-23 and to evaluate them simultaneously along with other factors. Similar to other studies,4, 14 IS and FGF-23 increased with the severity of CKD and were able to act as predictors of CKD progression in cats. Interestingly, multivariate logistic regression showed that IS and FGF-23 were independently associated with CKD progression after adjustment for other reported progression-related variables, such as HCT, Hgb, and phosphate.16 This result confirms the importance of IS and FGF-23 having association with the progression of CKD, and these findings are similar to previous studies in human medicine6 and veterinary medicine.4, 14 However, we also used both of these factors to construct an equation that has a higher AUC when predicting progression, and thus using the 2 factors together is a more precise measure overall.
与先前在人类慢性肾脏病（CKD）患者的研究相似，我们的结果也显示FGF-23和IS不仅呈显著相关性，而且彼此之间具有显著的线性关系。值得注意的是，在汇总其他被认为会影响FGF-23浓度的变量（如血清肌酐和磷浓度）后，IS是唯一不能通过逐步回归法剔除的变量，并且与FGF-23有显著对应关系。此外，血浆IS和FGF-23浓度均与血清磷浓度显著相关。研究表明，在患CKD的人类和猫中，成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF-23）-23（FGF-23）会随着血清磷浓度增加而升高。高磷血症被认为是FGF-23分泌的生理刺激，而FGF-23的增加是一种旨在纠正异常血清磷浓度的反应；它通过抑制肾小管钠依赖性磷转运蛋白的表达和抑制活性维生素D（calcitriol）的生成来实现。在我们的研究中，令人惊讶的是，IS与磷的相关性高于FGF-23。
Similar to a previous study in humans with CKD,15 our results also show that FGF-23 and IS not only show a significant correlation but also have a significant linear relationship with each other. Noticeably, after pooling together other variables that are believed to affect FGF-23 concentration, such as serum creatinine and phosphate concentrations, IS was the only variable that cannot be removed with the stepwise procedures and has significant correspondence with FGF-23. Furthermore, both plasma IS and FGF-23 concentrations are significantly correlated with serum phosphate concentration. Fibroblast growth factor-23 has been shown to increase in parallel to serum phosphate concentration in humans9 with CKD and cats12 with CKD. Hyperphosphatemia is thought to be the physiological stimulus for FGF-23 secretion and the increase in FGF-23 is a response that is aimed at correcting the abnormal serum phosphate concentration; it does so by suppressing renal tubule sodium-dependent phosphate transporter expression and inhibiting the production of calcitriol.17 In our study, surprisingly, IS showed a higher correlation with phosphate than FGF-23 did.
此外，当控制了IS时，FGF-23与磷之间的显著相关性变得不显著。相反，当控制FGF-23时，IS与磷仍保持显著相关性。一项最新研究表明，IS可能通过氧化应激和破坏成骨细胞功能诱导继发性肾性甲状旁腺功能亢进。成骨细胞和骨细胞是FGF-23合成和分泌的主要来源。此外，一项研究还发现，对慢性肾病（CKD）大鼠施用IS会降低肾脏中klotho的表达，klotho是FGF-23受体相互作用中的辅因子，可增强其降低磷的功能。这些发现表明，IS可能通过干扰FGF-23的降磷功能而对矿物质代谢产生不利影响。此外，我们的结果还提示，在患有慢性肾病（CKD）的猫中，IS也可能是影响FGF-23浓度并参与磷代谢的潜在因素。然而，IS、FGF-23和磷之间的相互作用仍需进一步研究。
Additionally, when IS was controlled for, the significant correlation between FGF-23 and phosphate became insignificant. In contrast, IS and phosphate do maintain a significant correlation when FGF23 is controlled for. A recent study indicated that IS is a potential factor in the induction of secondary renal hyperparathyroidism via oxidative stress and impairment of osteoblast function.7 Osteocytes and osteoblasts are the primary sources of FGF-23 synthesis and secretion.8 Moreover, 1 study also has determined that administration of IS to rats with CKD decreases renal expression of klotho, a cofactor in FGF-23 receptor interactions that enhances its phosphate-lowering function.18 These findings imply that IS may have a deleterious effect on mineral metabolism, which occurs via a disturbance of the phosphate-lowing function of FGF-23. Furthermore, our results also suggest that IS in cats with CKD also may be a potential factor affecting FGF-23 concentrations and be involved in phosphate metabolism. However, the cross talk among IS, FGF23, and phosphate warrants further study.
在我们的研究中，IS 与 UPC 显著相关，但通过调整 UPC，IS 和 FGF-23 对 CKD 进展的预测能力均下降至不显著。同样，作为一个重要的风险因素，蛋白尿也已被报道与猫的 CKD 进展相关。众所周知，IS 通过氧化应激导致肾小球硬化，并可损伤肾小球足细胞，进而导致肾小球滤过屏障缺陷。最终，蛋白质过度滤过可能会使近端小管细胞超负荷。此外，IS 可能会对肾小管细胞造成损伤，并导致白蛋白重吸收减少，最终导致蛋白尿增加。因此，这种蛋白尿可能进一步损伤小管细胞，导致 IS 积累。综合来看，蛋白尿和 IS 对肾小管的有害影响可能是造成 IS 预测能力受 UPC 影响的原因。
In our study, the IS was significantly correlated with UPC, but the predictive power of both IS and FGF-23 regarding CKD progression was decreased to insignificant by adjusting for UPC. Similarly, proteinuria, as an important risk factor, also has been reported to be associated with CKD progression in cats.16 It is well known that IS leads to glomerular sclerosis through oxidative stress18 and can injure the glomerular podocytes,19 which then results in defects in the glomerular filtration barrier. Eventually, excessive filtering of protein may overload the proximal tubular cells. Additionally, IS might cause damage to renal tubular cells1 and lead to decreased reabsorption of albumin and finally to increased proteinuria. Consequently, this proteinuria might further damage tubular cells resulting in the accumulation of IS. In combination, the detrimental effects of both proteinuria and IS on renal tubules might be the reason why the predictive power of IS was affected by UPC.
然而，尽管当前的设计阻止我们评估这些推测，但仍然需要进一步的研究。据报道，更高浓度的FGF-23与慢性肾病（CKD）患者的蛋白尿有关。氧化应激与klotho表达不足有关，并最终会导致FGF-23抵抗和蛋白尿。因此，蛋白尿和FGF-23浓度升高对肾小管的类似影响可能是UPC能够影响FGF-23预测能力的原因。然而，在我们的研究中，FGF-23与UPC没有显著相关性（数据未显示），需要进一步研究来探讨这一问题。
However, although the current design prevented us from evaluating these speculations, further studies are warranted. Higher concentrations of FGF-23 have been reported to be related to proteinuria in humans with CKD.20 Oxidative stress was related to deficient expression of klotho and eventually will cause both FGF-23 resistance21 and proteinuria.22 Therefore, the similar effects of proteinuria and increased FGF-23 concentrations on renal tubules might be the reason that UPC is able to affect the predictive power of FGF-23. However, FGF-23 was not significantly correlated with UPC in our study (data not shown), and further investigations are needed to explore this issue.
在我们的研究中，晚期猫的FGF-23浓度与之前的研究结果有所不同。12, 14 我们使用的ELISA试剂盒是用于检测猫完整FGF-23的（支持信息S1），与之前报道的用于检测人类FGF-23的试剂盒不同。尽管该试剂盒已成功用于猫的研究，但该试剂盒对人类和猫样本的反应水平（交叉反应性）是否相似仍需厂家确认。对反应水平差异的一个可能解释是样品稀释效应（即反应超过测定线性范围后，需要进一步稀释样品），这可能开始表现出反应的不成比例增加。23 此外，我们的研究重点是进展组和非进展组之间的差异，其中一些数据来自年轻和病情较轻的CKD猫，这可能导致FGF-23中位浓度较低，并大多处于测定的线性范围内。
In our study, the FGF-23 concentrations of late stage cats were different from those obtained in previous studies.12, 14 The ELISA kit we used was designed for the detection of feline intact FGF- 23 (Supporting Information S1), and it was different from the previously reported kit, which was designed to detect human FGF-23. Although this kit was used successfully for studies in cats, whether the response level (cross reactivity) was similar for human and for feline samples remains to be confirmed by the manufacturer. One possible explanation for the difference in the response level is the sample dilution effect (in which after the response goes beyond the linear range of the assay, the sample must be further diluted) and might start to exhibit a disproportionate increase in response.23 In addition, our study focused on the difference between the progression and non- progression groups, in which some data came from younger and less severe CKD cats that could have brought the median FGF-23 concentration lower and mostly within the assay, linear range.
我们认为，由于使用了不同的已验证ELISA方法，这两组数据都是可靠的，并且具有独立性。然而，未来仍需进一步研究以揭示不同ELISA试剂盒在不同动物物种中在特异性和反应强度上是否存在差异。我们的研究存在一些局限性。首先，由于FGF-23和IS的结果来源于先前存储的血浆样本，并非所有病例都能获取所有血液和尿液变量。其次，尽管没有统计学显著差异，但CKD第4期猫似乎比CKD第2期和第3期猫更年轻，这可能被认为有争议，因为年龄可能是导致CKD晚期的一个因素。尽管样本量，尤其是CKD第4期猫的样本量较小，可能无法代表更大范围的猫群体，但这些猫可能同时存在可能影响肾功能的不同肾脏疾病。
We believe that both sets of data are legitimate and stand on their own, given the fact that different validated ELISAs were employed. However, further studies are warranted in the future to decipher whether different ELISA kits show differences in specificity and response intensity in different animal species. Our study had some limitations. First, because FGF-23 and IS results were derived from previously banked plasma samples, not all of the blood and urine variables could be obtained for all cases. Second, although there was no statistically significant difference, CKD stage 4 cats seemed to be younger than CKD stage 2 and 3 cats, which may be considered controversial, given that age can be a factor leading to a later stage of CKD.24 Although the sample size, especially the CKD stage 4 cats, was small and may not be representative of the larger feline population, it is possible that different kidney diseases that may compromise renal function also coexisted in these cats.
如果没有活检和进一步检查，慢性肾病（CKD）的确切原因无法确定，这可能会限制我们结果在其他类型肾脏疾病中的应用。第三，FGF-23 的辅因子 klotho 的表达没有同时评估，这限制了我们解释 IS 与 FGF-23 相互作用机制的能力。第四，缺乏连续测量，因此无法比较这些尿毒症毒素在慢性肾病（CKD）进展中的持续变化。第六，由于随访时间不固定，进展天数的计算可能不够准确。因此，需要进一步的研究来回答这些问题。
Without biopsies and further examinations, the definite cause of CKD cannot be ascertained and might limit the application of our results to other types of kidney disease. Third, the expression of klotho, the cofactor of FGF-23, was not evaluated at the same time, which limits our ability to explain the mechanism of the interaction between IS and FGF-23. Fourth, serial measurements were not available for comparison of the continued change of these uremic toxins in CKD progression. Sixth, because the duration of follow-up was not fixed, the calculation of progression days might not be precise. Therefore, further studies are required to answer these questions.

5. 结论

5. CONCLUSIONS
总之，IS与FGF-23独立相关，这提示IS可能是调节患有CKD的猫矿物质代谢的重要因素。不仅FGF-23，IS在磷代谢中也发挥重要作用，而且它们是与肾功能相关的独立因素。因此，在研究猫的磷控制和矿物质代谢时，应同时考虑它们。IS和FGF-23都似乎是有前景的肾脏生物标志物，可用于早期检测CKD猫的疾病进展。
In conclusion, IS is independently correlated with FGF-23, suggesting that IS may be an important factor in the regulation of mineral metabolism of cats with CKD. Not only FGF-23, but also IS play important roles in phosphate metabolism, and they are independent factors in relation to renal function. Thus, they should be considered simultaneously when examining phosphate control and mineral metabolism in cats. Both IS and FGF-23 appear to be promising renal biomarkers for the early detection of progression in cats with CKD.
